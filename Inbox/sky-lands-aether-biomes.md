- 2026-11-15 18:42 ## Findings: Sky Lands biomes for Aether adaptation

Sky Lands (modrinth.com/mod/sky-lands, MC 1.20.1, Forge, GPL-3.0, by DeepCanionStudio, 97.7K downloads) adds sky-island biomes plus an underground dimension. Source: github.com/DeepCanionStudio/SkyLandsForge. Requires Geckolib, PlayerAnimator, Cloth Config API, Terralander — all client-side mods Powers doesn't use, so any adaptation would need Fabric-native reimplementation.

### Sky biomes (overworld islands)
1. Golden Islands — golden floating islands (main biome)
2. Floating Cherry Grove — variant of Golden Islands
3. Floating Swamp — variant of Glaciated Islands
4. Glaciated Islands — ice-themed floating islands
5. Lavic Islands — lava-themed floating islands
6. Arid Islands — desert-themed floating islands

Each has island clusters, great sky islands, floating towers/dungeons. Structures include cherry floating tower, cherry floating dungeon, swampy great sky island.

### Underground dimension "The Abyss" (v0.6.0, Dec 2025)
Y 0-384, inspired by Tears of the Kingdom's Depths. Enter via master key + underground portal entrance structure near bedrock.
Biomes:
- The Underground — sulfur spikes, celestial trees, water deltas
- Abyssal Forest — fossils, giant celestial trees
- Abyssal City — skyscraper complex

Structures: Roof Scraper, Lighthouse, Celestial Root (giant celestial wood tree).

### Notable mechanics
- Movement: slow fall, sky dive, glider with stamina system; "Light Weight" enchantment reduces stamina use
- Custom cloud system: smaller particles in clumps, fade in/out, bigger Y range in sky biomes
- Celestium ores (Inky, Pearlecent, Pale) — progression tier above netherite; Sword of the Sky infusable with celestium types
- Celestial Wood tree family
- Grimstone stone family; sulfur blocks/spikes/sand
- Mobs: Trust Phantom (rideable), Sky Cow, Happy Slobert (tameable), Artificial Aracnid

### Comparison with Powers' Aether (jaymospowers:aether)
Powers already has 16 Aether biomes across 4 climate zones (Highfields, Magnetic, Arctic, Irradiated) + Hestveil Caverns + Expanse, generated by tools/gen_aether_world.py. Sky Lands' 6 sky biomes are fewer but each has distinct visual identity (cherry grove, swamp, glaciated, lavic, arid). Powers has no underground counterpart to The Abyss. Sky Lands' glider-with-stamina is close to Powers' aercloud gliders (durability-based, cold/golden/blue/purple). Sky Lands' custom cloud particles could inspire Powers' aercloud rendering. Celestium progression mirrors Powers' ambrosium/zanite/gravitite tiers.

### Adaptation angles for the Aether
- Add cherry-grove, swamp, glaciated, lavic or arid variants as new Aether biomes (Powers uses biome tags + climate routing, so new biomes plug into the existing depth/router system)
- An underground Aether dimension (The Abyss analog) would be new territory — Powers has no sub-dimension
- Glider stamina system could replace or augment the durability-based aercloud gliders
- Custom cloud particles could enhance Powers' aerclouds
- Celestium-style ore tier could inform future Aether gear progression
