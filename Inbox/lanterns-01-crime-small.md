**Andy's words:** "done. add it to the vault for pickup later, in order of build size" (after ticking these rows on the Buder's Lanterns pick list).

**What's wanted:**

- **Sleepers get woken and told** (`cr-wake`): A witness wakes a sleeping housemate and tells them, so a quiet crime at night can still come out. _Our side:_ `Theft#witnesses` skips sleepers today; the witness (or the runner from patch 4, if it is in) wakes the nearest sleeper of the faction.

**Where to start:** Theft#witnesses, Suspicion (guard watch).
