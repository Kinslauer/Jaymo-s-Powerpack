# Astronomy art: high renaissance, drawn as a study

The look for the whole astronomy set. Andy: "add this but in the high renaissance artstyle", with a reference image he supplied.

## The reference

`Assets/astronomy-reference.png` in the vault (also `E:/Jaymo Vault Live/Assets/astronomy-reference.png`). A da Vinci notebook page: an old scholar at a table, a long wooden telescope on a tripod, and the background full of faint circles, diagrams and handwriting.

## The four rules

1. **Ink, not paint.** Sepia to brown-black line on warm paper white. Saturated colour only where a material demands it — brass, glass, flame.
2. **Hatch for volume.** Shade with layered parallel lines that follow the curve of the thing; no gradients, no soft shadows, no glow.
3. **Leave the working visible.** Faint circles, centre points, guide lines and margin notes stay on the page. The picture should look worked on, not finished and varnished.
4. **Instruments are studied, not sleek.** Wooden tubes bound with brass, ring joints, tripod legs that look jointed. Nothing sci-fi, nothing glossy, no floating parts.

## What that means per asset

- **Journal and study pages**: this is where the style can be literal — parchment, ink, circular diagrams, a real hand in the margins. Full room to work.
- **Armillary sphere**: the centrepiece. Brass rings on a wooden base, drawn with hatching, should read as a workshop object from three feet away.
- **Telescope**: long wooden barrel, brass fittings, tripod. It should look like it was made, not summoned.
- **Item icons (16×16)**: only suggest it — brown ink lines, one hatch direction, no gradients. Do not try to fit a drawing into a pixel grid; make it read as an engraving at a glance.

## Notes

- Our own art, in our own style: nothing is copied from the Astronomy mod (its code and catalog are MIT and cited, its art is not needed).
- Keep one palette across every asset in the set, so a telescope and a journal page look like they came from the same desk.
- Deliver art with the code PRs that need it (`astronomy-sky`, `astronomy-telescope`, `astronomy-armillary`, `astronomy-scholar-journal`, `astronomy-sun-moon`) rather than as one giant drop; the first one that needs art is the telescope.
