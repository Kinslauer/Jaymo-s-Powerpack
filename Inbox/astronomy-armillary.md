# Astronomy 3: the armillary sphere

The measuring instrument, and the centrepiece of an observatory room.

## What's wanted

From the pick-list, row **The armillary sphere**: an instrument that measures anything already discovered — position, location, world time — with animated mechanical feedback, and records the measurement.

## Notes

- **Depends on `astronomy-sky`** (discovered stars) and pairs with `astronomy-telescope` (the same reading rules: weather, daylight, horizon).
- This is the object the high renaissance art direction exists for: brass rings, wooden base, a study drawn in three dimensions. It should look like something a workshop built, not a magic device.
- Animated feedback means the rings move while it measures — that movement is the whole charm; do not skip it as polish.
- A measurement is a record, not a score popup. Where it is written down belongs to `astronomy-scholar-journal`.
