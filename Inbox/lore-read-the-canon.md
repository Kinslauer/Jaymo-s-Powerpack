## Canon check, 2026-11-11 (Claude, cloud)

Read `Lore.md` in full and checked it against the codex books, every lore lang part (patron, commandments, creed, tier,
heritage, spirit*, history*, hollow-blight, heavens, bestiary, underworld, story*, lore-films, prejudice), the Powers
lore lines (chrono omen pieces, Galactic Patrol stories, rig logs, Maw, afterlife) and `docs/Spirits-Plan.md`.
Nothing was changed, in the page or the mods. What disagrees, worst first:

**The page against the game**
1. **The spirits.** The page says "four mage-spirits wake with you". In the game one spirit wakes per player, for
   life, out of five (Ambrose, Grimsby, Thessaly, Varro, the Quiet One), unlocked 1 in 18 on a brush with death.
2. **Varro led orcs.** The page says the spirits held the Overworld against the Warrior-born *and the orcs*. Varro says
   "I had orcs under me once. Leagues of them" and "My legions wore my mark" (spirit-varro teach.1/teach.3), and
   in-balming turns beaten orcs into allies.
3. **Are the Warrior-born still raiding?** "The vacuum" says they thinned and orcs and pillagers took their place, but
   the heritage entry says they "have been raiding other people's worlds ever since", and in the game Warrior-born
   Raiders come down in pods today (zfighter_patrol story.6, greet.pursuit.2). The page needs one answer.
4. **What ruined Los Perrito.** The page: the same war that destroyed the Maw, the dead Warrior-born still fighting it.
   The chrono omen pieces tell a different fall: the city's own androids "stopped answering and started marching",
   then the dead got up; "Nobody has found out why". No Warrior-born. They are also first-person accounts of the fall,
   which sits close to "nothing explains the old war".
5. **The long defence was against the Grue.** The page has the spirits fighting the Warrior-born and orcs. The book
   and film *The Long Defence* show five holding a ford against a legion from the dark with one figure at the back
   (the Grue), and Grimsby, Thessaly and Varro all say they beat the Grue once ("I bled to drive him out once",
   "We buried it"). The page only hints at it in the Grue's own line. Worth adding to "The long defence".
6. **"Earth".** A dead future rig's log says "It's just more Earth, burning" (rig fallen.lore.0). The page never names
   the world Earth (only the Overworld; "Earthling" is a heritage).

**Things in the game the page doesn't cover yet (for lore-canon-rewrite)**
- **Rubber-folk**, the 22nd heritage ("born at the fairs where the strangest folk gather"). The page says
  twenty-two but lists twenty-one.
- **The far worlds**: Rustfall, Glacia, Cinder, the Pale Moon (tall dark watchers), and the green Verdant World
  (Verdant heritage text says "like the folk of the Verdant World"; a start is "Raised under the green sky"). The page
  has Verdant as an Overworld blood.
- **The Other World**: the dead wake there (a site in the Aether), with halos; the Divine-born start comes from it.
  The page has the Aether only as the sky world.
- **Demons**: the demon master says of the old war "We remember it. We were there." The page has no demons.
- **Humans and the havens**: villagers split folk into humans and non-humans, and non-human havens grow "every year"
  (prejudice haven/gossip lines). The grudges section doesn't have the human/non-human line or the havens.
- **Galactic Patrol** and the pods' maker ("some old genius ... and his big clock-machine just weren't there").

**Small wording mismatches**
- Grimsby: "four hundred years of study" (page) but also "four hundred years dead" (grue.warn.2) and "Forty years of
  steel" (clash.win.1), which reads more soldier than wizard.
- The page writes "Biobuilt" and "Divine"; the game says "Bio-built" and "Divine-born". One Powers wish says
  "Frost-born".
- The page calls the Quiet One "it"; the book calls the fifth figure "he".

Nothing else I found contradicts the page: the eight faiths, the creeds, the tiers, the Knowledge ladder, the hollow
blight, the Grue's and the Quiet One's lines, the heavens rumours, the Wet Page and the Maw starts all match it.
