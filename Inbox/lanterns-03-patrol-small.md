# Lanterns patch 3 (S, Powers): Patrol: relief assignments and your Patrol file

**Andy's words:** "done. add it to the vault for pickup later, in order of build size" (after ticking these rows on the Buder's Lanterns pick list).

**What's wanted:**

- **Relief assignments** (`pt-relief`): Errands from the Patrol that aren't fights, such as bringing herbs grown on a far world to a town's healer (their example: a 6-herb supply run). _Our side:_ `ZFighterSpaceMissions` + `PatrolWrit`, using far-world crops; given by patrolmen.
- **Your Patrol file** (`pt-file`): A records page: your rank, warrants served, arrests and assignments done. _Our side:_ A tab on the star map or the patrolmen's talk screen; reads `ZFighterPatrol` rank data.

**Where to start:** ZFighterPatrol, ZFighterSpaceMissions, PatrolWrit.
