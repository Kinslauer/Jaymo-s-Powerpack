# Astronomy 5: sun, moon and eight phases

## What's wanted

From the pick-list, rows **Eight lunar phases and a Sun you can observe** and **Looking at the Sun without Optics blinds you**:

- A simple orbital model, eight lunar phases, a Sun and Moon you can observe and record.
- A click on the Sun before the Optics skill applies a simulated blindness.

## Notes

- **Depends on `astronomy-sky`** for the observation rules, and on `astronomy-scholar-journal` for where the records go.
- Our nights already have weather, cold and monsters; phases should give moonlit nights a rhythm — a full moon that matters, a new moon that is genuinely dark.
- The blindness should reuse the feel of our existing blindness work (a darkening, a moment of not being able to read the sky) rather than a new full-screen effect. Do not promise it protects a real player's eyes anywhere in the text; the source mod was careful about that and so are we.
