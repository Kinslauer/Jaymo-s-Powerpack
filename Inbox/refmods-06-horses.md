## Patch 6: Horses: Icy's Better Horses rows
Owns: kingdoms equestrian.
ON HOLD: Andy asked whether to put Icy's Better Horses in whole (decision in the thread). Do not start until that is answered.

- **R1 A command wheel for your horse** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). Hold a key near your horse: follow, stay, graze here, this stable is home, go home. We have: The trust whistle in Bonding.
- **R2 A stable ledger of your horses** (Icy's Better Horses, size M). A ledger book (bought from a stablehand) listing your horses, where each was last seen and how it's keeping. No command needed. We have: Stables board horses.
- **R3 Look a horse over before you tame it** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). Look at a wild horse with a saddle in hand to see its breed, sex and a rough read of its build. The scouter (S2) reads exact numbers. We have: Horse Expert is in the pack; our hidden stats stay hidden.
- **R4 A medkit on the saddle** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). Saddlebag bandages a farrier sells: used once on their own when the horse is badly hurt.
- **R5 Through the leaves, up the steps** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). Horses push through leaves and bushes and step up a block on their own; they swim with their head up instead of sinking.
- **R6 Mine from the saddle** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). No slow-down breaking blocks from horseback.
- **R7 See past the horse** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). In first person, looking straight down fades the horse's head and neck.
- **R8 A cart that ploughs, a wagon for six** (Icy's Better Horses, size M). Hitch a plough to a draft horse to till a field row by row; draft horses pull the big wagon our caravans use. We have: Caravans, wagons, rickshaws.
- **R9 Charge, kick, and nerve** (Icy's Better Horses, size M). A galloping horse rams what it hits; a horse kicks back at foes behind it; green horses bolt in a fight, trusted and destrier horses don't. We have: Courage spooking (equestrian stage 1).
- **R10 A gift for each breed** (Icy's Better Horses, size M). Destriers barge fences down, steppe ponies herd loose livestock, desert horses shrug off heat and sand, drafts pull heavier loads, coursers keep their wind longer, palfreys never throw a rider. We have: Breeds differ by stats only.
- **R11 Bring back a fallen horse** (Icy's Better Horses, size S). A dead horse can be brought back once by a healer or priest, for a price. We have: Your call: you ruled horse death permanent until a necromancer power can raise them from a mane lock.
