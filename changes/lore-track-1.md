# Draft lore, Track 1

(new) **The crater spider.** The Mad Tinkerer sweeps star-iron filings off the floors of the old pods, and a grey cave spider that gets into them and is kept under glass takes on something of the star: its bite is not poison alone but a summons. A person already leaning towards the web is woken by it; anyone else only sickens. Nobody made it. It is what the fallen stars do to what lives near them.

(new) **Deeds.** A power sleeps in a person until the wait is over, but it can be woken early by a deed that matches its nature: the wind answers to lightning, gravity to a fall survived, the cold to a night frozen through, the deep to a drowning refused, smoke to a fire breathed, time to a night watched with a clock, the arsenal to a kill among many blades, the fist to a hard fight won while hurt. A deed wakes only the power already stirring in that person; it never gives another.

(new) **The Draught of Mettle.** An alchemist's brew of air and spirit, steeped with a rabbit's foot, a phantom's membrane and sugar. For someone with no power in them it lends a long while of a quick, light body and one more leap in the air, a taste of what the gifted carry always. A gifted body rejects it and only turns sick.
