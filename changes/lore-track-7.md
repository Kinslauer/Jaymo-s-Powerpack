# Lore draft, track 7 (fusing with a villager) - (new), for Andy to approve

(new) **Fusing with a villager.** The old dance was made for two warriors, but it only asks that two people want to be one for a while, and a villager who has come to trust a fighter will answer it: the family of the house, the one who has been fed and defended and traded with for a year, a hired hand who has stood at a Z Fighter's back. A stranger just steps away. The fighter keeps the body and the will; the villager rides inside, safe as a coin in a pocket, and lends what their hands have always known: the farmer's green thumb, the mason's quick grip, the cleric's slow mercy. When the dance ends they are set down beside the fighter unharmed. They remember a warm, wide afternoon with no edges, and most of them cannot say more, but they greet the fighter afterwards as one greets someone who has seen them at their best.
