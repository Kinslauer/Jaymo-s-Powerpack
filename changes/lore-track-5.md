(new) **Costume patterns and plated costumes.** A costume is sewn by the village tailor, and the tailor works from patterns: folded paper scraps marked in chalk with a cut, a mask or a cape. The old tailors passed their patterns down and lost many; they turn up in buried chests, in bastion treasure, in a wandering trader's pack, and on the bodies of the costumed gifted, who sew their own and keep the patterns they wore. Anyone who studies a pattern can sew that piece from then on. A costume can be plated: the tailor sews the cloth, and the smith fits the plates, copper, iron, bronze, steel or the black plate of netherite, over the breast, brow, thighs and feet. Plated costumes turn a blow a little, as armour does; the folk say a costume is no replacement for mail, and whoever wears real armour in a place wears the plate for show. A costume can be hung on an armour stand, and swapped for the one you wear.
