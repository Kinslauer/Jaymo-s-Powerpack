# Lore drafts, track 6 (for Andy to approve)

(new) **The golden shell.** The Divine-born do not armour themselves; they are left unhurt, and the light gathers. Where nothing has touched them for a while, a little gold settles round them, a heart's worth at a time, slow as dew on a high place. A blow scatters it, and the calm has to be earned again. The Judge's people call it the shell, and say it is not given but kept.

(new) **The ward.** A Divine-born who has been through the high places can draw their light out wide enough to stand a few people in, and for a few breaths the ground inside is theirs. Friends and the folk they shelter are mended there, and what means harm is pushed to the edge and held, not struck. It tires them, and they will not do it twice in a minute.

(new) **Banishment into smoke.** A Smoke user who closes their hands on a creature does not kill it. They hand it to the smoke, and the smoke keeps it, unseen and unmoving, where it stood, for as long as they can hold the knot. Strike the knot and the smoke tears and gives the creature back, giddy and slow. The great and the old (the Dragon, the Wither, the Warden) and people are too much for it: the smoke slides off them.
