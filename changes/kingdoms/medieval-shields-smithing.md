# Medieval patch 8: shields and smithing
- **Great goats**: big mountain goats live in herds in the high mountains. Only a Stoneborn can tame, saddle and ride one. Anyone else gets reared at, though anyone may feed one. A great goat is sure-footed: it steps up a block and a half, jumps high and shrugs off falls. Struck, it butts back. It crops the grass and plants of its slope (grass blocks go to dirt and only grow back from the turf around them), and once its slope is bare its herd moves on uphill. A tamed goat its keeper feeds comes to trust them: each feeding makes it a little quicker and surer under its rider, and one that trusts enough gives its Stoneborn keeper milk. Our own model and coat (`beasts/GreatGoat`, `client/beasts/GreatGoatModel`, `GreatGoatRenderer`, `tools/gen_great_goat.py`).
- **Mountain goblin raids**: a goblin village up in the mountains or the high hills now swarms down on the north's settlements (snowy or taiga land) even when it's fed, in a bigger horde than a camp's raid (`Raids`).
- **Realm designs and steels** (with Jaymo's Powers): a kingdom keeps its knights' helm and a war arm as its own designs, which only its sworn smiths (a fiefdom's go by their overlord's) or a holder of a captured pattern can forge (`HeraldryWear.mayForge`). Deep steel is the Stoneborn's to work, bright steel the Sylvan's (or a haven of their blood, or a mixed haven's), and ash bronze belongs to havens that live alongside the tribes (`PowersCompat`).
- The Inscription Table needs the same Knowledge as the enchanting table (`Knowledge`).
