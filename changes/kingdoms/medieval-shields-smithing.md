# Medieval patch 8: shields and smithing
- **Great goats**: big mountain goats live in herds in the high mountains. Only a Stoneborn can tame, saddle and ride one. Anyone else gets reared at, though anyone may feed one. A great goat is sure-footed: it steps up a block and a half, jumps high and shrugs off falls. Struck, it butts back. It crops the grass and plants of its slope (grass blocks go to dirt and only grow back from the turf around them), and once its slope is bare its herd moves on uphill. A tamed goat that is fed comes to trust its keeper: each feeding (at most one every five seconds) makes it a little quicker and surer under its rider. One that trusts enough gives its Stoneborn keeper milk, and a trusting pair fed their wheat will breed, more readily in autumn (the rut) with Jaymo's Kitchen's seasons. A grazing goat lifts its head and moves on after each bite. Our own model and coat (`beasts/GreatGoat`, `client/beasts/GreatGoatModel`, `GreatGoatRenderer`, `tools/gen_great_goat.py`).
- **Mountain goblin raids**: goblin villages are now also founded in the mountains, hills and groves (with the usual site checks), and one up there, or high enough anywhere, swarms down on the north's settlements (snowy or taiga land) even when it's fed, in a bigger horde than a camp's raid (`Raids`).
- **Realm designs and steels** (with Jaymo's Powers): each kingdom has its own marked version of its knights' helm and a war arm. Its knights wear the helm and carry the arm, and one a player slays sometimes leaves a captured pattern of one of them. Only its sworn smiths (a fiefdom's go by their overlord's) or a holder of a captured pattern can forge that version. The plain helm and arm stay anyone's (`HeraldryWear`). Deep steel is the Stoneborn's to work, bright steel the Sylvan's (or a haven of their blood, or a mixed haven's), and ash bronze belongs to havens that live alongside the tribes (`PowersCompat`).
- The Inscription Table needs the same Knowledge as the enchanting table (`Knowledge`).
