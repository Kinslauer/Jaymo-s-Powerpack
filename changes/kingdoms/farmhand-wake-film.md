# Family farmhand: kit only in the chest, the wake-up talk as a film
- **No more doubled kit**: the farmhand's starting kit goes into the chest by the bed and no longer still shows on you. The kit was handed out with a slot packet per item and taken back out the same tick, so the inventory menu never told your game it was gone; `FarmhandWake.stow` now sends the whole inventory over once it's emptied.
- **The wake-up is a letterboxed scene** (Andy: "the letterboxing from waking up should stay for a speaking scene with him ... panning to the chest at the end"): black bars come in as your eyes open and stay for the whole talk, the HUD clears, and the camera is from your pillow. It starts on the ceiling, comes down to the farmer, and frames whoever is talking with the words over their head. The farmer walks over and stands still to talk, and says it all: how they found you, carried you in, that your things are in the chest, the welcome, and a new line on farm work (fields by day, harvest in the house chest, indoors by dusk). The child, if there is one, chimes in once. Someone of another blood hears the farmer's kindly "not of our blood" line here now, not behind the loading screen. At the end the camera pans to the gear chest and holds, then you're up beside the bed facing it.
- Andy's film rules: plain FOV for the whole film (`FilmPlainFovMixin`), nothing can hurt you while it runs, you aren't drawn (the camera is at your head), and the in-bed screen is hidden and ignores keys, so Escape can't get you up mid-talk. Backspace skips it (hint in the lower bar); the rest of the talk goes into chat and you get up facing the chest.
- Classes: `FarmhandWake` (Shot/Skip payloads, speaker hold, damage guard, stand-up), `FarmhandHome`, client `films/FarmWakeFilmClient`, `HandshakeCameraMixin`, `FilmHidesViewerMixin`, `InBedChatScreenMixin`, lang `farm-wake.json`.
- Built; not played.
