# labyrinth-builders' craft: rune gates, finder compass, bombardier beetles, living braziers, the Afterblow
- **Labyrinths**: when a player first comes near a labyrinth that holds a minotaur, its halls get braziers (about a third of them are living braziers that wake and spit fire when you step close or strike them). The minotaur's hall is shut behind rune gates across its doorways; three rune tablets (Anthu, Kesh, Orrim) lie each in a chest in the other halls. Use a tablet on a gate to set it in a socket (every block of the gate takes it); with all three set the gate opens for good. Behind it, on a pedestal, lies the Afterblow. If a player is already in the minotaur's hall, or the hall has no doorways to shut, it stays open.
- **The Afterblow**: a bronze blade; every blow that lands is followed 8 ticks later by a second at three quarters of your strength.
- **Labyrinth Compass**: built from three geared plates, a compass and a bronze ingot; held in a hand it turns toward the nearest labyrinth (spins when none within 64 chunks). The plates turn up in chests in drowned colonnades (any chest in a structure, or a chest table, with "colonnade" in its name; Moog's own ids were not checked).
- **Bombardier beetles** (the crystal beetle's model, crystals off, repainted rust and blue-black): wild in savanna, jungle, badlands and desert. A player who isn't sneaking coming within 3.5 blocks, or anything that hurts it, gets a boiling spray (3 damage and set alight); it holds three bursts and makes one more every 15 seconds. Sneak up with a glass bottle to milk a bombardier gland. The **Gland Flamer** (bronze ingots, flint and steel, a glass bottle) burns one gland every 2 seconds while you hold use, in a short cone that sets creatures alight and never blocks.
- **Beetle colonies**: in the same lands a great tree (7 or more logs of trunk) now and then holds a colony: a queen at its foot, workers and pupae (small and still, they hatch in two minutes). She gnaws the highest log of her tree every minute (she lays a pupa each meal, up to eight beetles), swarms to found a colony in another great tree when full and fed, and with no wood within reach lays no more. A log held out to her is a meal (the tree is spared); every third log she gives two glands. If she dies the workers scatter and leave.
- Built; not played. Not tested in-game: gate and brazier placement in a real labyrinth, the colonnade plate chance, and the compass needle are the parts most likely to need a tweak.
