# duellists - footwork, closing blows, punishing, flinch (BossRise plan, patch 1)
- Andy, on the pick list: "PLEASE, this is the MOST IMPORTANT part of the modpack, we NEED good combat". Armed humanoids already swung their weapon's combo (`MobCombat`); this adds what goes on round the swing (`Duellists`), for guards, crews, raiders, orcs, illagers and armed skeletons, friend and foe. Zombies still only walk at you.
- **Footwork**: after its own blow a fighter circles to a side for a moment; careful ones (timid, suspicious, gloomy, veterans) step back out of reach instead. The hotheaded, brave and spiteful never give ground, and a shield-bearer holds the front. Nobody steps off a ledge or into water.
- **Closing blows**: from 4 to 7 blocks a fighter may leap in with its weapon's heaviest move, with the usual glint. Step aside and it misses.
- **Punishing**: eat or drink in front of a fighter, or turn your back, and it drops its guard and comes at you: a closing blow from range, a quicker swing up close.
- **Flinch**: a hard blow on a fighter that is winding up spoils its swing (a fifth of its health in one blow, more for a veteran; any two-handed weapon). Bosses never flinch.
- **Roles**: a leader with two or more of his band still fighting fights carefully and doesn't charge. When a barbarian chief or raid captain falls to a player, the timid of his band run, the gloomy, suspicious and greedy often do, the brave, hotheaded, proud, dutiful and spiteful never.
- MCA villagers only start their footwork once they have struck at you once (their melee task only reports in then).
- Not built here: bowmen keeping behind the shield-bearer, and an animation for the flinch (the spoiled swing still plays out, it just doesn't land).
