# The Hollow's blight reworked (signs, cures, spread), new horde kinds, the dead's looks
- **How it takes you**: armour wards off a dead one's blow (up to 70% with full armour; broken armour wards nothing), and it gets in twice as easily through a bleeding wound. A fresh blight settles in for 2 minutes, and binding the wound in that time clears it most times. Only someone with Medicine training feels it go in.
- **Wider symptoms**: hunger or slowness once it takes hold, weakness and nausea near the end, rests between bouts, and one short withering in its last hour.
- **Signs others can see**: the skin greys toward the Hollow being made (hidden by a full mask), a wet cough now and then (our own synthesised sound), and flies at the end. Villagers who hear the cough say so ("That's no regular cough."), back away and think less of you.
- **Telling a tainted wound**: anyone with a Medicine perk sees grey-green motes on a blighted body that only they see, and a town cleric who sees you says so.
- **Tainted flesh**: raw rotten flesh can give you the blight. Seared flesh (smelted, smoked or campfire-cooked rotten flesh) is safe. Gilded flesh (seared flesh in 8 gold nuggets) is the old druid remedy: it sets the blight back a third of its course, and villagers sneer at it.
- **The Hollow's Cure**: brewed only by the old druids of the groves (give them gilded flesh and an empty bottle) or a Druid-class player (gilded flesh on a water cauldron over a fire, with a bottle). A bottle dipped in a witch's hut cauldron finds the one she hid there (once a hut), and witches sometimes drop one. Give it to a sick villager to cure them.
- **Witch thralls**: after dark a few zombies rise from the mud by a witch's hut (up to 3) and keep it. Her potions never hurt them, and they go for whoever strikes her.
- **Cured for good**: every cure (the Cure, a golden apple while weakened, a healer, a totem) leaves you unable to catch it again, kept through death. A held totem spends itself instead of letting you turn, and that counts as a cure.
- **Temple healers** burn it out for 12 emeralds' worth; a healer who minds the Hollow turns an unmasked Hollow away.
- **How it spreads**: near the end a carrier's cough can pass it to whoever stands close in front (a full mask keeps most of it out), and a carrier's own blows pass it once it has hold. Zombie blood on your skin or armour can give it to you, more through an open wound; scrub it off, or get soaked through to wash it all away.
- **Villagers catch it** from the dead's blows, carriers and zombie blood: they cough and slow for a day, then rise as zombie villagers unless cured. Kin who see it grieve. A villager you cure remembers it (a new memory, +6 hearts).
- **Guards keep the sick out**: a righteous guard who hears your cough orders you out of town; still there and coughing a minute later, you're taken (downed or yielding) and shut in the cells until you're cured or turned. A cure lets you out at once. Hotheaded and spiteful guards just attack.
- **Outbreaks**: now and then a town near you falls sick and its sick pass it to their neighbours. The town board then posts Cure the Sick and Burn the Blighted Dead work ahead of other jobs.
- **The blighted dead rise**: a villager who dies with the blight gets up the next night as a zombie villager unless the body (Jaymo's Kitchen's corpse) is burned or buried.
- **New horde kinds**:
  - Builders carry a few blocks (shown in the off hand) and, when they can't find a way to you, pillar up, lay steps or bridge a gap. They only use what they carry, and what's left drops when they fall. Only with mob griefing on.
  - Runners sprint once they see you and leap 1 to 2 block gaps. More of them as the danger climbs.
  - Brutes are big, slow, tough to shove and to kill, and every blow they land on a door counts three times. Grave-captains stay the rarer leaders.
  - Scouts are quick and frail and range ahead of the horde. When one finds you it howls: the rest come and keep on your trail a while, and the scout hangs back out of reach.
- **Tools and sieges**: a horde zombie with the right tool (pick on stone, axe on wood, shovel on dirt) breaks through much faster, and the dead's old tools now include a wooden pick. Stay within a small area after dark and, once you're dug in, waves keep coming every few minutes until dawn, each bigger than the last.
- **Horde animations**: arms reach and claw as they walk, a shoulder into the door they pound, a stagger when hit hard, a lunge at the last step, and a scout's head thrown back to howl. Fresh Animations steps aside for them.
- **Where the dead rose**: the present's zombies are dressed by where they rose: dried in deserts, badlands and savannas, bloated by swamps, rivers and shores, pale from caves, the deep dark and deep sunless ground, frostbitten in snowy and frozen lands, else plain. Each wears its land's villager clothes and keeps its look wherever it walks (RisenLooks).
- **Three stages of rot**: still with its face and nose, down to bone at the hands and shins, or a skull in rags with the ribs showing. 189 new skins of our own (kingdoms/tools/gen_old_dead.py). The old dead now wear their skins in every world but Chrono's future, not just the Overworld.
- **Eyes that glow**: at night a cold light sits in every zombie's and husk's eyes, brighter and red under a blood moon (DeadEyes).
- **Tissou's Zombie Pack** (by Tissou, with thanks) joins the pack for the future only: half of the future's zombies wear its skins when Entity Texture Features is loaded and the pack is on, tails and all. While it is on, the present's drowned, zombie villagers and villagers keep the game's own textures (TissouPack, deadlooks mixins). Its zombie sounds replace the game's everywhere while it's on.
