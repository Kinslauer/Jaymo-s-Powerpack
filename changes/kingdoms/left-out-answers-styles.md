# Seven sword styles, each trained at its master or learned from a found book; Wheel Cut on a held attack
- Andy's answers on the left-out page (2026-11-12).
- **Styles** ("you should have to train every one of the styles or study technique books that are findable around"). There are now seven styles: Iron Gate, Long Road, Still Water, Long Measure, Deep Root, Low Flame and Red Ember. You start knowing none. A school's master teaches its style once you stand well with the school. Iron Gate and Long Road have no school: you learn them by drilling, hitting a Training Post or a Mannequin dummy (300 and 700 blows). Each style also comes as a technique book, found now and then in smithy, armoury, stronghold library, dungeon, outpost, mansion, bastion, temple and shipwreck chests; read it to learn the style. Sneak and use a Training Post to switch between the styles you know that fit your weapon, or go plain (`Styles`).
- **Wheel Cut** ("Hold attack"). Hold attack for half a second with a blade to start Wheel Cut, if you know it. The belt still starts it as before (`Forms.HoldAttack`, `FormsClient.holdAttack`).
