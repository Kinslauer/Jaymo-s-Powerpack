# Archers take posts they can reach and stand on
- Archers posted at keeps, citadels and seats now stand on the wall walk, tower roofs and roof floors they can path to, not on battlements and parapets they can't reach.
- A post is used only when it has two air blocks above it and a solid floor beneath that isn't leaves, and seats skip roofs with no ladder, vine, scaffolding or door access nearby, so archers no longer post inside blocks or in mid-air.
- Built; not played in game.
