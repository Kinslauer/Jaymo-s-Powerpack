# Youth answers follow-up questions, speech bubbles
- **Youth** answers a follow-up without its name: while your own wisp is beside you, a line that asks something (a "?"
  anywhere, or starting with what, how, why, where, who, can, is, does and the like) goes to Hermes like a "youth, ..."
  line. Before, Evgeny called it ("YOUTH!"), then asked "what are these things that show when i sneak?" and got
  nothing. Other chat near the wisp ("lol nice") still isn't sent. The right-click hint now says so too.
- **Speech bubbles**: everything Youth says (Hermes's answers, "be right back", the trip home, its jokes, the
  right-click hint) also floats over the wisp in its leaf-green, like villagers' talk; players nearby see it too. Chat
  still gets the whole line, since a long answer is cut to the bubble's five rows.
- Classes: `inhabitants/YouthWisp` (`QUESTION`, `heard`, `speak`), `inhabitants/YouthCommand` (`sayall`). Lang: `lang/parts/youth_wisp.json`.
- Built (compileJava); not played.
