# Proving grounds: old-temple champions fought in an arena of your own
- **The offering** (Andy's pick from the Kageborne gap check: boss trials through altar offerings, in arenas of their own; the idea only): a lit old-age temple altar now wants its patron's offering **and 16 emeralds** (`ProvingGrounds.EMERALDS`). Without the emeralds it smokes and says so, and nothing is taken. With them, the patron's light takes you, and everyone in your party within 16 blocks of the altar, up to a **proving ground**. The altar goes out until it relights, as before.
- **One ground per fight**: a new lightless void world, `jaymoskingdoms:proving_grounds`, holds the fights in plots 512 blocks apart. Each fight takes the first free plot, raises a round stone floor (radius 14, a low wall, a ring of lamps) and clears it after, so two groups never share one. A champion left there by a crash is removed when its chunk loads.
- **The fight**: three seconds after you arrive, the patron's champion comes down in a beam across from you. It's the same champion as at the altar, scaled to how many of you came, with the same rewards (weapon-head chance, the patron's boon, deeds). Once it falls you get 15 seconds to pick up what it left, then you go home. Anything still lying there goes home with you.
- **Falling costs only the fight** (Andy): dying or going down on the ground carries you back to where you stood, standing up at low health, with everything you carried. The others fight on. When the last one falls, the trial is lost and the offering is spent. It's also lost after 10 minutes, or if the champion vanishes without falling.
- **Logging out, the server stopping, a crash**: back where you stood at your next login.
- Without the grounds (a world missing the dimension), the altar calls the champion into the open world as before, with no emeralds taken.
- Classes: `oldage/ProvingGrounds` (new), `oldage/OldTemples` (offering), `PowersCompat.getUp` (a downed player stands up through Powers).
- Built; a dev server started to Done and loaded the new world. Not played: no one has made an offering, fought a champion on the ground, gone in as a party, fallen or logged out inside.
