# Medieval patch 1: Webbed up
- **Spiders web you up** (with Jaymo's Powers): a cavern spider's spit, a flyer's dragline as it hauls you up and a mother's bite wrap a layer of Powers' Webbed round you. Without Powers the spit still holds you in a cobweb as before (`wild/SpiderKinds`, `PowersCompat.web`).
- **Waking in a spiders' lair**: on your sure wakes, if a spider downed you or you fell underground with spiders near, you come to wrapped head to foot in their lair: a little way off a spider nest (a spawner among cobwebs) if one is near, else a round hollow they dug in the ground under where you fell, with a burrow up to the air. Everything you carried is in a chest by the wall, cobwebs round it and eggs laid on top (`wild/SpiderLairs`, through `PowersCompat.tellLairs`).
- **Guttering lamps**: lanterns and soul lanterns in abandoned places (ruins and other old structures with no living villagers near, the Irradiated remnants round their monoliths, the seats of fallen settlements) gutter down near dark and catch again, sometimes stuttering, while someone is near to see. Where folk live or travel again they burn steady. A guttering lantern still burns its fuel and goes out when it's spent (`GutteringLamps`, `mixin/LanternBlockMixin`).
- **The Everburning Mote**: hand a court wizard a lantern (or a burnt-out one) and they name their price; hand it again and pay, and they shut a mote of their light in it. The mote lantern never needs fuel, never goes out and never gutters; a little mote of light hangs in its glass (`MoteLanterns`; art from `tools/gen_webbed.py`).
- Built; not played.
