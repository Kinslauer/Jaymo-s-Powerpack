# Hunters Road 3: grave creatures and bone blocks

Adds the Hunter's Road 3 cave mobs and their blocks, all built from our own Blockbench models and GeckoLib animations:
- grave-gnawer (hunched pack corpse-eater) plus a bigger spined pack-leader variant
- bloated dead (swollen) plus a spined variant
- grave-brute (deep-cave miniboss, big charger)
- bone-burrower (burrows and leaps) plus a heavy variant
- a nest block of bones and filth
- old-bones blocks (old skeleton, fallen warrior)

Each mob has idle, walk, run and attack animations, plus lunge (gnawer/burrower), swell (bloated dead), charge (brute) and burrow/emerge (burrower). Textures are flat-shaded vanilla-style pixel art with no gradients or noise.
- Built with ./gradlew build; not tested in game.
