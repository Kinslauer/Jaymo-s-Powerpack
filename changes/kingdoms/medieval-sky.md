# Medieval picks, patch 4: The sky

- Sky pirates: once a steam town stands, a sky cove may rise on a high peak near a kingdom. It has a tarred shack over a hoard of powder kegs, gunpowder and shot, a fire, and a mast with a black flag over the drop. Moored in the open sky beside it is the pirates' airship, black-bagged with a red band, two guns a side. She roams the sky round her cove. Whatever her lookout spots (a ship, an airship, a caravan's cart, a player on the ground, a town) she circles for a while out of reach, then comes down on it. Ships get broadsides as she circles close; anything on the ground gets bombing runs. Once she is spent she flies home to restock from the hoard. Her captain is the cove's leader: kill him and the cove is cleared. Lose her without him and the cove builds another in two days. Their flying follows Valkyrien Pirates (JSJBDEV, MIT), with credit; our own code on our own ships, no Valkyrien Skies. (`SkyPirates`, `SkyShips`, `Camps`, `Raids`, `Corsairs`)
- Kill the helmsman and she drifts: a ship with nobody steering her drifts on the wind, and an airship also sinks slowly to the ground. This covers a crew's ship whose captain is dead, a steam town's airship or sky ferry with nobody at her wheel, and a player's own ship. (`Ship`, `Winds`, `Fleet.wheelManned`, `SteamAirships`, `SkyFerries`)
- Realm air patrols: a rich realm (treasury of 1,000 or more) keeps a patrol airship in its own colours. The nearest steam town lends her for a fee out of the treasury. She circles the seat and watches for war parties, raiders, outlaws and bandits near the realm's land. Over a band she fires a real signal rocket from her hold and blows a goat horn that carries far over the land. The seat's bell rings the alarm and its people run in. Nothing is said in chat. She fights any sky pirate in reach with her gun. Lose her and another is lent a day later. (`AirPatrols`, `WarAlert.sighted`)
- War drums: an orc or barbarian band marching on a town beats a war drum (our own sound, `tools/gen_war_drum.py`). You hear it from far off, louder as the band closes, and mobs hear it too. (`WarDrums`, `Raids`)
- Airship guns and bombs: airship guns load from the ship's hold, a gunpowder and a ball spent each time; with neither left, the guns go quiet. Her gunners lay their guns well down at what's below. A trapdoor hatch in her deck lets powder kegs (TNT from the hold) fall, the fuse cut to the drop. Her crew bomb through it, and a player aboard can too by using the hatch. No keg in the hold, nothing falls. (`SkyShips`, `Ship`, `Corsairs`)
- Boarding by hook: throw the lasso up at an airship and the loop catches on her rail or side, holding as she flies. Hang below her and hold jump to climb the rope; at the top you're over her rail onto her deck. Sneak, or sneak and use, lets go. Her hands come at anyone hostile who boards her, before they shoot at other ships. (`Lasso`, `Corsairs`)
- Downed airship wrecks: now and then in the mountains or a forest you come across a steam town's airship that came down there. Her deck lies broken over the ground and her bag lies slack and torn across it. Her stores barrels are thrown about, and her dead lie by her: Kitchen's bodies with Jaymo's Kitchen, bones and a skull without it. Her log in her chest names the town she left, the one she was bound for, and which way and how far that lies. (`SkyWrecks`)
- Redstone cannon: the ship's gun on its carriage works ashore off redstone too, the same real cast-cannon model with no dispenser. Set a chest, barrel or hopper against its breech: a pulse loads it from there (a gunpowder, then a ball, both spent) and fires it. If there is nothing to load, it clicks. (`ShipCannonBlock`)
