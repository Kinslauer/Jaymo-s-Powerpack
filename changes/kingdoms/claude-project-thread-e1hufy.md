# skill-tree locks - abilities everyone had are learned in the tree now
- **Andy's 52 picks** (pick list https://claude.ai/artifact/CHjt6P5YQaJiohpLGURnsr, answers https://claude.ai/artifact/S9LXDMdLmvLgYsDkddr7sp): moves and gear every player could use from the start are learned in the skill tree now. Until then they're refused with a line on the action bar naming the perk ("You haven't learned that yet: Slide (skill tree, K)") and a dull sound, and a villager who sees you fumble sometimes says they had to learn it too (8 lines, at most every 90 s). `TreeLocks` holds every lock (perk, rank, the classes that get it free), the station map and the checks; Powers asks it by reflection.
- **48 new perks**, after Air Roll so no saved ranks move. Stealth: Slide, Long Jump, Charge Jump, Strong Swimmer (swim burst and breach), Path of Stealth, Second Calling. Strength: Shove, Called Shots (aim at body parts), Sweep Kick, Boxer, Haul the Bound, Command (orders), Two Blades, Shieldbearer (slam, charge, brace), Thrower (thrown weapons and the belt), Shield Vault (Combat Arts), Swat (Will), Path and Second Calling. Survival: Horseman (whistle, then tricks; Rider free), Skyfarer (paraglider, then balloon), Read Scrolls (Mage free), Sorcery and Forbidden Arts (Sorcerer only), Path and Second Calling.
- Existing perks now gate too: Wall Runner the run up a wall, Freerunner rank 1 the trick jumps, Shield Throw the shield throw, Rally and War Cry the no-power Second Wind and Battle Cry, Light Fingers pickpocketing and Lockpicking Prowess picking locks (Thief free for both), Meditation the meditate key (it already was).
- **Trades page** (Survival, new): Smith, Brewer, Sawyer, Angler, Alchemist, Smelter, Millwright, Gemcutter, Whaler, Rigwright. Each trade's stations refuse to be used (sneak with a block in hand still places against them) and refuse to come out of a crafting grid until it's learned; a saw cuts only for a Sawyer; the harpoon throws only for a Whaler.
- **Heritage page** (Survival, new): Blood Awakening wakes your blood's own moves (its heritage-key move and its held move); its description names your blood's move. Gifts that need no key stay free, and so do letting go of a Bio-built or Rubber-folk hold and bringing a swallow back up.
- **The Night page** (Stealth, new), hidden until you're turned (or a dhampir or wolfblood): Lifeleech, Night Sense, Groundpound and Howl, Bat Wings (shown only to vampires).
- **Class paths** need a Path perk (any pillar, level 10); without one an existing path lies idle and the Growth screen says why. A **second class** needs a Second Calling perk (level 15, after that pillar's Path) instead of the Skeleton Lord.
- **Agility** is traversal speed now: its share goes on swimming strokes and ladders, vines and scaffolding too (`AgilityTravelMixin`), and Powers puts it on wall climbing, leaf climbing, shimmies and climbing over ledges. Its K-screen line says so.
