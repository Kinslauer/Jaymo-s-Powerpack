# The Long Defence: jointed actors, a move library, and the film on tape
- **Every actor in the film is a GeckoLib model now** (`FilmActor`, drawn by `FilmActorRenderer`): men, slim women, orcs and zombified piglins share one jointed body with elbows, knees, hands and a waist (`art/film_actor*.bbmodel`, from `tools/gen_film_actor.py`). The host's Warrior-born are the draugr drawn alive, which got the same joints. The old posed player models (`FilmPose` and its hooks) are gone.
- **A move library anyone can draw on** (`tools/humanoid_moves.py`, 40 moves, written into both the film actor's and the draugr's animation files; names in `Moves`): the gaits the game plays (idle, walk, run), swings (slash, backhand, overhead, chop, thrust, lunge), block, stagger, disarm and being disarmed, point, four casts, kneel, kneel up, bow, rise, flip, roll, three deaths, and the dead clawing up out of the ground. Any GeckoLib humanoid with the same bone names can play them (`triggerAnim`).
- **The film is recorded once and replayed.** The performance is staged in the game one time and written to a tape (`FilmTape`: the cast, the set, where every actor stood and faced each tick, and every move, weapon change, wound, sound, particle, lightning bolt, thrown head and tether). The tape shipped with the mod (`assets/jaymoskingdoms/films/long_defence.tape`, about 1.3 MB) is what every player now sees, the same every time, on any ground. With no tape the film is staged live as before.
- **Re-recording:** in creative, `/kingdomfilm record long_defence` stages it afresh and writes `<game folder>/jaymoskingdoms/films/long_defence.tape`, which is played in place of the shipped one. `/kingdomfilm play long_defence` plays it.
- **The Quiet One** is the white-hooded, white-cloaked first thief. His cloak has four tethers with a mind of their own: two throw themselves at each tree ahead and swing him round it (he flips and rolls off the trunks), two take the sorcerer's guards by the chest and throw them aside without his looking. He carries a rapier (the pack's, where Jaymo's Powers is loaded; an iron sword otherwise).
- **Fights read better:** blows are blocked on shields and blades, finishers use the library's moves, Varro's disarm has room between him and the orc, and you no longer stand in your own shot (`FilmHidesViewerMixin`).
- **The forest burns** (brought over from the other film branch and put on the tape): lightning and the sorcerer's bolts light the trees, the fire climbs and spreads all battle long, and once the sorcerer is gone the defenders still standing run to the burning trees and beat the flames down. The film is 43 seconds (`LoreFilms.FILM_TICKS` 860). Anything else that throws fire in the film can light trees with `LoreFilmClient.igniteNear`.
- **Golems:** the redstone and skulk golems' GeckoLib renderers are registered again (`BeastsClient`). A merge had put back the old ones, which load Blockbench models that no longer exist and failed the client's resource load.
- Watched in a dev client start to finish, staged live and replayed from the shipped tape. Not watched in the full pack, and the dev client has no sound.
