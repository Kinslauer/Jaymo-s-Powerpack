# the Reaper - death magic's own spirit
Andy: "anyone attuned to death magic or warlocks now attune to the same magic as the grue, meaning they gain an evil mana
spirit like entity called the reaper. it grows in stages ... death magicians get stronger the more corpses are nearby. the
same for them all, unlike the 5 spirits, and is immune to the grue's corruption as they are one in the same", then "it should
be visible for ANYONE who is near death ... its death itself", "whenever a ghost is nearby ... animated harvesting it with a
scythe", and "the reaper is now one you can pact with".
- **Who has it** (`Reaper`): a Warlock whose pact is with the Reaper, and anyone of another class who has learned a
  forbidden death spell (not the old raising and draining scrolls). Never Death Sorcerers (Andy: "death sorcerers should NOT
  be attuned"), never Blood Sorcerers, never a keeper of one of the five spirits, and none of the five ever answers its keeper.
- **The Reaper as a Warlock's pact**: a ninth choice beside the eight patrons. It has no shrine and no favour; its debts are
  blood or "a death by your hand" (paid by your next kill). It starts you with Blight and lets you learn every death spell.
- **The Toll** (0-100) is paid by the dead: 1 a kill, 2 a body raised or one of the downed reaped, 1 a corpse harvested, 3 a
  ghost. Five stages at 0/20/40/60/80, each announced by one line of hint:
  1. **The Pall**: smoke off the shoulders. The dead near by make your magic stronger (below).
  2. **The Mantle**: thicker smoke. It acts on its own: under 30% health it slows and weakens whatever hunts you, and once a
     day it refuses a killing blow and leaves you on half a heart. Each act flashes the screen's edges red.
  3. **The Reach**: the hooded figure looms over your back (Minecraft Comes Alive's own Grim Reaper model and texture, used
     from MCA's jar), and the spirit key (L) is its hand: at a foe, **Grave Hand** drags them to you; at a corpse, it drags
     that; at one of the downed, **the Reaping** executes them; at nothing, **Reap** sweeps what is in front of you, and what
     it kills gets up as your thrall for a minute. Sneak + L on a corpse is **Harvest** (the body is drunk for mana).
  4. **The Wing-arms**: the Grue's wings out of its back, opening when it strikes. They stop one hard blow every 30 s, and
     sneaking while you fall lets you down gently.
  5. **The Shroud**: the wings fold and shadowflame climbs round you. Whoever strikes you from close by burns and withers, the
     undead leave you alone unless you hit them first, and sneak + L with no corpse in sight is **Death's Company**: every
     body within 12 blocks rises (up to 8, every 3 minutes), the first as a lasting grave-captain with double health.
- **The dead near by**: each corpse within 12 blocks (Jaymo's Kitchen's; without it, deaths in the last minute), counted to
  8, adds 5% to your magic damage and takes 3% off your mana costs.
- **The hunger**: three days with no kill and no corpse near and it takes a heart of your max health a day, to three; back
  the moment it is fed.
- **Who sees it** (`ReaperClient`): anyone at all who is at or under 30% health or downed, attuned or not; nobody else, its
  keeper included, who in health has only a HUD line (stage, Toll, dead near by). The exception is a ghost: with a grave's
  ghost or a ghostly monster (vex, wraith) within 10 blocks it steps out beside the ghost, draws the scythe back and brings
  it down, and everyone sees that. A grave's ghost is gone for good (mana and Toll for the keeper); a monster takes 10.
- **The Grue**: the Reaper's keeper has no spirit for it to take, and their forbidden casts no longer leave corruption.
- **The five spirits**: each has three lines for a Reaper keeper within 16 blocks (once per ten minutes; the Quiet One says
  "Don't."), and a spirit at bond 60 or more halves a Blight laid on its keeper.
- Lore.md has the paragraph "The Reaper" under The dark.
- Built with Gradle. Not played: the figure's place over the back, the wings' fold and the scythe swing are set by eye from
  the numbers. Not built from the plan: NPC necromancers carrying one, a clash film against a mana spirit, the smoke threads
  from each corpse.
