# moose cows - Cube Animals' moose come with our bulls
- **Bulls and cows** (Andy: "keep our bull and the cube moose are cows"): a Kingdoms moose that spawns on its own in the
  swamps and taiga now brings 1 or 2 Cube Animals moose with it, drawn without antlers (their antler scale set to
  nothing). Cube's moose still never spawn by themselves, so a cow is always met beside a bull.
- The bull keeps by them: every few seconds, if its nearest cow has got more than 10 blocks off, it walks back to her
  (never while it is after someone). It still charges any player within 15 blocks, so a herd is guarded. The cows are
  Cube's own animal otherwise: neutral, tamed and bred with spruce saplings, ridden with a saddle; a calf of two cows
  has no antlers either.
- Moose already in a world are unchanged; a Cube moose from a spawn egg keeps its antlers. Without Cube Animals the bull
  spawns alone as before.
- `wild/Moose` (`finalizeSpawn`, `bringCow`, `KeepByCows`). Compiled; not played.
