# cyclops and satyrs (Mythika's, our own make)
- **Cyclops** (`beasts/Cyclops`, `CyclopsCaves`): a one-eyed giant (170 health, club), a rare monster of legend on stony and jagged peaks, frozen peaks, stony shores and windswept hills, only once a player nearby has reached Borderlands. Where it spawns it cuts a hall into the nearest slope: an arched mouth, a fire on a hay bale with signal smoke up a shaft, a fold of three sheep, a shelf of skulls and bones, and a door stone outside. Swings its club, slams the ground for a shockwave ring (8 blocks; jump it), roars. Sleeps by its fire at night and wakes to a moving, non-sneaking player within 7 blocks or a blow. An arrow to the eye (60%) or any blow while it sleeps blinds it for 12 s: it then hunts only by the sound of non-sneaking movers within 10 blocks. Lead one of its sheep away and it comes after the thief. Giant (`jaymospowers:giants`), goes down, dismemberable. Gossip lines, an encounter message, quests and trophies as other legends.
- **Satyrs** (`beasts/Satyr`, `Satyrs`): goat-legged groves of 3-5 in forests, birch woods and meadows (creature spawn, weight 3), under a grey-coated eldest. At dusk they lay a campfire (or use one near home), the eldest plays one of three pipe tunes of our own (`kingdoms/tools/gen_satyr_pipes.py`) while the rest dance in a ring or sit; at dawn the fire they laid goes out. Strike one and the whole grove fights (head-butt charge, kicks) for 2 minutes. Give one honey, sweet or glow berries or wine (`jaymoskingdoms:satyr_gifts`) and the grove counts you a friend: by their fire while they play you get Regeneration and Luck. Goes down, dismemberable; drops leather and sometimes a goat horn.
- Models, textures and animations from our own generators (`kingdoms/tools/gen_cyclops.py`, `gen_satyr.py`); two new loading-screen hints.
- Built; not played.
