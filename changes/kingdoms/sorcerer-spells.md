# Nine new spells: volcanic glass, fire, the black flame and the storm cloak

- **Volcanic glass**, four spells any Sorcerer can learn whatever their element: **Glass Shards** (a fan of splinters
  that cut and open wounds), **Glass Spires** (a row of spikes out of the ground ahead that lift and slow), **Glass
  Skin** (a held coat that turns blows and cuts whoever lands one, bursting after five) and **Molten Glass** (a pool
  that burns, then cools hard round the feet of whoever is still in it, then shatters).
- **Fire**, for fire Sorcerers: **Fire Ring** (a held ring round you that burns and throws back whoever crosses it),
  **Fire Pillars** (three held columns that turn about you) and **Fire Lance** (a stream of fire held for six seconds).
  With Jaymo's Powers the lance meets Wind Whistle, ki waves and the other streams head on; press your class key to
  shove.
- **Black Flame**, a forbidden art like Death's and Blood's (a crime where a realm's people see it, and it leaves
  corruption). Water does not put it out, it leaps once to the nearest body, friend or foe, and only healing magic or
  a dispel ends it.
- **Storm Cloak**, for lightning Sorcerers: quick feet, a shock for whoever strikes you and an arc to a foe close by.
- A Sorcerer who reads one of these scrolls keeps the spell for good (Knowledge 4, as for any spell) and casts it from
  the Magic screen, where learned spells are now listed. A Sorcerer of another element can still read a fire or storm
  scroll once. Mages and other staff casters learn all nine as usual; Warlocks may learn glass, fire and Black Flame,
  Druids Storm Cloak.
- The scrolls turn up on court wizards and camp shamans; Black Flame on necromancers.
