# The Unburied is made of its dead, and comes apart at daybreak
- **Made of the bodies it rose from** (Andy: "can it be made from a conglomerate of the bodies?"): the corpses it gets up from are no longer deleted. Each Kitchen corpse is taken into the giant whole (its saved data, loot and all, kept in the Unburied's own save) and the giant is drawn as them: two bodies stand as its legs (their feet its feet), two hang head-down from its shoulders as its arms (their heads its fists), three lie crosswise heaped as its trunk, one lies across the top as its head. More dead thicken it: a yoke across the shoulders, bodies clinging to its back and front, doubled legs and arms, a crown, up to 24 drawn (any past 24 are carried and fall out like the rest, just not drawn). Each body looks like the corpse it was: its own kind and skin (an MCA villager's face and clothes), parts it had lost missing, eyes shut and limbs splayed as Kitchen draws its dead. The frame moves as the old bone giant's model did: legs and arms swing with its stride, the head body turns to look, and the bodies flash red with it when struck. One raised from a spawn egg (no dead) is still the bone giant.
- **Comes apart at daybreak, never burns** (Andy: "it NEEDS to disappear / dissassemble during the day instead of buring"): once it's no longer dark out it stops where it stands (no target, no moving), groans, shudders and sags, and its dead drop out of it one by one from the top down, every 4 ticks, each a real corpse again tumbling to the ground from where it was in the giant (Kitchen's let-go fall). When the last is out it falls away as bone dust and is gone: no death, no loot. A bone giant from an egg just falls away. One loaded during the day comes apart at once.
- **Killed**, all its dead fall out of it at once (its bone loot still drops).
- The dead it lets go are ordinary corpses again: left lying, eight or more can raise it again the next night; bury or burn them.
- Code: `blossomland/Unburied` (bodies, `SLOTS`, `take`, `spill`, coming apart, save), `NightThings.unburied` (takes the heap instead of deleting it), `KitchenCompat.spill`, client `creatures/UnburiedRenderer` (rebuilds and draws the bodies; Kitchen's Ragdoll/ClosedEyes/Severed by reflection).
- Built; not played.
