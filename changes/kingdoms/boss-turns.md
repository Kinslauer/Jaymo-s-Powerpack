# boss turns - the half-health turn, the fall, armoured hides (BossRise plan, patch 2)
- **The turn** (`BossTurns`): the first time a great foe is brought to half health the fight stops for two and a half seconds. It can't be hurt, it roars, and everyone within 32 blocks who can see it (and is on the ground and not downed) gets a short film of it, through the boss intro camera (`BossIntros.play`, a `mode` on the film packet). Once per foe.
- After the turn, a foe without a changing fight of its own is 15% quicker, hits 20% harder and has one new move, **the shove**: a red glint and a roar, a wind-up of 24 ticks, then everyone it is fighting within reach is struck (60% of a plain blow, can't be parried) and thrown back. That is the minotaur, the gorgon, the Piglin King, the Skeleton Lord, the Giants, the Nether warlords and the Powers bosses in the entrance tag.
- Film only, no shove or buff (their fights already change as they go): the patrons' champions, every dragon, kings and chiefs, the grizzly, the Ogre King, the Wither and the Ender Dragon.
- **The fall**: a great foe killed by a player stands dying for two and a half seconds first (it stops, its head sinks, it shudders and smokes, nothing can hurt it), those near are shown it, and then it dies to the blow that killed it, so loot and credit are unchanged. The dragons, the Wither and the Ender Dragon keep their own deaths.
- **Armoured hides** (`BossPosture.armoured`): the Aether Giants and the Ogre King take half of every blow while on their feet, with a dull ring of metal, and all of it while their posture is broken. Their posture wears as if the whole blow had landed, so breaking it is the way through.
- **Impact frames**: a riposte on a kneeling boss, a shove landing and the killing blow of a fall flash Powers' impact frames (each player's own setting still turns them off).
- **The audit** ("three moves and a turn"): the minotaur (swing, slam, charge), gorgon (arrows, strafing, sky volley), Piglin King (rake, embers, burst, brutes), Skeleton Lord (staff, slam, spell, Grave Call), grizzly (rake, charge, maul, slam), Ogre King (three phases) and Giants already had three or more. Warlords and chiefs fight with their weapon's combo and the duellists' footwork. Nothing needed a new move set.
- Not built: a different death for each boss (they share the one fall), and impact frames on power ultimates (that is Powers' side, patch 12).
- Compiled; not played.
