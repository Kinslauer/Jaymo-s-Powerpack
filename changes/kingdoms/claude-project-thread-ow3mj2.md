# captives explain before you take them home
- **Talk first**: a plain right-click on a captive in chains no longer frees them and sets them following you. They turn and beg you to hear them out (a speech bubble). Sneak and right-click to talk: the talk card has them say who they are (one of the folk, a guard of the watch, or the ruler's kin), the settlement they were taken from by name, and how far and which way it lies from here (a stone's throw, or about so many paces, north-east and so on). Then you choose: "Break the chains and take them home to <town>", which frees them and they follow you as before, or "Not now", which leaves them in their chains (they ask you to come back).
- A captive whose home has fallen, or who was taken from nowhere, asks to be taken to any town and names the nearest one, how far and which way.
- A gagged captive only mumbles on the talk card; "Pull the gag out" takes it off (you get the rag) and they talk at once. A plain right-click with a rag gag in hand ties one on, as the gag was always meant to.
