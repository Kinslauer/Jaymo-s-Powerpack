# the open sea: larger oceans, a higher wavy sea, islands, sea life far out, more ships
- **More sea** (Andy: "the oceans should be larger"): the Overworld's continent noise is pushed further toward the sea (`overworld/continents.json`, offset -0.06 to -0.2). By a rough estimate about half of a new world is ocean, up from about a third. New worlds only.
- **A higher sea** (Andy chose "waves and a higher sea", new worlds only): in a world made from now on the Overworld's sea stands 8 blocks higher (`seas/HighSea`, `HighSeaLevelMixin`). Low coasts and river flats go under and headlands become islands; caves under the new sea fill by the game's own aquifer rules. The land isn't raised. A world first started before this keeps its old sea (a marker file in the world folder decides). No village or other jigsaw building is started where the ground at its middle is under the sea (`HighSeaStructureMixin`).
- **The swell**: out on open ocean, long low waves roll across the water's face, higher in rain and highest in a storm, a little higher over deep ocean (`client/SeaSwell`). Only the look; the water blocks stay flat.
- **Sea life far out**: fish, squid and other water creatures in ocean and beach biomes are mostly turned away near the coast (15% kept at the shore, rising to all of them on open sea), so a coastal walk is quiet and the life is out where the ships go (`seas/SeaLife`, `SeaLifeSpawnMixin`).
- **Islands** (`seas/Offshore` finds islands from the ground's shape: dry land with sea all round within 256 blocks, so hills the risen sea cut off count; `seas/IslandStructure`): island-only content no longer spawns inland. The vanilla beached shipwreck stands only on island beaches. Moog's Voyager Structures' diorite tower and prismarine houses, when that mod is loaded, stand only on islands. Tideborn island villages (`isle_village`, a village on an island) are always Tideborn havens. Sunken shipwrecks lie only out at sea. Buried caches lie on islands (uncharted caches).
- **Sea hermits**: a hermit in the diorite tower is a sea hermit with lines of their own (`Hermits`, `lang/parts/seas.json`).
- **The white whale** is born only far out at sea.
- **More ship blueprints**: cog, frigate, fishing smack and galley build kits (recipes beside the old kits). Charted plans of Jaki's five ships (galleon, drakkar, war trireme, cargo ship, trader) are sold by merchantmen at sea and laid like any ship's plans (`ShipPlans.charted`).
- **Fishing smacks**: a realm with a harbour sends out a smack now and then. She sails to a fishing ground well off the coast, lies there a few minutes working her lines (splashes, a fish now and then), and comes home with her catch for the realm's stores. She runs from pirates (`Fishers`, crew side `fisher`).
- **Hailing at sea**: blow a goat horn within 96 blocks of a merchantman or a smack and her bell answers. She heaves to while you're near. Go aboard and talk with her captain about work to trade: a merchantman sells stores, shot, sails, cargo and charted plans and buys fish and whale oil; a smack sells her catch and buys bread. Prices are in her realm's coin (`SeaTrade`).
- Measured on a dev server (seed 4242, samples over 24,000 blocks square): with this PR's continents, land is about 35% of the world at the old sea and 18% with the higher sea. Islands: an uncharted cache 440 blocks from spawn, an island village about 4,000-6,000 blocks out. Not played.
