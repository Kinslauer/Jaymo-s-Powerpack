# larger oceans
- **More sea** (Andy: "the oceans should be larger"): the Overworld's continent noise is pushed further toward the sea (`data/minecraft/worldgen/density_function/overworld/continents.json`, offset -0.06 to -0.2). By a rough estimate about half of a new world is ocean, up from about a third, and the seas between coasts are much wider (the Large Biomes scale from before is kept). Coasts, islands and continents are still there.
- New worlds only: chunks already generated keep their land, and the edge where new terrain starts in an old world will show.
- Built; not played. The share of sea is an estimate from the noise's spread, not measured in a world.
