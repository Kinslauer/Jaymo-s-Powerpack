# A real night sky

The overworld night sky now shows 8,870 real stars from the HYG catalog, turning with the world's time and your position.
88 constellations appear star by star as you discover them by looking up at night. Light pollution dims the sky near villages; rain thins it like vanilla.

Shader packs (Iris, Sodium) keep their own sky — these stars simply ride alongside it.

Data credits: star positions from the HYG Database v4.1 (The Astronomy Nexus, CC BY-SA 4.0); constellation lines from ConstellationLines (MarcvdSluys, CC BY 4.0). Notices in the license folder and in source files.

Built; not tested in game.
