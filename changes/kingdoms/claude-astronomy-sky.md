# A real night sky

The overworld night sky now shows 8,870 real stars from the HYG catalog, turning with the world's time and your position.
88 constellations connect star by star as you discover them by looking up at night — about four seconds of steady
gazing per star. Light pollution dims the sky near villages; rain thins it like vanilla.

The vanilla sky renderer is never replaced, so shader packs (Iris, Sodium) keep their own sky and these stars simply
ride alongside it.

Data credits: star positions from the HYG Database v4.1 (The Astronomy Nexus, CC BY-SA-4.0); constellation lines
from ConstellationLines (MarcvdSluys, CC BY 4.0). Notices in the license folder and in the source files.

Built; not tested in game.
