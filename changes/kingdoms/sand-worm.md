# The sand worm

- **The Sand Worm.** Going into a desert pyramid wakes the worm under it, once per pyramid, out in the sand beside it.
- Under the sand it can't be seen or hurt, only read: the ground churns and hisses along its path toward you. It can
  only pass through loose ground (sand, gravel, earth), so stone and the pyramid's floors are safe footing.
- Under you it stops, the sand boils for a moment and it breaches, hurting and throwing everyone above it. It then
  stands about eight blocks out of the sand for some nine seconds, the only time it can be hurt, striking down along a
  line at whoever is in reach, with a tell. Hurt it by a fifth of its health in one showing and it sinks early.
- A Stoneborn who sees it come up, player or villager, faints at the sight, once a fight (with Jaymo's Powers).
  Stoneborn villagers have two new things to say that hint at it.
- Our own model and hide (`kingdoms/tools/gen_sand_worm.py`), a boss bar, an entrance film, and a spawn egg.
- Compiled, not yet played.
