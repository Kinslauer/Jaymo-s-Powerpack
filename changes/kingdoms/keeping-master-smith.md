# The Keeping: the smith keeps a master's stall
- **Master smith**: the old home's smith sells nothing while he's kept, sick, or not yet thanked. The master weaponsmith's stall ATi gives him is taken off him, and the game doesn't hand it back on reload. Cure him awake and see The Keeping through to his thanks (any armour piece, or "Nothing"), and he becomes a master armourer with his own stall at the old home, open to anyone and restocked each day. He sells iron armour (helmet 5, chestplate 9, leggings 7, boots 4 emeralds), chainmail (1/4/3/1), diamond armour (13/21/19/13, 3 a day), and a shield (5). He buys coal (15), iron ingots (4), a bucket of lava and diamonds, an emerald each. Cured in his sleep, he never opens a stall.
- The tale's last words from him and the "Nothing" ending now say his forge is lit again.
- `KeepingCells` (MASTER attachment, `ended`, `stall`, `wares`; the prices are its constants), hook in `Tales.end`, `the_keeping.json`.
