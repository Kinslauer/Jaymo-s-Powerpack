# knight quips - rare easter-egg lines in an old knight's voice
- **Quips** (Andy's ask, after the Spyder-Knight of Ultimate Spider-Man): 39 rare lines, all thee and thou, said over the speaker's head (never chat) at the right moment. Knights and guards: "Town fool, come hence, if thou value thy life" (walking up armed to a knight who doesn't know you), "Have at thee!" (a knight's fight begins), "What manner of monster be this?" (you climbing a wall), "Thy trickery cannot pierce mine armour" (a knight's armour or shield turns your blow), cleave our enemies in twain (a great foe near), worse shot than a hero (three arrows missed in a row), squire or town fool (part armour), unarmoured jibes, sprinting into a guard, bumping a knight's horse, a spider about to web you, the arrow of justice, leaving a guard's talk twice. Magic and monsters: witch (casting a spell near a guard), "we call it science" (a Frostborn watching a spell), a guard killing a spider, a great foe at half health, the fortress knight's "Back, lest I be forced to harm thee", the headless rider and zombie knights' "Alas... I was wrong" at a quarter health. Townsfolk: the monster is upon us, touched in the head (you hitting a giant), thank-yous when a great foe falls, a flying player, a teleport ("What sorcery is this?"), a lit redstone lamp or copper bulb overhead, helping a downed player up, a foreign-tongue villager's "from my tone, thou hast reason to worry", a hermit's strong robes, and two rare bits of gossip.
- Odds per moment in `Quips#ODDS` (1 in 1 to 1 in 900); a player hears one line at most once a game day and any quip at most every 30 s, and a speaker quips at most every 20 s. Lines in lang part `quips.json`.
- Hooks: `Arcana#cast` (spells), `Conversations#goodbye` (leaving a talk), `PowersCompat#onWall` (Powers' wall climb), `HeadlessRider.TAG` now package-visible.
- Built (compileJava); not played.
