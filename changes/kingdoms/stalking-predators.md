# predators at home and on the hunt - big cats in the jungles, bears in the woods, every predator stalks like a wolf
- **Homes** (`wild/PredatorRanges`, Andy: "tigers in jungle w other big cats, bears forests"): each predator loses the spawns its own mod gave it and spawns only in its home biome tag. Naturalist's big cats (`jaymoskingdoms:has_big_cats`): the jungles and the mangroves, so the jungle holds tigers, the sparse jungle leopards, the bamboo jungle white tigers and the mangroves black panthers (Naturalist picks the coat by biome); no more tigers in deserts, badlands, snowy plains, cherry groves or mountains. Lions on the savannas in prides of 2-4 (`has_lion`), Cube Animals' snow leopards on the groves, snowy slopes and peaks (`has_snow_leopard`), brown bears (Naturalist's, Bear Minimum's) in the taiga, windswept forest, meadows and groves (`has_brown_bear`, off the frozen peaks and ice spikes), black bears (Naturalist's, Bear Minimum's, Cube Animals') in the forests and plain taiga (`has_black_bear`). The beast talk and the hunt quests (`Encounters`) name the same homes.
- **They stalk you** (`wild/WolfStalkers`, Andy: "they should stalk you the way wolves do"): coyotes, polar bears, Naturalist's tigers, lions, brown and black bears, Cube Animals' snow leopards, tigers and black bears and Bear Minimum's bears (`jaymoskingdoms:stalkers` entity tag) hunt a player as the wild wolves do: by scent and the wind, shadowing you from cover, freezing when looked at, then the rush. On the game's wolves' hours: at night, in mist, or when you're hurt; never on a full belly (`FedPredators`), asleep, tamed, leashed or as a cub. Their own search for quarry no longer sends them straight at a player they'd stalk (`mixin/wild/PredatorTargetMixin`): they still go straight in at someone who walks right up on them (4 blocks), hurts or angers them, or comes near their cubs. A big cat or bear breaks cover with its own call instead of the wolf's growl. The wolves keep their rules as they were.
- **The stealth eye** (`Senses`, Andy: "not activating your stealth eye until your stealth is sufficient or a perk on tree"): a beast stalking you (wolves too) stays off the awareness eye until you reach Stealth 15 or learn **Sixth Sense** (new Stealth perk on the Shadows page, under Shadowed, 1 point, Stealth 5); then the eye fills as it creeps in. Once it rushes you the eye opens red as for any attacker.
- Left as they were: the grizzly (its own warn-roar-charge territory), crocodiles and the other reptiles (their own water ambush), lynx and caracal (Cube Animals doesn't spawn them in the wild), foxes.
- Built; not played. Spawn changes take effect in chunks generated after the update (and animals that respawn).
