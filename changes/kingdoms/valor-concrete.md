# Valor: Concrete look - slabs pillar up out of the ground onto the arms
- **Concrete**, a seventh Valor look (after the concrete sheets of inFamous's concrete power, ideas only): thick rough-cast sheets stacked up the arm, three faces a row, a pauldron at Unbroken. When the coat goes up each slab pillars up out of the ground as a tall column, shortening into its sheet as it slams home, several rising at once from the hand up; the ground at your feet heaves and chips fly up in columns at both sides. Sounds: a grinding piston and heavy stone place going up, stone breaking and deepslate when struck; light grey concrete powder grit.
- Who gets it: any new Valor can roll it; a Rooted Valor's leaning look is now Stone or Concrete, half each. Names: "Slab", "Pillar", "Rampart".
- No new art: the slabs use vanilla's light grey concrete texture, the shell under them, the wings and the King's glint reuse Stone's.
- Classes: `Valor.Look.CONCRETE` (+ `artKey`, `concreteRise`), `ValorPlates`, `ValorLook`, `ValorWingsLook`.
- Built; not played.
