# Family farmhand start is kind to every blood

The family that takes you in at the Family farmhand start never holds your heritage against you: no hearts lost, no dearer trades, no fear, no distaste. Plain folk who welcome a stranger whatever their blood. They may notice your race and say so kindly, but it never carries a cost. The bargain stays "as long as you help if you stay": leaving is allowed, idling is what sours it, not who you are.
