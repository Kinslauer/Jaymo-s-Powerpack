# lasso reworked: whirl, tether, hogtie, struggle
- **Throw**: hold use to whirl the loop over your head; it goes round faster and wider as it builds up (full speed in 1.8 s). Let go once it's up to speed (0.5 s) and the loop flies where you look, faster and flatter for a longer whirl; sooner and it only flops. The rope is 20 blocks long. You walk at 60% while whirling or tying (no running).
- **Tether**: a caught target is on the rope at the length paid out. Walk toward it and the rope winds in (it never pays out again); back away and the taut rope drags it after you. Anything bigger than a person drags you back a little. Use while it's out of reach still hauls it a step; sneak + use lets go.
- **Hogtie**: at its side (3 blocks), hold use 1.5 s on a humanoid (players with PvP on, folk, villagers, illagers, witches, piglins, zombies, skeletons, anything built like a person) to hogtie it. Tied, it lies on its side with wrists and ankles roped behind it, squirming harder as it nears getting free, and can't walk, jump, strike, use or break anything. The rope stays on and walking off drags the body along the ground; let the rope go and they stay tied. A tied body is lifted the way someone downed is (sneak + hold use on it, Powers' Carry): if their armour is too heavy for your brawn a piece comes off first. Picking them up takes the rope off. A tied player's first-person view is down on the ground.
- **Grab**: anything that isn't a humanoid is grabbed with a tap of use at reach: pinned on a short rope (1.6 blocks), it can't attack, and it's dragged after you (you walk 20% slower while dragging anything grabbed or tied).
- **Struggle**: anything roped, grabbed or tied can break out at any time. Mobs work at it all along (on the rope 15 s, grabbed 25 s, tied 45 s at 30 health; stronger ones sooner). Players mash sneak: 8 presses on the rope, 20 tied, and only one press counts every 4 ticks however fast you mash (a bar shows on the action bar). The old sideways/jump mashing is gone. A bare-fisted punch on anyone roped deals no damage: it knocks their struggle back by a fifth and stalls it for 1.5 s.
- Clicking the one on your rope with the lasso works the rope (no villager talk screen by accident). Hogtied or grabbed mobs can't target anyone. Blood Sorcerer rites take hogtied or grabbed captives as before. New loading-screen hint 172.
- `forms/Lasso` (Hold, Tie, TIED/HOLDING attachments), client `forms/LassoPose` (whirl/tying/tied poses, cord layer), `LassoRope` (the whirling ring), mixins `LassoLyingMixin`, `LassoCameraMixin`, first-person hand raise in `LassoSocketFirstPersonMixin`; `PowersCompat.boundCheck/dropFromArms`; lang part `lasso.json`.
