# ranged NPCs carry a sidearm and switch to it up close; their carried weapons show on the body
- **A sidearm at the belt** (`Sidearms`): every guard, archer and gunner, camp or ship fighter, forest ranger and folk fighter who carries a bow, crossbow or gun is given one sidearm, once (one a player takes is not made again): an iron dagger if they wear any metal (mail, plate, a gunner's Templar legs), a stone dagger in leather or cloth, a flint dagger for a camp raider or a pirate (Powers' daggers, else the Rogues' ones, else a sword). A player's recruits get none: they carry what the player gives them.
- **Switching** (`WeaponSense.pick`): a foe inside 4 blocks of a gunner (handgonne, arquebus, plain musket, caravan musketoon) or 6 of an archer (4 for a crossbow already loaded) and the sidearm comes out; the gun or bow goes onto the belt and comes back once the foe is past 9 blocks, after someone else, or the fight is over. A gunner out of shot fights with the sidearm, never with the gun as a club. A musket with a bayonet fixed keeps its bayonet. Each swap trades the two stacks between the hand and the belt, so nothing is made or lost, and an MCA archer's bow (the pack's own stack) stays in the pack. While the sidearm is out the gunner still counts as a gunner: his kit (tassets and sabatons only), his hands and his place on the gun roll stay as they were, and the realm never hands him a second gun.
- **Down or dead**: going down or dying with the sidearm out, the gun or bow goes back to hand first. Taking a downed body's weapons takes the belt's too. The sidearm is never dropped on death.
- **On the body**: what an NPC carries but doesn't hold (the belt's sidearm or slung gun, then the weapons and shield in an MCA villager's pack) is hung on them by Powers' body-weapons layer, up to three (`Sidearms.show` -> `PowersCompat.carry`). NPC archers with arrows left wear a quiver over the left shoulder (`QuiverLayer`, synced `Sidearms.QUIVER`).
- Built; not played.
