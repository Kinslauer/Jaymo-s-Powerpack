# Living nightmares, occult exposure and being overcome
- **Phantoms are nightmares now** (`LivingNightmare`, `PhantomSpawnerMixin`, client `NightmareClient`): no phantom is ever spawned in the world. Where the sleepless night would have sent them (same rules and odds as vanilla), the sleepless player alone sees 1-3 phantoms made on their own client: they come out of smoke off and above, circle closer, then dive at your face and vanish the instant before they strike (a bite in your ears, a jolt of the view, a dark flash). Struck at, one is gone at once. Nobody else sees them, the server never makes an entity, and they never hurt.
- **The living nightmare** (a curse, `LivingNightmare.NIGHTMARE`): a supernatural thing can leave it on you: 6% on a wound from one, 25% from an overwhelming one, 10% on the day's first sight of an overwhelming one, always when you faint at one. It lasts 3-7 sleeps. Each sleep through the night spends one and, 40% plus 0.4% per exposure point (most 80%), brings a waking nightmare: for a minute after you wake, 2-5 phantoms come at you, and the screen's edges breathe a cold dark blue. Living one through adds exposure. A priest's or cleric's restoration lifts it (`PowersCompat.cureCurses`).
- **Occult exposure** (`Exposure`, after Bloodborne's insight: idea only): players start at 0; villagers and folk are born with 0-10 (+20 priests, +6 guards). Gains: a new supernatural kind seen (+3), a kind seen again (+1 a day each, +2 for overwhelming), a supernatural kill (+1 a day each kind, +4 every overwhelming one), a wound from one (+1 a day each kind), a nightmare lived through (+1), a faint (+2), unease faced down (+1). A low tone on each gain, a bell on a new rank, no text. Ranks: Untouched 0, Unsettled 5 (whispers from the way an unseen supernatural thing is), Seasoned 15 (no more faints, unease slower), Initiated 30 (invisible creatures show to you as pale see-through shapes; players excepted), Unveiled 60 (the hidden cursed near you, vampires, werewolves in their own skin, the blighted and stolen-life copies, are outlined in violet; nothing unnerves you). The H menu's Character page shows "Exposure: Seasoned (17)", and "Sleep: Haunted" while cursed. New tags `supernatural` and `overwhelming`.
- **Overcome** (`Overcome`, client `OccultClient`): with an overwhelming thing (the `overwhelming` tag, supernatural bosses, the frightful that are supernatural, Banes, anything supernatural with 100+ health) in sight within 24 blocks, unease builds, faster for the timid and gloomy, slower for the devout, proud and hotheaded, slower the more exposure, none at all for the Brave or the Unveiled. Uneasy (35+): a player's screen edges go cold and their heartbeat thumps louder and quicker; villagers say so in a bubble and may withdraw (timid 85%, gloomy 60%, kind/cheerful 45%, others 35%, dutiful/devout 15%, proud/hotheaded 8%): a fighter (guards, companions, hired swords) breaks off and runs, anyone else runs home. Below Seasoned, at full unease, a 20% chance each half second to faint: players go down in Powers' faint (wakers have new lines for it), folk crumple out cold for 15 s (a blow wakes them).
