# snails slide and squash into slime; glow-wood textures
- **Snails slide** (Andy: "the snails seem to jump like frogs or crickets instead of sliding"): a snail never jumps now. The game hopped it up every one-block ledge on its way, so it bounded about like a cricket. It glides on its foot instead, and at a ledge it creeps up the face, about a block in two seconds, the way real snails go up walls. Drawn into its shell it stays put until it comes out (a hidden green snail was the little green cube hopping about in the clip). Its model glides too: a slow wave runs along it, the tail drawing out and in, easing in and out as it starts and stops, instead of the old tail flick (`monsters/Critter`, `client/monsters/SnailModel`).
- **Snail slime** (Andy: "they should squash into slime and you should be able to collect it"): a snail or slug stamped flat (`wild/SpiderSquash`, new tag `jaymoskingdoms:slimy_bugs`: our snails, Naturalist's and Cube Animals' snails, Alex's Mobs' banana slug) leaves its slime on the ground as an item, Snail Slime, to pick up. Four make a slimeball.
- **Glow-wood textures** (purple and black squares in the same clip): the glow-woods' inkcap, glowcap and bouncecap blocks named 24 textures that were never added (caps, stems, stripped stems, planks, hanging gills, and the mushroom, door and lantern items), so their mushroom trees, planks and items showed the missing-texture checkerboard. They're drawn now from the fungi's own art (`tools/gen_glowwood_missing.py`). The armillary sphere had no blockstate or item file and showed as a missing model; it has both now.
