# tundra - qiviut clothing
- **Qiviut**: a muskox now sheds qiviut (its fine underwool) instead of string. Qiviut makes a cap, shirt, pants and socks (`QiviutClothes`, the same shapes as the leather and wool ones). Worn, qiviut counts for more cold protection than wool does (`WoolClothes.warmth`), and Kitchen's body heat reads the lining as warmer than wool. Textures come from `tools/qiviut_assets.py`; the names are in the new lang part `left_out_tundra.json`.
- Built (`compileJava`); not played.
