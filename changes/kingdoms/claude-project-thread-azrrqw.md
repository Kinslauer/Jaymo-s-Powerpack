# underworld stage 2 - speakeasy raids, smuggling runs, the trade spreading
Andy ticked the left-out row "Speakeasy dens are never raided and contraband never gets smuggled between cities" (planned in #525 as stage 2: "guard raids on dens that run too hot, smuggling runs between hotbeds, and the trade spreading from city to city") with "this needs to be there".
- **Raids** (`DenRaids`): a den that's a known hotbed and runs at heat 70+ may draw a raid (3% each look, doubled while the watch is cracking down). The den's hands warn anyone in the room a minute before. Seen: up to 4 of the realm's free guards from within 96 blocks march on the hatch, throw it open and climb down the ladder. Anyone in the den or its fight pit is charged (being found in a speakeasy) and hunted, then sentenced by Busts as ever. Every barrel below is emptied of contraband. Each guard takes one of the den's hands (fence, dice-thrower, bookie, pit attendant, fighters not on the card), walks him to the ladder and up, and hands him to Arrests (marched before the ruler and sentenced). Down every raider and the den has beaten the watch off: it stays open, the watch cracks down, the Tidings say so. Otherwise the den is shut for 3 days: the hatch is nailed down under an iron trapdoor, nobody is hired, no smuggler goes out, it stops being a known hotbed and its heat drops to 5. Hands no guard was free for, and any found below while it's shut, are taken off by the watch (removed). Unseen (nobody near, or no free guard), the raid happens out of sight with the same result. After 3 days the wooden hatch is put back and the den hires again.
- **Smugglers** (`Smuggling`, a `smuggle` caravan in `Caravans`): a known hotbed sends a man out about once a day pushing a handcart of potatoes and wheat with 12 of the den's good under the sacks. He goes to the nearest other den (the one dealing in the other good first), or about 4 times in 10 to a city with no den. At a den the fence gets that stock for the next day he lays out his wares (more of the other good, at 75% of the price), both cities warm, and the smuggler goes home with the far den's good for his own fence. His cart only shows potatoes and wheat when looked at. With coin in hand, someone who has found either den can buy what's under the sacks from him (Pmeh 6, Shash 14; dealing in the open is a crime if seen). Breaking his cart open spills everything and costs no reputation. A guard of the land he passes who sees him within 8 blocks may search the cart once a run (8%, 50% in a crackdown): the goods are seized and that city's den gets a crackdown. Unseen, the far city's watch finds the goods 10% of the time (35% in a crackdown).
- **The trade spreads**: a city with no den gets 2 points per smuggler visit and 1 per contraband sale a player makes to its townsfolk; at 6 it takes up the trade and gets a back room dealing in what came in, rich or not. The Tidings carry it.
- **Sealed bundles** (new item): each fence sells a Sealed Bundle (4) for each of the 2 nearest other open dens, one a day each, and pays 14 for any sealed for his own. It's contraband all the way (open carry, busts take it) and adds to the far fence's stock.
- Two new loading-screen hints. Iron-trapdoor dens are skipped as Hideout story starts.
