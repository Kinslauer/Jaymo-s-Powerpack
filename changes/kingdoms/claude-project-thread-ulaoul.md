# fighting together - leanings, callouts, quick orders, field medics
- **Leanings**: every fighter has a main and a second habit (biggest foe, shooters, the wounded, guard a friend, mind the fallen, strike first, loot, hang back), drawn once and kept, nudged by temperament and role. They only pick among foes already in the fight, never start one.
- **Callouts**: fighters say what they're doing over their heads (taking a foe, a friend's back, a friend down, going to them, badly hurt, the fight won; from the other side "they're out of arrows", "get the one tending them"). Gold to friends and onlookers, red to whoever they're fighting, in their own tongue and a voice from their temperament. No repeats: a gap per speaker, one line of a kind per side per spot, the quiet speak less, nobody repeats their last six lines.
- **Quick orders** (rebindable, Jaymo's Quick Orders): Go on Up arrow (press on, or scout ahead out of a fight), Come on Down arrow, Wait on Right arrow (hold and stop attacking), Help on Left arrow (get the fallen up, or patch you up when nobody's down). The nearest companion answers aloud.
- **Band leaders**: camp chiefs, warchiefs, captains, warlords and raid captains give the same orders to their band: send someone to a downed comrade, call the band onto whoever is hurting them, spread out when they outnumber you. A leader going down is shouted out.
- **Field medics**: a fighter who minds the fallen kneels by a downed friend of its side mid-fight and gets them up in 7 s; any hit starts it over. Works for your companions on you and for raiders on raiders.
- **Out of danger** (patch 2): a medic who reaches someone downed with foes close round them drags them about 9 blocks clear by the shoulder first (Powers' drag), then gets them up. Your companion drags you out of the press; a raider drags his mate out of your reach.
- **Holding and pinning**: a companion who guards or goes for the biggest may get your foe from behind and hold it still for 4 s while you hit it. Raiders with a mate on you may pin you: you can't walk or jump, a second can join, and you break free by hitting one of them or jumping. Any blow on a holder makes it let go.
- **A leg up**: jump at a wall 2 or 3 blocks high with a companion beside you and it boosts you up. Raiders boost each other up to reach you on a low roof.
- **Before the fight**: a companion who spots foes ahead before anything's started says so, and your companions close up round you. A camp's band that sees you first goes to ground, crouched and still, and springs all at once when you come within 8 blocks, look straight at one of them, or hurt one.
- **Grabbing loot**: fighters who go for the spoils pick up weapons, tools, armour and enchanted things that fall mid-fight. Your companion hands them to you; a raider fights with what it picks up and drops it when it falls; a goblin runs off with it.
- **Foe knowledge** (patch 3): companions earn up to three stars on each kind of creature from the kills they help with. With stars they favour that kind, hit it up to 24% harder, and say they know it; a star earned beside another player is remembered, and they say who taught them.
- **Bands learn you**: every camp, village and realm keeps count of how often it has met you and how you hurt it. From the third meeting they come ready: shields against a shooter, sure footing and toughness against a brawler, speed against a caster, fire resistance against fire. Parries count too (Powers' guard): against a player who parries them, their blows go past the guard and they feint, showing a blow that never comes to draw your guard up early. A band meeting you for the first time may say you fight like another player it knows.
- **Catching up**: hired companions keep learning past ten steps, more slowly, to sixteen; one behind its fellows learns twice as fast until level. Captains catch up too: once a day a standing captain two or more ranks under the best of his tribe may climb a rank.
- **Drift**: your orders, your kills and the companions they travel beside slowly shift your companions' leanings (they say so). A band that keeps losing its archers or leaders sends its next archers out with shields and the rest guarding them.
- **Talk on the road** (patch 4): companions remark on the land as it changes, ruins, a trap close by, a glad or grim town, and on you: badly hurt, starving, new armour, back on your feet. One who saw a quest of a kind through tells you which way and how far its end lies; learned ones (well trained, close, or tongue-speakers) pass on lore tips like the loading screen's. Bands warn you off their camp's ground ("This is our wood, outlander"), know who sent you when your quest is about them, and taunt a badly hurt player, a broken shield and the last arrow.
- **Sitting down together**: sit on a seat by a campfire or at a table with a companion near and one comes over, eats a bite of your food with you (healed and rested), and asks on a small screen: keep or change their second leaning, talk more or less, and take a win or a rescue loud, warm or quietly. A sworn captain also asks how his men should fight. Once a day each; a packhorse hands back what it carried.
- **Camp suppers**: at dusk a camp's band sits in a ring at its fire and eats. Creep up (sneaking, they don't see you past 4 blocks) and the first blow on one who hadn't seen you lands twice as hard while the rest scramble up slowed; sneak and use a spider eye, poisonous potato, pufferfish or poison potion on their fire unseen and that supper sickens them all; walk up openly and they stand and call you out.
- **The bond**: each companion's bond with you rises with MCA warmth (talk, gifts), sitting down together and being got up with you beside them, and falls if you leave them lying downed far off. Close, they greet you when you come back, keep nearer you, and hold out against being turned. A sworn captain's bond makes him likelier to keep faith at a dawn he'd turn on you.
- **Gestures**: idle companions show their main leaning (a guard watches outward, a medic looks over the hurt, a biggest-taker shadow-boxes...); after a win the loud leap, the warm turn to you, the quiet kneel; anyone picking a new foe stops and points at it a moment before going in.
- **Specialties**: a townsman who likes you well gives you, once, his trade's little book: tongue-speaker (you understand every tongue with them by), field medic (minds the fallen, twice as fast), forager (finds food on the road), haggler (cheaper prices beside you), packhorse (carries what you throw at their feet), quiet one (sneaks when you do; distant foes lose them). Use it on a companion to teach them. Band members may be trackers (keep after a player they lost), medics, or tongue-speakers (understand your companions' calls and warn the mate they picked).
