# fighting together - leanings, callouts, quick orders, field medics
- **Leanings**: every fighter has a main and a second habit (biggest foe, shooters, the wounded, guard a friend, mind the fallen, strike first, loot, hang back), drawn once and kept, nudged by temperament and role. They only pick among foes already in the fight, never start one.
- **Callouts**: fighters say what they're doing over their heads (taking a foe, a friend's back, a friend down, going to them, badly hurt, the fight won; from the other side "they're out of arrows", "get the one tending them"). Gold to friends and onlookers, red to whoever they're fighting, in their own tongue and a voice from their temperament. No repeats: a gap per speaker, one line of a kind per side per spot, the quiet speak less, nobody repeats their last six lines.
- **Quick orders** (rebindable, Jaymo's Quick Orders): Go on Up arrow (press on, or scout ahead out of a fight), Come on Down arrow, Wait on Right arrow (hold and stop attacking), Help on Left arrow (get the fallen up, or patch you up when nobody's down). The nearest companion answers aloud.
- **Band leaders**: camp chiefs, warchiefs, captains, warlords and raid captains give the same orders to their band: send someone to a downed comrade, call the band onto whoever is hurting them, spread out when they outnumber you. A leader going down is shouted out.
- **Field medics**: a fighter who minds the fallen kneels by a downed friend of its side mid-fight and gets them up in 7 s; any hit starts it over. Works for your companions on you and for raiders on raiders.
- **Out of danger** (patch 2): a medic who reaches someone downed with foes close round them drags them about 9 blocks clear by the shoulder first (Powers' drag), then gets them up. Your companion drags you out of the press; a raider drags his mate out of your reach.
- **Holding and pinning**: a companion who guards or goes for the biggest may get your foe from behind and hold it still for 4 s while you hit it. Raiders with a mate on you may pin you: you can't walk or jump, a second can join, and you break free by hitting one of them or jumping. Any blow on a holder makes it let go.
- **A leg up**: jump at a wall 2 or 3 blocks high with a companion beside you and it boosts you up. Raiders boost each other up to reach you on a low roof.
- **Before the fight**: a companion who spots foes ahead before anything's started says so, and your companions close up round you. A camp's band that sees you first goes to ground, crouched and still, and springs all at once when you come within 8 blocks, look straight at one of them, or hurt one.
- **Grabbing loot**: fighters who go for the spoils pick up weapons, tools, armour and enchanted things that fall mid-fight. Your companion hands them to you; a raider fights with what it picks up and drops it when it falls; a goblin runs off with it.
