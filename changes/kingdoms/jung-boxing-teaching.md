# Boxing teachers
- **"Speak of the fist"**: the Fist school's master, village elders and veteran guards of the peoples who box teach
  Jaymo's Powers' boxing styles. Emberborn teach the Arrow Slit, Stoneborn the Long Guard, Earthlings the Bastion; the
  Warriorborn and the school's master teach all three. They set the tasks and say how you stand, in bubbles.
- A veteran guard's drill adds to a boxer's style, and orcs, camp chiefs and bandits beaten down or killed with fist gear count toward
  a teacher's tasks.
- Two loading-screen hints (129, 130).
- Classes: `BoxingTeachers` (new), `PowersCompat` (the boxing bridge), `forms/GuardDrills` (one line), the two MCA
  dialogue mixins and `dialogues/main.json` (the new answer), lang part `boxing.json`. Needs Jaymo's Powers; without it
  nobody offers the answer.
- **Not built locally and not played.**
