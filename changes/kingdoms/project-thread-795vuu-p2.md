# Someone else at the helm, and word of the land in the east
- **A captain or a companion sails your ship for you** (Andy: "can there be an MCA captain or companion that can pilot the ship for you? thats just too long to be sailing manually"). Cast off as always, then press **Take the Helm** on their Interact page while you are aboard: they go to the wheel and ask for a course. Steady as she goes, due east, west, north or south, or follow another ship. They hold the course along a line, work round islands and come back to it, and you are free to walk the deck, fish, fight or sleep in a berth. They heave to and say so when land lies across the course, when you are no longer aboard, or when told; they drop the anchor for you, or hand the wheel back. Taking the wheel yourself overrides them while you hold it.
- **Who will do it**: a companion, a hired hand, family, or a villager who trusts you and is following you. They carry three quarters sail, less in a storm. Or a **hired sea captain**: a sailor ashore between voyages (not a pirate) has **Hire as Captain** on the Interact page, 12 emeralds' worth for six days. He follows you, carries full sail, and signs on to no other ship meanwhile.
- **The crossing to Mohknee**: one sailor in three has made it and says so before you pay. Only he has the course "Make for the land in the east". He lies off the shore when he raises it.
- **Word of it**: villagers on the home continent now and then gossip of the land in the east (strange ships, lacquer, a steel no smith can match, men who sailed after them), more often by the shore, and sailors most of all. No name, no bearing, no map.
