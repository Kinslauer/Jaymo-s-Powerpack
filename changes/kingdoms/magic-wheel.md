# Magic wheel - the Magic screen is now a spell wheel
- **Magic wheel**: the Magic key (and the Magic page of the H menu) opens a wheel in the symbiote wheel's look instead of the long list of buttons that ran off the top of the screen. The first ring is the kinds of magic you have (Everyday, Rituals, Sorcery, Choices); click one and its spells fan round the ring, each with an icon. Point at a spell and its full description shows in a box beside the wheel (under it on narrow screens), as tall as the text needs, so nothing scrolls. Click to cast; the middle, right click or Backspace goes back. Whisper opens a line to type in, Enter sends. A choice (Command, Suggestion, Weather rite, Familiar) changes on click and the wheel stays open on that ring. More than 12 spells on a ring page with the mouse wheel. With only everyday spells the wheel opens straight on them.
- Everyday spells and Call Back Familiar got descriptions; the window's title is now "Magic".
- The identify page sits under the menu strip, wider, and a long page scrolls slowly through.
- Classes: `MagicScreen` (rewritten), `MagicPageScreen`. Lang: `parts/magic-screen.json`.
- Built; not played.
