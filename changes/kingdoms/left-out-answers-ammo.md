# Ember Knives are real knives, rogues throw a Fan of Knives, trap darts can be picked up
- Andy's answers on the left-out page (2026-11-12): the Cinder-Shade's Ember Knives count, rogue mobs should throw a Fan of Knives, and trap darts should be pick-up-able.
- The Cinder-Shade carries 10 to 15 of Powers' iron daggers. Ember Knives throws five of them in a fan, heated red so they set what they hit alight. Each lands as a dagger anyone can pick up, and she gathers hers again once nothing is after her. With none left the fan throws nothing (`MobAmmo.Kind.KNIVES`, `CaptainWays.throwBlade`).
- A camp's Knife chief and an assassin carry 3 to 6 knives. When their foe is out of reach, they throw a Fan of Knives of up to three, never more than they carry (`FanOfKnives`).
- The trap hall's wall darts are arrows that anyone can pick up where they land (`Trials`).
