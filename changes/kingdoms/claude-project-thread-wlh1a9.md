# narrator reads the silent story lines (Google voice)
- **200 story lines that were silent now have the narrator reading them**: the 62 plain tales, the faith page (its
  question, the line underneath and each faith's tale) and the 126 retold birthplaces and upbringings for every blood.
  They are spoken by Google's Chirp 3 HD "Charon" voice, so they sound different from the lines the narrator already
  read (those keep his old voice). `tools/voices/google_clips.txt` lists which clips are the Google ones;
  `python tools/voices/google_voices.py --redo` remakes every narrator line in the Google voice.
- Built; not played.
