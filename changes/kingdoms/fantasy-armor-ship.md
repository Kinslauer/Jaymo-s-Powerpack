  the risen dead now and then comes up in a whole dark set (dead gladiator, fog guard, dark cover, grave sentinel),
  enchanted as legendary gear; a king in gilded attire wears a gilded set (gilded hunt, dawncrest) under his crown
  instead of trimmed netherite. Nobody below a king wears it, so the royal tiers hold. The Grave Sentinel helmet is
  never put on anyone.
