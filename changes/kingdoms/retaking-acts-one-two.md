# The Retaking - the ford, and village talk by act
- **The keep above the ford** (the field of The Long Defence): one per world. It rises the first time someone in Act II (Goblin King fallen) is out in the wild near a river with woods on its bank. It's a ruined keep of mossy and cracked stone with a broken corner tower and its gate facing the water, a worn road down to a shallow gravel ford, rubble, vines and cobwebs. A chest in the keep holds **A Loose Page** from the gate-warden, plus a lantern, arrows and nuggets.
- **Where the Five Stood**, an Act II milestone: stand at the ford. The first time it counts, The Long Defence's wordless film plays again, there.
- **Village talk by act**: seven new talks for any grown villager, each heard once, which shift as you reach each act. In Act I a father who watched the sky, and someone who can tell you've read the warden's book. In Act II, which way the old keep lies, and beasts grown hardier since a giant "came down". Then lights that come down slow, something at the bottom of the dark that keeps count, and songs being made about you. They're hints only; nothing is said outright.
- Ops: `/kingdom ford where|raise` (raise looks for a forest river near you).
- Classes: `Ford` (new), `Retaking` (ford milestone), `TalkBook` (retaking_* talks).
- Compiled (main + client); not played.
