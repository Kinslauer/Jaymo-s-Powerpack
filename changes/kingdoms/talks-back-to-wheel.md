# Talks go back to the wheel until they truly end
- **Back to the wheel** (Andy's clip: "conversations end prematurely it should go back to the wheel unless you end the conversation, they get bored or refuse talking to you, or something outside influences it"): after any talk action (a reply, a chat, a joke, an answer from Ask of them...) MCA's menu still shuts for the villager's reply shot, but once their reply has been shown the menu opens again on the wheel, with no new greeting and the reply spoke answering what they just said. Family Tree, or one of our own talk screens on them ("Have a word", work, training...), sets the talk aside and it carries on when that shuts. (`Conversations.again`, `DialogueCue.Again/Aside`, `McaTalkResumeMixin`, `DialogueCinematic`, `TalkFirst`)
- **It ends only when**: you press Esc or pick the new "Take your leave" spoke (last on the first ring; they bid you goodbye in their own tone); they get bored (each pick spends their patience for this talk: 6 picks, +1 per 20 hearts (-2 to +5), +/-1 per 5 points of mood, +1 warm nature, -1 timid or cold to you, at least 2; a refused pick costs 1 more; 45 s left waiting on the wheel also does it; they say so, and won't start another talk with you for 1 minute); they refuse to talk to you (a grudge turning a chat down, MCA's "we've talked enough today", a back turned; asking for an audience also ends it); or something outside breaks in (either of you hurt or set upon, a monster within 4 blocks or one within 10 after either of you, fire on either of you, a fire alarm, someone asleep, anything that makes them busy, plus the old checks: distance, water, a mount, out of play). An outside break now also shuts the menu. The patience is worked out once at the start, so their attitude stays the same all talk. (`Conversations`)
- Lines in `lang/parts/talk-endings.json` (goodbye, bored, idle, worn out; gestures for those without your tongue).
- Not changed: Trade still ends the talk (it is a button, not a talk action). Illager talks end as before.
- Built; not played.
