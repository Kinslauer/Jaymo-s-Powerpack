# A captain takes your real head, and only orcs always do
- Andy ("cedric here duplicated my head?"): a captain who killed a player wore a fresh copy of their head while the real one, cut off in the kill, still lay on the ground. Now, if the head was cut off and lies loose within 12 blocks, he picks up that one (it's gone from the ground); otherwise he wears a copy as before.
- Andy: only orcs always take the head ("they see it as a trophy"); any other captain takes it 15% of the time. One who doesn't keeps the head he already wore (or none).
- `Captains.HEAD_TAKING`, `HEAD_REACH`, `Captains.trophy`, `PowersCompat.takeHead`. Built by CI only; not played in game.
