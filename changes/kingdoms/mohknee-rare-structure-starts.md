# Mohknee lets rare mainland villages and surface structures generate
- **Rare outsider structures on Mohknee**: Mainland villages and other mods' surface structures are no longer banned outright on Mohknee. Distance keeps them rare instead, letting a small share of valid starts through (`MohkneeStructureMixin`: 1 in 10 mainland village starts, 1 in 12 other-mod surface starts) while crossing islands still keep only their own village.
- Built in `kingdoms/`; not played in game (new Mohknee chunks and `/locate` checks not run in a live world).
