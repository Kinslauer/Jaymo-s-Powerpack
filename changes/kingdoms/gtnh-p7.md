# GT New Horizons picks, patch 7 - grown veins are mixed and show small ores
- **Grown veins are mixed** (`GroundChanges`): a vein a world tier lays under loaded chunks now carries short streaks of a partner ore, as Jaymo's Powers' worldgen veins do: iron with copper, copper with Powers' tin, gold with iron (lapis none). About one block in 14.
- **Small ores over grown veins**: each grown iron, copper or gold vein also leaves up to 6 of Powers' lone small ores in the stone 3 to 20 blocks under the ground of its chunk, most of its own metal and a quarter its partner's, so Powers' small ores hint at it as they do at worldgen veins. Tin ore and the small ores are looked up by id: without Powers there's no tin streak and no small ore.
