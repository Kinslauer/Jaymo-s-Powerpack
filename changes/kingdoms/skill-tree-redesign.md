# Skill tree: roomier web, own pictures, framed panels

- The skill web (K) is spread far wider: rings and columns sit about twice as far apart, a branch's three columns are staggered so names never share a line, and nodes are half as big again. It opens zoomed in on the first rings of your strongest pillar instead of squeezed whole onto the screen; scroll zooms smoothly out to the whole web ("Whole web" at the foot does it in one press).
- Every node carries its own picture (an item that says what the skill is about), dimmed while it can't be learnt yet. Names show for every node from normal zoom up, and each branch is named at its gate as well as at its far end.
- A click now picks a skill instead of spending the point at once: the view glides to it, the path to it lights, and the right panel shows it in full (picture, branch and rank, what it does, costs, needs, leads to). Click it again, press Enter or press the panel's Learn button to learn it.
- The screen is laid out in framed panels: the title across the top, your character level, attribute points, the three pillars (level, experience, points; press one to go to its part of the web), class, alignment and attributes on the left (press the attributes to open their tab), the picked skill on the right, and the mouse and key hints along the foot.
- The left panel also lists the weapon arts on your belt, slot by slot with their rank, the ones that fit the weapon in your hand bright; press them to open the belt menu. On a short screen the wheel scrolls the panel.
- Make Do and Read the Ground no longer sit on top of each other.
- Classes: `SkillScreen`, `SkillWeb`, new `SkillIcons`. Compiled; not played. Perk keys, costs, requirements and saved ranks are unchanged.
