# captive chains reach 9 blocks like a lead, pearl out without a trace, escape hints once each, crude lockpick
- **Chains** (Andy: "tethered and able to move 8-10 blocks ... wont be yanked back with a teleport. it should be like the lasso or lead"): a shackled captive goes 9 blocks from their stake (was 2.5). Past that the chain draws taut and pulls them back toward it, harder the further over, with no step further out; only another world, or being flung 8+ blocks past the reach (a blast), sets them back at the stake. The drawn chain or rope sags when slack and runs nearly taut at full reach (`StoryHard.CHAIN_REACH/CHAIN_PULL/CHAIN_SNAP`, client `ShackleTether`).
- **Pearled out** (Andy: "a pearl should teleport you out of captivity without a trace. it will leave the captors VERy confused"): an ender pearl thrown in chains, when it lands (a jump of over 2 blocks in a tick within 10 s of the throw), takes the shackle off with no hunt, ever. For the rest of the day each of the camp's people who comes within 12 blocks of the empty chain with a clear sight of it stares at it and wonders aloud once (6 lines; orc and goblin dialects). No escape hint tells of it. `StoryHard.vanish/baffled`.
- **Escape hints** (Andy: "show a hint when players get captured ... each hint should play once"): new `CaptiveTips`. The first time a player is in chains they get, one card every 10 s: how captors treat them (acting up, three strikes), slipping the shackle at night, picking it (kneel, Lockpicking Prowess or Thief, the crude lockpick), and breaking the chain or sawing the rope (whichever holds them). In the cells: waiting it out, the door (fine or lockpick), breaking out (outlaw; forged writ). Hogtied: mash sneak. Each is saved on the player (`captive_tips`, kept through death) and never shown again. Sent as action-bar lines listed in Powers' `hint_keys.txt`, so they come up as top-right tip cards.
- **Crude Lockpick** (Andy: "craftable with a stick and a rock. for a weaker one with less durability"): stick + any loose rock (tag `#jaymoskingdoms:lockpick_rocks`, Powers' rocks) at the grid. It works every lock a lockpick does, but its strain builds 1.8x as fast, so it snaps sooner (`Lockpicking.CRUDE_STRAIN`). The pick in hand is the one used; with neither in hand, a proper lockpick is used before a crude one. Picking still needs Lockpicking Prowess or the Thief's calling.
- Built; not played. The pull on a player is sent as velocity each tick (as CaptiveLeads' posted rope does), not seen in game.
