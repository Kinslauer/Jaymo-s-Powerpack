# faith page shows the faith's champion

On the life story's faith page the left preview shows the selected faith's champion instead of the player,
changing as the selection changes; the faith's sigil is drawn large and semi-transparent behind it and stays
behind the preview on every later page. The Truth has no champion, so it is drawn as a bright yellow-white ki
ghost instead.

- com.jaymoskingdoms.client.LifeStoryScreen: champion/ghost on the faith page, sigil behind it and behind later
  pages' previews.
- com.jaymoskingdoms.client.KiGhost: draw accepts colours.
- Built; not played in game.
