# Seat compound castles no longer float
- **Castles sit on the ground again**: a seat compound's castle (Andy's clip: Dragard, Selholm's fiefdom) could generate one to two blocks over the land with the ground dug out under it. The box that clears the castle's spawners and loot reached two blocks below the castle, and the compound's levelled ground (beard_box) treats every piece's box as ground to level to, so the land was carved down to that box's floor. The box now widens only sideways.
- **Ones already generated get propped up**: a compound laid before this fix, not yet founded, gets ground filled under its castle down to the land when it is founded (the first time a player comes near its bell). A castle that is already someone's seat (Dragard in Andy's world) stays as it stands.
- Classes: SeatCompounds (Compound, found, underpin).
- Built; not played.
