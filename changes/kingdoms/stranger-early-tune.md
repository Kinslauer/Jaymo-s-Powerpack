# Awakened strangers toned down early, and they call out before fighting
- Andy's clip: a Z Fighter stranger beamed him from about 20 blocks two and a half minutes into a new life, before it
  had said a word, and kept on him once he was down.
- **Not early**: no stranger comes for a player who hasn't lived two in-game days in this life.
- **Call first**: they walk up, call out, and square up for 2 seconds before any blow or power move.
- **Softer by tier**: a stranger's harm on a player lands at 35% in Hearth, 60% Borderlands, 80% Wildlands, full from
  Dread, and one blow or move takes at most 20% / 30% / 45% of the player's full health in those tiers. No masters in
  Hearth (10% a tier from Borderlands).
- **Down is a win**: a quarry who goes down has lost; the stranger says so and walks off.
- Not from the harsh-lands tiers: strangers were never tiered as hostiles.
