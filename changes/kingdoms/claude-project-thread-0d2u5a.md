# Werewolves eat the dead
- Andy: "werewolves can also eat the corpses and parts". A werewolf's beast with nothing to hunt smells out the dead within 20 blocks, any Kitchen body with flesh on it (whoever it was, whoever killed it, monsters too) or any severed head or limb, and tears into it where it lies: a claw swipe and a bite every 10 ticks, healing 3 a bite; a body takes 3 bites plus 2 per block of its width, then it's gone (its meat spilled). Gorged after two, it rests from eating for 3600 ticks. By day's face it doesn't (`Devouring.DevourGoal`).
- A player wearing the beast (great or lean): right-click a body (a player's too) or a severed part to bite it (one every 8 ticks, reach 3): 2 food, 0.4 saturation and 1.5 health a bite, a growl when it's finished. Sneak + right-click still carries it.
- A body eaten is gone, so it no longer counts toward the 8 dead the Unburied rise from. Andy: "headless bodies with no limbs or legs still count, it just requires more if the body isnt complete": each body now counts for what's left of it, 1 whole, less 0.2 for the head and 0.15 per arm or leg, at least 0.2; the dead must add up to 8 (`NightThings.bodies`, `KitchenCompat.wholeness`).
- Players' bodies can be eaten too; only bones and iron are left alone.
