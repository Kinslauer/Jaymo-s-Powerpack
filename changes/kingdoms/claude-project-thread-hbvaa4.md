# ships rigged when they're made; pillager long ships, cannons and all
- **Every ship has her helm from the start**: Moog's long ships, hauling ships, small ships and travel ships get their helm as soon as the last of their chunks is made (`SeaSites.rig`, on chunk load), no longer only when their people move in. A player who spawns or lands on one finds the wheel there. Each boat is rigged once (saved, `sea_sites_rigged`), and moving in or casting off a smack uses that same helm.
- **The long ship is the pillagers'** (Andy: "if you cant get the pillagers to man that, cannons and all, then give it to the pirates"). Moog's mod already crews her with pillagers, vindicators, an evoker and a vindicator "Ship Captain" with a Jolly Roger. Her hold is now an illager camp (it was barbarians) that holds her and never raids. When it moves in, her own crew join it: the captain becomes its warlord (warlord health and boss bar, his own axe and flag kept), the rest its hands, and it brings in up to 8 hands in all. Any of her crew who load later join too (`Illagers.adopt`).
- **Her guns**: up to 3 ship's cannons a side are set in her rails where they meet her main deck, spread along her waist, facing out over the water, each with a barrel of 8 gunpowder and 8 round shot on the deck behind its breech. An illager standing by a gun lays it on his foe, or on the nearest player in its arc (about 50 degrees off her side, 5 to 52 blocks), loads it from that barrel and fires about every 10 to 13 seconds (`DeckGuns`, `ShipCannonBlock.ready/fireLaid`). Only real powder and shot are fired: a spent barrel leaves its gun quiet, and whoever takes the ship gets what's left. Players are fired on only once her hold's tier (Borderlands) is open for them. She lies where she was made and doesn't sail.
- Lore: "The pillagers' long ships" (new, for Andy to approve).
- Built. Not played and not run on a dev server. The gun places were checked offline against Moog's four long ship templates: 6 guns on the dark oak, oak and acacia ships, 4 on the cherry one, each where a lectern or slab rail met the deck.
