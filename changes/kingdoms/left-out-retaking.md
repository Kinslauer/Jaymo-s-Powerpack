# The Retaking: what the first builds left out
- **Milestones pay** (`StoryPay`): every milestone done pays once, however it was done: coin in your realm's coin (the culture coins; where you stand if you have no realm), reputation and standing with your realm, and, for the milestones that are things learned (stones, Giants, the Aether, the ford, stars, the Maw, far worlds, spirits, the dark below, the Judge, the Grue, the light, the dragon, the Second Defence), Scholar experience. Kings pay double. Said on the action bar.
- **Tellers**: the Keeper, the Judge and the Omen pilots (`StoryTellers`, through "Talk about work") each have a new line for every act, unlocked by a milestone of it, before their work; your spirit remarks on each king's fall in its own voice (not the Quiet One); priests, the Truth's clerics and court wizards (`TalkBook`) each have five new talks unlocked by milestones.
- **Heritage**: the Warrior-born are watched warily in Act I (a village talk) and recognised on setting foot in the Maw; the Frostborn hear "home" in Act III (a village talk and the first of the lights); the Bio-built find the Tinkerer colder.
- **Party sharing**: the raider band, star, pod, far world, Maw, demon layers, spirit, Bane, Judge and the Grue's shade now count for party members within 64 blocks, as the other milestones do.
- **Books**: Act I's find is a second page by the gate-warden (`the_wardens_second_page`, from greenskin chiefs and camp stores); Act V's is a last page in a newer hand (`the_wardens_last_page`), left in the keep's chest when the Second Defence is held.
- **Demon layers**: the Abyss milestone now wants the Gloam, the Ash and the Abyss, all three, shared with the party near.
- **The Judge**: dying and coming back counts as being weighed, not only visiting (Powers leaves the deed when a life is weighed).
- **Roads**: the Arcanist's Road gains "why Death and Blood are forbidden" and "the crucible whispers" (court wizard talks); the Faithful Road gains "a cleric of the Truth argues with you". Kingdoms leaves a tag for the Web Slinger's Road once the Quiet One has been met.
