# shipwrights build the harbours' ships at the pier; whaling starts with the first whaler
- **Shipyards** (Andy: "a shipwright job and mca villager that you can see assembling them at a kingdom's dock ... one large enough for whaling that would give a great way to unlock that feature"): a realm's warship and whaler no longer appear whole at the mooring. Its harbour's shipwright (the MCA villager on the pier) lays her down by the pier end and builds her block by block (`Fleet.Slip`: keel first, then up through her hull and decks from amidships out, masts and rigging as they come, her helm last), 3 blocks a beat by day and at peace, walking the work with his axe; each block knocks and chips as it goes in. A whaler (the 2,600-block cargo hull) takes about two in-game days. Done, he tosses a fish over her bow (a bell rings) and she's launched with her crew aboard, the realm's own. One ship on the slip at a time; progress is saved (`Shipyards.YARDS`), blocks someone takes out are put back, and a player standing where a block goes is waited for.
- **Whaling unlock**: a realm sends out whalers only once its own yard has launched one (`Shipyards.WHALERS`); a whaler sunk, lost on the far grounds or never home has to be built again. Realms that already went whaling count as having one. Life-story whaler starts still get their ship at once. A lost warship is rebuilt in the yard too (after the same two days). Without MCA nothing changes.
- **Unseen work**: with no player near, the work goes on unseen at the same daylight pace and is put in the world when someone comes; she's only launched with someone there.
- **Talk about work** with the shipwright: what's on the slip and how much of her is in; **Lend a hand** hands him your planks and logs (a log is 4 planks' worth, up to 128 a time): he lays 3 more blocks a beat until that much is used, and the realm thinks better of you (up to +6 rep). Nobody loses anything for leaving him be.
- Built; not played. Not booted in a dev server.
