# Lore - eight more pages dropped by what you kill
- After RPG Lore's dropped lore books (MIT; the words are ours, from the lore canon). Eight new loose pages drop from mobs
  and file into the Lore Codex like the others: **A Surveyor's Notes** (any of the four Giants, 1 in 2), **A Market List**
  (zombies and husks, rare), **A Physician's Case** (the Skeleton Lord), **A Count in Knots** (Hornkin of every kind),
  **Over Every Door** (the Night Bleeder and the Blightwing), **A Plate Rubbing** (sentry golems and detonation sentries),
  **Halfway** (cockatrices, zephyrs and tempests) and **A Letter, Unsent** (werewolves).
- Each only hints: none of them states the lore outright.
- Data only: `data/jaymoskingdoms/lore/*.json`, read by `LoreBooks` (drop filters `entities`, `chance`). The Lore Codex and
  the drop system were already built.
- Not played.
