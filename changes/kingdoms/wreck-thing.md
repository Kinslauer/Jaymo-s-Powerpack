# The thing under the wreck

- **Something lies under some of the treasure wrecks.** The first time anybody comes within 28 blocks of a treasure
  wreck, charted or happened on, the wreck is looked at once: about one in three has the thing lying in the water
  beside it. Wrecks already laid in your world are looked at the same way the next time somebody visits. A chest
  buried ashore never has one.
- **It never leaves the water and it fights from below.** It keeps itself about five blocks under whoever it is
  after, a squat body with eight arms standing up from it, and everything it does comes up at the column over it,
  whether you are swimming, in a boat or on a deck. Get ashore and stay there a quarter of a minute and it goes back
  to where it lay. 320 health, its own boss bar, and the boss introduction the other great ones get.
- **The lash:** its arms open, then clap shut over it (11 damage and a throw upward, with the usual warning).
- **The grab:** an arm takes you and draws you down to its beak, squeezing (4 damage every three quarters of a
  second) and taking your breath five times as fast, for about four seconds. Deal it 14 damage while it holds
  somebody and it lets go. If it reaches and misses, its arms close on water and it is open for a moment.
- **It takes hold of ships.** Somebody in a boat is held still and pulled out of it. A ship built of blocks is held
  fast by her hull for as long as the grab lasts: no way on her whatever her sails or oars are doing.
- **The ink:** at half health and again at a fifth it lets go of whoever it holds, blinds and darkens everyone in the
  water within nine blocks for seven seconds, and darts off behind the cloud before it comes round again.
- **Out in the far grounds too.** A whaler six or more steps out, with a player aboard, has a small chance each roll
  of dangers that it comes up under her. Nobody aboard says anything about it.
- **What it leaves:** a clinging coil, three to six deep teeth and ink sacs (Powers' items; a heart of the sea
  instead if Powers is not installed).
- Spawn egg for testing. Compiles; not played in game.
