# Blood Awakening per-blood upgrades, Patch 1 (Plain, Mountain & Elemental/Sky bloods)
- Added `docs/Blood-Awakening-Per-Blood-Plan.md` splitting all 48 heritages and looks into 5 patches (2 tracks per patch). Every upgrade adds on top of PR #2117's shared rule (halved waits, 1.5x durations) and applies only while Blood Awakening is learned.
- **Heartlander** (`HeritageKits`): extra Luck and bonus kill-loot drops via `LootTableEvents.MODIFY_DROPS`; critical strikes shave 3 s off Stroke of Fortune's remaining wait. **Sunfreckled**: Messenger's Dash travels farther with extra cooldown reduction. **Skymarked**: Unbreakable Stand lasts longer without Slowness, and Heavy Footed is halved.
- **Stoneborn** (`HeritageKits`): Shield Wall lasts longer and blocks knockback while active. **Obsidian** (`HeritageKits`, `BlastMend`): Rune of Warding lasts longer, shields allies in a 10-block ward circle, and rewinds explosion block damage inside the ward back into place over a few seconds without duplicating drops. **Magmavein**: Mountain Leap jumps higher and can leap twice in quick succession before cooldown starts.
- **Earthling** (`HeritageKits`): Second Wind heals more and pauses the Hungry flaw briefly. **Inkmarked**: Arcane Overflow blasts and shoves nearby foes in a wide arcane burst, and Frail is halved. **Warriorborn**: War Cry also grants Resistance to nearby allies (players, kingdom folk, owned pets) and frightens (`Fear.expose`) hostile mobs over a wider area.
- **Emberborn** (`HeritageKits`, retry picks): while Forge Blessing lasts, **Steam stride** boils off water and lifts Heavy Water on both sides (`STEAM_STRIDE` attachment), and **A forge-hot edge** sparks the held weapon and briefly sets struck foes alight.
- **Frostborn** (`FrostPrison`, `HeritageKits`): Frostbind's ice prison chills nearby hostile foes, and Fire Frail eases while an ice prison or FrostBeam (`jaymospowers_frost_beaming`) is active. **Stormborn**: Wet Grounded does not apply in the rain. **Nightborn** (`HeritageKits`, `Darkvision`, `DarkvisionClient`, `darkvision_far.json`): Shadowmeld doubles Darkvision reach to 32 blocks while active, and killing a mob while melded halves Shadowmeld's remaining wait.
- **Skill screen** (`SkillScreen`, `lang/parts/blood-awakening-upgrades.json`): Blood Awakening displays each blood's awakened bonus line.
- Built; not played in game.
