# Every trade in every town
- **Every town keeps its trades** (Andy, 2026-11-21, villages plan Track C): each kingdom, fiefdom and village now keeps a butcher, a healer, a cook, a cleric, a farmer and a tanner (the game's leatherworker), plus a fisherman where there's water round its seat. A missing one is taken up by someone idle (then a nitwit, then the greenest of a common trade), or a newcomer moves in, as the butcher and cleric already were (`TownTrades`). A miner and a woodcutter are hired the usual way once the town has a mine (or quarry) and a tree farm: a miner with no mine isn't kept on.
- **The healer** is a trade of its own now (`Professions.HEALER`, dressed as a cleric): they treat you on an infirmary cot and throw healing splashes like a cleric. The cleric keeps the shrine; the cleric's speech lines now talk of the shrine, the healer's of the sick.
- **Epic towns refitted** (`TownFitting`): the first time a player comes into an Epic town (new or already in the world; marked once in the Overworld's save), its job blocks are swapped for ours, no rooms added: the butcher's smoker becomes Kitchen's butcher block with meat hooks under the beam, other smokers and house furnaces (not smithies') become Kitchen's stove, the brewing stand becomes the shrine, one bed (in the cleric's house if there is one) becomes an infirmary cot with Powers' mortar beside it, the tanner's cauldron is filled for soaking hides with Kitchen's drying rack beside it, the pen gets Kitchen's feeding trough, and Powers' chopping block stands outside a house. A mod that isn't loaded leaves the game's block.
- **Job halls for trades with no house**: a butcher's shop, infirmary, the new tannery (`JobHalls.tannery`: soaking cauldrons, drying racks, bark barrel, the land's roof), a cookhouse, and a fishery where there's water, built just outside the town's edge facing in.
