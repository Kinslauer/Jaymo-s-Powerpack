# Guards only see a drawn blade in front of them
- **The blade stance is seen, not heard**: a guard (or any villager) now reacts to your drawn, crouched sword stance
  only when it faces you (the same view cone `Senses` gives humanoids) or you're within two blocks of it. Andy's clip:
  an orc guard with his back to him warned "Halt. Sheathe that blade" the moment he drew. `Menace` checked only a
  clear line between them, not which way the guard was looking. Drawing a weapon still makes no sound mobs hear.
- Built on CI only; not played.
