# The snow giant

- **The Snow Giant.** A white, shaggy great beast twice a grizzly's size, rare and alone on the snowy plains and among
  the ice spikes. It leaves alone whoever leaves it alone: it never attacks on sight. Hurt it and it rears, roars its
  warning, and fights.
- It has the grizzly's moves (rear-up slam, paw swipes, charge, maul, enraged roar) with a boss bar and its own
  entrance film, and three of its own: every blow freezes and slows; it leaps onto a foe standing off and the landing
  throws and chills everyone near; and once enraged it rears and hurls blocks of ice, four in a row when badly hurt.
- It always drops **Rimeclaw**, its gauntlet (with Jaymo's Powers). Rimeclaw counts as a claw weapon for the claw forms.
- The grizzly's den dressing is only placed for grizzlies.
- Compiled, not yet played.
