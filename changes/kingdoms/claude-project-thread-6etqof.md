# Unmasked shapeshifters fight or flee, and bystanders react
- **Never peaceful once found out**: an unmasked shapeshifter (a stolen life's copy) weighs its chances every half second. It **fights** (whoever unmasked it first, else the nearest threat it can hurt) or **flees** (runs from them at a sprint). It runs when the odds against it (players, guards, fighting folk, iron golems and anyone already after it, counted by how hale and how armed they are) outweigh its nerve, or when it is hurt past its breaking point. Each one has a temper: **bold** (takes on about two, runs under 25% health), **wary** (one armed foe, runs under 45%) or **craven** (only the weak, runs under 70%). **Cornered** (no ground to run to, or caught while running) it fights whatever the odds. A player in creative counts as a threat it can only run from. With nobody about for 10 seconds it stops running, but it stays a monster that goes for any player it sees.
- **Every way to unmask one** now goes through this: catching it out after overhearing a slip (sneak + empty hand), the real one meeting it, the real one brought home, a moonbeam, and (new) a blow that drops the copy to half health, downs it or would kill it. The thing comes out as hurt as the copy was (at least 30%).
- **Bystanders** within 16 blocks who see it drop its face: guards, fighting folk and brave villagers go for it (villagers strike with what they hold); the rest run and hide, the first crying out whose face it wore and the next calling the guards; every guard within 32 blocks comes. The stolen one, if there, cries out that it's the thing that took them. Players who see it are shaken as by anything of the other side (Occult exposure).
- `Shapeshifter` (fight-or-flight mind, tempers, flee goal), `ShapeshifterAlarm` (new), `Doubles.expose` (who found it out, health carried over, pain unmasking), `Captives`/`Moonbeam` pass the rescuer and caster. Lang part `shapeshifter-unmasked.json`.
