# Diggers, goblin scouts and loot goblins can lose limbs
- Diggers, goblin scouts and loot goblins were never in the dismemberable list, so a blade or a blast could never take a part off them. They are now (data/jaymospowers/tags/entity_type/dismemberable.json), and being small they lose parts more often than a person (Powers' size scaling).
- Imps are left out: they are GeckoLib models, which the lost-part hiding can't reach yet.
- Built; not played.
