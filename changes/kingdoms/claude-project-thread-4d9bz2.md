# Belt menu for Weapon Forms: tabs, a scrolling list, and fewer icons on the skill bar

- Andy: "stances should be set through a menu, and the icons are too numerous now, so maybe a tab along the top of
  the screen or a scroll wheel." The Five-Art Belt is now set in a menu: the character menu (H) has a **Weapon Forms**
  tab once you know a form. It has a tab along the top for each weapon family you know a form of, the belt's five
  slots on the left, and that family's forms on the right, six at a time, scrolled with the wheel. Pick a slot, then
  a form; click a picked slot again to empty it.
- The belt can be changed anywhere, not only beside a training post or a dummy. Sneak and use on one still opens the
  same menu.
- **Fewer icons**: the skill bar shows only the forms on your belt that fit the weapon in hand, instead of every form
  you know for it. If nothing on the belt fits the weapon, the first five forms you know for it show, so a new weapon
  is never left bare.
