# found-out changelings are attacked; a town in doubt asks questions only the real one would know
- Andy: "If you are found out and you lied or hurt the person you changed into the villagers will attack, and be suspicious
  as anyone could be a changeling or shifter now. They will try and ask questions only they would know."
- **Found out** (`BorrowedFaces.drop`): a borrowed face that comes off within 24 blocks and in sight of a villager is found
  out. If the wearer struck the villager whose face it is (before or after taking it) or lied as them, everyone who saw
  reacts as to a shapeshifter (`ShapeshifterAlarm`, now taking any living thing): guards and the brave attack, the rest
  run and cry out. If not, they only cry out. Dying sheds the face unseen.
- **A settlement in doubt** (`Doubts`): any face found out within 128 blocks of a seat (a shapeshifter's, a changeling
  impostor's or a player's) leaves that settlement in doubt for three days. Its grown villagers stop any player who comes
  within 5 blocks, at most once every two minutes, and ask a question on the talk card.
  - In your own face: the name of the place, among two others.
  - Wearing a villager's face: that villager's spouse, dog or the dog's colour, among two wrong ones. If you had met the
    villager before taking the face (MCA remembers it), you know the answer and the wrong ones can't be picked.
  - Right: that settlement asks you nothing more while the doubt lasts. Wrong is a lie: the face comes off on the spot and
    they attack; in your own face you are taken for a thing wearing one. Walking away costs nothing.
- Creative and spectator players are never asked or attacked.
- Built (compileJava); not played.
