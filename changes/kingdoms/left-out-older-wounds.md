# Left out of older PRs: companions bury the dead
- **Companions bury you, grieve and remember** (Andy: "if you have companions they should bury you and grieve and bring you up if asked about their previous adventures"). When a player's life ends for good (Powers' final death), every MCA villager within 64 blocks they were involved with (family, hired help, a trusted friend) is a companion. The nearest walks to the body and says the words; the others gather and each grieves in their own line; then the body is taken up (with Kitchen, the player's corpse), what it carried goes into a chest under the grave, and a gravestone goes over it with a sign: "Here lies", the name, what took them, and the day. Every companion remembers (`Mourning.MOURNS`), and MCA's menu offers **Ask about their old adventures** on one who does: they speak of the one they buried, to anyone, the dead one's next life too.
- Villagers no longer try to tend a downed body someone is carrying (`PowersCompat.isCarried`).
- Last Stand never saves a player who gave up where they lay.
- **Folk react to a Smog user, and drive them out** (Andy: "they should react, and repeat interactions should drive you out of the town. most people will come to realize the effects from corruption and the bad karma you must harbor to have gotten there"). A villager who sees you while the smog holds you remarks on it, at most once a minute a town, and thinks a heart less of you. The first two wonder at the ash and the cough; after that they know it for corruption and say what it says of you, and the fourth time you're warned. The fifth time the town drives you out for 10 minutes: a realm with a ruler has its guards see you out; a town without one shouts you off, and if you're still there after 30 s its nearest guard comes at you. A town forgets one remark every in-game day (`SmogFolk`).
- **Slipfin slides out of an Aether Giant's grab** (Andy: "fix it"): its hand closes and the slick alien squirts out between the fingers, even if they turn Slipfin while held.
- **Creative players switch class from a Class tab on the H menu** (docs/Classes-Plan.md promised it): the class screen opens with a class already chosen, and Choose swaps it.
- **Kingdoms keep court casters, and they march** (Andy: "they should be, they should have at least two if they do send one so that they are never without. richer kingdoms will have more"). A kingdom now keeps two court casters (wizards, or necromancers under a tyrant), three once it's prosperous or its treasury holds 300, four once it's wealthy. When a war party musters or a realm raids a rival, every caster but the one nearest the seat marches with it (none if it has only one), and they walk home to the hall afterwards instead of vanishing (`WarParties.casters`, `BorderClashes`).
- **Town business on the talk screen** (Andy: "they all matter and propose screens for all of them, available through talk options"). **Town business** in MCA's menu, on any grown villager of a realm or village, opens a screen: call a festival (a lord or ruler), march with your realm's war party, mark a plot where you stand, choose what goes up on it (a second screen of the buildings), give it up, and have a courier carry what's in your hand (a second screen of the players about, by the names they go by). Each does what its command did, with the same rules and words (`TalkErrands`).
- **Raise a dead horse from its mane lock** (Andy: "add a screen at the wizards and necromancers that does it..."). **Raise my horse** shows on a court wizard or a necromancer while you hold a mane lock. A necromancer asks 24 emeralds' worth and raises it on the spot. A wizard warns you off; if you insist, they set you a task (bring them amethyst, phantom membrane, a ghast tear, echo shards or glow ink), and once it's done they teach you the rite: from then on, using a mane lock raises its horse yourself. The horse comes back with its name and breed but slower, shorter of wind and wilder, and once each blood moon it has a 35% chance to turn into a Nightmare or a Bat Horse (a donkey, mule or undead horse takes a horse's body for it) (`HorseRevival`).
- **Rival captains: hooks, pegs, illager marks and five more traits** (Andy: "please add them all"). A captain who comes back without an arm now wears a hook on a leather cuff where it was, and one without a leg an oak peg strapped on, posed with the limb that's gone. Illager captains show their face marks now (on their taller heads), and their hooks while their arms aren't folded. Five traits the plan named are their own now: **Senses ki** (feels a Z Fighter from 40 blocks, through walls, hidden or not, and comes for them), **Sworn to a god** (one of the seven, the same each time he comes back: his blows on another god's faithful land at 1.25 times, his own god's faithful's blows on him at 1.5), **Wears bog iron** (5 armour and no slower, but none while he's wet), **Never sleeps** (never caught abed, and keeps a watch all night that sees you sneaking or unseen), and **Hates fire mages** (goes for an Ember user, or whoever burned him, before anyone else, and hits them 1.5 times as hard).
- Powers' escort wards are walked by Powers, as its patrolmen are.
