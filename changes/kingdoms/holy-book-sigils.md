# holy books - each patron's sigil on its book; a stored book is no broken commandment
- **Holy book looks**: every patron's book of commandments is now the vanilla book with that patron's sigil on the cover (the anvil, the hearth flame, the waves, the antlers, the lantern, the crowned sun, the hooded ember, the Truth's cross), and its lore book is the written book with the same sigil. Old books of commandments take the new look the next time they are in an inventory; old lore books keep the plain look. Sigils are drawn from the shrine glyphs by `tools/gen_holy_books.py`, as an overlay on the vanilla texture (no vanilla pixels copied).
- **Keeping the book**: the Truth's "Do not throw away My book" used to break the moment the book was dropped. Now it breaks only if the dropped book is lost (burnt, despawned, fallen into the void). Putting it in a chest, dropping it for a friend or into a hopper, or picking it up again is fine (`Commandments`, `LoreBooks`).
