# plague bearer: the orc camps' sick walker (From the Shadows Reborn)
- **Plague Bearer** (new mob, ported from From the Shadows Reborn by Sonmok14 and L_Ender, AGPL v3; their model, textures, animations and sounds, credit and licence in `licenses/from-the-shadows/`; the code is ours). A sick orc in rags and a plague doctor's mask. Half the orc camps that rise keep one at their edge (18 blocks from the fire), and an orc camp without one may take one in as it grows; they roam within 40 blocks. When his camp is cleared he becomes an exile, and now and then an exile wanders the overworld alone (rare natural spawn). He goes for what the camp's people would (or, as an exile, players, villagers and iron golems) and never the camp's own.
- **The plague is Nightfall's own** (Atrophy, no second plague): what he is hostile to catches it from his breath within 3.5 blocks (every 3 s, 10 s of plague), and from the knives he throws three at a time after a hop back (2.5 damage each, 15 s of plague). He cleaves for 7 damage (the original's 1 plus an iron axe; the axe is not drawn). 30 health, 2 armour, speed 0.3. He is immune to the plague himself.
- **Plague doctor mask** (new head item, 3 armour, 88 durability, repaired with suspicious cloth; 3 cloth in a helmet shape): wearing one keeps the plague out entirely (Nightfall#catches), as it did in the original. A bearer wears one and drops it 15% of the time; he drops suspicious cloth always and, killed by a player, sometimes an emerald (the original's loot). Spawn egg in the creative tab.
- Differences from the original: no raid role (he is an orc, not an illager); the knife is a plague knife drawn as an iron sword until the gear track brings the real throwing dagger; the mask is worn as a plain head layer (the original's beak and wide brim need GeckoLib armour, which Kingdoms doesn't use yet); the bearer's idle mumble is his ambient sound.
- New: `inhabitants/fts/PlagueBearer`, `PlagueKnife`, more in `Fts` and `FtsClient`; `Nightfall.infect` made public; `CampBeasts` keeps bearers at orc camps. Built by CI; not played.
