# The Second Defence lasts three nights; story errands are marked; the Quiet One's suit lies in the keep
- Andy's answers on the left-out page (2026-11-12).
- **Three nights** ("Yes go back and build as planned"; the plan: "you and your party hold it for three nights"). The Second Defence now comes on three nights, at most one a night. Each night has four waves, more dead each night. A night is held when its waves are beaten, or when dawn comes with defenders still at the ford. Only the third night ends with the Grue's shade, and only then is it over. A lost night doesn't count, but the nights already held do (`SecondDefence.NIGHTS`).
- **The closing film** now shows the ones who held it: you and every other player near the ford, in your own skins with what you hold, standing on the near bank as the dead go down (`LoreFilmClient.Morning.defenders`, `FilmGear` player skins).
- **Story errands** ("Mark some"). Only some quests count toward Act I's three errands: clearing rubble, a rescue and an escort always do; about one in three letters, goods, fetching and animal jobs do; a king's quests, killing, horses and grudges never do. The journal marks them (`Quests.storyErrand`).
- **The Quiet One's suit** ("you should be able to find his suit ... you are led to it inside the ford"). The Long Defence film shows him in his own clothes again. Once you've met him (the film or his spirit), white motes near the ford lead you to the keep's gate, where his suit lies: a white bodysuit, a full mask and a cloak gone to ribbons, as a Costume (`StoryRoads.quietSuit`).
