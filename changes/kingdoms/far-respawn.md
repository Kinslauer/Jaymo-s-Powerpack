# new-life starts keep 500 blocks from the last death
- **Starts**: a start in the wild or open land is never within 500 blocks of where the player last died (the vanilla last-death record); with no spot that far in its own ring the open ring widens by 1000. The setting-out point ignores spots within 500 blocks of it too. Starts that put the player inside a town, kingdom or fief (a capital, sworn, evicted, the moon-cursed village edge, the village starts) may stay close. A camp or wreck start with no such place far enough falls to the wild.
- Built by CI; not played.
