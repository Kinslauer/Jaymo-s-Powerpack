# Tinkerers copy the abilities they see as gadgets
- **Study like a Druid** (Andy: "the tinkerer class should be able to develop gadgets that mimic abilities he sees. researching them in the same way druid does"): a Tinkerer learns an ability by watching it in use, calmly (standing still, sneaking or walking slowly), in plain sight within 16 blocks, for 30 seconds in all; each hit they take from it counts 10 seconds; a creature kind known to one Foe Study star teaches its abilities at once, as a Druid's foes do. Powers are seen through Jaymo's Powers' "power in use" mark on a player; creatures by what they are doing (a shulker's bullet, a blaze's or ghast's fireball, evoker fangs, a breeze's wind charge, a witch's thrown potion, a swelling creeper, a guardian's beam, a spider on a wall, an enderman blinking, a warden's sonic boom).
- **19 gadgets** (`tinker/Mimic`, `MimicGadgets`): Lift Rig (Gravity, shulkers), Gust Bellows (Wind, breezes), Grapple Spinner (Web), Rewind Watch (Chrono), Frost Projector (Cryo), Smother Pump (Smoke), Sonic Horn (Resonance, wardens), Parry Gauntlet (Darktide), Chain Hook (Arsenal), Growth Band (Xenoform), Ki Blaster (Z Fighter), Blink Coil (endermen), Climbing Claws (spiders), Flame Spitter (blazes), Bellows Cannon (ghasts), Beam Lens (guardians), Jaw Rake (evokers), Hissing Charge (creepers), Potion Sling (witches). Every one that shoots or throws spends something (redstone, gunpowder, string, snowballs, blaze powder, fire charges, bones, the charge or potion itself); the gauntlet and claws wear out instead. Anyone holding one can use it (Andy's pick); only Tinkerers study and build them.
- **Notebook** (`MimicBenchScreen`): the Tinkerer's class key opens it (sneak + key still shows the infusion count). Each ability shows its study bar and where it is seen; once learned, Build turns the shown parts into the gadget at a tinkering station, crafting table or smithing table within 4 blocks.
- **The Mad Tinkerer** (Andy: "the mad tinkerer can sell and teach you these too. only to tinkerer"): his talk menu gets "Ask about his gadgets" for Tinkerers only, a trade screen to buy any gadget (12 silver) or learn its drawing (20 silver).
- Mixins: `EndermanBlinkMixin` (marks a blink), `ClimbingClawsMixin` (walls climbable with claws held).
- Built; not played.
