# no starts in the Aether but the Divine-born; far fewer Frostborn scouters
- **Starts keep off the Aether** (Andy: "NONE of the starts should be up there except divine born"). While a start is set out on, every place it looks at (a village or haven to be taken in by, a farmhouse, a realm's seat, a camp, a wreck, a monolith, a trader's milestone, a den) is passed over when it lies in the Aether, as is any spot to wake on there. In a One Dimension world the start's ground is worked out in the Overworld's band, so "the top of the ground" is never an Aether island overhead. A party mate in the Aether isn't followed up there. A realm road naming a realm in the Aether goes to the nearest free realm on the ground instead ("... lies in the sky, where only the Divine-born are raised"), else the wild. A last guard sends anyone the start still set down in the Aether to the wild, and anyone left standing there to the world's spawn. Divine-born players and the afterlife start are exempt (`LifeStory.GROUNDED`, `aloft`).
- **Frostborn scouters cut** (Andy: "way too common on the frostborn we find on the overworld"): a Frostborn raised by their people now wears a scouter 12% of the time (was 70%), and one who rolled no scouter, arm or leg no longer gets a scouter anyway. About 9% of all Frostborn villagers wear one, down from about 59%. Made arms (30%) and legs (12%), galactic patrolmen (always, bar 4%) and everyone else's 5% are unchanged (`FrostTech.SCOUTER`).
