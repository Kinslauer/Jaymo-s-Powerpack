# Hardrock patch 7 - caught by the smell
Andy's TFC Hardrock picks: "if you stink youll be caught, especially among non humans with better noses. Villagers will
comment on this" (`Stench`, reading Powers' smell).
- A smelly player is noticed from further off (Senses): keen noses (beasts, monsters, goblins and orcs, vampires,
  werewolves) up to twice as far at the worst, villagers and illagers a quarter further.
- A villager near enough to catch a whiff now and then says so (6 lines; goblins and orcs approve, 3 lines), at most
  once every 2 minutes.
- Compile-checked; not played.
