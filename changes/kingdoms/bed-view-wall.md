# bed view no longer slides through the wall
- **Night view out of bed**: when the night runs by and the camera leaves the pillow, it used to slide straight back through the wall behind the headboard (and through the walls and roof of the house) on its way out, so for a few seconds you looked at the inside of a plank. Now the screen dips to black while the camera passes through blocks and comes up again once it is clear, whichever way the bed faces. The same goes for a second pull-out in one night (when the night stops and starts again).
- Wherever the circling camera settles it keeps a little room from every block (fences, slabs and the like too, not only full blocks), so it never sits pressed into a wall.
- `NightCam` (`veil`, `blocked`), `HudSleepMixin`.
- Built; not played.
