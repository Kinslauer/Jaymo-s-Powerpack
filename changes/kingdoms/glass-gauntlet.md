# Seven Facets, the glass gauntlet's form

- **Seven Facets**, a claw form that only Powers' glass gauntlet can do, claws out. Nobody teaches it: the hand that
  holds the gauntlet knows it. Seven blows at the foe ahead (within 5 blocks), one every 4 ticks, each closing the
  distance: six quick cuts of 0.35 times your attack, then a seventh of 1.5 times that throws the foe back and up and
  leaves it bleeding. Switching the gauntlet away, or the foe dying or getting far off, ends it early. 13 seconds.
  It sits on the belt and on the skill bar like any form (`ClawForms`), and is not offered while the hand is open.
- The gauntlet is in the claw family, so Rending Claw and Feral Pounce work with it.
- Compiles; not played in game.
