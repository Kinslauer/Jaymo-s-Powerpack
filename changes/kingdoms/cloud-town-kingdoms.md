# Towns raised from nothing land on the ground, not on the clouds
- **The bug** (Andy: "brinmark is NOT a kingdom, but it is labeled as such. there isnt a single building"): in a One Dimension world a kingdom raised where no village stood (the first capital at spawn, a trader's second town, a whaler's shore) had its Epic town generated by the stacked level's own generator, which answers "where's the ground" with the highest band that has any: the Cloud Sea's overcast. Brinmark's land was claimed on the coast at spawn while its whole village, king and storehouse stood at y 512-571 over it.
- **Fixed**: `GrandSeats.generate` builds by the Overworld band's own generator, random state and heights and moves the pieces into the level's heights (`Stacked.overworldGround`); an ordinary world is unchanged. The new kingdom's "is this the mainland, not an island?" check (`StoryGround.mainland`, `Offshore.island`) reads the Overworld's ground too; it read the clouds, so every spot looked like dry mainland. Havens only call a settlement an island haven when it stands in the Overworld's band.
- Existing worlds keep what they have (Andy is starting a new map).
- Built; not played.
