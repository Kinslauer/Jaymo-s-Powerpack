# Hunter's Road 4 - the drowned, hags and fog models, textures and animations

- **River drowned**: a low, waterlogged riverbank ambush monster with webbed limbs, a serrated dorsal crest, a sculling paddle tail, and animations for clumsy land strides, fast horizontal swimming, lunging from the bank, emerging, and dragging fallen prey under.
- **Mire hag and mud scoop**: a stooped swamp crone in a sodden peat-and-reed shawl with bog-gourd charms and wide scooping claws that stoop to gouge a clump of mud from the bank and hurl it, paired with a claw-furrowed mud-scoop block model left behind.
- **Grave hag**: a gaunt cemetery crone in a tattered mourning veil wearing a collar of small trophy skulls across her ribs, with a three-jointed barbed tongue that whips out in a long lash and a surge pose after kills.
- **Fog-wight and decoy wisp**: a tall marsh-barrow wight crowned with deadwood bog-antlers and a cold glowing lure-light inside its hollow ribcage, with mist-shroud animations for drawing fog, fading unseen, emerging, and casting a mist-tailed decoy wisp of itself.
- Built; not played yet. The creature and block code for these assets lands in the Hunter's Road 4 patch.
