# Medieval picks, patch 6: Troops, formations and castles

- One ladder for every fighter: militia, soldier, knight (or archer, for those who fight with a bow), veteran and leader. Each rank is tougher and hits harder, and wears better kit. Recruits climb it by their wins (Learners), hired drifters and made veterans join it at their rank, and a realm's guards and war parties stand on it too: at home a guard leader walks his round with his squad in column behind him, and stands at the gate with them in a line. A recruit's rank shows by their name on the orders screen, and a promoted one says a word for their realm with a sound and a sparkle. (`Ranks`)
- Each realm kits its own tree: levy leather in the realm's brown for militia, a spear or a hatchet by the realm's custom (jungle realms always spears), bow or crossbow by realm, iron or copper by the land's veins, trims and dyes from the realm's heraldry. Knights in knight lands wear Patch 8's heraldic plate (`HeraldryWear.knight`); elsewhere the gladiator, ronin or paladin sets by climate. Veterans may carry a glaive, and a leader carries the realm's banner. Militia and soldiers carry shields. Issued kit only fills an empty slot or replaces something worse, and what it replaces goes into their pack (or drops at their feet): a recruit keeps better gear you gave them, and still picks the best blade or bow from their pack for the range. (`Ranks`, `McaEquipmentMixin`)
- Formations for your own followers, from the orders screen's new Formation panel and three keys (F6 next shape, F8 go where you look or set on whoever you look at, F9 charge): line, wedge, square and column; go there, hold, follow, charge, set on my mark, break ranks. No commands. Shields go to the front and bows behind; a line turns to face the nearest foe. (`Formations`, `FollowersScreen`, `QuickOrderKeys`)
- Shield walls: militia and soldiers with shields lock into a line facing the foe and raise them (MobShields) while archers shoot from the rank behind. This holds for your own line formation and for realm war parties, which now march in squads behind a leader in columns and form a shield wall before the seat they besiege while the other blades storm it. Soldiers' arrows pass over their own side, and a shield an axe knocked aside stays down. F8 sets them only on monsters, on whoever is fighting you or yours, or on a realm you are at war with. (`Formations`, `WarParties`, `MobShields`, `ProjectileAlliesMixin`)
- Keeps fly their holder's colours: when a realm's seat passes to an overlord in war, the banners at its keep are rewoven in the winner's colour and pattern. Only the keep's own banners change, never one a player hung. (`KeepColours`)
- Who holds what on the map: with Xaero's Minimap, each realm's seat is a waypoint in its holder's colour, named for the realm and whoever holds it, and the realms' borders are dotted over the minimap in the same colour. Without Xaero nothing changes. (`RealmMap`, `XaeroRealms`, `XaeroRealmsMap`)
