# Sky villages: cloudwrights, havens, pulse-tree mana

- Sky folk now work: a new Cloudwright profession trades in aercloud, skyroot and ambrosium. Idle adult sky folk take the job (two to a village), and the wares restock daily.
- Each sky village is a kind of Haven: it is founded as a village with a divine haven, its folk and Dawnborn kin are at home, humans are not stopped at the gate, and blood is not held against anyone there. The Warden of the Green and Keeper of the Well are the haven's titles.
- Pulse trees of the Verdant World feed an attuned caster: each living pulse tree block within five blocks gives a little mana a second, up to a cap.
- **Sky folk jobs** (Andy: "They need their own job, like sky whaling or ambrosia farming"). Beside the two cloudwrights, each sky village and town now takes on a **sky whaler**, who carries a harpoon and sells what an aerwhale gives (meat, blubber, oil, bone, now and then ambergris) and harpoons, and two **ambrosia farmers**, who carry a skyroot hoe and sell the sky orchards' fruit (white apples, blue berries, oranges, wyndberries and the enchanted ones). Each stall restocks daily (`SkyTrades`, `Professions.SKY_WHALER`, `Professions.AMBROSIA_FARMER`).
- **The sky castle can be seized** (Andy: "there can be travelers with specific temperaments that make them vie for power"). Now and then a proud, greedy, hotheaded or spiteful traveller climbs to a held castle with two sellswords, says what they've come for, and goes for the lord while the sellswords take on the wardens. If the lord goes down while the usurper still stands, the usurper takes the seat and wears the mantle, the sellswords become the new wardens and the old ones stand down. If the usurper goes down first, the bid fails and the sellswords scatter. A player can side with either. At most one bid every three days per castle (`SkyUsurpers`, `SkyCourt`).
- Built (`compileJava`); not played.
