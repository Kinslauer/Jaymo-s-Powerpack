# Sky villages: cloudwrights, havens, pulse-tree mana

- Sky folk now work: a new Cloudwright profession trades in aercloud, skyroot and ambrosium. Idle adult sky folk take the job (two to a village), and the wares restock daily.
- Each sky village is a kind of Haven: it is founded as a village with a divine haven, its folk and Dawnborn kin are at home, humans are not stopped at the gate, and blood is not held against anyone there. The Warden of the Green and Keeper of the Well are the haven's titles.
- Pulse trees of the Verdant World feed an attuned caster: each living pulse tree block within five blocks gives a little mana a second, up to a cap.
- Built (`compileJava`); not played.
