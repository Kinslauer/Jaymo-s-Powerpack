# Trophies with their own likeness, and what dragons leave

- **A great one's trophy now looks like it.** The trophy a great foe leaves used to be the same carved mask for all of
  them. Fourteen now have their own: a horned skull for any dragon, the wither, the warden, the elder guardian, the
  minotaur, the gorgon, the grizzly, the snow giant, the sand worm's maw, the beak of the thing under the wreck, the
  fortress knight's helm, a great whale's flukes, an ogre's head for any ogre and a face of the old stone for the
  Aether's giants. The rest keep the mask. Trophies already in your world keep the mask too: only new ones get the look.
  It shows in the hand, in the inventory and on the trophy stand.
- **Wild dragons leave bone and hide.** Any dragon killed while it is not tamed drops 3 to 6 dragon bones and 2 to 4
  dragon hides (Powers' items, so only with Powers installed). The bone keeps the dragon's breath: ember from Notus and
  Ignivorus, rime from Boreas and Atroxiia, venom from Zephyrus and Varasuchus, storm from Eurus, Aeolus, Aetherion and
  Stegonaut.
- Compiles; not played in game.
