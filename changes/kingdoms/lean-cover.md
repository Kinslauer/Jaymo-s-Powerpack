# Leaning round corners, cover that counts while you lean, shooters that duck to reload
Andy's Ragdolls and Cover picks (ln-player, ln-cover, ln-npc). The idea is Leaning Or Not's; nothing of it is copied.
- **Lean** (`Lean`, `client/LeanClient`): sneaking, **Q** leans left and **E** leans right (rebindable under "Jaymo's Leaning"; without Shift Q still drops and E still opens the inventory). Hold to lean: the view and the upper body tilt out sideways about the feet, the eye moves out half a block (stopping short of a wall), and every shot leaves from where the eye is: arrows, bolts, EarlyGuns' balls, WeaponMod's and TaCZ's bullets (each is a projectile with its owner, moved out as it enters the world). Other players see you lean (a synced attachment, `Lean.DIR`). The server drops the lean the moment you stop sneaking, mount, swim, fall, are downed or crawl.
- **Cover while leaning** (`Cover`): a leaning body is aimed at where it has leaned out to, so a wall hides the covered side, and only the leaned-out part can be hit (a lean is never capped at the usual 85%).
- **Shooters take cover** (`RangedTactics`): a shooter that is shot at, or whose shot was stopped by cover, goes down behind a wall or block (80%); a gunner that has just fired a gun that has to be reloaded (folk musketeers, MCA guards with muskets, arquebuses and handgonnes) ducks for the reload and stands to lean out again 10 ticks before it is ready. Where it stands already counts as cover if a wall or parapet hides the crouching body but not the standing one, so wall archers duck at their posts instead of walking off. The cover found is kept 200 ticks while the foe stays near, and a shooter looks no more than every 40 ticks (a few clips, no wide scan).
- Camera roll and tilt directions, and the Q/E claim while sneaking, are untested in game.
