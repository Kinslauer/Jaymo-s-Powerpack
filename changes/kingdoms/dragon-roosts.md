# Dragon roosts, a staged entrance and two lairs

- **Dragon roosts.** Blackstone watchtowers now rise in the Nether now and then, out of sight, the way the war forts do
  (never within 480 blocks of another). A stair winds up the inside past a floor of wither skeletons every six blocks, to
  an open roof with a low battlement and soul lanterns. A chest of fortress loot sits on the ground floor.
- **The Ignivorus' entrance.** Climb out onto a roost's roof and its dragon comes: a wild Ignivorus near by, or a new one
  some way off, flies in and lands on the tower with its roar. Anyone on the tower who has never met one sees its
  entrance film as it comes in, staged for the landing instead of whenever it first wandered into view. It then keeps
  the roof for four minutes. A roost answers once a day. A dragon on its roost is violently territorial: once it has landed it goes for anyone on the roof.
- **The gorgon's ruin.** Where a gorgon spawns by nature, a broken ring of mossy stone columns stands round the spot.
- **The grizzly's den.** Where a grizzly spawns by nature there is its den ground: scraped bare earth, a rub tree with
  the bark worn off, and old bones. Neither lair is placed over water, under a roof or on built ground.
