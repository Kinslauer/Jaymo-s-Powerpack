# The fortress knight

- **The Fortress Knight.** Every Nether fortress now has one, once: the first time a player walks its halls he rises
  somewhere in them. A wither skeleton grown to a champion's size in blackened plate, with a boss bar, an entrance
  film the first time you meet one, the half-health turn and the fall.
- He fights like everyone armed does: his greatsword's whole combo, sidesteps and closing blows. He can be parried and
  his posture broken. His own: a long lunge whenever you stand off from him, and once he has turned the halls answer
  him, a possessed haunt rising near by every so often (three at most).
- He always drops **the Long Vigil**, his greatsword (with Jaymo's Powers; a netherite sword without). It is found
  nowhere else.
- Ops: `/kingdom knight` raises one where you stand.
- Compiled, not yet played.
