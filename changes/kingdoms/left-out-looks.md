# looks - thicker clouds, mist as cover, air for the other lands, moon and indoor shadows
- **Layered clouds**: the single flat sheet of cloud is replaced by two decks, each a stack of slices that lean along the wind, with a bright top and a grey belly; they slide past each other, fill with grey as rain gathers, glow at dawn and dusk and go dark at night (`CloudDecks`, `CloudRendererMixin`, textures from `tools/cloud_layers_art.py`). Off with the cloud setting, in a shader pack and outside the Overworld; low-end mode draws one deck of two slices.
- **Mist as cover**: how thick the morning mist lies at a spot is now worked out on the server too (`Mist`, which the client's fog reads as well; its numbers moved out of `SkiesClient`). In thick mist mobs notice a player at up to half the usual range (`Senses`), a stalking wolf or creeper counts a spot as cover once it is farther than anything is seen through the mist, wolves hunt in it by day, creepers creep on while the player cannot see them (`WolfStalkers`, `Stalkers`), and the Gaunt walks in it (`Gaunt`).
- **Air of the other lands**: crimson forest (red spores and haze), warped forest (teal spores), soul sand valley (rising wisps, pale blue banks), basalt deltas (thick ash, smoke banks, sparks) and the End (violet and pale motes), all typed motes and miasma, never recoloured dust (`DimensionAir`).
- **Shadows**: the Moon casts shadows at night, fainter at a thin moon and low in the sky; shadows fall on floors indoors (as far as daylight reaches them: patches through windows, none in caves) and on the sides of blocks that face the light (`SunShade`). Caustics stay by day.
- No new lang keys, so no `left_out_looks.json` part.
- Built (`compileJava`); not played.
