# The life story and the creator's Life tab run side by side

- "On second thought..." then "Skip the story" now always takes you to MCA's creator and its Life tab, also when MCA had already shown its creator once (after a rejoin partway through making a character, or after going back to the story). Before, you could be left on the story screen's own list of rows.
- On the Life tab's first card, Back is "< The story": it takes you back to the life story to tell it after all, and the creator comes back when the story is told or skipped again.
- On the Life tab's cards, a click on the picture or in the text box steps the pick on, and a right click steps it back (the arrows still work).
- When there is no MCA creator to go to, the story screen's "Make your character" page now uses the same cards as the Life tab (with its Harder, Nature and Presets pages) instead of its list of rows, which did not answer to clicks.
