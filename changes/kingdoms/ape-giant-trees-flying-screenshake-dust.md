# Ape/Giant: sprint hit trees fly, scaling screenshake, scaled dust particles

- **Aether Giants' footfalls, slams and stamps** now scale their particle counts, spread, and screen shake with the giant's size (height relative to a player). Larger giants kick up more dust, wider particle spreads, and stronger ground shake.
- **Sprint-hit tree throw** for Aether Giants: when an Aether Giant sprints into a tree, the tree is torn up and thrown outward (uses Kingdoms' FallingTree.hurl).
- Classes: `remnants/AetherGiant` (getScaleFactor, scaled footfall/slam/stamp).
- Built; compiles. Not played: scaled particle visibility at distance, shake intensity feel across giant sizes, sprint-hit tree throw on mob giants.
