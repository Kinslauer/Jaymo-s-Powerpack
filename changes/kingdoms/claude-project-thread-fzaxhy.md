# Illager Structures - the old pillager holds, and who lives in them now
- **36 new places** from Illager Structures (GNU GPL v3, by the Dawn of Time team; carried in this mod under `illager_sites/`, licence in `licenses/illager-structures/`): forts, towers, a lookout, balloon towers, tipis, a stable, the lava extractor and smeltery, three moles, a lone house, wells, a cart, a totem, a monastery and its tower, an herbalist's house, a galleon, the ocean citadel, a floating outpost, two submarines, iceberg outposts, wreck, shipyard and dig site, a tower ruin and a ruined church. The Mohai Illager is left out. The places come without their mobs; Amendments' lanterns and banners are vanilla torches, lanterns and chains; the Tipi places the single tipi; the church's suspicious gravel has its own finds; the Monk Tower's framed totem is a book.
- **Empty until its tier.** Each place stands empty, its chests holding only junk, until a player near it has opened its world tier and nobody is close enough to watch. Then its people move in where the old mobs stood, and every chest nobody has opened gets their loot (no gold gear, emeralds or totems; coins instead; netherite only in Dread places).
- **Who lives where** (Andy's picks, and Claude's for the ones he left): illagers in the fort, the lookout tower, the balloon tower and the iceberg dig site; bandits in the camp and maybe the cart; the tall and dark-oak towers held by the nearest realm's soldiers, or by bandits where no realm is in reach; smugglers (with masked bandits as muscle) in the stable, and smugglers in the floating outpost, both submarines and the shipyard; barbarians in the tipis; orcs (with their ogre band) in the extractor and smeltery; goblins in the moles, digging copper and iron (the deep mole most); pirates in the balloon, the galleon, the citadel, the iceberg outpost and maybe the wreck; monks, a prior and an herbalist in the monastery, its tower and the herbalist's house; a recluse in the lone house; the wells, totem, icebergs, ruin and church abandoned.
- **Camps and garrisons.** The fort, the camp, the tipi camp, the smeltery, the galleon, the citadel, the iceberg outpost and the dig site are camps: they raid, sit down to supper, keep captives (where Andy picked them), and fall when their leader dies. The rest hold their place and never raid. A held place is never torn down or built onto, and its people are made up again one a day at their posts. A place takes on at most 40 people, the ocean citadel 60.
- **Bandits** are a camp kind of their own: masked outlaws (MCA folk at home), cleared by killing their leader. **Smugglers** let you be unless you strike one of them or go through their goods (then the whole crew comes for you for a day); their fence sells Shash, Pmeh, forged papers and sealed bundles for the nearest dens, and buys Shash.
- **Monks, the herbalist and the recluse** are MCA villagers who stay near home and greet each visitor once a day; the herbalist sees to a hurt visitor's wounds. The recluse has a past (an old soldier, a widow, an exile or a deserter).
- Two new loading-screen tips. `IllagerSites`, `SitePosts` (generated by `tools/illager_sites_port.py`).
- Built and dev-server checked; not played.
