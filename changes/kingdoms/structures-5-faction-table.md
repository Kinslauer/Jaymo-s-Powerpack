# Structures 5: faction table and relations

Each faction now carries an allegiance — the people it belongs to (Human, Nonhuman, Bandit, Pirate, Greenskin, Pillager, Demon, Avatar or Pallid) — saved with the faction and read by placement and sieges. Starting relations between factions are seeded from their allegiances. Greenskins and Pillagers are sworn enemies: they can never ally or make peace.
