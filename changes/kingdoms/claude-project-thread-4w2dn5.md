# Crocodiles death roll their prey and drag it into the water
- Andy (2026-11-25): "crocodiles should be among the first, they should deathroll with a mob in its jaws, dealing damage every violent turn, then drag it's prey into the water to feed. like it's natural behavior".
- **Death roll** (`wild/DeathRoll`): a grown crocodile (Cube Animals) or alligator (Naturalist) that bites something no bulkier than 1.5 times itself keeps it in its jaws. It rolls with it, a violent turn every 12 ticks that does 1.5 hearts and splashes in water, 4 turns, then drags it to the nearest water within 16 blocks, holds it under there and keeps rolling (every 24 ticks). With no water near it keeps rolling where it is. Prey that dies in its jaws becomes a body it drags into the water and lets go of there to eat. Hurting it 3 hearts' worth since the bite makes it let go.
- Players are held too; fight back to break free.
- Built with Gradle; not played. The roll is a fast spin of the crocodile's own body; it doesn't turn onto its back (that would need a change to the other mods' models).
