# Crocodiles death roll their prey and drag it into the water
- Andy (2026-11-25): "crocodiles should be among the first, they should deathroll with a mob in its jaws, dealing damage every violent turn, then drag it's prey into the water to feed. like it's natural behavior".
- **Death roll** (`wild/DeathRoll`): a grown crocodile (Cube Animals) or alligator (Naturalist) that bites something no bulkier than 4 times itself (up to a horse or an ox) keeps it in its jaws. It thrashes side to side with it (Andy: "for the ones we cant roll, they should thrash side to side"), a violent wrench every 12 ticks that does 1.5 hearts and splashes in water, 4 wrenches, then drags it to the nearest water within 16 blocks, holds it under there and keeps rolling (every 24 ticks). With no water near it keeps rolling where it is. Prey that dies in its jaws becomes a body it drags into the water and lets go of there to eat. Hurting it 3 hearts' worth since the bite makes it let go.
- Players are held too; fight back to break free.
- **Bigger prey** (`Crocodiles.takePrey`): Andy, "go after bigger prey". Besides monsters, a crocodile or alligator now goes for wild beasts in sight, the bigger the likelier (a cow or a deer before a chicken), never pets. A fed one takes up nothing (Kitchen). Alligators get the crocodile's longer reach in water and at night too.
