# hollow captains - the risen dead's named captains are Hollow men
- **Named zombies and skeletons are Hollow NPCs now** (Andy: "the named zombies and skeletons should be replaced with skeleton, pallid, or zombie hollow npcs instead... they should fight the same as their counterparts at default only learning as they progress"). When a captain of the raised rises (a zombie or skeleton near a player, or one that kills a player), an MCA villager of the Hollow's blood stands up in its place: a skeleton gives a Skeleton Hollow, a zombie a Zombie Hollow or (30%) a Pallid. They keep their captain's name, lines, ladder and grudges, and can be talked to. Raised captains still in a zombie's or skeleton's body from before are made Hollow the next time they load. Without MCA the raised keep their zombies and skeletons.
- **They fight as the dead they rose from**: a zombie's or skeleton's health, blow, pace and sight under the captain's build; Zombie Hollows and Pallids close and strike bare-handed or with what they held, Skeleton Hollows keep off (5 to 15 blocks) and loose arrows every 2 s. A new one starts at rank 1 with no strengths or moves: he gains a strength for each player he kills, learns from each death when he comes back, and gets his own move at rank 4.
- **They come back as themselves**: each one's look and saved self (face, name, blood) is kept, so he returns with the same face in the next of the dead to walk his land. The raised now always return unless finished while down, beheaded or burned (was 75%).
- The dead never target them, and they count as outlaws (killing one is no crime).
