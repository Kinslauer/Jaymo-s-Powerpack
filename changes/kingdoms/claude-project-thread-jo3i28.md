# pallid captains - Pallids lead the risen dead
- **The Pallids lead the dead** (Andy: "the pallids are intelligent hollow and will end up leading armies of the skeleton and zombies, as another faction. the captain ladder should work primarily through them... that also gives skeletons and zombies reason to gain titles again"). When a new captain of the raised rises near a player, 3 times in 4 a Pallid stands up in the zombie's or skeleton's place: an MCA villager of the Hollow's blood who keeps the captain's name, lines, ladder and grudges, and can be talked to. Otherwise the zombie or skeleton takes the title itself, as before, and so does one that kills a player: the titled dead are the Pallids' lieutenants. Without MCA the raised keep their zombies and skeletons.
- **Their armies**: a Pallid captain calls up 2 of the dead as he rises (1 more a rank) and gathers the dead near him, titled or not, up to 10. They keep with him and go for whoever he fights. The dead never target him.
- **Their old class** (Andy: "pallids should fight with their old class abilities and weapons"): each Pallid has one of the player classes from his life, kept between deaths. He carries its starting weapons and armour and has its strengths: Warlords and Brutes hit harder, Brutes are tougher, Thieves and Monks are faster, Paladins and Monks take less damage, Rangers and Marksmen shoot from range, Mages, Sorcerers and Warlocks throw a fire bolt every 5 s at a foe out of reach, and Clerics and Druids heal the most hurt of their army (or themselves) every 10 s. His body underneath is a zombie's.
- **Learning as they climb**: every new captain of the raised (Pallid or titled dead) starts at rank 1 with no strengths or moves. He gains a strength for each player he kills, learns from each death when he comes back (75%, as before), and gets his own move at rank 4.
- **The same man back**: each Pallid's class and saved self (face, name, blood) are kept, so he returns with the same face in the next of the dead to walk his land.
- They count as outlaws (killing one is no crime).
