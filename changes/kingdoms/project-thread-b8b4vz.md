# boss intros - a short film the first time you see each great creature
- Andy: "can bosses get intro cutscenes? ... for the first encounter with ignovorius maybe, even passive boss mobs can get them. just to further illustrate the majesty of the encounter."
- **What plays**: the first time a great creature is within 40 blocks and in plain sight, your game plays a 6.5 second film: bars slide in, the HUD clears, a low shot from behind your shoulder tilts up to it, then the creature alone from near the ground as it roars (the frame shakes), then a wide side shot of the two of you. Every distance scales with the creature's size. The camera keeps to one side of the line between you (180 degree rule) and puts subjects on the thirds. No words on screen.
- **Who gets one, once per kind per player** (kept through death): every wild dragon (Ignivorus, the wind dragons, Aetherion, the ground dragons), the Aether Giants, each patron's champion, the Ogre King, chiefs, Nether warlords, the Piglin King, the grizzly, the minotaur, the gorgon, great whales, plus Powers' Slider, Visage of War and Aerwhale, and vanilla's Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden and Elder Guardian. Kings, lords and quest champions get none. A data pack can add more through the entity tag `jaymoskingdoms:boss_intro`.
- **Calm creatures** (whales, a dragon that isn't after you): the same film with no shake and its own call instead of a roar, and no boss bar after. A foe that is after you shows its boss bar as the film ends.
- **While it plays**: your movement keys don't answer and you take no harm; striking a blow or leaving the spot ends that. Skip with a fresh attack or use press, or by holding sneak. Other players see nothing and can keep fighting.
- If the film can't play (a screen is open, another film is running) it isn't counted as seen and comes again later.
- Ops: `/kingdom bossintro` replays the nearest great creature's film; `/kingdom bossintro reset` forgets the ones you've seen.
- Classes: `films/BossIntros` (server: who, when, seen list, protection, roar), `client/films/BossIntroClient` (shots, bars, skip), `BossBars.met`, the shared camera and input mixins.
- Compiled clean; not played.
