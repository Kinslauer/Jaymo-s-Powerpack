# Newborn Rubber-folk climb out of the Candy Cavity

- Now and then (about once an hour spent down there) a newborn Rubber-folk villager stands up on the floor of Alex's
  Candy Cavity some way off. Once nobody is watching it climbs up to the surface, then walks to the nearest settlement
  and lives there from then on.
- Meet a newborn and you can take it yourself: sneak and right-click it and it follows you (again to let it go). Bring
  it into any town and it stays there, fond of you. Leave it far behind for a couple of minutes and it sets off alone.
