# swordplay left-out parts - a drawn blade is a threat, one orders menu
What the SWGC swordplay patch (#1341) left out of Andy's ticked picks, built now.
- **A drawn, crouched blade is a threat** (`Menace`; Powers draws the stance and says who is in it, `PowersCompat.threatening`): within 12 blocks and in sight, a grown villager mutters a wary line and backs away from you with its eyes on the blade; a child runs and hides as from a monster. A guard or archer (ours or MCA's) warns you first, polearm levelled; if you keep the stance within 8 blocks of them for five seconds more, they take you for hostile and set on you. Barbarians and chiefs don't care. Without Powers nothing is drawn, so nothing is read.
- **Orders replace MCA's Follow and Stay** (`Followers`, `McaScreensMixin`, `McaWayPageMixin`, `FollowersScreen`): in MCA's Interact menu the Follow Me, Stay Here and Go About Your Day buttons are one Orders button that opens the orders screen for that villager, and the rows below move up. Orders now serve everyone already following you (an escort, a rescued captive, a child you lead, a bearer behind a bier) and a ship's hands aboard a ship you have the helm of, as well as family, hired help and friends. A hand leaves the ship for Follow, Fight beside me, Patrol or Clear only while she lies still; otherwise they keep their stations. Strangers still refuse. Needs MCA.
- Built (`compileJava`); not played.
