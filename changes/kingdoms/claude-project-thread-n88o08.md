# fire smoke settles low and makes you sick
- **Big-fire smoke hangs low**: the plumes over a burning house or forest rise about a third as high as before and spread out sideways as they settle, and the dark smog bank now gathers a few blocks over the flames instead of 30 to 48 blocks up. Every large flame's bank spreads as wide as only the biggest blaze's did before (18 blocks each way, 24 downwind). It shows less from far away than the old tall column did.
- **Smoke sickness** (`SmokeSickness`): anyone breathing that smoke (players, and villagers and other humanoids near a player) builds it up, faster nearer the middle and over a bigger blaze. After about 2.5 minutes of thick smoke the screen starts to swim (nausea, topped up while you stay in it). Out of the smoke your chest clears a little faster than it filled. Indoors the smoke is thinner, and crouching, crawling, going prone or lying down there keeps you under it so it clears even while you stay. Outdoors, staying low doesn't help. No smoke under water; creative and spectator are left alone. No chat text.
- `FireSmog` now builds its list of large flames once a round and shares it with the sickness check; `FireSmoke` and `StormParticle` (new `spread`) carry the lower, wider smoke.
