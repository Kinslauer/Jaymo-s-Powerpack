# Medieval patch 9: Disasters and the world's ending

- **Magnitude**: every disaster now rolls a strength from minor to catastrophic (a Blood Moon makes it one worse). It sets the disaster's size, length, speed and damage, not only the harm: a minor quake rattles a few dozen blocks, a catastrophic one opens sinkholes across two hundred. Relief costs rise with it (`disasters/Magnitude`, `Earthquakes`, `Calamities`, `DisasterRelief`).
- **Settlements remember**: a great disaster (magnitude 4 or 5) that strikes a settlement is kept with the world. For months after, its folk talk about it to anyone passing, in their own words: the year the mountain burned, the night the sea came in (`DisasterMemory`).
- **Volcanoes**: a tall peak in hot or Nether-touched land swells and smokes for days, with small quakes, then erupts. Thrown burning rocks, lava welling from the summit, burning clouds running down its slopes, and grey ash that falls in layers and buries the land, greying the fog. A caldera opens when it's spent, and its lava cools to obsidian and basalt (`disasters/Volcanoes`, ash layer block).
- **Tsunamis**: a strong quake under the sea sends a wave at the nearest shore with someone on it. It rears up as a wall of real water, runs inland, smashes what is weak (glass, fences, crops, and at its worst timber), carries people off their feet, then draws back, and every block of its water drains (`disasters/Tsunamis`).
- **Hurricanes**: a great storm comes in off the sea with a calm eye. Its wall of wind drags and lifts, flings wreckage, and brings lightning, floods where it makes landfall, and tornadoes. It weakens over land. Ships caught in it are holed and boats capsize (`disasters/Hurricanes`, `Storm.eye`).
- **Avalanches and landslides**: snow or loose earth breaks off a slope, gathers speed and buries what's below. Blizzards and quakes set them off (`disasters/Avalanches`).
- **Lightning seeks high ground**: in a supercell, lightning picks the tallest thing about (a tower, a tree, a rider on a hill), and a lightning rod draws it safely. A settlement under a thunderstorm gets waxed rods on its tallest roof and on its church (`disasters/HighGround`, `Storms`).
- **Giant hail**: in great hailstorms, fist-sized stones fall that hit hard and batter mobs. Any helmet turns them, and they wear it (`Calamities`).
- **Chains**: one disaster sets off the next. An undersea quake brings a tsunami, a meteor starts fires and quakes, a volcano shakes the ground, a hurricane spawns tornadoes and floods, and a blizzard or quake looses an avalanche.
- **Signs before it comes**: before a great disaster, the herds near it run and the villagers hide. The ground trembles, the sky darkens over the land, and there are distant rumbles. No text. The storm bell now also rings for the signs, an eruption, a wave coming, a hurricane overhead, and the world's ending. The almanac foretells the new disasters (`disasters/Signs`, `Herds.dread`, `Instruments`).
- **Falling stones**: meteors that blow craters lined with scorched stone. Great ones leave a day of grey dust in the sky, dimming the light and chilling the air (`disasters/Bolides`, `RedSky`).
- **The Last Bell**: a relic that Jaymo's Powers' Pocket Arsenal pulls through, at its full mastery only, one in the world at a time. Ring it and a map of the land around you opens. Where you pick, the disaster that land holds falls: a wave out of the sea (or a hurricane), a mountain opening in hot lands, the snow letting go, the hills breaking, and anywhere else a falling stone. Each ringing is worse than the last. It costs levels, blood and hunger, and won't sound again for a day. Each one shows its holder worse visions: the sky flashing red, the ground shaking under them, a toll from nowhere. After four ringings it rings only for the end: held through its last warning, it shatters (`disasters/LastBell`, `client/disasters/BellMapScreen`, `LastBellClient`).
- **The world's ending**: for three days, everything at once. The sky turns red, day and night. Stones fall and punch down to the bedrock. The sea floods in over every low coast. Great quakes break the ground and open any tall peak into a volcano. The tsunamis come in threes. Then it passes, and the land is left scarred. The Last Bell brings it. Very rarely, it comes without the bell: on a Super Blood Moon in an old world that has come far (Wildlands floor or a Legend), the night tolls with its omens and it comes at dawn. Operators can test it with `/kingdom disaster doom start|stop` (`disasters/Doomsday`, `RedSky`).
- **Bounded**: every block the great disasters change shares one budget a tick (`disasters/BlockBudget`), they only touch loaded land, and their state is saved with the world.

Built; not played.
