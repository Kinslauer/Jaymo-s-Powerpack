# Youth, quicker and cheekier; say its name; cry for help

- The Youth wisp is remade as a cheeky flying boy rather than a floating glow: a leaf-green tunic with a ragged hem, a belt,
  bare legs and soft boots. Its eight idles are new and quick, as suits something so small: hovering with kicking legs,
  crowing with hands on hips, a back flip, sitting cross-legged on the air, a swoop, lounging on its back, a spin and a
  belly laugh. It comes and goes in half a second.
- Say "youth" anywhere in a chat line and your wisp comes, whatever else you said.
- Villagers now and then tell you about the wisps: some say plainly to just say "youth" aloud, others only hint.
- Cry "help" or "help me" in chat while a wisp is in sight (within 24 blocks, in front of you, not behind a wall) and it
  flies you to your bed or respawn point: the screen flashes green, and layered light (a sigil, a ring, a blast, rising
  motes and leaves) marks where you left and where you land. With no bed, it tells you so.
