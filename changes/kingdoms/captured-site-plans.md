# Captured sites as kingdom plans; the illagers' desert arena
- **Captured Plans** (Andy: taking over a place from bandits or illagers "should allow you to add it to a schematic, so your kingdom can build it"): when any camp's leader falls (barbarian, illager, goblin, orc and pirate camps, bandit holds, smugglers' dens, every illager site and the new arena; cleared, or its seat taken by a sworn captain), the place is drawn as it stands that moment, before anything rots. Only what its people built is kept: no ground, stone, sand, snow or ice, water, plants, ore, or the trees growing through it; containers come empty. Whoever struck the blow gets the plans (a sealed parchment roll); a kill by anyone else leaves them where the leader fell. Nothing is taken from a camp the Reaper's host takes.
- Using the plans copies the place among your designs (named after the site: `illager_fort`, `bandit_camp`, `arena`, a second one `_2`; counts toward your 24) and turns them into a drawn Building Plan: lay it out and fill it, have a Mason's Table build it, or offer it to your settlement at one. A settlement's builders put up a captured design as big as 64 x 64, 64 high, 60000 blocks (others' designs stay at 32 x 32 x 48, 16000).
- Tiers: the plans keep their place's tier (an illager site's own, else the tier its taker stood in); a player who hasn't opened it can't make them out yet.
- **The arena** (our own design; Illager Arena is All Rights Reserved, so none of it is used): a desert-only illager site, 47 x 47, a sand fighting pit sunk four blocks with a parapet, stepped stands, a north gate and corridor down into the pit, rooms under the stands (prep area with targets, armory, sleeping quarters, smithy, barred pens) and a dark oak caster's box over the gate with the vault chests and two caged allays. Moves in at Wildlands like the other sites: an illager camp with its warlord in the pit, pillagers on the stands, vindicators, evokers, a ravager kept for the fights, and villagers penned to fight (captives). Its vault holds its own loot (`arena_vault`) once it's held. `tools/arena_site.py` draws it (and `ArenaPosts`).
- Classes: SitePlans (new), ArenaPosts (generated), Designs (keepCaptured, captured limits), IllagerSites (arena plan, allay posts, vault loot), Camps (hook in chiefSlain).
- Built; dev-server checked: the arena generates in deserts, its illagers, evokers, ravager and two caged allays move in, and killing its warlord drops plans of 45 x 45, 13 high, 8337 blocks that read back as a design builders can put up. Reading the plans in hand and a plain camp's capture not tried with a player; not played.
