# Captured sites as kingdom plans; the illagers' desert arena
- **Captured Plans** (Andy: taking over a place from bandits or illagers "should allow you to add it to a schematic, so your kingdom can build it"): when any camp's leader falls (barbarian, illager, goblin, orc and pirate camps, bandit holds, smugglers' dens, every illager site and the new arena; cleared, or its seat taken by a sworn captain), the place is drawn as it stands that moment, before anything rots. Only what its people built is kept: no ground, stone, sand, snow or ice, water, plants, ore, or the trees growing through it; containers come empty. Whoever struck the blow gets the plans (a sealed parchment roll); a kill by anyone else leaves them where the leader fell. Nothing is taken from a camp the Reaper's host takes.
- Using the plans copies the place among your designs (named after the site: `illager_fort`, `bandit_camp`, `arena`, a second one `_2`; counts toward your 24) and turns them into a drawn Building Plan: lay it out and fill it, have a Mason's Table build it, or offer it to your settlement at one. A settlement's builders put up a captured design as big as 64 x 64, 64 high, 60000 blocks (others' designs stay at 32 x 32 x 48, 16000).
- Tiers: the plans keep their place's tier (an illager site's own, else the tier its taker stood in); a player who hasn't opened it can't make them out yet.
- **The arena** (our own design, drawn from scratch): a desert-only illager site, 47 x 47, a sand fighting pit sunk four blocks with a parapet, stepped stands, a north gate and corridor down into the pit, rooms under the stands (prep area with targets, armory, sleeping quarters, smithy, barred pens) and a dark oak caster's box over the gate with the vault chests and two caged allays. Moves in at Wildlands like the other sites: an illager camp with its warlord in the pit, pillagers on the stands, vindicators, evokers, a ravager kept for the fights, and villagers penned to fight (captives). Its vault holds its own loot (`arena_vault`) once it's held. `tools/arena_site.py` draws it (and `ArenaPosts`).
- **Illager Arena joins the pack too** (Andy chose "Both"): the mod (SuperWarioModTeam, 1.0.0, shipped unchanged) is in the pack's mod list, so deserts get its great arena beside ours. Its own mobs never stay: every illager, ravager, allay and villager of its (and the pillagers its spawners make) is cleared from its arena as it loads, and the place stands empty until the Wildlands open near it; then one of our illager camps moves in, up to 60 strong, standing where its mobs stood (vindicators, pillagers, evokers, a warlord), with its three penned villagers as captives and its two allays caged. Its chests keep the mod's own loot. Our illagers' tongue counts time spent in it. Its warlord's fall gives Captured Plans like any other site (61 x 64, 40 high, about 15000 blocks).
- Captured plans of a site keep its own stone and sand walls: only what it set into the ground below the land about it, and the bed of land it was laid on, are left out; spawners, vaults, structure and jigsaw blocks never go in a design.
- Classes: SitePlans (new), ArenaPosts (generated), Designs (keepCaptured, captured limits), IllagerSites (arena plans, Illager Arena's mobs cleared, allay posts, vault loot), IllagerTongue, Camps (hook in chiefSlain).
