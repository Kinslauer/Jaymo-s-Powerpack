# Hardrock patch 4 - iron is scarce
Andy's TFC Hardrock picks: "most villages will not have iron armor or tools and will be more encouraged to trade for it".
- Only 1 realm in 4 has iron for its folk (`Outfits.IRON_REALMS`). Everywhere else guards, archers, builders and camp
  folk wear copper plate where they wore iron and carry copper swords, spears and axes (trims and dye kept). Kings and
  lords keep their iron.
- Iron and diamond are dearer on the trade routes: an iron ingot is worth 14 coppers (was 6), raw iron 8, a diamond 160
  (was 48), gold 12.
- The Ground Changes tier veins match Powers' new big rare veins: one big vein in some chunks instead of several small
  pockets in every one (iron 1 in 24, copper 1 in 20, gold 1 in 48, lapis 1 in 12; diamond and debris almost never).
- Compile-checked; not played.
