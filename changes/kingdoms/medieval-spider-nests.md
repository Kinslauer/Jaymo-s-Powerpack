# Medieval picks, patch 2: Spider nests

- The Silk Deeps, a new cave biome under the Overworld's temperate inland ground (placed beside the dripstone caves, and about as often met): its walls and roofs hang with matted nest silk over dark spider stone, shed skins lie on the floor, cobwebs are strung everywhere, and egg sacs sit on the floor and hang from the roof. Spiders and cave spiders are most of what lives there. (`SilkDeeps`, `NestBlock`, `SpiderNests`)
- Egg sacs hatch: walk close to one and it splits and spills two to four spiderlings; break one and they come out onto you. (`EggSacBlock`)
- The nest feeds and grows: small things that blunder into its silk (bats, silverfish, rabbits, chickens, frogs, barrow beetles) are wrapped and eaten, and a fed nest lays new egg sacs and spreads its silk a little, up to a cap. Burn it (any of its silk, skins or sacs catching fire, or an Emberborn burning through it) and that part of the nest is burned out for good; burn anything in a brood queen's lair and the whole lair is dead: her egg never grows back and no new queen comes. Emberborn contact burns nest silk, skins and sacs.
- Netcasters: a long-legged deep-cave spider (and the commonest in the Silk Deeps) that holds back and throws a silk net you can sidestep; caught, you're webbed (Jaymo's Powers' Webbed) and it closes in. Sometimes drops a silk gland (four string). (`SpiderKinds`, `SilkShots`, `NetcasterLegsMixin`)
- Spinies: small, pale, spiny spiders of the Nether's crimson and warped forests that run at you and burst in a spray of silk, webbing everyone near; kill one at range and it bursts where it stands. (`SpiderKinds`)
- The Brood Queen, a boss asleep over her great egg in the deepest chamber of the Silk Deeps (the brood lair). Strike the egg three times to crack it and wake her (it needs Knowledge 1; for anyone else it only rings dull and puffs dust). She has a boss bar and an entrance film (she rears up over her split egg and screams), and she: leaps at you and lands in a blast; slams her legs down twice, sending shockwaves along the ground to jump; spits silk that webs you; seizes you in her fangs (struggle with your movement keys or blows to break free, or be bitten and poisoned); sheds spiderlings off her back as she fights and at each quarter of her health; and grows bigger and faster the more she's hurt. Fire hurts her more. (`BroodQueen`, `BroodEggBlock`)
- Her egg grows back an hour after it split: if she's still alive she goes home and settles back onto it to sleep, healed; if she was killed a new queen is there (never two: the egg knows its queen). Not if the lair was burned.
- Nest watch huts: small spruce huts above the Silk Deeps, webbed over, with a chest holding a Nest Map to the nearest brood lair and a few supplies. (`NestStructures`)
- A spider's wake (patch 1) now carries you to the Silk Deeps when they're near, and the eggs on your cache are egg sacs that stay still for two minutes after you wake. (`SpiderLairs`)
- New blocks: spider stone, nest silk, shed skin, egg sac, brood egg; and the silk gland. Our own textures and the queen's own model and animations (`kingdoms/tools/gen_spider_nests.py`), with netcaster and spiny skins (`tools/gen_spider_kinds.py`).

Built; not played.
