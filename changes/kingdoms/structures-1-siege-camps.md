# Structures 1: siege camps spawn in groups; muster period; supply-dependent sieges; victor takes all

## What the player will see

- **Camp groups:** Instead of single camps spawning randomly near players, groups of 3-4 camps now spawn in a rough ring around kingdom/castle seats, like quickly thrown-up raid shelters preparing to siege.
- **Muster period:** When a war party arrives at a target, a 60-second muster period begins before the first wave. During this time, the siege draws supplies (food, logs, iron, arrows) from nearby allied camps. If a camp is cleared mid-siege, the siege is weakened.
- **Victor takes all:** When a siege is won, the defender faction is routed - its people scatter, its land transfers to the victor, and ALL its treasury and storage go to the victor's spoils (not just a share).
