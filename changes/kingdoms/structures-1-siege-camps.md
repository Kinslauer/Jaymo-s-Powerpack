# Structures 1: siege camps spawn in groups; muster period; supply-dependent sieges; victor takes all

## What the player will see

- **Camp groups:** Raider camps now spawn in rings of 3-4 around a kingdom or castle seat, like quickly thrown-up raid shelters preparing to siege. Illager camps, goblin villages, orc camps, pirate coves and sky coves still appear one at a time.
- **Muster period:** When a war party arrives at a target, a 60-second muster period begins before the first wave. During this time, the siege draws supplies (food, logs, iron, arrows) from nearby camps. If a camp is cleared mid-siege, the siege is weakened.
- **Victor takes all:** When a siege is won, the defender faction is routed, its people scatter, its land transfers to the victor, and all its treasury and storage go to the victor's spoils.
