# Endurance carries more
- **Endurance carries more**: every point of Endurance now also adds 0.5 kg to what you carry before Jaymo's Powers'
  pack weight slows you (40 points, Hearth's cap: +20 kg; 100 points: +50 kg), on top of its 0.375% less hunger a point.
  The Attributes tab's Endurance line shows both.
- `Stats.ENDURANCE_CARRY_KG`, `Stats.carryKg` (Powers' `PackWeight` reads it by reflection).
- Built; not played.
