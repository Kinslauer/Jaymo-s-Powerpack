# Layered, reactive sounds for Kitchen's held sounds
- Kitchen gets its own copy of Powers' sound helper (`sound/Soundscape`): a sound is a stack of layers, each picking one of its variants at a varied volume and pitch, played at uneven intervals instead of every Nth tick, and loud ones echo in cellars and caves. The recipes and their tuning are in `sound/KitchenSounds`.
- **Hob**: a pot bubbles with a rolling boil under it, now and then a boil-over hiss or the lid knocking; a pan crackles with fat spitting, a low hiss and Farmer's Delight's skillet sizzle; an oven baking crackles with the metal ticking as it heats. A dish finishing on the oven boils or sizzles harder and more often.
- **Cookware, stoves, hearths and ovens** (their animation ticks) play the same layers: a fire crackles with a pop of sap and the draw of the flue.
- **Casks** work slow and low with the odd creak of the staves; **stills** bubble quicker and higher with a drip and a hiss.
- **A body roasting** crackles with fat dripping and spitting into the flames.
- **Rats and scavengers** eat with varied bites and the odd tear.
- Built; not played.
