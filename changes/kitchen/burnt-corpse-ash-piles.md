# burnt corpses leave ash piles

When a corpse burns away on the ground (not in lava - lava burns leave nothing), it leaves a pile of ash where it
lay: a zombie a small heap, a horse or bear more, a ghast or giant a big pile across several blocks. Ash piles
stack 1-8 layers and stay put - they don't melt in rain and can't be shovelled up yet.

- `com.jaymoskitchen.corpse.CorpseEntity`: a burnt corpse leaves an ash pile where it lay before the body is
  discarded; lava burns leave nothing, as before.
- `com.jaymoskitchen.corpse.AshPileBlock`: new snow-style ash block, 1-8 layers, with loot table and lang.
- Built; not played in game.
