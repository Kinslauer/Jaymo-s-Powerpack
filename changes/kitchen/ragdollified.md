# Ragdollified: corpses lie down where its ragdoll lands
- **Dying bodies**: with Ragdollified loaded, a mob or player that dies falls as its ragdoll (their physics, their blow impulses). Our corpse is made at once with its loot as before, but isn't drawn while the ragdoll is (`CorpseEntity.origin`, a new synced id; a client that has no such ragdoll draws it at once, and a corpse nobody reports on is drawn again after 30 seconds). When the ragdoll comes to rest, the client sends `RagdollNet.Settled` (where its middle and ground are, which way its head points, from Ragdollified's `getState`); the corpse lies down there (`CorpseEntity.settleAt`, Kitchen's fallen pose, not carried, hung or dragged) and the ragdoll is removed the moment the corpse is drawn. Butchering, carrying, hanging, burial and corpse costumes work on that corpse as before. The server checks the report (the corpse still waiting, the reporter within 128 blocks, the spot within 64 blocks of the corpse and in a loaded chunk).
- **Limbs**: Ragdollified is told which limbs a body lost (the `missing_*` tags Dismember sets, `Corpses.missingParts`) through its `SeveredPartProvider`, so the ragdoll falls without them.
- **Reactions**: with Ragdollified Reactions loaded, our player limp (crashes, blasts, carts, hard landings, the limp key) and the mob limp from big hits and heavy blades stand down; its own do those. The first-person head camera yields to Ragdollified's camera while its ragdoll plays, and is ours again once the corpse lies.
- Without Ragdollified (the dev game) nothing changes: our ragdolls do all of it. All calls into Ragdollified are by reflection (`compat/RagdollifiedCompat`, `client/ragdoll/RagdollifiedBridge`).
- Not played. Corpses lie in our fallen pose, not the pose the ragdoll landed in (its pose isn't mapped onto our rig).
