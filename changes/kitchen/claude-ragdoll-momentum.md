# Killed and knocked-down mobs fall with the blow instead of popping into the air

- Andy: mobs killed or hit hard "should instantly be affected by the ragdoll, so the momentum carries them in the proper direction; right now they are dying then popping into the air". A ragdoll made from a body on its feet started with its feet sunk into the ground; pushed out on the first step, they threw the whole body up a fifth to half a block, and the blow's push was lost in the hop. Bodies now start standing on the ground, so a dying or knocked-down mob slides and crumples along the blow.
- A mob killed by a blow (a sword, an arrow, a bite, a blast) goes limp at once and falls the way it was hit, even when the blow hardly moved it. Only deaths from nowhere (fire, poison, hunger, drowning) still sway and keel over first.
- The keel-over plays out in full: it used to stop after three ticks because the body, stood right on the ground, never counted as touching it.
- A mob already knocked limp and then killed by an arrow or another mob keeps its body as its corpse, instead of standing back up and falling again.
