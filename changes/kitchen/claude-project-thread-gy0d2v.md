# The long dead come apart: limbs tear off, skeletons drop in pieces, struck corpses lose parts
- **Lifting a zombie's or a skeleton's body** (Andy: "the most that should come off is a limb, same with zombies, they are decaying and dead"): sneak + hold on one no longer brings the whole body up. A limb comes away instead (an arm or a leg, "It comes apart in your hands") and the rest stays lying; with Jaymo's Powers the limb is in your hand. Once it has no limbs left the trunk lifts as before. People-shaped ones only (not a zombie horse); guards, carts and Gravity still take them whole.
- **Skeletons fall to pieces on death** (Andy: "retaining maybe the central torso and legs if not otherwise knocked off"): the skull and both arms come loose as it drops; the ribs and legs stay together. One that gets back up is whole again.
- **Hits to a body on the ground dismember it** (Andy: "hits to these corpses on the ground should further dismember"): each blow may knock a part off (head, arm or leg), by its damage: 12% a point on bare bones, 8% on a zombie's body, and 3.5% on a fresh body but only under a sword or an axe. Fresh bodies only of the kinds Powers lets lose parts alive; never a player's body, a machine's or an animal's.
- **Too big to lift** (Andy: "i also should not be able to lift something that large"): any monster's body wider than 1 block or taller than 2.4 standing is dragged, not lifted (only animals had a size limit); the strong still lift those, but nothing 3.2 blocks long or tall. The Unburied leaves no body at all now (`no_corpse`).
- Parts off a corpse go to other mods through `CorpseEntity.setOnPart` (Powers makes them severed parts); without one a skeleton's is a loose bone and a zombie's a piece of rotten flesh.
- Code: `corpse/CorpseEntity` (`comesApart`, `losePart`, `fallToPieces`, `knockApart`, `tooBigToLift`, the `APART_` chances), `Corpses.spawnCorpse`, lang part `corpse_apart.json`.
