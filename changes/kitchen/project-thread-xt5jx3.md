# Other long-dead bodies come apart

- A new entity tag, `jaymoskitchen:comes_apart`: bodies of the mobs in it come apart as a zombie's does (a limb comes
  away when you lift one, blows knock parts off) without being a zombie's body in any other way: they don't get back up
  or burn in the sun. Jaymo's Kingdoms puts its draugr and its reaped illagers in it. Newly turned MCA villager zombies
  are not in it and stay whole.
- A body of one of Jaymo's Kingdoms' Blockbench mobs (the zombie knight, the revenant) is drawn without the parts it
  has lost.
