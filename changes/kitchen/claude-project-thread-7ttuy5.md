# Bodies knocked into walls slump against them
- Andy: "can enemies who ragdoll into walls pose similarly? as if they were knocked into the wall and slipped down onto the floor? basically so they dont all default to lying flat."
- A person-shaped corpse (players, zombies, villagers, illagers, skeletons...) that's thrown into a wall, or killed beside one hard enough to hit it, now slides down it (turning round if it hit face first, bouncing a little off it) and ends sitting on the floor with its back to the wall: legs out in front, arms fallen to its sides, slumped a little to one side, head lolled forward. About 85 in 100 do; the rest crumple as before. A body lying on the floor that gets shoved into a wall stays lying, and animals never sit (only the humanoid ragdoll does).
- It stays sat there (and still sits after a reload) until something moves it: a hit, an axe, rummaging, someone walking into it, picking it up or dragging it, or the wall or floor being broken. Then it falls over as a normal ragdoll.
- It needs a solid wall face where it struck that goes down to a floor no more than 3 blocks below it; a body thrown at a wall high over a drop just falls.
- `ragdoll/WallSlump` (the slide and the pose, tuning at the top), `CorpseRagdoll` (spotting the hit, holding it sat), `CorpseEntity#slumpWall` / `#stillPropped`, `RagdollBody#settle`, `CorpseImpacts#setOnSlump` (tells Jaymo's Powers). Checked in `tools/ragdoll_sim` (new wall-slump section); not played.
