# Balloon mushroom dishes

- Two new dishes from the small balloon mushroom of the Verdant World's mycotic forest: Balloon Mushroom Soup (a slow-falling bowl food) and Balloon Mushroom Skewer (a campfire grill that lifts the jump). Powers' mushroom is reached by registry id, and the recipes load only when it exists.
- Kitchen's lang now merges small part files (`lang/parts/*.json`) into `en_us.json` at build, so new features keep their own keys.
- Built (`compileJava`); not played.
