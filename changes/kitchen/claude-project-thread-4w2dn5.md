# Eaten bodies leave their remains; fed zombies stop hunting
- Andy (2026-11-25, clip of baby zombies eating a guard): "when he was fully eaten he dropped nothing. i'd like to change that so anything the monster couldnt eat is dropped, and a realistic amount of entrails should drop, that npc died and there was one intestine and no bones", and "this small zombie was ravenous, it should only be this aggressive when hungry".
- **Nothing walks off with the dead man's things**: what falls off a body as it's eaten or torn (its helmet, boots, what it held, its pockets, its bones) is tagged as remains (`Corpses.REMAINS_TAG`) and no mob takes it up (`RemainsPickupMixin` on `Mob.pickUpItem`). Before, the zombies at the meal picked up the guard's armour and spear as they fell, so nothing was left. Players pick it up as usual; the meat stays the scavengers'.
- **What an eaten body leaves** (`Gibs.eaten`, `Gibs.bonesLeft`): the ribcage, the spine, a skull (a person's body), 2-3 bone shards, 1-2 organs and two ropes of gut it didn't finish, 2-3 gut piles round it (intestines and offal in turn), the tatters of its clothes, and 3-5 bones as items (was a rib, a spine, a bone or two, one gut rope and one pile, and 1-2 bones). A bigger body leaves more, a smaller one fewer.
- **Fed monsters stop hunting** (`Scavengers.fedPace`): a monster that has swallowed a part lately or is full no longer takes up prey (full now counts too, not only the two minutes after a part), drops whoever it was after unless they hurt it in the last 10 seconds, and ambles at 70% of its speed. Hungry, it hunts as hard as ever. Wild scavengers only stop taking up prey, as before.
