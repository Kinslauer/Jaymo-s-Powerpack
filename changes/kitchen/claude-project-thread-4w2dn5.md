# Eaten bodies leave their remains; brains first; only hordes and blood moons finish a body
- Andy (2026-11-25, clip of baby zombies eating a guard): "when he was fully eaten he dropped nothing. i'd like to change that so anything the monster couldnt eat is dropped, and a realistic amount of entrails should drop, that npc died and there was one intestine and no bones", and "this small zombie was ravenous, it should only be this aggressive when hungry". Then: "they should also prioritize brains, then guts/entrails/then limbs."
- **Brains, then guts, then limbs** (`CorpseEntity.nextPart`, `CorpseEntity.GUTS`): a scavenger tears off the head first, then opens the belly and pulls the guts out (`Gibs.gutsPulled`: the organs, gut ropes and gut piles it didn't finish fall around the body, and it's drawn gutted), then the arms and legs, and only then tears the trunk open. Before, it was the limbs in any order, then the head.
- **Most bodies are left half eaten** (`Scavengers.finishes`, `CorpseEntity.pickedOver`): Andy, "the only time someone should be completely devoured is during an encounter with a horde (as they all will want a bite) and during blood moons". Once only the trunk is left, an eater leaves it lying with what it wore, unless 4 or more scavengers are within 8 blocks of the body or the eater is ravenous.
- **Ravenous under a blood moon** (`Scavengers.ravenous`, `compat/MoonCompat`): during Jaymo's Kingdoms' Blood Moon or Super Blood Moon a monster is never fed or full, so it hunts on and eats until the body is gone. Without Kingdoms there is no blood moon.
- **What a finished body leaves** (`Gibs.eaten`, `Gibs.bonesLeft`): the ribcage, the spine, a skull (a person's body), 2-3 bone shards, 1-2 organs and two ropes of gut, 2-3 gut piles round it (intestines and offal in turn), the tatters of its clothes, and 3-5 bones as items (was a rib, a spine, a bone or two, one gut rope and one pile, and 1-2 bones). A bigger body leaves more, a smaller one fewer. Its gear and pockets drop as before; monsters that can pick up loot still take them up and wear armour that's better than theirs (Andy: "thats fine ... they should equip armor too").
- **Fed monsters stop hunting** (`Scavengers.fedPace`): a monster that has swallowed a part lately or is full no longer takes up prey (full now counts too, not only the two minutes after a part), drops whoever it was after unless they hurt it in the last 10 seconds, and ambles at 70% of its speed. Hungry, it hunts as hard as ever. Wild scavengers only stop taking up prey, as before.
