# Gun gear from the future: tracers, magazines, plate carriers
- Future guns now fire tracers: about one shot in four leaves a glowing amber streak to wherever it lands and lights the muzzle for a moment. Tracer Rounds, right-clicked onto a loaded future gun in your inventory, take the place of that many of its rounds (you get those back) and always streak.
- Magazines for handguns (17), SMGs (30), rifles (30), shotguns (8) and machine guns (60). Right-click rounds onto one to load it; right-click it onto a gun of its kind to swap: the gun takes its rounds and the magazine takes the gun's. Snipers have none. TaCZ's own reload from loose rounds still works.
- Plate Carrier: chest armour (iron's protection, a little toughness) some of the future's gunners wear.
- None of it is craftable or in a creative tab: dead gunners and echoes drop magazines for their gun, tracer rounds and the carriers they wear.
- Classes: GunGear (new), FutureGuns, MuzzleFlash. Built; not played in game.
