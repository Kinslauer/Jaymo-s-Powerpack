# Medieval patch 8: shields and smithing
- **Shield reflect**: a perfect parry with a raised shield sends an arrow, trident, fireball or thrown potion back at whoever threw it, and it counts as the thrower's (`combat/ShieldCraft`, `mixin/ShieldReflectMixin`; after Guarding by Team-Abode, CC0).
- **Instant block**: a raised shield blocks at once, with no quarter-second wait (`mixin/ShieldBlockMixin`).
- **Pummelling** (shield enchantment, I-III): a parry shoves much harder. **Barbed** (shield enchantment): whoever strikes your shield takes thorns damage, and the shield wears for it. Both are learned from scrolls (ravagers drop Pummelling, pufferfish drop Barbed) and taken at the enchanting table. The plain shield now takes enchantments too.
- **Netherite shield**: a smithing-table upgrade of the shield with a netherite ingot and the upgrade template. It is twice as tough, fireproof, gives a little knockback resistance in hand, and takes armour trims at the smithing table, drawn on its face in place of a banner (`client/combat/NetheriteShieldRenderer`).
- **Reheat**: every hammer blow takes heat out of the piece on the Smithing Anvil, and its glow dims. Once it's cold it goes back in the forge to heat again, then on to be finished and quenched (`SmithingAnvilEntity`, `SmithingAnvilRenderer`).
- **Realm steels** (Alloyer): steel and lead make the Stoneborn's deep steel, steel and silver make the forest folk's bright steel, and bronze run with ash makes the orcs' ash bronze. They cast into ingots and forge everything steel does. Deep steel is the hardest to work: its blows shove harder and its armour stands firmer. Bright steel bites the undead. Ash bronze is easy to work but crude, never better than Expertly Forged. With Kingdoms, only one of that people, or one sworn to a haven of theirs, can work each steel on the anvil. Lead is a new ore deep under the mountains (`smithing/RealmSteels`, `smeltery/Molten`, `smeltery/Melting`).
- **Realm designs**: with Kingdoms, a kingdom's knightly helm and its war arm can be forged only by its sworn smiths, or by anyone with a captured pattern (a blueprint taken from a piece at the Drafting Table, or a copy of one). Another realm's design is left off the anvil's list (`smithing/RealmPatterns`, `Patterns`, `Blueprints`).
- **Mail from rings**: hammer iron nuggets into mail rings, link nine rings into a mail sheet, and lace sheets into the chainmail helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Rings and sheets melt back down (`smithing/MailRings`).
- **Inscription Table**: lay gear on it, set a gem beside it and take a chisel to it to carve a word of power into the gear (one enchantment level). The gem decides which words are open, and each gem lasts three carvings. The Enchanting overhaul's limits still apply (`enchanting/Inscription`, `InscriptionTableBlock`, `client/enchanting/InscriptionScreen`).
