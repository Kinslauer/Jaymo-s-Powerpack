# Pull up onto shore from the water; right-click held climbs
- **Out of the water** (Andy: "i also tried to climb onto shore from the water and couldnt right click the ground to pull up like normal"): an empty-hand right-click at a shore's edge now catches it from the water, and the hang under the edge (which is in the water) isn't lost to it. A swim into a wall still doesn't catch it by itself; only the click does. Shift-scroll climbs from shallow water too. `Ledge#canHang(Player, boolean)`, `LedgeClient`.
- **Right-click parity** (Andy: "the keybind for climbing should also be a right click hold with empty hand when you unlock it"): holding right-click with an empty hand on a ledge pulls up as Jump does (a tap still only hangs). With Climber learned, holding it at a wall does what forward + Jump do: jumps onto it, clings and climbs up, and over the top. Falling, a held right-click is still the wall slide's. Not with the crosshair on a mob, door, chest or the like. `FreerunClient#useClimb`, `LedgeClient#useHeld`.
- Built on CI; not played.
