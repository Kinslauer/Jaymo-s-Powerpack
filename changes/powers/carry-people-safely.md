# carrying people safely - no drops, set down on purpose, willing folk carried
- Andy's clip (2026-11-27): a freed prisoner (Teimuraz) carried in the arms was set down by a right-click, then dragged downhill and died. "you should be able to carry prisoners and anyone willing, and you shouldnt be able to drop them".
- **Set down on purpose**: a person in your arms (a player, a villager, anyone downed or hogtied) is set down with right-click *held* for 1 s (a bar fills), never with a click, and only with your feet on the ground (or in water, or mounted). In the air (flying, Vapour Form, mid-jump) you keep hold of them and are told to land first. They're laid in front of you, not dropped. Animals, blocks and severed parts still go with a click. Throwing (sneak + hold) is unchanged. Letting go of a *dragged* body is still a click: they're already on the ground.
- **Never dropped by accident**: teleported, through a portal, or lurched past the leash, the person you carry comes with you (into the other world too). Mounting a horse or boat keeps them in your arms. Taking off into flight while dragging someone lifts them into your arms (too heavy in their armour: let go where they lie). Wind flight no longer fumbles a carried person. When you can't hold them any more (you're downed, your power goes), they're laid down gently, not flung.
- **No harm from the ground**: anyone carried, dragged or slung over a horse takes no fall, in-wall, cramming or fly-into-wall damage, and nor does anyone just set down or let go until they've lain on the ground a moment (up to 30 s). A dragged body is lifted clear of the slope it's pulled over (it lay in the hillside behind you going downhill).
- **Bleeding out holds**: a downed mob's bleed-out clock stops while it's carried, dragged or slung.
- **Willing people**: new `Carry.WILLING_CHECKS` (Kingdoms fills it): someone standing who's willing to be carried by you is lifted with sneak + hold right-click (2 s), as a player is, and held like anyone carried. Runs before other mods' sneak-use on villagers.
- Classes: core.Carry (SET_DOWN_TICKS, keptSafe, willing, sheltered, bringAlong, settle, clearOfGround, Let.LAY), wounds.Downed (step), wind.WindFumble, client CarryUseMixin. Lang: carry.carrying_person, carry.land_first, bar setting_down.
