# Flashlight from the future; future gunners run out of ammo
- **Flashlight** (Illuminated by LambdAurora and Akarys, added to the pack; needs LambDynamicLights, already in): a hand
  light that throws a real cone of light, right-click to switch it on and off. It's future tech, kept like TaCZ's guns:
  it has no recipe (`tinkering/Uncraftable`), it's in no creative tab or JEI, and it's only found in the Chrono future:
  15% of chests opened there hold one, and 30% of the corpse zombies (the dead who were people) carry a lit one in the
  off hand, dropping it half the time. One you bring back is yours to keep (`power/chrono/future/FutureFlashlights`).
- **Future gunners carry less ammo** (Andy: "mobs with guns should spawn with less ammo"): their shots used to be free.
  Now every shot spends a round from the gun they hold, which comes with 1 round up to a quarter of a magazine, and they
  carry 0 to half a magazine of spare rounds to reload from (a slower reload, with a click). Out of both, they sling the
  gun and rush you hand to hand. A dead gunner drops the spare rounds it never fired, instead of a random handful up to a
  stack. The mechs' and Omens' built-in cannons don't run dry (`power/chrono/future/FutureGuns`, `armory/TaczGuns.loadRounds`).
- Built (Powers and Kingdoms compile); not played. A dev server boot here stopped on an unrelated recipe registry error, so it isn't boot-checked.
