# Enemy Damage Balance

Reduces enemy damage across the board and makes heavy moves less frequent.

## Powers

### ZFighterRogue (Champions & Tyrants)
- Champion base damage: 3.0 → 2.0
- Tyrant base damage: 7.0 → 4.0
- Tyrant Super 2 damage: 9.8 → 5.6
- Basic blows: ~1.0 (champion) / ~1.4 (tyrant) per hit (was ~1.5 / ~2.5)
- Finisher: ~2.6 / ~3.6 (was ~3.9 / ~6.3)
- Slam: ~1.0 / ~1.6 (was ~1.5 / ~2.8)
- Ki blast volley: 2.0/4.0 → 1.5/2.5 per blast

### ZFighterNpcKi (Tyrant/Champion Ki attacks)
- Beam damage share: 1.4× → 1.0× base damage
- Volley bolt damage share: 0.35× → 0.25× base damage
- Charged blast damage share: 3.0× → 2.0× base damage
- Cooldowns doubled: beam 15-25s → 30-50s, volley 8-15s → 15-30s, charged blast 12-20s → 25-40s

### RivalAlien (Xeno-Band hunter)
- Base attack damage: 4.0 → 2.5
- Damage share from form: 1.0× → 0.7×

### XenoDouble (Duplex doubles)
- Attack damage: 3.0 → 2.0

### ChronoEcho (Time remnants)
- Attack damage: 5.0 → 3.0

### Unseeing (Blindness cult)
- Follower/Blindfolded/Gouged damage: 3.0 → 2.0
- Zealot damage: 5.0 → 3.5
- Pulse damage: 4.0 → 2.5

## Kingdoms

### Wild Beasts
- Grizzly: base damage 9.0 → 5.0; charge 11.0 → 7.0; slam 10.0 → 6.0; maul bite 4.0 → 3.0; attack gap 14/8 → 20/12 ticks
- Feral Wolf: 5.0 → 3.0
- Coyote: 4.0 → 2.5
- Moose: 7.0 → 4.5
- Wild Boar: 5.0 → 3.0

## Player Experience
- Basic melee attacks are now the primary threat, dealing low damage per hit
- Heavy moves (finishers, charges, slams, ki beams, charged blasts) hit harder but are used much less often
- Tyrants no longer one-shot players; a string of blows gives time to react, guard, or break away
- Wild beasts are threatening but not lethal in a single mistake
