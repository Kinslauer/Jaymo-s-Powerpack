# Rig patch 5: the Mind talks, flies and keeps watch

- **Talk to the Mind** (`md-talk`): the wearer's typed chat is caught and answered in the Mind's personality (hints only, never lore), heard by the wearer alone.
- **Autopilot** (`md-pilot`): the suit flies toward a marked waypoint, charge permitting; any movement takes control back.
- **Evacuate the host** (`md-evac`): near an empty Core, a hit that would take the wearer down throws them clear and the suit slumps as a wreck to repair at the Forge.
- All three are Rig menu entries (Sneak + J), gated by Mind level and charge, with poses and sounds.

- Built and compile-checked. Not tested in game.
