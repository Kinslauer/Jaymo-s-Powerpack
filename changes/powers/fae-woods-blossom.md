# Fae Woods: shipped mods for the Blossom land, balloon gusts
- **Pack**: Psithurism 1.4.0 and MModding Library 0.9.2-alpha (MModding Team) and Hot Air Balloons 0.0.30 (Profet; unchanged jar, credit and its CurseForge link in the build file) are added to the pack. Fantasy Mounts is not shipped (its author has not agreed).
- **Balloon wind**: our hot air balloon's wind now has gusts as well as layers by height (`HotAirBalloon.wind(level, x, y, z)`); Kingdoms' airships and ferries read the same wind.
- Built; not played.
