# The glass gauntlet, the clawed guard and the stretch dodge

- **Glass Gauntlet** (`power/arsenal/GlassGauntlet`). No recipe and no chest: one Reach in 300 by an Arsenal who has
  never held it closes on it. From then on the rift keeps it for that hand and brings it first on every Reach, as a
  pinned weapon comes (an ascended Arsenal's own pin comes before it). It is a summon like any other pull and goes back
  when its time is up. Sneak and Reach with an empty hand leaves it in the rift, so the usual pulls and stash relics
  come back; the same again calls it. Losing the power loses it.
  - **Claws out** (how it comes): a quick claw, 6 damage at a dagger's speed, one of the claw family, and it guards
    and parries like any weapon.
  - **Open hand** (sneak and right click, in the air or at a block): digs, chops, shovels and tills as a diamond tool
    of each kind. Right click strips a log or tills soil. It hits like a tool and does not guard. It makes no paths.
  - Shown in JEI with how it is come by.
- **The clawed guard.** The glass gauntlet and Rimeclaw held up are no longer a sword's guard: the claw hand goes high
  before the face, the other hand back at the hip, the body low and turned behind the claw (`ParryPose`). The lines a
  blow is met on are as before.
- **The stretch dodge.** Rubber-folk and Verdants no longer tumble when they dodge on the ground: the body leans far
  out the way of the dodge from the feet, drawn out long and thin, arms thrown up along it, and snaps back
  (`DodgeRollPose`, told by `Dodge.STRETCHY`). A look only: the dodge itself, its cost and the flip in the air are
  unchanged, and the first person view does not turn over for them.
- The gauntlet is drawn held in the hand, not worn on it. Compiles; not played in game.
