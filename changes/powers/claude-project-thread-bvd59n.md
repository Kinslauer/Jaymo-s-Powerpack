# Worn pieces follow the body (bauble looks, trinkets, hooded cloak, quiver)
- **Pieces worn over the body now move with it** under Fresh Animations, as armour does: the bauble looks, Artifacts trinkets (scarves, belts and the rest), the hooded cloak and Kitchen's quiver. Each set itself up again in the plain pose when drawn, so it stayed in the vanilla walk while the body bobbed and swayed under it. They now take the body's pose kept just before the render layers (`WornPose.keep` from `CostumePoseLivingEntityRendererMixin`, `WornPose.fit`; Kitchen's quiver through `KitchenQuiverPoseMixin`).
- On MCA bodies their head pieces also take MCA's head size (children and teens), as costumes do (`McaHeadScale`).
- **Hooded cloak**: its sway (the back and hem swinging with your stride, the hood hanging down when the head slot is empty, the narrower collar and mantles with no chestplate) was set when the cloak was submitted, and the model is set up again just before it is drawn, which put all of that back at rest. It is now posed as it is drawn (`HoodedCloakLayer.Model.setupAnim`).
- Third-party pieces checked and already following the body: Traveler's Tool Belt, Weapon Master's worn weapons, Not Enough Animations' sword on the back, Entity Texture Features' player extras.
- Built and loaded in a cloud test game (no errors); the pieces weren't each put on and looked at. Not played.
