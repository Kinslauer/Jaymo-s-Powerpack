# Rig patch 2: guns that unfold

A Rig wearer gets six new gun actives, each a Rig menu entry with charge cost, poses and sounds:

- **Palm blast held charge** (`gn-charge`): Tap for quick bolts, hold to charge a big blast. The limb glows while charging. (Mind level 2)
- **Gatling arm** (`gn-gatling`): Spin up, pour bolts while held, overheat if held too long; vents hiss to cool. Heat bar on HUD. (Mind level 3)
- **Railgun** (`gn-rail`): Charge, then release an instant line through everything; leaves BeamScars (scorched ground). (Mind level 4)
- **Sonic cannon** (`gn-sonic`): Hold to blast a cone of sound that throws and stuns creatures. Resonance users caught in it hear it amplified. (Mind level 4)
- **Twin beam** (`gn-twin`): Both arms become cannons and fire one wide beam together. Braced wide stance. (Mind level 5, Unibeam variant)
- **Siege mode** (`gn-siege`): Plant feet, artillery cannon unfolds from back; rooted, fire shells where you look until you fold it away. (Mind level 5, Siege frame only)

All need the whole set and Core charge. Poses go through RigMoves/RigPose, effects through MagicFx.
