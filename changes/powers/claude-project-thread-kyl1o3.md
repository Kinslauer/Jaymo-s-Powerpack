# fishing line from the rod tip, bait on the first click
- **Line from the rod tip**: the fishing line now leaves the rod's actual tip, in first and third person, either hand, on the 3D held rods, the cane pole, spinning and ledger rods, tinker rods and the metal rod, and it follows the rod's bend while a fish fights. While the rod is drawn, its tip is read off the real model matrix (`RodTip`, `RodTipLayerMixin`; first-person hand FOV and view bob are taken out so the line meets the tip on screen); the hook renderer starts the line there, else vanilla's old guess.
- **Bait on the first click**: using a bare rod with bait in your other hand, or anywhere in your pack (hotbar first), puts it on the hook straight away; no sneaking. The next use casts. Sneak + use still turns the drag, and a baited rod casts as before (`HookBait`).
- Built; not played.
