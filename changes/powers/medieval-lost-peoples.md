# Medieval patch 10: Lost peoples and the mineshafts

- **Arms of the old age**: seven iron weapons and shields from the lost peoples, each people's own. They are the sands' hooked sickle-sword, the plains' short legion blade, tall shield, leaf-bladed spear and round shield, Blossom's broad sabre and the north's bearded axe. Each swings with its own Better Combat moves. The sickle-sword's hook and the bearded axe's beard pull a raised shield down for a few seconds. The tall shield is slow to raise but covers a wide arc, and the round shield is quick and covers less (`armory/OldAgeArms`, `Armory`).
- **Forged only from a pattern**: the heads and blades are forged like any other weapon part, but only by a smith who carries a blueprint of the shape. The blueprint comes from taking a found piece apart at the Drafting Table. No cast is made of them, and the Part Builder won't shape them (`tinkering/WeaponParts.OLD_AGE`, `smeltery/Melting`, `Tinkering`).
- With Jaymo's Kingdoms they lie in the old ruins' tombs and houses and in their guards' hands.

Built; not played.
