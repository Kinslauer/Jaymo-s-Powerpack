# Z Fighter and weapon moves

- Great Ape moves: added alternating left and right arm swipes (quick left-click tap), a staggering shockwave roar (Sprint+R; R still charges ki), a two-handed overhead ground slam pose on earthwork stomps, a crashing leap smash (Sneak+B or B midair), and a boulder scoop-and-throw (Sneak+G), each with its own pose.
- Staged beam firing: Z Fighter beams now progress through gather, hold, fire, sustain (holding V extends sustain and tracks aim gently), and end stages; foes caught inside a beam you are holding are outlined in red on your screen.
- Instant Step: once Ki Sense has been learned, Z Fighters can raise two fingers to the brow (B while Ki Sense is active, or Sneak+B) and blink a few blocks ahead — or behind a sighted foe while sneaking — with a short ki shimmer at departure and landing.
- Overburn: hold Sneak+X to stack a crimson aura boost up to 4 stages (layered over base or any active form on the ki aura); stages above half hurt periodically, letting go holds the boost for a while, and when it fades you are left slowed.
- Chain Blades on the Chain Axe: Sneak+right-click while embedded (or Sneak on throw release) yanks a hooked foe straight to you, and Sneak+left-click launches a spinning 360-degree cyclone of chained blades around you.
