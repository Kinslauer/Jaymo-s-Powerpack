# Boxing: the three stances copied from fight footage

- (Jung, outside Wind) The three styles' stances, blocks and shoulder roll are reshaped after fight footage Jung sent
  ("Every Boxing Guard Explained in 8 Minutes": its peekaboo, long guard and Philly shell parts). Third person only,
  as before; the model has no elbows, so each is the nearest a straight arm gets.
- **Arrow Slit** (peekaboo): both gloves up on the cheeks, a forward crouch with the chin down, a wider side-to-side
  sway. Its block pulls the gloves in tighter and crouches lower.
- **Long Guard**: the lead arm straight out at shoulder height, the rear glove by the chin, the body upright and a
  little back. Its block puts both arms out ahead with the hands together (it crossed the forearms before).
- **Bastion** (Philly shell): side on with the lead shoulder forward and held up by the chin, the lead arm low across
  the belly, the rear glove by the cheek, the weight back and rocking slowly, the head tipped to the rear shoulder
  with the chin tucked. Its block turns further side on with the shoulder higher. Its shoulder roll now also sways
  back and tips the head away.
- When the body turns the shoulders go round with it, and when it leans the legs stay under the hips (neither did
  before). A cross now brings the rear shoulder forward; it turned the wrong way.
- `client/boxing/BoxingPoses`: every angle is still a knob at the top. Not built locally; not played, the angles are
  set from the footage without seeing them on a model.
