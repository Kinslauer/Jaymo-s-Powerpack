# Z Fighter gaps 9: borrowed time, spared masters' homes, themed tournaments
- **Borrowed time from the dead**: the fortune teller can lend the dead 10 minutes among the living (24 silver or 400 TP, paid like a return). The minutes burn down a tick at a time, twice as fast while the borrower is in a form, and she tells him when they are up; a wish (Make borrowed time lasting) makes them last. Her lasting return and the death rulings are kept as they were.
- **Masters' homes are spared**: ki no longer breaks blocks in a master's home - a kingdom seat or a barbarian camp seat, out to 24 blocks - nor inside a kept tournament ring. Mob griefing (the gamerule) decides everywhere else, as before.
- **Themed tournaments**: when a tournament opens it can be themed instead of a plain kingdom tournament - the tyrant's games (a tyrant takes a champion's place in the bracket, and the prize is double) or the fortune teller's five fighters (only five challengers may enter). They run beside the kingdom tournaments, last-one-standing and the tournament of the dead.
- Built; not played in game.
