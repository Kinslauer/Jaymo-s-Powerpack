# Legends Core animation library, landings by height, shared movement set and katana stance set

- **Legends Core animation library (LGPL-3.0 port):** Ported Legends Mod Core's full set of 55 Bedrock-format player animations (`player.json`) and 15 katana item animations (`katana.json`) under `assets/jaymospowers/player_animations/legends_core/`, alongside `CREDITS`, `LGPL-3.0.txt` and `GPL-3.0.txt` crediting Tihyo and the Legends Mod Team (`Legends-1.20.1-ss1.0.25.jar`, LGPL-3.0).
- **Landings by height:** Every power user now plays one of four height-tiered landing poses (`animation.player.landingposev1` through `v4`) based on fall distance, from a light crouch up to a full one-knee superhero landing (`v4`) at 14+ blocks that cracks the ground and kicks up landing dust (`CoreMotion.landed`, `ImpactShakeMixin`, `CoreMotionPal`).
- **Shared movement set for every power user:** Every power user can now perform the shared movement set — backflip (`animation.player.backflip`), front flip (`animation.player.frontflip`), double jump (`animation.player.double_jump`; powers that already have their own second jump keep that one instead), cat leap over a waist-high block (`animation.player.catleap`), sprint slide (`animation.player.sprintslide`), long roll (`animation.player.longroll`), and three directional dodges (`animation.player.evade_one`, `evade_two`, `evade_three`, plus `roll`) — driven through Player Animation Library (`CoreMotion`, `CoreMotionClient`, `CoreMotionPal`, `Freerun`, `FreerunClient`, `AirFlip`, `Acrobatics`, `PowerJump`). Procedural poses stand down cleanly while a PAL clip from this set is playing; the slide clip plays once per slide, and a long slide goes back to our own slide pose after it.
- **Katana stance set for Weapon Forms:** Holding a katana when Weapon Forms is known unlocks the Katana Stance key (`key.jaymospowers.kata`, `KatanaForms`, `KatanaFormsClient`, `player_animations/katana_forms.json`, whose blade tracks are retimed from Core's `katana.json`; the key is unbound by default). Letting go of a charge while blocking or eating cancels it. Tapping swaps into or out of draw stance (`stance_swap`), holding charges a sheathed draw (`draw_charge`) that arms a fast `draw_slash`, normal ground attacks chain through a four-hit combo (`slash_1`, `slash_2`, `slash_3_launcher`, `slash_4_finisher`), sprinting attacks lunging forward (`dash_slash`), and airborne attacks chain two air slashes (`air_slash_1`, `air_slash_2`) or a downward spike (`air_spike` while sneaking).
- **Not tested in game:** Built and verified with `./gradlew compileClientJava` and `python tools/pr_checks.py`; visual playback of the PAL keyframes and katana combo timing were not tested in a live client session.
