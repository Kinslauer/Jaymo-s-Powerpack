# Legends patch 6 fixes - air flips, air step, landings, katana forms
- **Andy's ruling kept**: power users get Slide, the Backflip, vaults and air flips free, without the skill tree. The skill-tree locks stay as merged (nothing changed there).
- **Air flips**: a Sneak tap in the air no longer flips when it is part of a power combo. If any power key (R, G, V, B, Z, C) is held when you press Sneak, or is pressed within the tap window after it, the flip stays out of it: Talon's takedown (Sneak + R), the tail sweep (Sneak + Z), Gravity's Sneak + R rocks and the other Sneak air moves are untouched. Feathers are spent only when a flip actually happens.
- **Air step (double jump / cat leap)**: there is no free key for a default bind (every letter but L and T is a power's, and those are vanilla's), so for powers with no second jump of their own a fresh Jump press in the air now steps (after a few ticks off the ground, 4 Feathers). The "Air Step" key is still there to bind if you want it. Powers that already have a double jump (PowerJump) keep theirs. The step now takes its lift off the fall distance instead of wiping the whole fall.
- **Landings**: the landing clip is dropped when the hero landing or the safety tap plays, and when a roll takes the landing. The 14+ block crack and dust ring now waits a tick to see what the landing was: no dust a second time after the hero landing, no cracks on top of Gravity's own leap cracks, none when a roll took the fall or the fall killed you. A roll made late (after the hero landing already threw its dust) still can't take that dust back.
- **Short landings**: the held-weapon pose and body weight stay on during the quick landing clips of small drops.
- **Katana forms**: the combo now steps on the swing (a miss too, so misses chain and the clip leads the hit); a hit adds that cut's launch, spike or push. The charged draw (hold, let go, 4 Feathers) now makes the next swing a draw slash with 1.5 blocks more reach and 75% more damage on the hit.
