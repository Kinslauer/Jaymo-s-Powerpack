# Vanilla+ mods join the pack
- From Andy's Vanilla+ list, shipped unchanged: **Break Free** (keep breaking a block when you switch items), **Shared Advancements** (advancements count for your team or everyone), **Horse Expert** (a horse's real speed, jump and health), **Incendium** (Nether biomes, structures, weapons and mobs), **VillagersPlus** with its library VillagersTradingPlus (new villager jobs, trades and workstations), **MasterCutter** (500+ stonecutter recipes, woodcutting and un-crafting), **Vanilla Fireflies**, **qraftyfied: Structures** and **Vanilla Constructs** (100+ small vanilla-style features).
- Break Free, VillagersPlus, MasterCutter, Vanilla Fireflies and Vanilla Constructs have no CurseForge file, so they're in `packModsNoCf` (server, profile and dev game, not the CurseForge import zip).
- `checkPackMods` passes (488 mods, loader 0.19.5). Not played: the overlaps with our Nether biomes, MCA villagers, the Saw recipes and Effectual's fireflies haven't been looked at in game.
