# Light bends round black holes, beams scar the ground, Pyre's Fire Dive

- **Light bending.** Gravity's Singularity and Nadir's Event Horizon now warp the picture round them: the world behind
  stretches into an arc about a dark middle with a thin bright ring. It is a screen effect on each player's own client,
  shown only while the hole is in view with nothing solid between, and never in low-end mode. If a machine cannot draw
  it, it switches itself off for the session and the black hole looks as it did before.
- **Beam scars.** A ki wave skimming soft ground, or ending on it, burns a shallow groove along its path, and Pyre's
  Sunray scars the ground where it rests. Only earth, sand, gravel, snow, clay, mud and the like; the earth under the
  groove is baked to coarse dirt. Nothing is dropped. It obeys the mob griefing rule and needs the right to build
  there, and the blocks go a few each tick so a long sweep never lands all at once.
- **Fire Dive** takes the place of Pyre's Magma Trail (B). From the ground you rise first, then dive the way you look
  as a bird of fire, steering a little and burning what you pass through; where you come down everything within 5
  blocks takes 10, is thrown back and burns. From the Flame Jet the dive starts at once and ends the jet. 15 seconds.
