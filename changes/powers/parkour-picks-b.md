# Dismemberment picks: blind headless undead, leg armour, crawling legless mobs, armour against severing
- **Headless undead go blind** (`pps-blindhead`): a beheaded zombie or skeleton no longer tracks you by sight. It turns to the last noise it hears (footsteps, hurt cries, shots, digging, doors, blasts: 8 blocks for quiet ones, 16 for loud, 32 for blasts; remembered 5 seconds), shambles to where it came from and swings at it, and swings at anything that bumps into it (other headless undead excepted). A beheaded skeleton with a bow stays put and looses arrows at the spot the noise came from, scattered as before, never at where you are now. New `BlindHead`, `DismemberGameEventMixin`; `Dismember` hands the AI step and the wild arrows to it.
- **Leg armour falls with the leg** (`pps-legarmour`): losing a leg drops the boots (one item for both feet); losing the second drops the leggings too, the way a helmet drops with the head. Players and mobs alike. `Dismember#lose`.
- **Legless mobs crawl** (`pps-crawl`): a humanoid mob in the dismemberable tag that is still up with both legs gone (no prosthetics) is drawn hauling itself along on its arms, face down, the way a downed zombie crawls. Its speed is already cut by the lost legs. Drawing only (as the downed crawl is). `DownedPose#legless`.
- **Armour guards against severing** (`pps-armourcut`): each armour point on the slots over a part cuts the chance that part comes off by 3% (at most 60%): helmet for the head, chestplate for arms (and the waist cut), leggings and boots for legs. `Dismember#ARMOUR_GUARD`, `#armourGuard`.
- Built; not played.
