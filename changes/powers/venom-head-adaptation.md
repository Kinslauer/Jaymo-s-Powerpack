# the big suit's new head
- **The big suit's head** is new: a heavy brow over a long muzzle, a white eye slanted down each side, a hinged jaw set with rows of fangs that stay out (they no longer slide into the gums now and then), and a green thread of spit off the chin. The eyes and the glints on the fangs glow in the dark. It grows in last as the goo builds the big suit and sinks back first when it comes apart, and the jaw hangs, breathes, snarls and opens for the tongue the way the old one did; the tongue now comes out of the new mouth. The white and red strains have their own colours of it. The big suit's body, the plain suit and Consume's maw are unchanged.
- The head is Andy's own art from his Symbiote mod (his ownership confirmed 2026-11-24), recovered from that mod's jar by `tools/recover_symbiote_head.py` (cubes, UVs, the mouth and eye anchors, and only the head's texture rectangles; no body, no other abilities). `tools/gen_web_bighead.py` makes `WebBigHeadMesh` and the four `web_symbiote_bighead*.png` textures from that data; `tools/preview_bighead.py` draws the old, new and original heads side by side. The head's texture is six times as sharp as the rest of the suit's.
- New `WebBigHead` (the model, its placement, the glow); `WebHulk` hides the old head's masses and shares its growth maths with the new head (`flowed`, `flow`, `thick`, unchanged for the body), and re-anchors the tongue; `WebSymbioteLayer` draws the head over the big body. The original's tongue segments aren't imported: the big body's own tongue stays.
- Lore placement added to Lore.md as a `(new)` paragraph ("Its own face") for Andy to approve.
- Built by CI only; not played. Not checked in game: how the head sits on the neck while crawling, climbing and in executions (it follows the same head pose as the maw), the glow, and the scale and offset (`WebBigHead.SCALE`, `OFFSET_Y`, `OFFSET_Z`), which were set from a drawn preview.
