# Blunderbuss and musketoon drawn from Frontier Armaments
- **Blunderbuss**: WeaponMod's blunderbuss is now drawn in the hand as the Flintlock long gun from cleannrooster's Frontier Armaments (MIT): a long grey barrel with a flared mouth on a curved walnut stock. Its cock snaps forward on firing and comes back at the end of a reload, our muzzle flash fires from the mouth, and it has a steel ramrod with a brass tip under the barrel: on reload the gun tilts up, the ramrod is drawn out, rammed and put back. The faces its maker left unpainted are painted like their neighbours. The inventory picture is still WeaponMod's sprite.
- **Musketoon**: Kingdoms' new musketoon is drawn as the same mod's Firecannon (a black barrel bound in brass with a bell mouth, its red gem painted steel, a ramrod added), animated the same way with a long reload. Its shot pours out more fire and smoke than any other gun (`GunBlast.Gun.MUSKETOON`).
- Source models and textures in `tools/bbmodels/frontier-armaments/` (with the MIT licence, also shipped as `licenses/LICENSE_frontier_armaments`); `tools/gen_guns_geckolib.py` `blunderbuss()` and `musketoon()` convert them. Our own cube-built blunderbuss is gone (git history keeps it).
- Built; not played. The holds are laid by eye from renders, not checked in game.
