# Blunderbuss drawn as Frontier Armaments' Firecannon
- **Blunderbuss**: WeaponMod's blunderbuss is now drawn in the hand as the Firecannon from cleannrooster's Frontier Armaments (MIT): a black barrel bound in brass bands with a brass bell mouth, a short walnut stock. It lies where the old one lay, so the long-gun hold is unchanged; its hammer still snaps forward on firing and cocks back at the end of a reload, and our muzzle flash fires from the bell. Its red breech gem is painted over in steel, and it gained a steel ramrod with a brass tip under the barrel: on reload the gun tilts up, the ramrod is drawn out, rammed down the bell and put back, then the hammer cocks. The inventory picture is still WeaponMod's sprite.
- Source model and texture in `tools/bbmodels/frontier-armaments/` (with its MIT licence, also shipped as `licenses/LICENSE_frontier_armaments`); `tools/gen_guns_geckolib.py` `blunderbuss()` converts it. Our own cube-built blunderbuss is gone (git history keeps it).
- Built; not played. The hold is the old model's, not checked in game.
