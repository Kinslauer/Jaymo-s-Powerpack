# Frontier Armaments and Heraldry picks
- **Blunderbuss**: WeaponMod's blunderbuss is now drawn in the hand as the Flintlock long gun from cleannrooster's Frontier Armaments (MIT): a long grey barrel with a flared mouth on a curved walnut stock. Its cock snaps forward on firing and comes back at the end of a reload, our muzzle flash fires from the mouth, and it has a steel ramrod with a brass tip under the barrel: on reload the gun tilts up, the ramrod is drawn out, rammed and put back. The faces its maker left unpainted are painted like their neighbours. The inventory picture is still WeaponMod's sprite.
- **Musketoon**: Kingdoms' new musketoon is drawn as the same mod's Firecannon (a black barrel bound in brass with a bell mouth, its red gem painted steel, a ramrod added), animated the same way with a long reload. Its shot pours out more fire and smoke than any other gun (`GunBlast.Gun.MUSKETOON`).
- Source models and textures in `tools/bbmodels/frontier-armaments/` (with the MIT licence, also shipped as `licenses/LICENSE_frontier_armaments`); `tools/gen_guns_geckolib.py` `blunderbuss()` and `musketoon()` convert them. Our own cube-built blunderbuss is gone (git history keeps it).
- Built; not played. The holds are laid by eye from renders, not checked in game.
- **Set schematics**: with Jaymo's Kingdoms in, the Smithing Anvil forges four of its Frontier armour sets: Templar plate, the Ranger's harness and the settler's gear from iron, and the black Templar, black Ranger and ashen Hedge sets from steel. Each needs its set's schematic (a blueprint of the whole set, sold by the armourers and tailors of the people who wear it) on the smith; without it the pieces are simply not on offer. The schematic grades and levels like any blueprint.
- **Fighting stamina**: armour now tires you. Sprinting, jumping, swinging and taking blows wear down your feathers, faster the heavier your armour: plate most, mail less, padding barely, and nothing at all with no armour on. When the row runs dry and goes pale, the next blow that lands knocks your weapon out of your hand; it lands a few blocks away and you can pick it up again. Adrenaline halves the wear and keeps your grip. There is no new bar: it is the feathers row.
- **Weapon sweet spot**: every weapon hits hardest at its own distance: daggers and claws close in, swords and axes in the middle of their reach, spears and polearms out near the end of theirs. Too close or too far and the blow lands dull, for as little as 0.7 of its damage; dead on, it rings and does a tenth more.
- **Cut, thrust and blunt**: weapons cut, thrust or crush, and armour answers each differently: mail turns cuts, thrusts find its gaps, plate shrugs off cuts but a mace crushes it, and padding soaks up blunt blows. With a lock-on, the piece over the region you aim at is the one that counts. Weapons and armour each say what they are in one grey tooltip line.
- Built; not played.
