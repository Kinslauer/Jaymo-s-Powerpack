# web slinger: loops, barrel rolls, a zip marker, springs, riding weblines, wind lanes, ledge leaps, more swing poses
Andy's Legends picks, patch 1 (web swinging and getting about), all our own code, poses and numbers; the Legends mods are only the idea.
- **Loop over the anchor** (`ws_loop`): on a fast upswing, hold forward and look up and the swing goes right over the top of the anchor in a full loop, then comes down the far side still swinging. The rope no longer stalls at the top: while a loop is on, the speed along the arc never falls under what keeps the rope taut at that height, and the speed cap is the full cap. The whole body goes round over the hand (new pose `LOOP_FLIP`). `WebLoop` (called from `WebSwing`), `WebPose.LOOP_FLIP`, `WebBodyPose`.
- **Dive rolls** (`ws_roll`): after a release, on the way down, hold use with A or D and you lay out along the dive and barrel-roll that way, the dive's heading bending toward the side a little each tick. The roll is sent again each turn to keep it going and is drawn for everyone (pose `DIVE_ROLL`, a continuing roll is sent with a side of 2 so it does not ease in again). `WebDiveRoll`, `WebTrickClient.sendMove`, `WebTricks`, `WebBodyPose`. Drawn in `WebBodyPose` (the pose everyone sees), not `WebArms`, whose body only exists while a web is out.
- **Zip landing marker** (`ws_marker`): while Zip (Z) is unlocked and off cooldown, the MagicFx ring sits on the block face Zip would carry you to, fading in over 8 ticks of aiming and red where the block is further than Zip reaches. It follows Zip's own ray (`WebPower.ZIP_RANGE`, symbiote and shooter reach scales). A button on the Arrange the HUD screen turns it off (kept as `web.zip_marker.off` in `config/jaymospowers-client.properties`). `WebZipMarker`, `HudArrange`, lang part `web_swing.json`.
- **Spring off on arrival** (`ws_spring`): hold Jump through a Zip (it rides all the way in instead of letting go early) or press Jump within 8 ticks of arriving, and you kick off the spot with the zip's own speed. At a wall the kick goes away from it along your look, and looking steeply up or down (30 degrees) shoots you up or down the wall's face. It wins over the ledge throw when Jump is held. `WebSpring`, `WebSwing.finishPull`.
- **Ride a webline** (`ws_ride`): sneak and use on a webline in reach to stand on it (feet at or above its height) or hang under it by the hands (below it); the line carries you to it. Forward and back slide you along it the way you look; at a crossing of two lines, A or D turns you onto the other. Sneak again (after letting go of the one that started it) drops you; Jump hops off. Everyone sees the pose (standing: the balanced crouch; hanging: arms up, legs loose, new pose `LINE_HANG`). New server attachment `WebLineRide.RIDE` and packet `WebLineRide.Ride`, counted as floating so a hang is not kicked. `WebLineRide`, `WebLineRideClient`, `WebBridges.SPANS` read on the client.
- **Springboard line** (`ws_springline`): standing on a webline you ride, hold sneak (a tap drops you) and the line bends under you, the jump bar filling as the slingshot's does (orange peak and all); a twang sounds at full bend, and letting go flings you the way you look, faster the nearer the peak you let go. The bent line is drawn dipping under you (your own view only). `WebLineRideClient`, `WebSwing.flung`, `WebLineRenderer`.
- **Wind lanes for the web wings** (`ws_wind`): gliding on the web wings (or the symbiote's), the ground round you is read from its height map every 15 ticks and lanes are found where open air at your height runs between two walls that both reach up to you, or over the lip of a cliff. Faint cloud streaks run along each lane; flying into one pushes the glide along it, past the wings' top speed for a moment. Particles only, nothing is placed. `WebWindLanes`, `WebSwing.tickWings`, `WebSymbioteWingsClient`.
- **Ledge leap from a wall run** (`ws_edgehint`): on a wall run, or climbing a wall you cling to, an open ledge up the wall (1.4 to 6 blocks over your feet) glints with web specks, and holding use leaps for it with the ledge launch's own throw, pulling you over. `WebEdgeHint`, `WebWallRunClient`, `WebParkourClient`, `WebLedge`.
- **More swing poses** (`ws_poses`): the swing's free arm, legs and head now go through reach (just after a web catches), pull (falling down the arc), swing through (the fast bottom) and let go (rising and slowing), in one of four looks per web, never the one the last web had. Nine ways off a released web instead of three (front flip, back flip, corkscrew, cartwheel, plus the new swan dive, pike flip, side roll, twist flip and handspring), never one of the last two used. New poses `SWAN_DIVE`, `PIKE_FLIP`, `SIDE_ROLL`, `TWIST_FLIP`, `HANDSPRING`. `WebSwingPoses`, `WebArms`, `WebTrickClient`, `WebBodyPose`, `WebPose`, `WebStunts`.
- No new keys: sneak, use, jump, forward and back, A and D, and Z as they already are.
- Built; not played.
