# Hunter's Road 7b: herbs, the poison garden, grafting
- **Herbs**: feverfew and valerian (meadow crop flowers), juniper (taiga berry bush), cave ivy (cave walls and roofs), bracken (tall fern), cardinal flower (red swamp flower), earthball and honey fungus (grow huge), mandrake root (sniffer digging), ergot (wheat in rain). Each has essences, a worldgen placement and a reason it grows there.
- **Poison garden**: jungle mint, nightshade, water hemlock, monkshood (blue frost flower) and bloodroot grow only on farmland worked with poison paste. Seeds come from grinding the plant in a mortar.
- **Grafting**: five new strains (dusk hemlock, sunbane, warwort, rime mint, eddy bell). Grafting without knowing the leans kills the crop.
- Classes: `alchemy/herbs/Garden`, `HerbCropBlock`, `PoisonedFarmlandBlock`, `JuniperBushBlock`, `Grafting`.
