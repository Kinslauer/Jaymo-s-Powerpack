# Elemental Corruption: the gods of the elements take back a power used to murder

- (Jung) A corrupted Wind, Smoke, Gravity or Cryokinesis user who kills a villager (never a barbarian or other camp
  folk) is warned twice, each time in one of five wordings ("You are angering things you cannot defend yourself
  from.", then "We will not stand for injustice in our name.", among others). The third killing: "You have enraged the Gods of your power and they now reclaim it".
- The reckoning, once: Wind brings a thunderstorm and eight bolts round the killer; Smoke a thin fog; Gravity lifts
  everything round them about ten blocks and blasts the ground in a ring ten blocks out; Cryo turns every block within
  twelve to ice, with ice spikes seven tall.
- Then five minutes of wrath: double damage, but a quarter of every blow comes back on you, the element burns you for
  half a heart every five seconds and pours off you, and every creature near you but a boss or an orc runs. Wind's
  lightning strikes nearby creatures and other players for a heart every seven seconds; Smoke chokes them; Gravity's
  loose ground cracks, drops, rises and slams into small craters and drags them in; Cryo freezes passive creatures,
  villagers and other players solid.
- When it ends, everything disperses and the power is gone for three in-game days. Purify it meanwhile (a Purifying
  Draught, or the power's own cure) and it comes back at once, clean; otherwise it comes back still corrupted. `/power wrath` tests it.
- Cryokinesis can now be corrupted: creatures killed while frozen fill its meter (people more), and a Purifying Draught
  drunk beside a lit campfire thaws it for good.
