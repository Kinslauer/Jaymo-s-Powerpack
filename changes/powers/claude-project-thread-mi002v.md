# blood off - an Off choice on the Blood button in Video Settings
Andy: "then we can make a setting to turn all blood off."
- The Blood button in Video Settings (Light / Normal / Heavy) gains **Off**. With it, this player sees no blood at all:
  no drops, sprays or flecks, no pools, stains, footprints, smears or wall marks, no blood on bodies, corpses, armour
  or held items, no weapon drips and no flies. Kitchen's chunks and insides of bodies blown apart or eaten are hidden
  too (it asks `BloodSettings.off()` by name).
- It is each player's own choice, like the other amounts: the server keeps its stains, so hunters still track trails and
  blood magic still works. Bodies themselves, sounds and gut piles are not hidden.
- Compiled; not played.
