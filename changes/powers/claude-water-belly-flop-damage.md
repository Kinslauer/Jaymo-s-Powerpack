# Belly flops into water hurt

- Falling into water from 4+ blocks now hurts unless you went in head first: a belly flop deals real fall damage scaled by fall height and impact speed (about 6 HP from 12 blocks, topping out at 8 HP); a cannonball - feet first, tucked - is the middle case (capped at 4 HP); a clean dive, looking straight down, takes nothing. Short drops stay harmless.
- The entry pose shows on everyone in view: cannonball tucked into a ball, belly flop flat on impact with a short wince, clean dive with arms forward over the head - each eases out under water.
- Built; not played in game.
