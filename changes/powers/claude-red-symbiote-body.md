# The red strain's slender body; taken humanoids fight in the strain's body; the red one hunts
- **The red strain's own body** (Andy: "keep our combined black suit from 2080, and place our mechanics tendrils blades and scripts onto the narrow taller slender body"): where the black and the white strains raise the big body, the red one now draws itself up into a narrow, taller frame (about 2.2 blocks): all sinew, a head of fangs on a long neck with a flicking tongue, loose strands off every limb, eyes glowing. It is the red form of Andy's own Symbiote mod (his, reuse authorised), recovered from the jar by `tools/recover_symbiote_slender.py` without the original's own tendrils and blades, and built by `tools/gen_web_slender.py` (`WebSlenderMesh`, `web_symbiote_slender*.png`). `WebSlender` keeps the player model's joints and moves them to its frame (`WebSlender.shift`, for every model drawn on that body), so our tendrils, the weapon arm, executions, poses and wings all ride it; it flows on chest first and head last, its forearms and shins bend at their own elbows and knees (`WebHulk.limbs`, now shared), and the wearer's own body is hidden inside it once it's mostly built. The black big body (#2080's, with its new head) is unchanged.
- **Its tendrils** (Andy: "a visible abundance of tendrils ... differ in size thickness and length ... on the back/rear of the model and shoulders, never out the front"): on the slender body our eight tendrils root on its back and shoulders, each with its own rest, curl, thickness and length (`WebSlender.TENDRIL_REST`), rising up and over in a crown, among fourteen thinner strands that only sway and ripple (`WebSlender.STRANDS`, `WebSlender.Strands`). They still grab, strike, stab, walk walls and wear the red strain's hooked blades.
- The weapon arm's hand, the wings' size and the goo globs follow the slender frame (`WebMorphLayer`, `WebSymbioteWingsLayer`, `WebGooGlobs`).
- Preview: `tools/preview_slender.py` (front, three-quarter, side, back; beside a player and the big body), in the vault's `Assets/red-symbiote/`.
- Built in CI only; not played. Not checked in game: the frame against every pose (crawl, climb, executions), the wearer hidden inside it, MCA's player model under it, the strands' sway.
