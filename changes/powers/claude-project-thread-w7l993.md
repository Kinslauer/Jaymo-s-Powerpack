# legless downed crawl, struggles that fit the body, ghosts unseen and drawn in third person
- **Legless and downed, you drag yourself**: a downed player with a leg or both legs gone now crawls by the arms at the legless crawl's pace (times the downed pace), instead of barely moving (the missing legs' walking slowness had stacked on the downed slowness).
- **Cut in two, you drag yourself a little**: a player halved at the waist by a killing blow now goes down instead of dying at once, and can drag the upper half by the arms for the 8 s bleed-out before dying of it (credited to the striker). Nothing gets them up meanwhile (rising, Second Wind, healing struggle, revives); the prompt says "Cut in two. Drag yourself while you can, or hold sneak to give up". Rubber-folk and mobs as before.
- **Struggles fit the body**: with nothing to stand on (a leg gone with no cane, crutch or fitted leg, both gone, or both broken off adrenaline), getting up is pulling yourself together where you lie: the prompt, bar and messages say so ("Hold sneak to pull yourself together", "Hauling yourself up on your arms"), never "to your feet", the rise is drawn only as the push up onto the arms (no knees, no standing), and you crawl on after it. The same for coming round by a revive, Second Wind, a healing item, a bed or a fire.
- **Passed out, the view stays put**: out cold (passed out or knocked out) the mouse no longer turns the view or the body lying there. Out of the body as a ghost it still looks about.
- **Mobs ignore ghosts**: a player out of their body or waiting in Limbo can't be seen by mobs: none targets them, keeps them as a target or looks at them (scavengers still find the body).
- **The ghost in third person**: the ghost drifts as a ghost (legs together trailing back, arms floating loose, leaning into the drift) instead of walking on air, for everyone; its owner now sees it in third person, drawn where their view is each frame, and Limbo no longer shows vanilla's still floating spectator head.
