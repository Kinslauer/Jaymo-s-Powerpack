# Hand of God: Celestial Hold and Celestial Turn

- **Hand of God** (`power/handofgod/`): a new power, the Hand of God — a patron god's favour, a fragment of
  divine authority in a mortal's hands. Not a random awakening (`fated()` is false); it comes through a patron
  god's calling. Icon: the Nether Star. Accent: gold.
- **Celestial Hold (Q):** the Overworld's day time is held at its current value for 5 s: the Sun and Moon stop
  moving, the light holds, the shadows stop. Everything else goes on. Global (the sky is one per level).
  Cooldown 60 s.
- **Celestial Turn (E):** the Overworld's day time jumps so the Sun stands where the player looks. The Moon
  follows on its own orbit (Kingdoms' `SunMoon` elongation). Cooldown 30 s.
- Built; not played.
