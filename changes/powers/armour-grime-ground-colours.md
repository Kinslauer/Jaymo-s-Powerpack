# armour grime - each material its own look, dirty clothes, ground colours, more dirt before washing
- **Dust takes the ground's colour** (Andy: "stretch it to different materials"): red off badlands sand, dark brown off a forest floor, grey off gravel, rusty soot off netherrack, near black off basalt and blackstone, pale off end stone. Mixed walks blend the colours by how much each added. Mud stays mud brown. Kept on the grime component as `tint` (old dirty armour keeps the plain tan).
- **More places dirty you**: dry bare earth (dirt, coarse dirt, podzol, rooted dirt, paths, farmland) throws a little dust anywhere, not only in dry country (grass, mycelium and moss don't); the Nether's rock and soul sand, nylium and end stone do too. Dragged bodies pick these up as well.
- **More grime shows before washing** (Andy: "increase the amount visible"): the masks cover about 90% of each sheet (was about 70%) with more variation in shade, cover grows faster at low dirt (a 20-dirt piece already shows splotches), a filthy piece is caked over 90% of its mask, and each splotch is stronger. Helmets and chestplates get more of each step's dirt (reach 1.0/0.7/0.4/0.2, was 1.0/0.6/0.25/0.08).
- **Each armour material wears grime its own way** (Andy: "they should all look different"): leather and cloth soak it in as stains (dust dulls them paler, mud darkens them), iron, chain and copper are caked over, gold sheds it from its polish and keeps it in its engraving, diamond packs it into its cracks, netherite takes a pale film of dust, turtle shell keeps it in its seams. Picked from the texture's name, so modded armour named leather/gold/diamond/etc. follows suit; anything else is treated as metal.
- **Bare clothes and skin get dirty** (Andy: "bare clothes should get dirty too"): players and villagers carry their own body grime (`ArmourGrime.BODY`, synced and saved), kicked up as armour's is (0.6 of a step's dirt, more lying down or dragged) and washed by the same water, rain, scrub and sponge. Drawn over the skin and its outer layer as soaked-in stains, legs first, the face least, under any armour, by `BloodCoatLayer` from new 64x64 masks `body_dust`/`body_mud`.
- **Dirt others can see**: `ArmourGrime.noticed(body)` (-1 none, 0 dusty, 1 muddy, 2 filthy, from the dirtiest armour piece or the body's own clothes) for Jaymo's Kingdoms' remarks and MCA hearts. The body's grime now counts toward how bad you smell, as armour's does.
- Classes: `ArmourGrime`, `ArmourGrimeTextures`, `BloodCoatLayer`, `Smell`; masks redrawn by `tools/grime_textures.py`. Previews in project files `art-overhaul/armour-grime/`.
- Built; not played.
