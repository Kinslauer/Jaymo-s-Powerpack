# Freerun ledge hang and mantle keyframed animations
- Players see smoother, Blockbench-keyframed animations when hanging from a ledge and climbing up over it, replacing hand-coded joint math with GeckoLib animation clips (player_freerun_ledge_hang, player_freerun_ledge_mantle). Also adds clip sources for cling, climb down and shimmy moves.
- Not tested in game.
