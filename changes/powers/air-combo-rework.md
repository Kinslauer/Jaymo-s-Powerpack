# Air combo: ragdolls, enemy step, Yank and tendril pull-ups, slam spares friends
- **Ragdolls** (Andy: "make them ragdoll, react"): a mob launched, juggled, slammed or stepped off goes limp in Jaymo's Kitchen's ragdoll and is thrown as its body (`KitchenCompat.throwLimp` -> Kitchen `MobRagdolls.thrown`): no mind while it flies, it turns over in the air, lands where it falls and gets up when it lies still. One that can't go limp (a boss, one just back up, in water) is stunned (`DarktideStatus.stun`) for `STUN_TICKS` 20 per blow instead of fighting on in the air. Players are thrown as before.
- **Enemy step** ("like a DMC enemy step"): anyone with a second jump of any kind (a power's, the Draught of Mettle's, the gravitite set's, a cloud in a bottle's, a Sorcerer's; `AirCombo.hasAirJump`) presses Jump in mid-air within `STEP_REACH` 1.5 blocks of a foe in a combo: they kick off it (`EnemyStepClient`, `STEP_LIFT` 0.6 up, `STEP_PUSH` 0.15 the way they steer), the combo's juggle count and time start again, and the foe is kicked down `STEP_DIP` 0.25. The second jump itself isn't spent. Once per blow: a foe can't be stepped off again until it's hit (or pulled up) again (`AirCombo.STEPPABLE`, synced). Works on anyone's combo, so a friend can step off your juggled foe.
- **Pull-ups** ("web and the symbiotes should be able to pull enemies up from the ground while midair"): Web's Yank in mid-air hauls the creature up to just in front of you (`HAUL_AHEAD` 1.6, `HAUL_SPEED` 1.3, 5-14 ticks) and it's in your combo; a symbiote's tendril grab in mid-air puts what it grabs in your combo, the combo waiting while it's held, and a blow knocks it out of the grip into the juggle or slam. On a foe already in your combo it extends it (juggles kept, time started again). Needs the Air Combo perk, as the launcher does.
- Anyone a symbiote rides (awake, not dormant) can now launch and juggle too (`AirCombo.can`).
- The slam's splash spares your pets, your team or Kingdoms party, and villagers, unless they're attacking you (`AirCombo.spared`).
