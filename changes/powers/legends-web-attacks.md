# web slinger: the web uptilt, web air launchers, zip strike, riding a launched foe, dive slam, pin, drop kick, shock lines, wrecking ball, web storm, five-hit fists, answering arrows, the layer cap, the hunting dive
Andy's Legends picks, patch 2 (web attacks and the web uptilt), all our own code, poses and numbers; the Legends mods are only the idea. Andy's words on the uptilt: "web users should get access to the uptilt attack as they get increased agility, their mid air uptilts should involve using the webs to swing them up or pendulum kick the enemy higher, like the insomniac games".
- **Launcher through Agility** (`wa_uptilt`): a web slinger with 20 Agility (`WebUptilt.AGILITY_POINTS`; what a longbow asks too, and inside the first world tier's cap of 40) launches with the sneaking blow like anyone with Might's Air Combo; that perk still opens it for everyone else. Not "the first level where Agility speeds up climbing" (that is a single point, so it would be open to everyone at once). Without Kingdoms Agility is nothing, so web slingers there keep the perk route. `WebUptilt`, `AirCombo`.
- **Web air launchers** (`wa_webtilt`): each juggle blow on a foe you launched, by a web slinger, uses the webs where there is something to stick to. A block overhead of you: a web from it swings you up past the foe (`SWING_UP_KICK`) or you swing through it and pendulum-kick the foe up and ahead (`PENDULUM_KICK`), taking turns. Nothing over you but a block over the foe: a web on the foe yanks it up with you (`WEB_YANK_UP`, one layer of web on it). Each throws the foe higher than the plain juggle (0.8 to 0.95 against 0.5), with a little extra kick damage, and both web lines show for a moment. Open sky: the plain juggle. `WebUptilt`, `AirCombo`, `WebStrikeLines`.
- **Zip strike** (`wa_strike`): Zip at a webbed foe (the Z key, no new key) flies you at it feet first (`ZIP_KICK`) and the kick lands on arrival: 7 damage and 1.5 more per web layer, thrown away and up, you rebound. A foe with fewer than full layers can be zipped from 24 blocks, one with all layers from a block's range (48). `WebZipStrike`, `WebPower.zip`.
- **Ride a launched foe** (`wa_surf`): zip onto a creature you launched a moment ago (it does not need to be webbed) and you stand on its back (`SURF`) for up to 3.5 seconds. Your look steers it (aim down to drive it down, up to hold it aloft), into a wall or the ground it crashes (5 damage plus more for the speed, the ground breaks as a slam's does) and you kick off clear; Sneak or Jump gets off early. Creatures only, not other players. `WebSurf`, `AirCombo.launchedBy`.
- **Two-line dive slam** (`wa_diveslam`): in the air, look 55 degrees or more down at ground ahead and press Zip with Sneak held: both webs go into it and haul you down (`DIVE_SLAM`) with both lines drawn all the way, into a slam that hurts and throws everything within 4.5 blocks, webs it and strews cobweb, and breaks the ground by how far you fell. From 2.5 blocks up; no fall damage on the way. `WebDiveSlam`.
- **Pin to the wall** (`wa_pin`): Sneak + Yank (B) on a creature the web can lift yanks it sideways into the nearest wall within 7 blocks and pins it there for four seconds (the impact web's pin: it cannot walk and takes half as much again); with no wall near, it is whipped down and stuck to the ground. `WebPin`, `WebStrength.pinAt`.
- **Rising drop kick** (`wa_rising`): a tap of Yank on a foe that is already webbed pulls it in, you flip upside down (`DROP_KICK`) and drop-kick it up, then webbing bursts round you and webs everything within 4 blocks. Holding Yank on a webbed foe still leads it on the line. `WebRisingKick`, `WebPower`.
- **Shock lines** (`wa_shock`): with both swing webs fixed on one creature, a punch sends five jolts over two seconds down both lines, each hurting and stunning it (the taser web's jolt, now shared as `WebStrength.jolt`). Break line of sight or get further than 28 blocks and both lines snap. Sent as a new `WebWhip.Hold` mode. Copper in the web shooters does not gate it (not built). `WebShock`, `WebWhip`, `WebSwing`.
- **Wrecking ball** (`wa_wreck`): with both swing webs in the ground close together, a punch gathers the loose debris (dropped items and falling blocks within 7 blocks) into a ball held on both lines and swung round you (`WRECK_SWING`): it knocks foes down and swats arrows and other missiles out of the air; letting go of the punch throws it, smashing where it lands. A corpse near adds its weight but is left lying. The ball is drawn as rubble and web, with the two lines to it. `WebWreck`, `WebWhip`, `WebSwing`.
- **Web storm** (`wa_cage`): Sneak + Jump + C (once every ability is unlocked) casts lines from both wrists to the blocks within 12, a dome of them going round (`WEB_STORM`), drawing the creatures that are a fight for you in toward you for a second, then bursts and webs them all. Needs four blocks to hold it from and a shooter's shot like a blossom. `WebStorm`, `WebBlossom.fight`.
- **Five-hit web fists** (`wa_combo`): Webbed Fists chain jab, kick, uppercut, twist and crush, each within three seconds of the last on the same foe, each with its own pose (`FIST_JAB` to `FIST_CRUSH`) and more damage and shove than the one before; the third still cocoons, a foe wrapped in web is staggered harder, and the crush frees it and throws it. `WebFistCombo`, `WebStrength`.
- **Dodge and answer** (`wa_sense`): Spider-sense dodging an arrow opens a two-second window; click within it and you turn (`SENSE_TURN`), zip at whoever shot it (up to 40 blocks, in sight) and hit them for a quarter more. `WebCounter`, `WebZipStrike.answer`, `WebAscension`.
- **Web layers cap** (`wa_layers`): web stacks to three layers here (thin, thick, dense), not five, so three is "fully stuck": a fully stuck foe takes a quarter more damage, and once it tears free of the last layer it cannot be webbed again for four seconds. `Webbed`, `WebStrength.modifyDamage`.
- **Hunting dive** (`wa_ult`): once ascended, Sneak + C with a foe locked on (middle mouse) leaps back, then dives at it five times from different sides, each pass with the two lines drawn to it, ending in a stomp that pins it to the ground; a film (`UltimateFilm.CUTS`) plays. Twenty second wait. `WebHunt`, `WebPower.onUltimate`.
- New whole-body poses 39 to 56 (`WebPose.ZIP_KICK` to `SURF`), drawn in `WebAttackPoses` beside `WebBodyPose`. No new keys, packets or attachments: Zip, Yank, the punch (new `WebWhip.Hold` modes), the ultimate key and Sneak/Jump as they already are. No new lang strings.
- Built; not played.
