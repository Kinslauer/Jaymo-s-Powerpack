# Big symbiote combat

The big suit gains a thunderclap, aimed leap, rolling rubble, pounding fury, heavy steps, wall bounds, hooks, rampage, tracking cracks, fury nova and ground upheaval. It can clap upward out of the air, throw boulders with its ground pounds and seize control when the host is badly hurt. Tap Sneak with the pulse to fight back. The seven attack keys can be rebound in Controls.
