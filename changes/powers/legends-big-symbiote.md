# Big symbiote combat

Built; not played.

The big suit gains the fifteen rows of Legends Patch 3:

- Thunderclap (`hk_clap`): a shockwave twice as far and hard ahead, a weaker ring all round, more fury for a bigger clap.
- Airborne clap (`ot_clap`): clapping in the air while looking down throws you upward.
- Aimed leap (`hk_leap`): hold Jump to charge, taking less damage the longer you hold; at full charge, aim at a foe up to 40 blocks away and leap onto it.
- Rubble wave (`hk_rubble`): tear up a slab and flip it forward as a rolling wave that eats arrows.
- Pounding fury (`hk_tectonic`): repeated ground pounds that knock foes down and drag them in, the last throwing them up.
- Seismic boulders (`ot_quake`): each pound throws up boulders that fall on foes.
- Ground-shaking steps (`hk_steps`): at full fury every footstep shakes screens and nudges foes, and the suit forming and falling away sends out a harmless shockwave.
- Brute wall climbing (`hk_wall`): climbs in bursts, hops sideways, lets go on Sneak, heaves over the roof edge and leaps where you aim when Jump is released. Facing the wall is needed only to start a climb; the wall you are on is kept while you turn to aim.
- Heavy hooks (`hk_punch`): plain punches swing left and right in turn, every fourth a lunging blow that knocks foes flat.
- Brute walk, run and jump (`hk_walk`): heavy idle, stomping walk, knuckle run, crouch before jumps, fall pose and heavy landing.
- Rampage (`hk_rampage`): a charge that shrugs off most damage, steers toward foes you aim at and swats each aside.
- Tracking cracks (`hk_crack`): cracks that bend toward a foe you aim at, then the ground erupts along them.
- Losing it (`hk_losing`): badly hurt, the suit bursts out and you fight back in time with a pulse.
- Fury nova (`hk_nova`): spend all fury in one blast after a two-second build-up; the suit is usually spent afterwards.
- Ground upheaval (`ot_flip`): a long strip of ground torn into flying slabs that throw foes up.

Deviations from the plan:

- The aimed leap charges by holding Jump, not by crouching.
- The moves use their own remappable keys instead of Sneak + R, so they can be rebound in Controls. Defaults: Thunderclap F6, Rubble Wave F10, Pounding Fury F12, Rampage F8, Tracking Cracks Pause, Fury Nova Num Lock, Ground Upheaval Scroll Lock.
- The losing-it pulse is a screen shake and a heartbeat; only the black strain bursts out of you. Presses off the beat cost progress, so timing beats mashing.
- Rubble wave is its own key.
- Foes too big to haul (as the tendril haul measures) and bosses take every blow but are not shoved or dragged.
- Thrown stones belong to the world they were thrown in and are cleared when you change world.
- The normal slam now breaks the ground under the same rules as the new moves: it needs build permission, mob griefing on, and every block in reach to be one you may interact with. A landing counts on the ground or within a block and a half above it.
