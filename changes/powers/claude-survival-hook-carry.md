# both arms for anything bigger than a small animal; carried blocks are seen
- **Carrying**: anyone or anything bigger than a small animal now takes both arms: a person over your shoulder or at your side gets your other hand on them too, first and third person. With both arms full (that, a Kitchen corpse in your arms, or a block in Carry On's arms) the web wings won't spread (and fold if you take something up mid-glide), and the aercloud glider and paraglider won't open ("Your arms are full").
- **Carried blocks**: a block picked up with Carry On is drawn in your arms again, first and third person. Carry On draws nothing while First-person Model is installed; it now draws anyway, except in your own first-person hands while First-person Model is drawing your body (which already shows it).
- `core/ArmsFull`, `CarryPose`, `FirstPersonArms`, `KitchenCompat.corpseInBothArms`, `WebSwing`/`WebPower` (wings), `AercloudGliderItem`, `ParagliderItem`, `client/mixin/carryon/CarryOnDrawMixin`.
- Built by CI; not played.
