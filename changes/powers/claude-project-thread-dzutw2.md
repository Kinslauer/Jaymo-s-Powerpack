# Alex's Caves joins the pack; its Magnetic Caves in the Aether's magnetic stone
- **Alex's Caves Continued 1.1.3** (Codx.io's 26.3 Fabric port, LGPL-3.0, shipped unchanged from Modrinth; CodxLib 1.6.1 is already in the pack) is in the modpack: six rare cave biomes under the Overworld. This is the world-gen track; the Rubber-folk, flood, demon, Toxic Caves and future-item tracks follow.
- **Magnetic Caves** (Andy: parity with the Aether's magnetic biomes, no red and blue): the caves' galena is our ferrosite and its scarlet, azure and neutral energized galena bands are ichorite (`mixin/alexscaves/MagneticCaveStoneMixin`; the stone rules wait for the Aether's blocks if Alex's builds them first, `compat/AlexsCavesCompat`). The ferrocave's pool walls are ferrosite (`FerrocaveStoneMixin`). The biome (`data/alexscaves/worldgen/biome/magnetic_caves.json`, read only when Alex's is loaded) drops the red and blue neodymium nodes and the galena ore and swarf, and grows ferrosite pillars, spikes, ferrosite sand and magnetic shrooms on its floors (new `jaymospowers:cave_*` placed features). Its buried ruins swap galena, energized galena and neodymium for ferrosite and ichorite as they are placed (`UndergroundRuinsStoneMixin`).
- With the nodes gone, Alex's scarlet and azure neodymium (its magnet material) no longer generates; a later track can bring it back in our colours.
- Built; not played. Only new chunks change.
