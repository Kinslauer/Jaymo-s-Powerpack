# Drafting Table screen; trapdoors take a second use
- **Drafting Table** (Andy: "it should have a GUI like the other tables"): using it now opens a screen in the Tinkering stations' look. Put a blueprint and a sheet of paper on it for a copy one level lower (the blueprint stays), or a forged weapon, head or armour piece and a sheet of paper to see its pattern; taking the pattern destroys the piece. No paper needed in creative. Taking the pattern replaces the old "use the table again" warning. Holding something no longer changes what a click on the table does, so it can't fall through to equipping the item.
- **Trapdoors** (Andy: "too easy to flip them and alot of the world gen uses them as detail"): a hand works a trapdoor on the second use in a row: double-click it, or hold use a moment. The first use only shows a tip card ("Use the trapdoor again (or hold use) to open or shut it"). Every trapdoor a hand can open (wood, copper, other mods' that extend the vanilla trapdoor); iron is left to redstone as before. Redstone, villagers, wind charges, locks and lockpicking aren't affected. Sneaking with something in hand still places it against a trapdoor.
- Classes: `DraftingTableMenu` (new), `DraftingTableBlock`, `Patterns` (the hold-and-click take-apart and its warning are gone, `refused` keeps the gates), `client.tinkering.smithing.DraftingTableScreen` (new), `buildblocks.TrapdoorUse` (new), `buildblocks.mixin.TrapdoorTypeAccessor` (new).
- Built on CI; not played.
