# Grassier Grass and Punchy! leave the pack (lag)
- Andy: "i think the new grass and punchy are lagging the game, can those be removed?" Grassier Grass (the swaying tall
  grass) and Punchy! with Punchy! Moves (first and third person animations, added with the Sensible MC picks) are out
  of the pack: the server and modpack/mods drop them on the next build, and Build Modpack moves them out of your
  CurseForge profile into removed-mods (by mod id: grassiergrass, punchy, punchy_moves, punchy_expressions).
- The Fluid & Foolish resource pack (only Punchy! Moves' moves) is no longer fetched or switched on, and is switched
  off at launch where it was on (client/PackResourcePacks RETIRED). Its file stays in resourcepacks/.
- Built; not played. Whether these two were the lag isn't measured: if it stays, a spark profile would show what is.
