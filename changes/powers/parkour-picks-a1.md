# Parkour picks: ladders, shallow swimming, breach leap, camera roll, move hints
- **Ladders, vines, scaffolding and ropes** (one mixin on vanilla climbing, so Kitchen's rope and Kingdoms' climbing rope get it too; ported in shape from Better Climbing, CC0): moving sideways inside one is at walking pace, not a crawl (sneaking in the air still holds you still); looking steeply down (45 degrees, full at 80) and not holding Sneak slides you down fast (up to 0.4 blocks a tick); standing on the ground inside a ladder or vine, Jump is a real jump instead of the start of the climb (`LadderMoves`, `LadderClimbMixin`).
- **Hand-over-hand ladder climb**: every player on a ladder, vine or rope (off the ground) uses the wall-climb animation, hands and feet taking turns keyed to the distance climbed, in place of the vanilla arm wave; on a ladder block the body turns to face its wall; dropping fast (the look-down drop) is the claw slide with both hands up. In first person the climbing hands show while both your hands are empty (`WebParkourPose`, `ClimbFirstPerson`, `LedgeFirstPersonMixin`). The freerun cling and Web's cling are unchanged.
- **Swim in shallow water**: sprinting while touching water (at least 0.4 blocks of it over the body) starts a swim at once, in a single block of water or the moment you enter it, where vanilla needed the eyes under water; the stroke pose and the fast swim come with it (`ShallowSwim`, `ShallowSwimMixin`, `ShallowSprintMixin`).
- **Swim burst**: a fresh press of Sprint while swimming surges you along your look (costs 2 feathers and 30 air, never below 40 air; 30 tick wait). It is not the fast swim, which is the sustained faster swim after a long sprint swim: they run together and the surge lifts the fast swim's speed cap while it lasts (`SwimBurst`, `RunMovesClient`).
- **Breach leap**: swimming flat out at the surface (head above water, not standing on the bottom), Jump throws you out of the water along your look (always at least a little up), in the water's clean-dive pose with the arms ahead; costs 3 feathers. Landing on the ground while still sprinting goes straight into a head-first slide; landing on the water shallow and fast enough skips off the surface (twice at most). (`Breach`, `RunMovesClient`, `RunMovesPose`, `DivePose.cleanDive`, `FreerunClient.slideOutOf`).
- **Camera follows the body**: the first-person view rolls 3 degrees away from the wall in a wall run (`CameraFollow.WALL_RUN_ROLL`, 0 turns it off; never a field-of-view change). The dip on a landing roll and the roll-turn with the dodge roll were already built (`DodgeRollPose.applyCamera`: the view turns over with every dodge roll, a landing roll being the dodge, dipping at its middle; `VIEW_DIP` and `VIEW_ROLL`, 0 for none), so they are not doubled.
- **Move hints**: a small prompt in the top-left rail names the key you have bound and what it does in the move you're in (Jump: wall kick, Sneak: let go on a wall; Jump: pull up on a ledge; bars, poles, slides, wall runs, sky dive, Sprint: surge and Jump: breach swimming, Sneak: hold on a ladder); it comes up after a move holds the same hints for 5 ticks and fades out when the move ends (`MoveHints`, lang `freerun_hints.json`, `docs/HUD-Layout.md`).
