# Prone-only dodge roll, slower and weighed by armour
- **Dodge roll** (Andy: "Rolling should be slower and look like snake from metal gear, it should be even slower the more weight your gear has. To an upper limit.", then "you can only initiate the roll from prone, and you should stay until you get up"): Alt now rolls you only while lying prone (not downed). It's a log roll to the side you hold (Left or Right, right with neither), turning over twice along the ground at a steady pace (0.9 s bare, about 3 blocks, no hop), and you are still prone at the end until you get up as usual. Standing or crouched, Alt no longer rolls, and the landing roll is gone with it. The flip in the air, the shield vault, boxing's evades and the safety tap are unchanged.
- **Gear weight**: armour points plus toughness (full netherite = 32, the cap) stretch the roll up to 1.6x as long and as slow (about 1.45 s); nothing past the cap slows it more. Knobs: `Dodge.ROLL_TICKS`, `ROLL_PACE`, `ROLL_TURNS`, `HEAVIEST`, `HEAVY_SLOW`.
- The roll's length is synced, so others see a heavy roll at its own pace; it's drawn with the crawl's own log-roll pose. The walking keys don't add to it, and a new roll can't start until the last one ends. The evade window and feather cost are unchanged.
- `Dodge`, `DodgeClient`, `DodgeRollPose`, `CrawlPose`, `StanceInputMixin`. Compile-checked; not played.
