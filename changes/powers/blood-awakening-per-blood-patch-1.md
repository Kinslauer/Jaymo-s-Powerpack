# Blood Awakening per-blood upgrades, Patch 1 (Frostborn, Stormborn & Dawnborn)
- Every upgrade adds on top of PR #2117's shared rule and applies only while Blood Awakening is learned (`core.TreeLocks.awakened`).
- **Frostborn** (`power/zfighter/FrostBeam`): while holding the frost beam, frost spreads outward in a growing radius around its tip (1 to 4 blocks over 3 s), chilling and freezing nearby foes and water; carries scoreboard tag `jaymospowers_frost_beaming` while active so Kingdoms eases Fire Frail.
- **Stormborn** (`power/heritage/HeritageMoves`): Thunderclap chains lightning from the strike point to up to 2 nearby mobs with spark trails.
- **Dawnborn** (`power/heritage/HeritageMoves`): Sunflare blinds mobs twice as long and deals direct radiant damage to undead on top of igniting them.
- Built; not played in game.
