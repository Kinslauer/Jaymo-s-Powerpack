# Three blows on the ground and you're finished
- **Downed players die on the third blow**: besides one blow of three hearts or more, the third weaker blow (a swing,
  an arrow, a bite, a blast) since you went down now kills you too, as the killing blow always has. Andy, cut down on
  the ground by a zombie captain: "either one blow of 3 hearts or more, or 3 weaker blows total, not this infinite cap."
  Fire, falls and bleeding don't count; knocked out cold from standing, blows land as before. A downed mob hit by a
  player still takes its ten wounds. `Downed#PLAYER_BLOWS`, `Downed#lastBlow`.
- Built on CI only; not played.
