# Arsenal: what the wing's weapons are matters (flying blades, patch 6)
- **Guns on the wing**: hand the wing a flintlock or musket (G) and when it is that blade's turn to strike it fires from where it hangs, 9 damage with its muzzle flash, then goes home (your own gun, back to your pack). A bow or crossbow on the wing looses an arrow each turn and stays.
- **Working hands**: steer the wing (hold Sneak + B) at a block and the first tool on it that suits the block digs it, at that tool's speed and with its drops. Your own tool wears as it would in your hand; a pulled one does not. Claims, spawn protection and adventure mode are respected.
- **Relic gifts carry over**: a relic flying as an Attendant Blade keeps what it does on a cut: the Dreaded Blade heals you, Voidreaver blinds, Tidebreaker hits harder on something wet, Sunfang sets alight, Rimeclaw chills, the Long Vigil withers.
- **The Void wing**: Endermen within 16 blocks of a corrupted holder's blades turn on the holder, one every 4 seconds.
- **Gifted villagers**: an Arsenal-gifted villager in a fight has two blades at their shoulders. One strikes their foe every 3 seconds (3 damage, within 12 blocks) and one meets a blow or shot for them every 5 seconds.
- Classes: `power/arsenal/ArsenalWingKit` (new), `ArsenalWing`, `RelicWeapons.flyingCut`, `RiftBlade`, `ArsenalGift`, `ArsenalWingRenderer`.
- Not done: the Sword in the Stone's gift on the wing. Compiled; not played.
