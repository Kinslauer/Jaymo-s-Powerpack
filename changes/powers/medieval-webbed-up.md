# Medieval patch 1: Webbed up
- **Webbed, in layers**: every web hold (Web's shots, the web bomb, webbed fists, cocoons, hanging targets, the Aether's webs, Kingdoms' spiders) now wraps a layer of web on you, up to three (thin, thick, dense), instead of slowness and a cobweb at your feet. While any layer holds you can't walk, only hop; milk doesn't clear it. The top layer lasts as long as the web that put it on, then each under it a few seconds more (`power/web/Webbed`, `WebbedLivingEntityMixin`, `WebbedMilkMixin`; Derelict's Cobwebbed, MIT, as the idea).
- **Getting free**: mash your walking keys to struggle (each press jolts your view; six tear a layer off), a blade's hit cuts a layer, a friend can cut you out by using a blade on you, and fire burns the lot. Emberborn burn any web that touches them: they can't be webbed, and cobwebs they walk into burn away (`WebbedWebBlockMixin`).
- **The look**: a webbed body (players, mobs, Kingdoms' GeckoLib creatures too) is drawn wrapped in our own web skin, sparse to dense by layer, with tufts of silk standing out of it (`client/power/web/WebbedLook`, `WebbedGeoLayer`). In first person, strands of web cling round the edges of your view, reaching further in with each layer and shivering when you struggle (`WebbedView`). Art drawn by `tools/gen_webbed.py`.
- **Spider lairs**: when a spider downs you (or you fall underground with spiders near) and you're left for dead, Kingdoms can have you wake in their lair, wrapped in every layer, instead of with the bite's poison (`Mortality.LAIRS`).
- Built; not played.
