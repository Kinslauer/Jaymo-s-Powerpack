# animals eating you alive finish you off
- Andy (2026-11-20, clip of a brown bear tearing at him for most of a minute while he lay downed): "why didnt the bear
  execute here? it had more than enough time". A bear, wolf or big cat that goes to eat a downed player alive
  (`LimbEaters`' tearing) drops its target to do it, and an animal that isn't a monster only executes someone it's
  fighting, so it never did. Going for a downed body or tearing at it now counts as fighting them for 20 s
  (`LimbEaters.toreAt`, read by `MobExecutions.fighting`): after the usual 5 s grace it stands over them and finishes them.
- A meat-eating animal's execution is the mauling (`Move.FEED`: two tearing bites, then the last), however big it is,
  with its own growl at the wind-up, not a ravager's crush and roar.
- Big executioners (a bear, a ravager) count as there once their bulk reaches the body, so they can't stop short
  of the 1.6-block reach and give up after 10 s.
