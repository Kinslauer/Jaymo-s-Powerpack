# punch and barrage effects
- Heavy punch impacts land with a shockwave ring, a dust wave along the ground and a short screen shake: on spirits' Spirit Punch, Gravity's Inversion (GravityGutPunch), the Z-Fighter rushes' finishers, and Chrono Punch's last hit.
- Chrono Punch's flurry paints blurred fists in front of you and throws sparks where its punches land; Kingdoms' Spirit Barrage clashes throw sparks where the two flurries meet.
- Powers: ChronoPunch, GravityGutPunch, ZFighterRush. Compiles; not played in game.
