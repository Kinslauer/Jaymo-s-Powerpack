# punch and barrage effects
- Heavy punch impacts land with a shockwave ring, a dust wave along the ground and a short screen shake: on Gravity's Inversion (GravityGutPunch), Z-Fighter Rush's finishing blows, and Chrono Punch's last hit.
- Chrono Punch's flurry paints blurred fists in front of you and throws sparks where its punches land; Time Flow's barrage does the same.
- Powers: ChronoFlow, ChronoPunch, GravityGutPunch, ZFighterRush.
