# GT New Horizons picks, patch 4: coke and steel, tin and bronze
- **Coke oven** (`tinkering/coke/CokeOvenBlock`, `CokeOvenEntity`): clay and sand pressed in the brick form (2 clay balls or clay dust, 2 sand) make 4 **unfired coke oven bricks**, fired in the kiln or on a campfire. 4 bricks make a block of **coke oven bricks**; 8 in a ring make the **coke oven**. Build a hollow 3x3x3 of coke oven bricks with the coke oven in the middle of one wall, facing out, and the middle empty. It needs no fuel: right-click logs, coal or coal blocks into it and it bakes them one at a time, a log into charcoal (45 s), coal into **coal coke** (90 s), a coal block into a **block of coal coke**. Right-click empty-handed to take what's baked (sneak to take back what's waiting). Each bake leaves creosote in its 8000 mB tank (250 mB a log, 500 a coal); a full tank stops it. A bucket or an empty clay bucket used on it draws 1000 mB. The gauge on its face shows the tank in four steps. It glows and smokes from its top while it bakes, and counts as a soot source for Jaymo's Kitchen.
- **Creosote**: a real fluid (it pours and flows slowly, never makes new sources), in a **creosote bucket** or a **clay creosote bucket** (an empty clay bucket also scoops it up). An iron bucket of it burns in a furnace (4800 ticks; the clay one doesn't, since the furnace would eat the clay bucket). 4 rags and a bucket of creosote make 4 **creosote rags**; 8 planks round a bucket of creosote make 8 **treated planks**, which Kingdoms' weathering never ages (`#jaymospowers:weather_proof`). The bucket comes back empty (the clay one a use more worn).
- **Torches**: coal coke and a stick make 5 torches, a creosote rag and a stick 3 (copper and soul torches likewise; `tools/gen_stone_age.py` `TORCH_YIELDS`). Coal coke burns twice as long as coal in a furnace, its block likewise.
- **Charcoal pit** (`tinkering/coke/CharcoalPit`, `SmoulderingLogBlock`, `SmoulderingLogEntity`): heap logs (up to 128, touching face to face), cover every face of the heap with earth (`#jaymospowers:charcoal_pit_cover`: dirt and the dirt tag, farmland, paths, clay, packed mud) but one, and light a log through that gap with flint and steel or a fire charge. It smoulders (smoke seeps out of the gap) and after 2 in-game days every log of the heap is a **charred log**, which breaks into 2 charcoal. Open the heap anywhere else while it smoulders and it flares up: the lit log is wood again and fire takes the openings. A heap that isn't buried just lights like any wood. The smouldering log is a heavy soot source for Kitchen (`#jaymoskitchen:heavy_soot`). Counted on the world's clock, so a far-off pit finishes when its land loads.
- **Brick blast furnace** (`tinkering/coke/BlastFurnaceBlock`, `BlastFurnaceEntity`): clay and flint dust pressed in the brick form make 4 **unfired firebricks**, fired in the kiln or on a campfire; 4 make **firebricks**, 8 in a ring the **brick blast furnace**. Build a 3 wide, 4 tall, 3 deep tower of firebricks, hollow from the second layer up and open at the top, with the brick blast furnace in the middle of one side on the second layer. Right-click iron ingots and coal coke into it: every 2 minutes an ingot and a coal coke become a **steel ingot** and an **ash**. Right-click to take the steel, then the ash (sneak to take back what's waiting). It roars, smokes from its top and is a heavy soot source for Kitchen.
- **Tin** (`tinkering/Tin`): **tin ore** and **deepslate tin ore** lie in great veins (`ground/BigVeinFeature`, 70 blocks, one chunk in 24, y 48 to 200) in mountain biomes only; stone pickaxe or better; they drop **raw tin** (fortune works). **Tin sand** lies in patches in river beds (in `#minecraft:sand`): a pan or sluice on it turns up 1 or 2 **tin nuggets** 30% of the time before anything else (the sluice remembers it was loaded with tin sand until it's washed empty). Tin ingot, nugget, raw tin and its block, the block of tin, and **tin dust** (an ingot in the mortar), all in their `c:` tags (`#c:dusts/tin` now holds tin dust, so bronze dust can be mixed). Raw tin and the ores smelt or blast into tin ingots, slowly like other ores (`#jaymospowers:slow_smelt`); the prospector's pick and vein rumours know tin.
- **Bronze**: **molten tin** and **molten bronze** in the smeltery; three of copper to one of tin alloy into bronze. Tin and its ores melt like other metals (ores doubled); bronze dust melts into bronze. **Bronze ingot**, nugget and block (cast in the basin, or in the grid). Bronze is a **Tinkering material** between flint and iron (durability 230, speed 5.5, the Dwarven trait), cast into every metal part, bow limb and grip, and armour plating and maille (plate armour between copper and iron). A **bell** is made from 5 bronze ingots and a stick, and melts back into bronze (it melted into gold before).
- **Bronze kettles**: with Jaymo's Kitchen loaded, its kettles (the cooking pot: a bowl and 3 bronze ingots; the saucepan: a stick and 2 bronze ingots) can be made of bronze as well as iron (plain crafting, on JEI's crafting page).
- **The charcoal pit gives charred logs, not charcoal blocks** (the plan said charcoal blocks): each charred log breaks into 2 charcoal, so the heap keeps its shape and what it gives shows log by log.
- **Smiths trade tin**: every smith (vanilla's, and Kingdoms' smiths, who have the same professions) buys 6 tin ingots for an emerald at level 2 and sells 3 for one at level 3 (`#minecraft:common_smith`).
- **JEI**: a **Coke Oven** page (each bake, its time and its creosote; drawing creosote into buckets), a **Charcoal Pit** page (logs, cover and a light into charred logs, and charred logs into charcoal), a **Brick Blast Furnace** page (iron and coal coke into steel, its time and ash) and a **Tin Ore** page (what the ores and tin sand give); info pages that list the coke oven's, the blast furnace's and the charcoal pit's layers (on the controller, its bricks and the charred log), treated planks and creosote. Tin sand's panning is on the Sifting page; the alloy, melting, casting and bronze parts on Tinkering's pages; the bricks' firing on the kiln and campfire pages; the grid recipes on the crafting page.
