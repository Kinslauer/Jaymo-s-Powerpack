# Why you're hurt, every time: a line under the crosshair and a mark on the side it came from
- **Every hurt says why** (Andy's clip 2026-11-27 13:51, "what is hitting me here? the player needs to always know why they are taking damage so they can prevent it"). A short red line under the crosshair, in your character's voice, for every hurt you take, downed too (it used to stay quiet while you were down, and only spoke for hurts nobody dealt): "Maiko hit me!", "Skeleton got me from range!", "The sun! My skin's rotting, I need shade or a hat", "Too hot, I'm scalding myself". The same cause again keeps its line up; up to 3 causes stack, newest on top; each fades 3.5 s after its last hurt. It sits in the zone under the crosshair after the hold bars, so it doesn't clash with them (Andy picked under the crosshair on the decision card).
- **Where it came from**: a hurt from somewhere (a blow, an arrow, a blast) puts a red crescent round the crosshair on that side (top: in front, bottom: behind), outside the incoming-blow dots, following the one who did it while they're about (2 s).
- **Nothing unnamed**: a hurt dealt as vanilla `generic` or `magic` (about 110 of ours did), or a vanilla type one of our features borrows (water burning a Pyre or Shade was "Need air!"), is now named by the feature that dealt it (`core/wounds/HurtCause.from`, the first of our classes on the stack, `hurt_cause.jaymospowers.from.<Class>`; 111 lines). Lines added too for the vanilla types that had none (fall, campfire, cramming, falling blocks...), our own damage types without one, and attackers (`hit_by`, `shot_by`, `by.<type>`).
- The server log now names every hurt a player takes, once per cause per 10 s, attacker hits too: "X hurt by generic from RunningHot (1.0 health), nobody did it", "X hurt by mob (3.0 health), done by Maiko (entity.minecraft.villager)".
- Server: `core/wounds/HurtCause` (`Hurt` payload); client: `client/wounds/HurtCauseHud`. A client without the mod still gets the old action-bar line.
- Built; not played.
