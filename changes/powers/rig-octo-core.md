# Rig Octo Core - four mechanical arms, tendril moves for Tinkerers and web slingers
- **Rig Octo Core** (Andy: "adapt the octopus arms to another rig chestpiece, available through the tinkerer ... and to web tinkerers who find a rig and use the forge. they can fashion tendril like abilities through the 4 mechanical limbs rather than biological ones through the symbiote"): a Rig Core with four jointed gunmetal arms folded down its spine. Only a Tinkerer (Kingdoms' calling) or a web slinger sees it at the Rig Forge (one more pick after the Rig leg: 15 salvage points, 8 iron, 4 pistons in the standard frame); anyone else's Forge screen skips it and the server refuses it. It is a normal Core in every other way (charge, Mind, the whole set's actives) (`rig/RigOcto`, `RigForgeEntity.OCTO`, `RigForgeBlock`, `RigNet.ForgeOpen` carries whether you may pick it).
- **Its moves**, all in the Rig menu (Sneak + J) and only shown while it is worn; they need only this Core, not the whole set, and cost its charge: **Arms Out** (unfold or fold the arms); **Arm Grab** (snatch a creature, player, item or corpse in front and hold it up; J again hurls it and it hurts what it hits; Sneak + grab at a wall tears a chunk loose instead); **Skewer and Hoist** (Mind 2: stab, lift overhead, slam, with a splash); **Pinwheel** (Mind 3: the arms spin, shots are batted back at their shooters, everything in reach is struck); **Arm Vault** (Mind 2: flung forward and up; again in the air dives arms first); **Arm Anchor** (Mind 2: an arm telescopes out, hauling you to a wall or a creature to you); **Arm Watch** (Mind 3: the arms strike the nearest monster and bat away shots on their own, a little charge each, standing down when the Core runs low).
- **With the arms out**: the lower arms grip walls (Forward or Jump climbs, Sneak holds still, no fall damage), and every full-strength blow you land is followed by one arm in a four-step chain: lance, crescent sweep, clamp (held still), launch. The moves are the Black Panther mod's waldo harness (CC0, by Riley) rebuilt as our own code; the Marvel names and gold look are left out.
- **Telescoping arms** (Andy: "they can be that size at rest and should telescope further out, so they can do wall climbing and walking like the tendrils, same with the grab throw and a strong jab cross and blast combo"): the arms keep their size folded, but each upper and fore arm now has a rod that slides out, so a claw reaches to whatever it is aimed at (up to 16 blocks for the anchor). The claws are posed by reaching for a real point, not a fixed swing.
- **Arm Walk** (new move): the four arms take your weight and stride like the symbiote tendrils: they plant on the ground or a wall, step in diagonal pairs with a clank and a crack in the stone, hoist you about a block off the ground, carry you up walls and along ceilings. No fall damage while carried; it drains 1 charge a second and sets you down when the Core runs dry.
- **Arm Grab** now telescopes out to 14 blocks (was 9): both upper claws reach out, clamp the target's sides and reel it in; the throw stays on J.
- **Jab, Cross, Blast** (new move, Mind 2, 50 charge): one arm jabs out up to 7 blocks, the other crosses harder a moment later, each blow landing as its claw gets there, then all four claws meet in front of you and fire a short charge-paid beam (24 blocks, heavy knockback and a shake). It is a beam paid from the Core's charge, not a free shot.
- **The arms go around you, not through you** (Andy: "does it clip through the body in game?"): each elbow now swings out a way that keeps the arm clear, the upper pair back and out over the shoulders and the lower pair out past the hips, and the arm turns in its socket to point there. The hold, hurl, jab and cross, blast (its forearms come over the shoulders), climb, walk, skewer, pinwheel (a wheel behind your back), vault, dive and the four-hit chain were all re-posed so the forearms reach round the body (the crescent sweep passes well out in front of your face). Measured against the body, head and arms in the preview: no clipping, bar a frame or two as the blast gathers.
- **The Mad Tinkerer** has a new aside, "Ask about the four-armed Core", for anyone who talks to him.
- Art: the arms' model is our own (`client/rig/RigOctoClient`, drawn on the body like the back rig), texture (now 64x32 with the rods) and the item icon from `tools/gen_rig_octo.py`; the arm walk is `client/rig/RigOctoWalk`, ported from the tendril walk.
