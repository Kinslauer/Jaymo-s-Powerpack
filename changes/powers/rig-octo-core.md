# Rig Octo Core - four mechanical arms, tendril moves for Tinkerers and web slingers
- **Rig Octo Core** (Andy: "adapt the octopus arms to another rig chestpiece, available through the tinkerer ... and to web tinkerers who find a rig and use the forge. they can fashion tendril like abilities through the 4 mechanical limbs rather than biological ones through the symbiote"): a Rig Core with four jointed gunmetal arms folded down its spine. Only a Tinkerer (Kingdoms' calling) or a web slinger sees it at the Rig Forge (one more pick after the Rig leg: 15 salvage points, 8 iron, 4 pistons in the standard frame); anyone else's Forge screen skips it and the server refuses it. It is a normal Core in every other way (charge, Mind, the whole set's actives) (`rig/RigOcto`, `RigForgeEntity.OCTO`, `RigForgeBlock`, `RigNet.ForgeOpen` carries whether you may pick it).
- **Its moves**, all in the Rig menu (Sneak + J) and only shown while it is worn; they need only this Core, not the whole set, and cost its charge: **Arms Out** (unfold or fold the arms); **Arm Grab** (snatch a creature, player, item or corpse in front and hold it up; J again hurls it and it hurts what it hits; Sneak + grab at a wall tears a chunk loose instead); **Skewer and Hoist** (Mind 2: stab, lift overhead, slam, with a splash); **Pinwheel** (Mind 3: the arms spin, shots are batted back at their shooters, everything in reach is struck); **Arm Vault** (Mind 2: flung forward and up; again in the air dives arms first); **Arm Anchor** (Mind 2: an arm telescopes out, hauling you to a wall or a creature to you); **Arm Watch** (Mind 3: the arms strike the nearest monster and bat away shots on their own, a little charge each, standing down when the Core runs low).
- **With the arms out**: the lower arms grip walls (Forward or Jump climbs, Sneak holds still, no fall damage), and every full-strength blow you land is followed by one arm in a four-step chain: lance, crescent sweep, clamp (held still), launch. The moves are the Black Panther mod's waldo harness (CC0, by Riley) rebuilt as our own code; the Marvel names and gold look are left out.
- **The Mad Tinkerer** has a new aside, "Ask about the four-armed Core", for anyone who talks to him.
- Art: the arms' model is our own (`client/rig/RigOctoClient`, drawn on the body like the back rig), texture and the item icon from `tools/gen_rig_octo.py`. Placeholder quality: no first-person view of the arms, and the icon wants a proper redraw.
- Built; not played.
