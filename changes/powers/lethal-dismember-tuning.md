# Killing-blow dismemberment: armour, blunt breaks, short blades
- **Chances, not certainties**: a killing (or downing) hit on a humanoid mob now takes the part it landed on at: blades, the head 50%, an arm or a leg 25% (was always); daggers and short blades, the head 75%, a limb 12.5%.
- **Blunt weapons and fists** break the part instead (a head is dazed), and sever it only 5% of the time.
- **Armour** over the part lowers every one of these chances, as it does for other cuts.
- Tuning: `Dismember#LETHAL_*`; `Dismember#shortBlade`.
- Built by PR checks only; not played.
