# Boxing: every punch winds up for 0.75 seconds

- (Jung, outside Wind) Every punch now winds up before it lands: click, the arm draws back, and the blow lands 0.75
  seconds later (`Boxing.WINDUP`, 15 ticks). It lands on the one it was aimed at if they are still in reach then;
  step out during the windup and it hits air. The punch's stamina is spent as the windup starts.
- The next punch can't start until the last has landed, so **no boxer punches faster than once every 0.75 seconds**.
  That replaces the old top speed (about three a second at level 10): level 1 still punches every 0.8 seconds, levels
  2 and up all punch every 0.75, and from there a level only makes a punch cheaper. A click during a windup is still
  kept and thrown next. Strong attacks (uppercut, pull counter, roll straight) wind up the same way.
- The windup shows: a jab or cross draws the arm back and coils the body the other way before it snaps out; a hook
  lifts the arm out wide as the body coils back; an uppercut drops the fist and dips. The pose starts on the click
  and is at full reach as the blow lands. First person draws the punching arm back the same way.
- Not built locally; not played. The timing is worth a feel test: it changes how boxing fights.
