# Chrono: the wormhole, time skip, blitz poses, a fighting afterimage, blur steps
- **Run into the wormhole** (`ch_wormhole`): in Overclock's top gear, running flat out for about 7 seconds tears the air 14 blocks ahead open into a swirling hole of blue and gold light (three turning arms, a gold rim, bolts jumping in to a glowing heart). Run into it and a short skippable film plays (a new `UltimateFilm.WORMHOLE` shot): the camera rides behind you down a tunnel of streaking gold, yellow, blue and grey light toward a pale glowing sphere ringed with ripples, bright yellow lightning trailing off you, the sphere swelling to fill the screen. Then you come out in the future, or from the future back home, under Future's rules (only a Chrono whose echo is slain; its recharge; the city in place: `Future.mayLeap`, `Future.allowed`, `Future.go`). Anyone sprinting within 8 blocks comes too, as Sneak + B brings companions. No portal block is ever placed and there is no portal dimension: it is only the leap's look. It only opens for someone who has somewhere to go (a mark, or a leap). Classes: `ChronoWormhole` (server), `ChronoWormholeClient` (the holes, the catch key), `ChronoWormholeArt` (every shape drawn by code with MagicFx's tube, sphere, orb and bolt: no painted texture).
- **Wormhole to a marked place** (`ch_marked`): run into the wormhole with a place marked (Sneak + the mark key, as before; the server is now told where, and only where you stand) and you come out there in this world, over any distance, with those behind you, instead of the future. No echo needed.
- **Drag a foe through time** (`ch_drag`): in top gear at speed, press the new **catch key** (`]`, `key.jaymospowers.speed_catch`) at a creature ahead (within 5 blocks; never a player, the Ender Dragon or the Wither). It is held, unhurtable, while a new `UltimateFilm.DRAG` film drags it down the wormhole, out over a flat-colour world from orbit (a blue disc with white swirls turning on it, drawn by code), back down the tunnel, and into the live world, where you uppercut it up and a lightning bolt slams it down (damage grows with mastery). **Art hook:** the world-from-orbit picture is not made yet; put a PNG at `assets/jaymospowers/textures/fx/world_from_orbit.png` and the disc shows it instead, with no code change. Skipping the film lands the uppercut at once.
- **Time skip** (`ch_skip`): the new **skip key** (`[`, `key.jaymospowers.speed_skip`) while Overclocked shatters the moment for 2.5 seconds: foes within 12 blocks are slowed hard, creatures' aim wobbles (head and body jolted every tick, and now and then they forget their target) and they wear `ChronoSlowLook`'s trail of gold motes (new `ChronoSkip.OUT_OF_STEP`); you are pushed forward at speed and your screen warps (a new `chrono_warp` post effect, strength written each frame the way the black hole's lensing is, off in low-end mode). Player foes are slowed but their aim is their own. Class: `ChronoSkip`, `ChronoSkipClient`.
- **Swap with an afterimage** (`ch_mirage`): one of `ChronoDecoys`' afterimages now fights: it runs at the nearest hostile creature within 16 blocks and strikes it every 8 ticks. Sneak (a fresh press) swaps places with it.
- **Blitz poses** (`ch_blitz`): foes struck by Chrono Punch or the held flurry's fists are knocked into one of six different poses in turn (doubled over, spun round, knocked back arms wide, rolled to a side, lifted off its feet, dropped into a crouch), and one foe in five drops what it held in its main hand. Classes: `ChronoBlitz`, `ChronoFlow.Reel` (new `pose` and the `STRIKE` kind), `ChronoReelPose`.
- **Blur steps** (`ot_flicker`): in Overclock's low gears (the starting gear and under), Left Alt (the dodge key) with left, right, back or jump held is a short burst of speed that way, 5 ticks at about 0.95 blocks a tick, carrying you along the path in a blur (you cross every block; walls stop you), a little hunger each, a 14-tick wait. Chrono only: the Z Fighters' dodge is built on other rules, so it is not shared. Class: `ChronoFlicker`, `ChronoFlickerClient`; `DodgeClient` offers the press to it first.
- Keys: `]` catch a foe, `[` time skip (both rebindable); the dodge key and the mark key are as before. Lang: `lang/parts/chrono.json`.
