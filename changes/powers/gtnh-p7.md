# GT New Horizons picks, patch 7: mixed veins, small ores, the dowsing rod
- **New world**: the worldgen below (mixed veins, small ores) only reaches chunks made after this patch; land already generated keeps what it has. Kingdoms' grown veins (world tiers) get the same mix and small ores (see Kingdoms' note).
- **Mixed veins** (`ground/BigVeinFeature`): each great vein carries streaks of a partner ore: now and then a lump of it (18%) is 40% the partner. Iron with copper, copper with tin, gold with iron, tin with copper, silver with copper (the `partner` fields in `worldgen/feature/big_vein_*.json` and `ore_silver.json`). A vein without them is as before.
- **Tiny dusts** (`ground/Ores`): washing crushed ore (`tinkering/ore/OreWashing`, the existing cauldron and running-water washing) now also gives each ore a 20% chance of a **tiny dust** of its vein's partner (tiny iron, copper or tin dust), beside the old 15% nugget. Nine tiny dusts make the mortar's iron, copper or tin dust in the grid; one melts in the smeltery as a nugget's worth.
- **Tin crushes and washes** like the other ores: raw tin and a hammer in the grid, or tin ore mined with a hammer, give **crushed tin ore**; washed it's **washed tin ore** (its nugget byproduct is iron); both smelt to a tin ingot (crushed slowly, like raw ore) and melt doubled.
- **Small ores**: lone ore stones (iron, copper, tin, gold, silver; stone and deepslate) 3 to 20 blocks under the top of the ground. They break into **one crushed ore** of their metal (fortune adds as on vanilla ores; silk touch gives the block) and need the tool their full ore needs (stone pick for iron, copper and tin; iron pick for gold and silver). One lone small ore is tried in every chunk (iron and copper 4 parts, tin, gold and silver 1 each), and every great vein tries 8 more over itself, most of its own metal and a quarter its partner's, so the small ores a country shows lean toward the veins under it. They count as their metal's ore for the prospector's pick and the ore tells.
- **Dowsing rod** (`ground/DowsingRodItem`): 3 sticks in a fork, bound with a string. Right-click a sample onto it in the inventory (raw ore, crushed ore or small ore of iron, copper, tin, gold or silver; or the rod onto the sample), right-click it with an empty hand to take the sample out; the sample shows in its fork. Held in either hand, it ticks (a wooden click only the holder hears) when ore of the sample's metal lies within 40 blocks: slow at the edge, faster and a little higher the nearer it is, silent beyond. It looks again every 2 seconds over the loaded sections round you that hold that ore at all, nearest first, so it hears old veins, grown veins and small ores alike and never scans every tick. No text, no particles.
- **JEI**: an **Ore Washing** page (each crushed ore: washed ore, its nugget and its partner's tiny dust, with their chances), a **Small Ores** page (each small ore and its crushed ore), info pages for the dowsing rod, the small ores and the tiny dusts; the rod's, tiny dusts' and tin crushing's recipes on the crafting page. JEI's simple pages can show two byproducts.
- Classes: `ground/Ores` (tiny dusts, small ores, the scatter feature `jaymospowers:small_ores`), `ground/DowsingRodItem`, `client/jei/OresJei`; changed `ground/BigVeinFeature` (partner and small-ore fields), `tinkering/ore/OreWashing` (tin, partner tiny dust; the washing lives in tinkering), `client/jei/SimpleCategory`. Data, art and lang from `tools/gen_ores.py`.
- Built; not played.
