# Dragon bone, deep tooth and the dragonhide shield

- **Four dragon bones, each a Tinkers material.** Ember, Rime, Venom and Storm Dragon Bone are shaped at the Part
  Builder like bone, into every part bone makes (heads, handles, bindings, bow limbs and grips, arrowheads).
  Diamond-tier: 560 durability, mining speed 6.5, attack 2.75, a handle that swings a tenth faster. Each keeps its
  dragon's breath as a trait:
  - **Ember Marrow:** sets what it strikes alight for 3 seconds a level.
  - **Rime Marrow:** slows what it strikes for 3 seconds and chills it toward freezing.
  - **Venom Marrow:** poisons what it strikes for 3.5 seconds.
  - **Storm Marrow:** one hit in five a level shocks for 4 more damage.
- **Deep Tooth is a Tinkers material now** (it was a plain item): iron-tier, 280 durability, attack 2.25, quick in the
  hand. Its trait, **Hooked**, adds 2 damage a level against anything in water.
- **Dragon Hide and the Dragonhide Round Shield.** Six hides and any dragon bone make a round shield (900 durability,
  repaired with dragon hide at the anvil) that cannot burn. While it is raised no fire of any kind gets through, from any
  side, and you are put out. It bashes and blocks like the other round shields.
- The bones and hide come from wild dragons (Kingdoms). Every new item has a JEI page.
- Generated by `tools/gen_great_parts.py` (151 part and tool sprites a material, recoloured from bone's).
