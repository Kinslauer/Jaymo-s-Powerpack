# ground sticks look like sticks, and make a firepit
- **Loose sticks on the ground** were the rock pile's chunky boxes wearing a mostly see-through texture, and the block draws solid, so they showed as dark slabs. Each stick is now a thin rod (about 1 pixel thick, 6-11 long) lying at its own angle, with lengthwise bark, pale end grain and a side twig on two of them; a 2-4 pile crosses them over each other. The pile still turns four ways per block.
- The outline box now hugs the sticks (1.5 high instead of 3). The creative-tab Loose Sticks item has a model now (it had none).
- **Firepit** (TFC style): a full pile of 4 loose sticks with a rock pile on all 8 blocks around it becomes a Firepit, whichever goes down last. It is a campfire underneath (campfires tag, vanilla campfire block entity), so flint and steel, a flint-and-rock strike or a fire charge lights it, it cooks four foods, smokes, burns, and a shovel or water puts it out; sitting by fires counts it. It burns fuel, though: a stick 22.5 s, a plank 45 s, a log about 1 min 52 s (TFC's times), up to 15 sticks' worth; right click with fuel to feed it. Out of fuel it dies to an ash bed and won't take a light until fed; rain puts it out. Breaking it gives back up to 4 sticks. `ground/FirepitBlock`.
- **Laying sticks**: right click the top of bare ground (soil, sand, gravel, stone) with a stick to start a pile, or sneak + right click to lay one on anything; right click a pile with a stick to add one (other items still pick one up).
- Assets from `tools/gen_loose_sticks.py`. Built; not played.
