# Animals eat severed limbs; vultures carry them off
- Andy: "bears, wolves, and other animals should eat the limbs on the ground". A meat-eater with nothing to fight smells a severed head or limb within 16 blocks, goes to it and eats it where it lies: head down, worrying it, a bite every 12 ticks (3 for a limb, 4 for a head), blood, chewing and a bone crack at the end, and a bone left half the time. It heals 3 per part and is full for 4800 ticks after two (`core/LimbEaters`, `SeveredPart.edible/bite`).
- Who: `jaymospowers:limb_eaters` (wolves, foxes, polar bears, cats, ocelots, Kingdoms' feral wolves, coyotes and grizzly, Naturalist's and Bear Minimum's bears, big cats, hyenas, alligators, komodo dragons, Cube Animals' cats and bears, and everything in Kitchen's scavengers tag), plus any other mod's animal whose food is raw meat. Not pets, the young, the leashed or ridden. Kingdoms' werewolves are left to their own way of eating (Kingdoms `Devouring`).
- What: only parts with flesh on them, lying loose. Not skeleton bones or machines (no blood), not the undead's rot, not carried, burning, nailed up or kept trophy heads, and never a dead player's own head while they're still looking out of it. Living bodies, downed or not, are never touched.
- Andy: "is there a bird that can grab and fly off with the limbs? like a vulture?" Naturalist's vulture (`jaymospowers:limb_carriers`) spots a part from 32 blocks, swoops down, takes it up in its talons (the part rides under it), flies 20-40 blocks to a spot on dry ground as far from the nearest player as it can find, puts it down and eats it there. Hit on the way, it lets go; a reload keeps it carrying.
- Built; not played.
