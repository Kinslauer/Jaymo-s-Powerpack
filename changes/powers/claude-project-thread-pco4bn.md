# Kingdoms' sextons can wash and bury
- `ArmourGrime.washAll` is public, and `SeveredPart.litter()` says whether a part is lying loose (not carried, not in a bird's talons, not a kept trophy, not a dead player's head they still look out of), so Jaymo's Kingdoms' sextons can wash the guards and golems and bury what a fight left lying. Nothing changes in play without Kingdoms. Built; not played.
- **Steel takes knowing** (for Jaymo's Kingdoms' steel blueprint): the smithing anvil hammers plain steel only for a smith `RealmSteels.steelKnown` lets through (Kingdoms: one carrying a realm's steel blueprint, or sworn to a steel realm); anyone else gets the dull clank, as with a people's steel. Alone, Powers lets everyone. Folded steel and the realm steels are unchanged, and steel ingots are still made as before.
