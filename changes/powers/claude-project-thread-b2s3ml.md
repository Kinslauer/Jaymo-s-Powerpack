# limbs twice as tough
- **Limbs** (arms, legs, feet, wings) hold twice the damage before they break or a blow severs them: a humanoid's arms 16, legs 24, feet 16 on 20 health (were 8/12/8). Falls land on the feet first, so short drops no longer break legs. Head and body are unchanged. This is for every creature with a body plan, mobs too. Saved wounds keep their share, so a half-hurt leg stays half hurt. Parts still mend whole in the same time by themselves; dressings and other fixed-point heals mend a smaller share of a limb now. (`BodyPlan.Pools`)
- Not built or played; only constants changed.
