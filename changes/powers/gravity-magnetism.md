# Gravity: iron answers first (the X-Men mod's Magnetism, worked in)
- **Tear Loose**: sneak + right click while Lift holds a creature tears the metal weapon or tool out of its hands (anything mended with an iron, gold, copper or netherite ingot, plus shears, buckets, flint and steel). It flies to your feet and a mob scrambles after it, as after a fist parry's disarm. You keep your hold. 14 s cooldown. Nothing metal: "No metal in reach". A player's weapon is flung 5 blocks away from them instead, and nobody can take it up for 2 s; a player you may not harm (no PvP, a teammate) and gear a mob never drops hold fast, and a mob gives up its metal only once. At the last Mastery rank (every ability and rank 5), the torn weapon comes straight into your empty hand every time, or your pack, or at your feet if both are full. Sneaking now tears instead of choking; let go of sneak to choke.
- **Heavy metal**: R at a metal block (by its sound: iron, gold, copper, netherite blocks, anvils, chains, bars, rails, lanterns; also cauldrons, raw-ore blocks, iron doors and trapdoors, weighted plates; not diamond, emerald or redstone blocks, which sound the same; a mod's block that sounds like metal counts) tears it loose twice as fast, and the metal touching it comes too, however hard: it goes first in the chunk, and adds up to 4 pieces past the usual size, within the cap of 9. Needs Lift fully unlocked.
- **Scrap**: iron, gold and copper nuggets and netherite scrap gather and fly with the rocks (B+G), and a ball or ring made mostly of scrap hits twice as hard.
- Any torn-out chunk (every power that tears blocks loose) now lets its neighbours know only once the whole chunk is out, so a rail, lantern or torch torn out with what held it up no longer also drops as an item.
- Classes: GravityMetal (new), GravityPower (grip, the rip), GravityRocks, GravityRockOrders (the ward spends scrap too), PassiveBlocks.scrambleFor, BlockCluster.of.
- Built on CI; not played.
