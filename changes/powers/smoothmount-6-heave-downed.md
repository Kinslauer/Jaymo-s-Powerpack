# Heave a downed friend onto a horse
- **Sling**: carrying a downed player, right-click a horse/donkey/mule/camel to heave them up and sling them over its back. They ride slung until they come to, then can sit up or get off.
- **Downed rules**: no bleed-out, crawl or give-up while slung; getting up (revive or recovery) leaves them on the horse as a normal rider.
- New `Downed.SLUNG_ON` attachment marks a slung player's horse; `Carry#heave` handles the mount action.
- Built; not played in game.
