# Undead seek real shelter from the sun

- **Deadlier sunlight**: zombies, skeletons, strays (and anything else that burns in daylight) now only burn when a ray from their head to the Sun's real position is unobstructed - a roof, a cave wall or a solid cliff shades them; leaves, glass, torches and fences let the rays through, so tree shade no longer counts. The ray test uses Kingdoms' SunMoon for the Sun's true position when that mod is loaded, and the game's own sky angle otherwise; nothing burns in the future's ashen sun (FutureSunMixin untouched).
- **Shelter search**: burning zombies, skeletons and strays now look for real shelter - a cave, somewhere underground, or under a solid roof - within a wide radius (wider than vanilla's 10 blocks) and path toward the nearest spot the rays can't reach; they stay put when nothing is reachable (no digging - that needs Andy's say-so).
- Built; not played in game.
