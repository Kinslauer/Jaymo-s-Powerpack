# Undead seek real shelter from the sun

- **Deadlier sunlight**: zombies, skeletons, strays and other daylight-burning undead now burn whenever any part of their body is in the Sun's rays. Tree shade no longer protects them - leaves, glass, torches and fences let the rays through. Only a solid roof, cave wall or cliff face blocks the Sun. The Sun's true position is used (from Kingdoms' astronomy when that mod is loaded).

- **Shelter search**: burning undead now actively seek real shelter - caves, underground spaces, or under solid roofs - within a wider radius than before. They path toward the nearest spot the Sun can't reach. If no shelter is in reach, they stay put (they don't dig).

- Not tested in game.
