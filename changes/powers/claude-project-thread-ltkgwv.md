# Climbing and wall-run animations redrawn
- **Climbing a wall** (freerun cling and webbed fists): the hands now grip up and out beside the head with the elbows out to the sides, as in a pull-up, instead of folding in front of the face with the hands drawn in toward each other; the top hand reaches as high as an arm goes. The body tips a little back off the wall for room, the knees turn out and the feet step against the wall instead of into it. Shimmying across, the hands reach the way you're going and the body passes under them, instead of climbing on the spot. Sliding down once the grip goes blends in as before (WebParkourPose).
- **Climbing in first person**: both bare hands are on the wall, going hand over hand on the third-person climb's beat (each grips at the top of the view and sinks as you rise past it, then comes up from below to its next hold); slipping, both claw at the top (ClimbFirstPerson, LedgeFirstPersonMixin). Before, first person showed only the held item.
- **Wall run along a wall** (Wall Runner and Web): the body faces the way you run whatever way you look (the head still turns to your look), the stride follows the ground covered so the feet don't slide, the knees bend (folding high as each leg swings through), the body bobs and the shoulders twist; the outer arm pumps with a bent elbow and the arm by the wall reaches down to it, palm flat, instead of sticking straight out through it. The pose eases out over 4 ticks when you come off the wall instead of snapping (WebWallRunPose).
- **Running up a wall**: gentler lean about the shoulders and half the hip bend, so the head and chest no longer sink a third of a block into the wall; the stride follows the height climbed and each knee folds and sets the foot on the wall under it (UpRunPose).
- tools/pose_preview.py draws these poses on a player model for checking them outside the game.
