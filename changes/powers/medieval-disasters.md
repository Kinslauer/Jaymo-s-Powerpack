# Medieval patch 9: Disasters and the world's ending

- **Great falling stars**: far rarer than the ordinary ones (at most one every five days). A point of light grows in the night sky for two minutes, then a huge blazing rock comes down, seen from far off. It throws a shockwave that hurts and flings everyone near, and shakes the land for hundreds of blocks. It blows a deep crater that is carved a few hundred blocks a tick on loaded land, with a scorched floor and a raised lip of thrown earth. Fires burn round the rim. With Jaymo's Kingdoms, the ground shakes, the fire spreads, grey dust hangs in the sky for a day, and the settlements near hear of it and remember (`xenoform/FallingStars`, `KingdomsCompat.greatStar`).
- **Meteoric iron**: the crater's heart is a Meteoric Iron Heart. Mine it with iron or better to get three lumps of meteoric iron. Each lump melts in the smeltery into two ingots' worth of star metal, for smithing (`Tinkering`, `smeltery/Melting.meteoricIron`).
- **The Last Bell**: at the Arsenal's full mastery, a Reach can now pull through Jaymo's Kingdoms' Last Bell, one in the world at a time. It is the relic that calls down disasters and, rung to its end, the world's ending (`arsenal/ArsenalQuestItems`, `KingdomsCompat`).
