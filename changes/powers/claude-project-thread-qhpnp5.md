# NPCs' carried weapons hang on their bodies like a player's
- `BodyWeapons.carry`: Jaymo's Kingdoms hands over what an NPC carries off its hands (a belt sidearm, a slung bow or gun, the weapons in an MCA villager's pack); up to three take their homes the same way a player's do (blades at the hips, bows and guns crossed on the back, a shield on the back, pistols on the chest). Tools aren't shown on NPCs.
- `BodyWeaponsLayer` now goes on every humanoid body (MCA villagers, guards, folk), not only players, and is skipped on a severed head or limb.
