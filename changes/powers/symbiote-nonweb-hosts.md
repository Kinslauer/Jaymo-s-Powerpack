# symbiote in hosts without the Web - every ability, a fight for the suit, a duel with the slinger
Andy's rulings of 2026-11-25, for a host who isn't a web slinger (whom the symbiote holds at bond 60).
- **Every ability at the held bond**: "the blob should give you all the abilities that it has". Spikes, Collect and Wings open at 60 for such a host, and the big body comes at 60 (a web slinger still needs 100), so the Suit tile now climbs to the big body instead of stopping at Living Armor. The wheel, tooltips and unlock whispers show 60 for them. Abilities that ride a web slinger's zips stay web-only.
- **The suit won't come off**: without the Web, every try to take anything off (the suit tile's sneak-pick or pick at the top form, the mask, a costume-shaped suit's mask, the Hide order) is a fight with it, every time (no bond, count or chance lets you off), the same struggle bar as a takeover. Winning gets the mask off at most; the suit, armour and big body stay. The Hide order only draws the tendrils in. The Suit tile says so. It still comes off when it leaves, sulks, locks you out or you die.
- **A web slinger in range**: the black and red one no longer just leave. They drive their host at the slinger and fight them (as a red hunt, for up to three minutes); when the host is at 30% health or under, or downed, or the slinger is downed, it pours out and binds straight to the slinger (or chases them if they're far or already bonded). A host who breaks free, or a fight that runs out, waits 30 seconds before the next. Hunger still makes it leave; a still-acclimating one, or a creative slinger, keeps the old leave-and-chase; the white one still stays.
- **/powerdebug web symbiote trust <0-100>** sets its trust and prints the state, which now includes trust.
- Classes: WebSymbiote (unlockBond, bulkBond, clings, fightSlinger), WebSymbioteBond (the forced fight), WebSymbioteInfection (the duel and the bind), WebSymbioteBlob (leapInto), Costume (maskOff), WebSymbioteCollect, WebSymbioteWings, WebSymbioteTalk, WebSymbioteScreen, WebDebugCommand, lang web.json.
- Not built locally (CI builds it); not played. Not checked in game: the 500-block driven chase to an untracked slinger, the client steering for it, the struggle bar through the big body, and that the host in the big body can stay in it with no way to put it away (its own withdrawal timers no longer fire at 60).
