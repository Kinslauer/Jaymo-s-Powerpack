# Other mods' grey screens painted dark

- Cooking for Blockheads (cooking table, oven, spice rack, sink, cookie jar), Pantry's artisan press, Shippy Ships' holds, the rune altar, the spell binding table, the elephant's packs, the creeper minion tracker, Better Advancements' window and Simple Voice Chat's panels are now dark like our own screens, with light labels.
- Nothing of those mods is copied: their light grey is swapped for our dark tones as the game loads their textures, and their icons and arrows are untouched.
- Their screens that reuse Minecraft's own chest picture (fridge, counter, fruit basket, cutting board) stay vanilla grey, like the inventory.
- `/screenlook grey` puts those screens back to vanilla grey and `/screenlook dark` paints them again. It is your own setting and is remembered.
