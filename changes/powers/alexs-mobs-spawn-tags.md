# Alex's Mobs: old spawn files mended, blobfish deeper
- **Alex's Mobs animals that never spawned now can.** Installs that had the old Alex's Mobs port (0.8.82 to 0.8.107) kept its biome files in `config/alexsmobs/`, and Alex's Mobs Continued reads them as they are. 23 of them name climate tags the game doesn't have (`c:is_hot_overworld` and the like, not Fabric's `c:is_hot/overworld`), so orcas, hammerhead sharks, mimic octopuses, gusters, jerboas, rain frogs, tarantula hawks and triops never spawned; seals and cachalots missed the cold seas, mantis shrimp the warm seas, rattlesnakes and roadrunners the deserts, bald eagles and banana slugs most of their woods; and flying fish, catfish, platypuses, terrapins, skunks and Tasmanian devils spawned in the cold or heat they should stay out of.
- `compat/AlexsMobsSpawnsPreset` (pre-launch, both sides) renames just those tags to the ones the new port's own files use, every start, before Alex's Mobs reads them. Fresh installs never had the bad files and are unchanged.
- **Blobfish** spawn only below Alex's depth limit (25); with the land and sea 8 blocks higher than the game's, the deep sea floor sat above that almost everywhere. Once per install, while it's still 25, `amc.json`'s `blobfishSpawnHeight` goes to 33.
- Built; not played. The Deck server's own `config/alexsmobs/` wasn't looked at; the fix runs there too.
