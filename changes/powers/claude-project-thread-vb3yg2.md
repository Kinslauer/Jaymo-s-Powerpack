# Aether mobs wear The Aether II's shapes and animations
- Andy: "i think i'd like the aether 2 ported. the mobs you built dont look good at all." Every Aether mob is redrawn with
  The Aether II's own model and its keyframe animations (LGPL-3.0 code by The Aether Team; each ported file says where it
  came from, licence text in `docs/licenses/Aether-II-LGPL-3.0.txt`). Behaviour, drops, spawns, hitboxes, the world and the
  lore hooks are unchanged.
- **Animals**: moa (with its saddle), sheepuff, phyg, flying cow, aerbunny, kirrid and burrukai (a different body per
  climate zone), taegore, skyroot lizard, prismallard, glitterwing, shroudwing, aerwhale.
- **Monsters**: zephyr, tempest, cockatrice, blue and golden swet (a solid core inside see-through gel that squashes and
  stretches as it hops), aechor plant, carrion sprout, skephid, arkenium and gravitite taluton, bladeshroom hunter (buried,
  only its branches show).
- **Sentry Ruins**: slider, detonation sentry, sentry golem (and the sky warden, which shares its frame), mimic.
- **Textures are all new and ours** (`tools/gen_aether2_skins.py`, 133 skins and glow layers): The Aether II's textures,
  sounds and data-file animations are All Rights Reserved and were not used. Where it only has a data-file animation
  (aechor plant, taegore dig, gravitite taluton's spin, bladeshroom hunter) the movement is our own code.
- The Excalibur-style pack's copies of these skins are regraded from the new ones.
- Compiled; not played. Worth a look in game: the swet's gel drawing over its core, the tempest hiding from the sun, the
  mimic leaping out of its chest, and each mob's size against its hitbox.
