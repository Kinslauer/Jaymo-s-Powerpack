# Downed folk sit against walls; blood smear down the wall behind a slumped body
- A bleeding body that slides down a wall to sit against it (Jaymo's Kitchen's new wall slump) smears the wall behind it: a line of drops from its shoulders down to the floor splat on the wall, most with runs trickling down, and a stain is left where it sits.
- `BladeBlood#wallSmear` (drop count, speed and size at the top of it), hooked up in `KitchenCompat`. Not played.
- Andy: "can we make downed players and mobs sit against a wall?" Someone downed beside a wall (a solid face within about half a block of their side, with ground under them and room above to sit up) now sits with their back to it: turned to face out of it, slid back to it and down onto the floor, leaning on it, legs out ahead, arms fallen at their sides, head lolled forward and to one side, breathing hard (a hand over a broken arm). Mobs that don't crawl sit as they go down (crawlers still crawl). One that dies sat there leaves a corpse still sat there (Jaymo's Kitchen).
- Andy: "the view needs to move to the exact height on the model. other players should be able to place them on the wall, and there should be a smooth animation for the 1.5s it takes to get onto the wall." A downed player who lies still beside a wall now gets up against it in one smooth 1.5 s move: rolls over onto their back, sits up, turns round to put their back to the wall, pushes back to it and leans on it as their head lolls. Crawling off (or starting to get up) plays it back down to the floor. Out cold, they don't sit up on their own.
- Carrying someone downed, look at a wall within 3 blocks and let go (right-click) to set them against it: they're stood there and slide down it into the sit, out cold or not, and stay sat until they move off the spot or are picked up again. Let go of someone you're dragging beside a wall and they're propped up against it the same way. Works on downed mobs too (crawlers stay sat until they crawl off).
- Andy: "I dont see the arms being used in the frames, that's fine if they dont have any or if they are broken, but they should use what they can". Getting up against a wall now uses the arms: the arm on top plants beside the shoulder and pushes them over onto their back, both hands go down beside them to prop them up as they sit up, they step round on the floor as the body turns its back to the wall, and two pushes shove the hips back to it before the arms fall to their sides. A planted hand stays put on the floor while the body moves over it, the elbow bending to suit. A broken arm, or one that's gone with nothing fitted, just hangs and the other arm does it all alone; with neither, or out cold, the arms hang limp. Set down against a wall by someone else, the arms just hang (sat up, a Minecraft arm doesn't reach the floor).
- In first person, a sitting player's view is at their model's own eyes all the way through: it moves from the crawl's view onto the head as they roll over, rises with it as they sit up (to about 1 block up), and follows the head as it lolls.
- `client/wounds/WallSit` (the sit, the get-up, the view; tuning at the top), `client/wounds/WallSitArms` (the arms' part: when and where each hand goes down, and the reach that puts it there; tuning at the top), a `WALL` style in `DownedPose`, hooks in `CrawlPose` (the sit's arms and legs laid on past the springs, and its elbows bent) and `ProneCameraMixin`; `DownedLie#wall` finds the wall (`#propSpot` / `#prop` set someone against one, `PROPPED` marks them), `Carry#release` uses them, and `markBody` tags a body to stay sat. Checked in a pose sim (no part goes into the floor, the sat head stays off the wall; every way of lying beside the wall with both, one or no arms, the Java arm code run against it); not played.
