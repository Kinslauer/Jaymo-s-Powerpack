# Downed folk sit against walls; blood smear down the wall behind a slumped body
- A bleeding body that slides down a wall to sit against it (Jaymo's Kitchen's new wall slump) smears the wall behind it: a line of drops from its shoulders down to the floor splat on the wall, most with runs trickling down, and a stain is left where it sits.
- `BladeBlood#wallSmear` (drop count, speed and size at the top of it), hooked up in `KitchenCompat`. Not played.
- Andy: "can we make downed players and mobs sit against a wall?" Someone downed beside a wall (a solid face within about half a block of their side, with ground under them) now sits with their back to it: turned to face out of it, slid back to it and down onto the floor, leaning on it, legs out ahead, arms fallen at their sides, head lolled forward and to one side, breathing hard (a hand over a broken arm). Mobs that don't crawl sit as they go down (crawlers still crawl); a downed player sits up against it once they've lain still beside it for 1.5 s, and drops back into the crawl as soon as they move. One that dies sat there leaves a corpse still sat there (Jaymo's Kitchen).
