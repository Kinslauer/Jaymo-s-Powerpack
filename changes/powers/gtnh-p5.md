# GT New Horizons picks, patch 5: the forge's dangers
- **Hot walls burn** (`tinkering/dangers/ForgeDangers`): leaning on or standing on a lit forge hearth, a kiln's brick with a fire under the kiln, or the walls of a working smeltery or foundry, coke oven or brick blast furnace burns you a little (half a heart a second, with a hiss and a puff of smoke). Anything between you and the wall (planks, a slab, a trapdoor, a carpet) keeps you off it. Creative, Fire Resistance, fire-immune bodies and the Emberborn are spared. Each player is checked once a second against the few blocks they touch; the multiblocks report their own walls hot while they work (`#jaymospowers:forge_walls`), so nothing is scanned.
- **Rain on the fire**: rain falling on a lit forge hearth heats its metal at half speed; on an open kiln it takes 1 off its heat and slows its firing; into an open smeltery it hisses away 5 mB of lava fuel a second; on a coke oven's roof or down a blast furnace's top it bakes or works at half speed. A thunderstorm's downpour puts them out: the hearth goes out, the fire under an open kiln goes out (a campfire, a fire or a hearth; lava and magma don't), the smeltery loses all its fuel and the shock cracks one of its seared bricks, the coke oven and blast furnace stop and lose the bake under way until the storm passes. A roof over any of them keeps the rain off. Only rain counts: snow and dry biomes don't.
- **Cracked bricks** (`tinkering/dangers/Cracks`, `tinkering/smeltery/SearedBricksBlock`): seared bricks have a cracked state with its own look. One finished melt in 400 cracks one of the smeltery's seared bricks; a cracked brick still holds the wall but now and then (1 in 40 each second) leaks a nugget's worth (10 mB) of the bottom liquid, which drips down the outside with a pop and is lost. Broken out, a cracked seared brick stays cracked (its item carries the state). One kiln firing in 20 cracks one of the kiln's bricks: bricks, mud bricks and red nether bricks crack into new **cracked bricks**, **cracked mud bricks** and **cracked red nether bricks**, nether bricks into vanilla's cracked nether bricks. Cracked bricks still wall a kiln but each one slows its firing.
- **Mending**: use a hammer (`#jaymospowers:hammers`: smithing and forge hammers, the sledge and vein hammers, the mace) on a cracked seared or kiln brick with **grout** in your other hand or your pack: one grout is pressed in, the brick is whole again and the hammer wears a point.
- **Don't burn your fingers**: a lava bucket or a blaze rod in either hand (`#jaymospowers:hot_to_hold`) sets you alight like carrying hot metal. With Jaymo's Kitchen loaded, a **cooking pot** broken off its fire while heated drops hot (the hot metal mark): it burns bare hands anywhere in your pack until it cools in a minute, and can be quenched like hot metal (`tinkering/dangers/HotPots`, found by id, nothing else of Kitchen's touched). Tongs in either hand, or the new **smithing gloves** worn in the wrist bauble slot, stop it and wear a point every 2 seconds instead (`HotMetal`). Smithing gloves: 3 leather and a string; 240 uses; plain leather, so they need no bond.
- **Creatures melt to what they're made of**: on top of their blood, at each burn in the smeltery an enderman gives 25 mB of the new **molten ender**, an iron golem 10 mB of molten iron, a slime 25 mB of molten slime and a blaze 25 mB of the new **molten blaze** (blazes now cook in the smeltery in spite of their fire immunity). Two new casts: the **orb cast** (gold poured over an ender pearl or a slime ball) casts molten ender into ender pearls and molten slime into slime balls, 250 mB each; the **rod cast** (gold over a blaze rod or a stick) casts molten blaze into blaze rods (250 mB); molten blaze in a nugget cast makes blaze powder (125 mB).
- **JEI**: a **Creature Melting** page (each creature's spawn egg, its blood and what else it gives a burn), a **Mending Bricks** page (each cracked brick, grout and the hammers into the whole brick), info pages for the smithing gloves and grout; the orb and rod casts and what they cast show on the casting page, the gloves' recipe on the crafting page.
