# Cross-section cut faces on severed limbs, necks and waist halvings

- **Cross-section cut faces**: Where a part was severed off — on a body's open limb stump or headless neck, on the severed attachment end of a `SeveredPart`, across the waist of a halved body (`Dismember#halve`), and on Jaymo's Kitchen's corpses — a vanilla-style cross-section is now drawn across the cut at every blood setting except Off (and hidden in Streamer safe mode).
- **Per-body insides and materials**: Bleeding creatures show meat and a ring of bone (plus organs at a waist halving) matched to their blood kind (red, rotten, ender, dark, bug, sap/Bio-built, golden Divine). Skeletons and Wither skeletons show bone, golems and machines show sheared iron or copper, slimes and Slime-born show goo, magma cubes, Blazes, Emberborn and Magmavein Stoneborn show cooling magma, snow golems show packed snow, Creakings show heartwood, Stoneborn show stone or obsidian, and bloodless Hollows and spirits show dry pallid husk (`Dismembered`, `SeveredPartRenderer`, `tools/gen_cut_faces.py`). Not tested in game.
