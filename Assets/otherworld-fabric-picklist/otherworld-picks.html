Otherworld Pick-List

Your ask: "port this to fabric then create a picklist of what it has and we dont? I'll then move the differing models and textures into our pack."

Otherworld v8 HF2 is a Forge 1.20.1 pack of 435 mods. It can't be ported as one piece, because most of its content mods have no Fabric build for 26.3. So each row below is one thing it has that we don't, with how we'd get it: add the mod where a Fabric 26.3 build exists, otherwise build our own version in Powers, Kingdoms or Kitchen.

About moving models and textures: a red licence chip (All Rights Reserved) means the art can't be copied into our pack; our rule is no other mod's art unless its licence allows it. A green chip means the code licence is open, but check that it covers the art before copying. Otherworld's own packs (quest icons, GUI, mob patches) are All Rights Reserved too.
