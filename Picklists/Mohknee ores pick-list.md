# Mohknee ores and tribal gear

Blossom smiths already make folded steel from river black sand. My pick keeps that tradition and gives the foothill tribes a scarce red ironstone of their own. Both produce gear in the existing progression. Tick the parts you want built.

## Questions

### Which source should supply tribal smiths? ^mk-source
The lore says Mohknee has little ore and describes iron washed from its rivers.
- (*) **Scarce red ironstone in dry foothills** ^mk-red
  Approve a small local exception. The tribes smelt it for ordinary iron and use the red ochre to mark their gear.
- ( ) **River black sand for both peoples** ^mk-sand
  Keep the current lore as written. The tribes work the same raw iron into thick fittings while blossom smiths fold theirs for blades and lamellar.

### How should players find the tribal material? ^mk-access
This decides whether the source adds a gathering opportunity.
- (*) **Gather it and encounter it in tribal gear** ^mk-gather
  Under red ironstone, add a few exposed cuts near suitable camps with ordinary iron output. Under black sand, use the existing gathering route.
- ( ) **Encounter it through tribal gear and trade** ^mk-trade
  Leave the land generation and gathering routes as they are.
