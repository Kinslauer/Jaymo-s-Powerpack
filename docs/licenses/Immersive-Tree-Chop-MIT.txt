Immersive Tree Chop (mod id timberfall), by Litewer
https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/immersive-tree-chop

Used in Jaymo's Kingdoms: the carved wood volume of a chopped log and its mesher
(kingdoms/src/main/java/com/jaymoskingdoms/WoodVolume.java, drawn by
kingdoms/src/client/java/com/jaymoskingdoms/client/ChoppedLogRenderer.java), ported from
dev.litewer.immersivetreechop.wood.WoodVolume and client.WoodMesh in version 0.5.2-beta.1.
Nothing else of the mod is used: none of its sounds, animations or other assets.
