# Worldbound gap pass — v0.0.14 (NeoForge 1.21.1)

Research only: nothing from Worldbound ships. Worldbound (by gts, CurseForge project 1637360, All Rights Reserved) is a vanilla+ 1.21.1 NeoForge pack built around dense structures in all three dimensions, a bigger Illager threat, an enchantment overhaul and bosses.

## How I compared

- Downloaded `Worldbound-v0.0.14.zip` (CurseForge file 9520711) and read its `manifest.json`: **315 mods**.
- Mod names from modpackindex's API listing for this pack's v0.0.14 (315 of 315 mods).
- Compared against our pack's **147 installed mods** (`mods/` folder), matching by mod name.

## What our pack already covers

- **Structures:** Dungeons & Taverns, Epic Structures (villages, dungeons, witch-huts), Structory + Structory Towers, Moog's Structure Lib / Moog's Voyager / Moog's Nether Structures / MMR Moog's Mineshafts Reimagined, Repurposed Structures, Grim Kingdoms Lost Structures & Ruins.
- **Illagers:** Friends & Foes (Iceologer, Illusioner, Wildfire among the mob-vote mobs); It Takes a Pillage Continuation.
- **Bosses:** Mutant Monsters.
- **Magic:** Spell Engine, Spell Power, Wizards, Runes.
- **QoL/performance:** JEI, FTB Quests, KubeJS, Lootr, AppleSkin, Sodium, Iris, Lithium, and equivalents of most of Worldbound's library and optimization mods.

Structures (all three dimensions):

- **MES - Moog's End Structures** — End dimension structures; we have Moog's Nether/mineshafts/voyager, but no End structures mod.

- **Luki's Grand Capitals** — rebuilt vanilla villages and Illager structures.
- **Luki's Strongholds / Luki's Woodland Mansions / Luki's Ancient Cities** — overworld stronghold, mansion and ancient-city variants.
- **Villages & Pillages** — hostile pillage structures alongside villages.
- **Illager Structures** — Illager-themed structures.
- **Hopo Better Underwater Ruins** — new ocean ruins (we have Hopo Better Ruined Portals only).
- **Ocean Lily Pad Village** — villages on giant lily pads in oceans.
- **Nether Trials & Chambers** — trial chambers generating in the Nether.
- **Medieval Buildings [End Edition]** — medieval structures for the End.

Illagers:
- **Illager Invasion** — port of Illager Expansion: new Illager foes, camps and fortresses (we have It Takes a Pillage Continuation and Friends & Foes already).
- **Raids: Enhanced** — mini-bosses for raids.
- **Glowing Illagers** — Glowing effect on raid participants (minor, cosmetic).

Enchantment overhaul:
- **Just a Lot More Enchantments** — many new enchantments.
- **BeyondEnchant** — raises the enchantment limit.
- **Easy Magic** — enchanting tables that keep items and allow easy re-rolls.
- **Vein Mining** — vein-mining enchantment for tools.
- **Air Hop** — multi-jump enchantment.
- **BEER** — extended bookshelf detection range.
- **Burn's Enchantment Extractor** — move enchantments from tools to books.
Our pack has spell mods but no enchantment-overhaul mod.

Bosses:

- **Ender Trigon Unofficial** — harder Ender Dragon fight.
(We already have Mutant Monsters.)

## Verdict

No single missing mod stands out as must-have: our structure coverage (Dungeons & Taverns, Epic Structures, Structory, Moog's mods) and Illager coverage (Friends & Foes, It Takes a Pillage Continuation) already answer Worldbound's advertised strengths. If Andy wants any of the leftovers above, name one and I'll scope a proper port task — nothing from Worldbound is All Rights Reserved-safe to copy, so anything we take would be remade from scratch.
