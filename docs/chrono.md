# Chronokinesis (`jaymospowers:chrono`)

Golden clockwork time magic. The server owns all of it. The client only draws the HUD from a small sync packet.
Particles and sounds are sent from the server, so every nearby player sees and hears them.

## Controls

| Key (default) | Ability | What it does | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | **Rewind** | Snaps you back to where you were 3 s ago, with the same facing. Restores your health to what it was then, if that was higher. Puts out fire and clears fall distance. A golden streak replays your path backwards, with a rising chime. | 15 s |
| G | **Stasis Field** | Stops time for 4 s in a 6-block sphere where you cast it. Mobs (not players) freeze in place with their AI paused. Projectiles in flight stop mid-air and then carry on with their original velocity. You can still hit frozen mobs; the knockback is stored and released (capped) when time resumes. | 30 s |
| V | **Overclock** | 6 s of Speed III, Haste II and ×1.6 attack speed (a transient attribute modifier). | 25 s |
| B | **Time Bloom** | 5 s of fast-forward in a 5-block sphere where you cast it. Gives extra random ticks (crops, saplings, cane, berries, amethyst...) and runs every ticking block entity at 4× speed (furnaces, smokers, blast furnaces, campfires, brewing stands, Cooking for Blockheads ovens). | 40 s |

Cooldowns start on cast. Pressing a key that is on cooldown flashes that clock red and plays a dull click, client side.

## HUD

Four clock faces sit right of the hotbar in the bottom-right corner, where the other powers keep their ability
chips. Each has its bound key in the corner and, while the ability is active, a duration bar above it.

- **Cooldown:** a dark sector covers the part of the dial still to go, a golden hand sweeps clockwise, and the
  seconds left are shown as a number.
- **Ready:** the clock gets a brief bright outline and a soft chime.
- **Active Stasis, Overclock or Bloom:** the face lights up and a thin gold bar under it shows the time left.
- **Overclock:** adds a faint pulsing golden vignette.

Art: `textures/gui/chrono_hud.png` (64×32), generated by `tools/gen_chrono.py`.

## How it works

- **`ChronoPower`** handles the glue, Rewind, Overclock and the HUD sync. Each player's `ChronoState` is kept in
  memory, keyed by UUID. It survives death and relogging, so relogging doesn't reset cooldowns, but not a restart.
- **Rewind history** is a ring buffer of 61 samples (position, rotation, health, dimension), recorded every server
  tick while the player is alive.
  - Rewind fails without using the cooldown in three cases:
    - there is less than 0.5 s of history (just respawned or just rewound);
    - any sample is from another dimension;
    - none of the past spots fit your hitbox any more.
  - It picks the oldest sample whose spot is still free.
  - It teleports with `ServerPlayer.teleportTo(level, x, y, z, Set.of(), yRot, xRot, false)`, so the client follows.
  - History is cleared after a rewind.
- **`StasisField`** is fixed where it was cast. It re-captures entities every 2 ticks, so mobs and arrows that enter
  the sphere freeze too.
  - **Mobs:** a mixin (`ChronoLivingEntityMixin`) cancels `LivingEntity.aiStep` on the server for frozen mobs. That
    stops AI, jumping, travel and gravity, while normal ticking goes on: hurt, death, fire. Nothing is written to the
    mob, so a crash can't leave mobs frozen. A creeper's swell is held at -1. The Ender Dragon and the Wither are
    exempt.
  - **Projectiles** (only ones in flight; fishing hooks and fireworks are exempt): the field stores their velocity and
    NoGravity flag, zeroes the motion and sets NoGravity (synced, so the client arrow stays put), and parks them at
    their position every tick. When the field ends they get both back.
  - **Shared registry:** one identity-keyed registry holds the frozen entities, with a holder count, so overlapping
    fields from two players work.
  - **Cleanup:** the field ends early, releasing everything, on death, logout, a dimension change, losing the power,
    or the server stopping.
- **`TimeBloom`** scans its sphere on cast and again every second.
  - **Random ticks:** each candidate gets an extra random tick with 30% chance per tick, capped at 64 per server tick.
    Liquids, ice, snow layers and weathering copper are excluded, so it can't melt things, spread lava fire or age
    builds.
  - **Block entities:** they get 3 extra ticks per tick (4× speed), capped at 60 extra block-entity ticks per server
    tick. The extra ticks come from the block's own `EntityBlock#getTicker`, with the same checks as
    `LevelChunk`'s ticker, so this works for modded machines too.
  - **Never accelerated:** spawners, trial spawners, vaults, moving pistons, end gateways, creaking hearts, command
    blocks and structure blocks.
  - **Errors:** a block entity type whose ticker throws is logged once and blacklisted for the rest of the session.
- **Network:** `jaymospowers:chrono_sync` (S2C) carries cooldown left and total, and active left and total, for
  all four abilities. It is sent on every change, every second while anything is counting, and on (re)join. The
  client counts down between packets.

## How to test

1. Run `/power set @s chrono` and check that four clocks appear right of the hotbar, labelled R, G, V and B.
2. **Rewind:**
   1. Sprint around for a few seconds, then press R. You should snap back about 3 s along your path and see a
      golden streak replay it.
   2. Take some damage, wait under 3 s, and rewind. Your health should come back.
   3. Set yourself on fire and rewind. It should put the fire out.
   4. Go through a Nether portal and press R straight away. You should get the "timeline is tangled" message and no
      cooldown should start.
3. **Stasis:**
   1. Run `/summon zombie` a few times, and `/summon skeleton`, then press G next to them. They should freeze; check
      that a mob in mid-air stays in mid-air.
   2. Hit a frozen zombie a few times. It should take damage, and fly back when the field ends.
   3. Have a skeleton (outside the field) shoot into it, or shoot an arrow yourself and press G. The arrows should
      hang in the air and then continue on their original path.
   4. Log out mid-stasis and log back in. Arrows should resume or fall normally and mobs should move normally.
4. **Overclock:** press V. You should get Speed III and Haste II icons, faster sword swings (watch the attack
   indicator), golden wisps and a faint gold frame. After 6 s, `/attribute @s minecraft:attack_speed get` should
   be back to its normal value (4.0 with an empty hand).
5. **Time Bloom:**
   1. Plant wheat on watered farmland and some saplings, then press B. The crops should grow several stages in 5 s,
      with green puffs where they grow.
   2. Load a furnace with `/give @s minecraft:raw_iron 16` and coal, press B beside it, and watch it smelt about 4×
      faster.
   3. Do the same with a smoker and a Cooking for Blockheads oven from Jaymo's Kitchen.

## Known limitations and upgrade ideas

- If a frozen projectile's chunk unloads during the 4 s field, it is saved with NoGravity and no motion, so it will
  hang in the air. This is unlikely, since the field sits next to its caster. Server stop and logout are handled.
- Mobs whose `aiStep` override does work before `super.aiStep()` (a few vanilla ones, some modded) still run that
  part while frozen. Contact damage, such as a frozen slime touching you, is not suppressed.
- Bloom's extra block-entity ticks run at the end of the server tick, not inside the level's block-entity pass. That
  is fine for vanilla and CfB-style tickers. A modded machine that breaks under it is caught and blacklisted.
- State is in memory only, so a server restart resets cooldowns.
- Upgrade ideas:
  - a passive "déjà vu" (auto-rewind on lethal damage, long cooldown);
  - a field that follows the caster;
  - slowing (rather than stopping) players in Stasis;
  - client-side shell rendering instead of server particles;
  - a "time-stopped" grey tint on frozen mobs.

## 0.1.5
- **Stasis Field (G):**
  - It now also stops other players in the bubble, never the caster.
  - The server holds them where they were caught; they can still look around.
  - Attacking, mining, placing, using items and interacting are all blocked (Fabric player callbacks).
  - The duration is now `ChronoPower.REWIND_TICKS` (3 s), the same window Rewind can undo.

## 0.2.0: Ascension & Corruption

Nothing here is explained in game. The only hints are a whisper in the action bar when you first see your echo, one
when you wake holding the clock, one when an echo dies for good, and the clock's tooltip.

### The Echo (Chrono's curse)

Chrono has **no corruption meter**. Its corruption is the echoes: time remnants of you that wear your face and are
hostile to everything.

- **Entity:** `jaymospowers:chrono_echo` (`ChronoEcho`, a `Monster`, mob category MISC so it doesn't eat the
  monster cap).
  - It is drawn with its owner's player model (wide or slim) and skin. The client takes the skin from the tab list if
    the owner is online, otherwise from the synced `ResolvableProfile` through `PlayerSkinRenderCache` (as mannequins
    do).
  - It is translucent and tinted pale cyan (`ChronoEcho.TINT`), with a slow flicker.
  - Client-side effects: pale cyan and grey shard dust, the odd spark, and now and then a short run of faint ticks.
  - The owner's UUID (an entity reference) and profile are synced entity data. Owner, bond, habits and route are
    saved on the entity, which persists through chunk unloads and restarts and never despawns.
- **Spawning:**
  - Every `Power.onLevelUp` (mastery levels 2–4 and ascension at 5) owes one echo, due after a random 5–25 minutes.
  - Pending timers live in the player attachment `jaymospowers:chrono_memory` (`ChronoMemory`).
  - Timers count down only while the owner is online, alive and in survival or adventure (not creative or spectator).
  - The echo spawns 16–28 blocks away on solid, dry ground within ±10 blocks of the owner's height. It prefers a
    spot the owner can't see (behind them, or blocked by blocks); if all 32 tries are in view, it uses one in view.
  - If no spot is found, it tries again in 10 s.
  - Any number of echoes can exist at once.
- **Habits** (recorded while you hold Chrono, in `ChronoMemory`):
  - The blocks you break (`PlayerBlockBreakEvents.AFTER`) and place (`ChronoBlockItemMixin` on `BlockItem.place`).
  - The item in your main hand, sampled every second.
  - The 96 most recent walked positions: one every 2 s while on the ground, at least 3 blocks apart, per dimension.
  - Each count list keeps 16 kinds; the least used is dropped.
- **Behaviour:**
  - It holds your most-held item. The item is only for show: melee damage is a fixed 5, and nothing drops.
  - With nobody to fight, it walks your recorded routes:
    - If one of your route points (in its dimension) is within 48 blocks, it walks 8 points of the route from there,
      where you walked them.
    - Otherwise it walks the same shape of route starting from where it stands.
    - It pauses 2–7 s between outings and strolls randomly when it has no route.
  - About once per 15 s while idle (1 in 300 ticks), it mines a block of one of your top 3 mined kinds within 3
    blocks, from its feet up to 2 above.
    - The block breaks with no drops, and only when `mobGriefing` is on.
    - It never touches block entities or containers, unbreakable blocks, or anything harder than obsidian.
  - About once per 35 s (1 in 700 ticks), it places your most-placed block beside itself on sturdy ground. This also
    needs `mobGriefing` and skips block-entity blocks.
  - It swings when it mines, places or attacks, and now and then at nothing.
  - It is hostile to every living thing within 16 blocks: players including you, mobs and animals. It ignores other
    echoes and armour stands.
- **Death:**
  - **Outside the Nether**, any damage it isn't immune to shatters it: shards, sparks and glass/amethyst sounds. A
    return is queued for 1–10 minutes later, near you, in whatever dimension you're in by then.
    - If you're offline when it shatters, the return waits in the overworld's `jaymospowers:chrono_orphans`
      attachment and is picked up within 5 s of you coming back.
  - **In the Nether** it takes damage normally (30 HP). Killing it there removes it for good, with the "time
    collapses" effect: an imploding ring of light, reverse-portal and soul particles, and a deep bell, beacon, anchor
    and glass chord. You get a whisper if you're online.
  - `/kill` always kills it for good, as an admin escape hatch.
- **Following:** when you change dimension, your echoes within 24 blocks of where you left come along. They arrive
  5–9 blocks from you on the other side. To lure one into the Nether, either take it through a portal with you, or
  shatter it and be in the Nether when it comes back.
- **Losing Chrono:** every loaded echo of yours fades, and your memory (pending timers, habits, stopped time) is
  dropped.
  - Each memory carries a random **bond** stamped on its echoes. An echo loaded later fades as soon as it sees its
    owner online without Chrono or with a different bond. Taking Chrono again doesn't revive old echoes.
- **First sighting:** the first time you have line of sight to one of your echoes within 32 blocks, the action bar
  whispers *"Someone is walking where you walked."*

### Ascension: Timeless

- **Milestone:** total ticks with a Stasis Field running, plus the ticks undone by each Rewind, stored as
  `ChronoMemory.stoppedTime`. It must reach 10 minutes (12000 ticks).
- **The Stopped Clock (`jaymospowers:chrono_stopped_clock`):**
  - Sleep through to morning (in bed long enough, and it's bright when you wake) with the milestone reached and
    `Fate.canAscend`. You wake holding the clock: in your main hand if it's empty, otherwise in your inventory, or
    dropped if that's full.
  - You get the whisper *"You wake. The ticking has stopped."* You won't get another clock while you still carry one.
  - It has a cryptic two-line tooltip, an epic rarity name, stacks to 1, and is in no creative tab.
- **Using it:** with Chrono and `Fate.canAscend`, it is consumed and `Fate.ascend` runs. Anyone else hears one tick.
- **Timeless form:**
  - The four abilities' cooldowns are divided by 1.25 (25% faster): Rewind 12 s, Stasis 24 s, Overclock 20 s, Bloom
    32 s.
  - Two slow clock hands (gold and pale cyan dust) circle the player at waist height. They are drawn client-side for
    every Timeless player within 32 blocks, and for yourself only in third person.
- **Paradox Cut (C, `onUltimate`):**
  - A slash 4 blocks deep and 60° to each side of your look direction, needing line of sight. It skips your own pets
    and armour stands.
  - It deals **9** (player attack) now.
  - Exactly **1.5 s** later it deals **9** again to everything it hit, as indirect magic from you, which bypasses
    armour. The target is found by UUID in any dimension, and the hit cooldown is cleared first, so fleeing doesn't
    help.
  - Until then each target carries a pale tick mark: a little hand sweeping above its head, with a clicking
    countdown.
  - **8 s** cooldown, which Timeless doesn't shorten.
  - HUD: a fifth, square chip right of the four clocks, shown only while you're ascended. It has the glyph, a
    draining shade with the seconds left, and the bound key (C) in the corner. It flashes red on a press during
    cooldown and outlined when ready.

### How to test (0.2.0)

1. **An echo now:** run `/power set @s chrono`, walk around, mine a few blocks of one kind and place some of
   another, holding a sword. Then run `/powerdebug chrono echo`.
   - An echo should appear 16–28 blocks away, translucent cyan, with your skin, holding the sword.
   - You should get the sighting whisper once you look at it.
   - It should attack you (switch to survival) and nearby animals.
   - Leave it idle with `/gamerule mobGriefing true` and watch it mine your mined kind (no drops) and place your
     block. With `mobGriefing false` it should do neither.
2. **Shatter and return:** hit it in the overworld. It should shatter and disappear. `/powerdebug chrono timers`
   should show a return due in 1200–12000 ticks.
3. **The Nether:**
   - Spawn one, stand in a Nether portal with it within 24 blocks, and go through. It should arrive near you in the
     Nether.
   - Hit it there: it should take damage and fight back. Kill it to see the collapse effect and get the whisper.
     `/powerdebug chrono timers` should show no new return.
4. **Level-up timers:** run `/power level @s 2` (after `/power awaken @s` if needed) and check
   `/powerdebug chrono timers` for a 6000–30000 tick echo. In creative the number shouldn't move.
5. **Persistence:** spawn an echo, `/save-all`, restart, and come back. It should still be there with your skin. Run
   `/power clear @s` (or set another power) and it should fade.
6. **Ascension:**
   1. `/power set @s chrono`, then `/powerdebug chrono stasistime 600`.
   2. Sleep in a bed at night until morning. You should wake holding the Stopped Clock and see the whisper.
   3. Right-click it to ascend. You should get the fanfare and the "Timeless" badge, and an echo is owed (check
      `/powerdebug chrono timers`).
   4. Shortcut: `/give @s jaymospowers:chrono_stopped_clock`. Someone without Chrono, or already ascended, should
      only hear one tick.
7. **Timeless:** after ascending, the four cooldowns should be 12/24/20/32 s, and press F5 to see the two hands
   circling you. Another player should see them too.
8. **Paradox Cut:** `/summon zombie` a few times, then press C facing them. They should take 9, carry a ticking mark,
   and take 9 again 1.5 s later even if they've walked away. The fifth chip should show an 8 s cooldown with "C".

### Risky or untested at runtime

- **Skin rendering:** the renderer swaps the wide and slim models in `submit()` and uses `PlayerModel`'s translucent
  render type with an alpha tint. The skin comes from the tab list or `PlayerSkinRenderCache`. On offline-mode
  servers the profile has no textures, so echoes of offline owners show the default Steve/Alex skin.
- **Spawning in odd terrain:** the ground search stays within ±10 blocks of your height. Flying high, in deep
  caves, or over oceans it may keep retrying every 10 s until you're somewhere with room.
- **Routes:** route points are where you stood. Pathfinding may give up on some, such as ladders or parkour; the
  echo then moves to the next point after the navigation finishes or after 10 s.
- **Damage that shatters:** anything outside the Nether shatters it: fire, cactus, drowning, fall, or mobs it picks
  fights with. Expect echoes to come and go a lot in the overworld; that's the nuisance.
- **Bond check** only runs while the owner is online. Echoes of an owner who lost Chrono while their echoes were
  unloaded stay until the owner is next online near them.
- **Paradox Cut** follows targets across dimensions only while their chunk is loaded. A target in an unloaded chunk
  when the second hit is due is spared.
- Echoes block sleeping nearby, like any monster.

## 0.8.0: Time Flow (the Z mode of a Timeless, corrupted Chrono)

### Sets of four (`ChronoFlow.land`)
- The existing combo counter splits landed blows into sets of four (`SET_SIZE`).
- **Blows 1–3** do no push and no lift. Even the hit's own vanilla knockback is undone: horizontal motion is zeroed.
  Each blow **stuns** the target for `STUN_TICKS` (15), refreshed on every hit.
  - The stun is the synced attachment `jaymospowers:chrono_flow_stun` (`ChronoFlow.STUN`, the game time it ends).
    It lives in `ChronoFlow`, which is already listed in `JaymosPowers.registerAttachments()`.
  - **Mobs:** `ChronoMobStunMixin` cancels `Mob.serverAiStep` (goals, navigation, move control), stops the path and
    zeroes the walk inputs. Gravity and thrown motion still apply.
  - **Players:** `ChronoStunInputMixin` (client, on `KeyboardInput.tick`) empties their movement keys and jump, the
    same way Gravity's held stun does.
  - The old 20-tick Slowness on every blow is gone; the stun replaces it.
- **Blow 4** is the finisher: the style's full push ×`FINISHER_PUSH` (1.3) plus its lift (uppercut, kick). It ends
  the stun so the target flies, with an extra crit sound and a sweep puff.
- Counters always push with their own riposte (0.7, no ×1.3). They still count as landed blows for the combo.
- Other systems can call **`ChronoFlow.stunned(LivingEntity)`**. It works on both sides, since the stun is synced.

### Counters (`ChronoFlow.counterOne`, `ChronoFlowClient.counterMotion`, `ChronoFlowPose.counter`)
- Countering **one** attacker picks one of three variants at random on the server. Countering several attackers at
  once keeps the old 10-tick crowd counter.
- `Move` has two new fields, `variant` (`CROWD`, `CIRCLE`, `SLIP`, `DEFLECT`) and `deflect` (0–4), both in its
  stream codec. `Move.duration()` / `Move.hitAt()` replace direct `DURATION[style]` / `HIT_AT[style]` lookups for
  poses and blows.
- The caster can't be damaged by anything for the whole animation (`Fighter.counterUntil`, checked first in
  `ALLOW_DAMAGE`). Right clicks during it are ignored.
- The attacker is staggered (Slowness IV and Weakness II, as before) and **stunned** until `COUNTER_STUN_SLACK`
  ticks after the blow, so it holds still for the move.

| Variant | Length / blow at | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Circle | 24 / 17 | Guard up and a brief crouch. Then the client carries you round the attacker in a half-circle (radius 1.6–2.6, `CIRCLE_TICKS` 10, either direction) to its back, facing it, and you strike from behind with a running stride. |
| Slip | 20 / 12 | A 3-tick sidestep with a touch of back-step, leaning out of the blow with the guard high. Then a short hook to the body, turned through the hips. |
| Deflect | 26 / 18 | One of 5 parries (below). The attacker is knocked back a step (`DEFLECT_SHOVE`) with a shield clank and sparks. Then a palm-strike riposte. You keep control of your view. |

The five deflects (`HIGH_BLOCK`, `OUTSIDE_SWEEP`, `SLAP_DOWN`, `CROSS_BLOCK`, `ELBOW_GUARD`):
1. **High forearm block:** the lead forearm snaps up across the face, then pushes up and out.
2. **Outside sweep:** the lead arm sweeps from the centre line outward, and the body turns with it.
3. **Slap-down:** an open hand drives the blow down and across the body.
4. **Cross block:** both forearms cross in an X before the chest, then spread apart.
5. **Elbow guard:** the elbow is raised to the side to take the hit, then the forearm swings out front.

Movement stays client-authoritative. The client watches its own synced `MOVE`, and on a new circle or slip counter
it drives the motion itself, the same way the lunge dash works. No packet is involved.

### How to test (0.8.0)
1. Run `/power set @s chrono`, `/power ascend @s` and `/power corrupt @s`, then press Z to enter Time Flow.
2. **Sets of four:** `/summon zombie` and strike it up close.
   - Blows 1–3 should not move it, and it should stand frozen (no walking or turning to path) between blows.
   - Blow 4 should throw it further than before, and an uppercut finisher should launch it.
   - Stop hitting: it should move again about 0.75 s after the last blow.
3. **Players:** with a second player, land blows 1–3 on them. Their WASD and jump should do nothing for about 0.75 s
   after each blow. Blow 4 should knock them back.
4. **Counters:** let a single zombie swing at you and right click in the white-flash window. Repeat several times:
   - you should see circles (you run round to its back and hit it from behind), slips (sidestep, body hook), and
     deflects with different arm parries;
   - nothing should hurt you during the animation (have a second zombie or a skeleton hit you mid-counter).
5. With two or more attackers held at once, right click: you should get the old quick counter on all of them.

### Risky at runtime
- **Circle near walls:** the arc is driven by velocity, so a wall in the way stops you short. The blow then lands
  only if you're still within `HIT_REACH` (3.8).
- **Latency:** the circle starts when the synced move arrives, and its progress is measured from the server's start
  tick. On a laggy connection it runs faster (capped at 1 block/tick) and may finish later than the blow.
- **Bosses and odd mobs:** the stun cancels `Mob.serverAiStep`. Mobs that act outside it (the Ender Dragon's phases,
  some modded mobs) won't be fully stopped.
