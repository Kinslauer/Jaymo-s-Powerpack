# 0.2.0 brief: Ascension and Corruption

Read `docs/AGENT_BRIEF.md` first. All of its rules still apply: environment, build command, "only touch your files",
id prefixes, lang parts, client/server split, multiplayer, original art, docs and the final report. This file adds
what's new for 0.2.0.

The mod has grown since that brief. Your power already has a lot of code. Read your power's package, client package,
mixins and `docs/<power>.md` before you change anything, and build on what's there. Don't rewrite it.

## What the user wants
Every power can **ascend**: a new form, plus one ascended ability with strong, reliable damage. Ascension comes from
an item or a milestone. Powers can also be **corrupted**, each in its own way. The user's words:

> I want these features to be emergent during gameplay for my friends.

So **nothing gets explained in game**:
- no tutorial text and no "do X to ascend"
- messages are short, atmospheric and vague
- item tooltips are cryptic
- the powers screen (H) only shows the ascended ability once you have it (the core already handles this)

Hints are welcome if they're subtle and in-world: a sound, a particle, a one-line whisper in the action bar at the
right moment.

The user will playtest and tune everything, so put every number in a named constant at the top of its class.

## Core API (already built, in the lead's 0.2.0 core commit)
- `com.jaymospowers.core.Fate` holds the per-player fate state. It is synced to all clients, saved, kept through
  death, and reset when the power changes.
  - Queries, which work on both sides: `isAscended(p)`, `isCorrupted(p)`, `isPurified(p)` and `corruption(p)`
    (0–100).
  - `canAscend(p)` is true when the player has a power, isn't ascended yet, and has mastered all 4 abilities. Gate
    your ascension on it.
  - Server-side changes:
    - `ascend(sp)` plays the fanfare, calls `Power.onAscended`, and counts as a level-up.
    - `addCorruption(sp, amount)` moves the meter. At 100 it calls `corrupt`. It does nothing once the player is
      corrupted or purified, or while they're in creative.
    - `corrupt(sp)` corrupts the power now, then calls `Power.onCorrupted`.
    - `purify(sp)` is the permanent cure. It calls `Power.onPurified`, and **the power can never be corrupted
      again**.
  - The core HUD badge shows the state: a gold rim when ascended, cracks and a purple tint when corrupted, and a thin
    purple sliver for the meter.
- New `Power` hooks:
  - `onUltimate(sp, down)`: the ascended ability key, **C** by default, "Power Ability 5". It is only sent while the
    power is ascended.
  - `onLevelUp(sp, level)`: `level` 2–4 means a mastery unlock, 5 means ascension. `/power level` also fires it when
    raising the level.
  - `onAscended(sp)`, `onCorrupted(sp)`, `onPurified(sp)`.
- New `PowerClient` hook: `onUltimate(localPlayer, down)`.
  `JaymosPowersClient.ultimateKey()` returns the key mapping, so your HUD can show the bound key.
- Lang keys (all optional) in your `lang/parts/<power>.json`:
  - `power.jaymospowers.<p>.ascended`, `.corrupted` and `.ascended_corrupted`: the power's name in those states,
    shown on the badge and the screen.
  - `power.jaymospowers.<p>.key.ultimate`: the line on the powers screen once ascended.
- Test commands (gamemaster):
  - `/power ascend <targets>`
  - `/power corrupt <targets> [amount]`
  - `/power purify <targets>`
  - `/power resetfate <targets>`
  - `/power level <targets> <1-4>`
  - `/power get <target>`, which also shows the fate state
  - For your own debug commands, register `/powerdebug <power> ...` from your package with
    `CommandRegistrationCallback`. Brigadier merges literals of the same name, so the four of you won't clash.
    Require `Commands.LEVEL_GAMEMASTERS`.

## Shared rules for 0.2.0
- **Ascension:**
  - Always gate it on `Fate.canAscend`.
  - The ascended ability needs a cooldown, shown on your HUD while you're ascended, with the bound key from
    `JaymosPowersClient.ultimateKey()`.
  - Its damage must be reliable: a guaranteed minimum, with no randomness or momentum needed to reach it.
- **Corruption** is a trade, not just a penalty. A corrupted power should be scary and strong, with real drawbacks.
- **The cure** comes from a specific circumstance in the world, never from an item. It calls `Fate.purify`, and after
  that the power can't be corrupted again (`Fate` enforces this).
- **Items:** register ascension items in your own package with `<power>_` ids. They need textures generated by your
  `tools/gen_<power>.py`. Keep them out of the creative tab. Players should find them, and `/give` works for testing.
- **Loot and worldgen:**
  - Use Fabric's `LootTableEvents.MODIFY` and `BiomeModifications`.
  - Datapack JSON goes under `src/main/resources/data/jaymospowers/...`. Prefix file names with your power.
- **Performance:**
  - The user's machine is an i5-6600K with a GTX 960, and the dedicated server is shared.
  - No per-tick world scans bigger than a few chunks.
  - Throttle particles, and spawn them on the client from synced state where you can.
- **Docs:** add a `## 0.2.0: Ascension & Corruption` section to `docs/<power>.md`. It must cover:
  - the exact triggers and numbers
  - how to test each piece with commands
  - what's risky or untested at runtime
- **Commit** in your worktree when the build is green, as described in `docs/AGENT_BRIEF.md`. Don't bump the version.
  The lead merges and bumps it.
