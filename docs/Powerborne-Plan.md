# Powerborne Heroes plan

Andy linked Powerborne Heroes (Palladium add-on, Forge/Fabric 1.20.1, All Rights Reserved) on 2026-11-19. Ideas only:
our own code, names and vanilla-style art, no hero names. Pick list: https://claude.ai/artifact/81KKHZFULY1YPDFRWb3FAH
(picks in its db at `picks/andy`). He picked 34 of 40 (not: pb-swat, pb-sun, pb-xray, pb-bane, pb-dog, pb-orbit).
His row notes are quoted in each track. One branch (`claude/project-thread-bncprk`), one commit per track, one PR.

## Track 1: Deeds (core awakening, Web crater spider, Alchemy, Xeno-Band)
- **pb-deed**: each power can wake early by one deed that fits it, next to the 3-5 day awakening timer. Only the
  power the player leaned toward (or fate's pick) wakes; a deed never gives a second power.
- **pb-spider**: a cave spider shut in a glass jar with meteoric iron dust becomes a crater spider; its bite wakes a
  Web-leaning player, anyone else gets sick. Andy: "a tinkerer villager should hint at this process" (the Mad
  Tinkerer's talk, an oblique hint, no screen text stating lore).
- **pb-vessel**: Andy: "the xenoform band. it retains scans and upgrades". The band itself is the vessel: taken off,
  handed on or dropped, it keeps its scans and upgrades for its next wearer.
- **pb-draught**: a rare Alchemy brew for players with no power (speed, jump, swim, slower hunger, a second jump).

## Track 2: Storm (Wind's Stormbound, the war hammer, Sora's Dagger)
- **pb-skycharge** draw from the sky in a storm; full charge opens a short storm state.
- **pb-hammer** a thrown war hammer: lightning where it lands; ride it (burst on landing, bounce foe to foe); spin to fly.
- **pb-recall** a bound weapon flies back from anywhere. **pb-twin** two storm weapons together fire one bolt.
- **pb-stormland** trigger, jump, lightning round you on landing. **pb-road** mark a place; a storm column carries you
  back (a teleport in the world, not a portal).
- **pb-worthy**: Andy: "work this into how soras dagger works": one discovery of Sora's Dagger only the worthy can
  take (many monsters killed, a village saved); the unworthy strain at it.

## Track 3: Web
- **pb-blossom** leap and web everything in sight. **pb-darksense** faint night sight in deep dark, toggled.
- **pb-whirl**: Andy: "i think we had something like this? ours was too difficult to pull off but we do use two hands
  though, so swap other than that or add both". Find the existing whirl, make it easy, keep both variants.
- **pb-webshield**: Andy: "right clicking that should equip it as a 3 hit no knockback shield".
- **pb-gaps** crawl through one-block gaps on walls and ceilings, pass through cobwebs.

## Track 4: A second jump for every power (pb-jump)
Andy: "all of them, arsenal uses a portal atop a mountain so it's peak projects him further up, chrono jumps off a
speed remnant wind just off air, so can wind, fire, and lightning sorcerers and any others you think applicable".

## Track 5: Costumes and tips
- **pb-patterns** pattern scraps unlock costume pieces; bosses, buried treasure, bastions, the wandering trader. Andy:
  "vigilantes and other costumed MCA will drop theirs as well."
- **pb-stand** costumes on armour stands. Andy: "can we work metal plates into the designs too? or allow users to place
  them in the table? could add armor values to the system as a whole, accomodate different styles, and give more
  incentive to these new systems".
- **pb-tips** first-time tips for every power's tricky inputs (TipCard, like ZFighterTips).

## Track 6: Light, dark and fire
- **pb-shell** (Andy: "div born") golden absorption hearts build while unhurt. **pb-ward** (Andy: "div born") a
  healing dome that pushes foes out.
- **pb-banish** (Andy: "smoke") a foe thrown into the smoke, gone a while; hitting the spot brings it back sooner.
- **pb-darkcorrupt** corruption builds faster from hits taken, kills and full dark.
- **pb-firecrash** Ember rise and crash. **pb-cook** cook raw food in an Emberborn's off hand while sneaking.
  **pb-blastsoak** a held fire shield drinks explosions into heat.

## Track 7: Fusion
- **pb-fusevillager** a Z Fighter fuses with a willing villager; the fusion gains their job's knack.
- **pb-reins** either partner hands over control at will; the one inside can ask to split.
- **pb-partner** fusion earrings show where the partner is.

## Track 8: Shield and rhythm
- **pb-shieldfall** block looking down as you land and the shield takes part of the fall.
- **pb-airslam** the shield slam grows with the drop. **pb-rising** launch up carrying the foes round you.
- **pb-rhythm** keep fighting without a break and attacks quicken a little; fades when you stop.

## Lore
Every new creature, item or deed gets a `(new)` paragraph in Lore.md for Andy to approve, quoted in full in the thread.
