## Hearing and stealth (Mob Senses)
Today only zombies hear sounds by kind; other monsters just notice a walking player within 8 blocks. These fill in the rest.
- [x] **ms-everyone** Every monster hears (M): Skeletons, spiders, illagers, orcs, raiders and the rest hear sounds and go to where they came from, each with its own ears. Ours: Awareness's sound lures, today zombie-only, for every hostile; a few deaf kinds stay deaf.
- [x] **ms-walls** Sound through walls by what they're made of (M): Each block between you and a mob muffles the sound: stone a lot, wool more, wood and glass less, an open door not at all. Ours: A ray through the blocks on every heard sound (code borrowed with credit).
- [x] **ms-surfaces** Footsteps by ground (S): Wool and carpet are quiet underfoot, gravel and metal loud, wet ground louder; Feather Falling boots and Swift Sneak leggings quieten your steps. Ours: The footstep sound of Awareness; joins Quiet Step and Rangers' quiet tread.
- [x] **ms-weather** Rain and thunder cover you (S): Rain and storms mask noise outdoors, and a loud nearby sound (thunder, a bell, a blast) drowns quieter steps for a moment. Ours: Awareness reach scaled by weather and recent loud sounds.
- [x] **ms-decoys** Throw something to draw them off (S): A dropped or thrown item makes a noise where it lands: iron or a bell carries far and pulls guards and monsters to it, wool lands soft. Ours: Our throwing (it spends the item: no free projectiles) and coin tossing.
- [x] **ms-suspicion** Look first, then come (S): A faint sound at the edge of hearing makes a mob stop and turn toward it; only a second sound brings it over. A searching mob looks about the spot a while, then goes back. Ours: Senses' search goal; shown by the mob's head and stance, no text.
- [x] **ms-ring** Call friends and fan out (S): A mob that hears you alerts nearby friends, and they spread round the spot in a ring instead of piling onto one block. Ours: Awareness and band leaders' calls.
- [x] **ms-guns** Gunshots carry, suppressors don't (S): Shots carry far (44 blocks), suppressed ones only close (16); a bullet striking a far wall makes noise there and can lure a horde away. Ours: TacZ guns and our muskets and cannons (black powder is never quiet).
- [x] **ms-voice** Mobs hear your voice chat (M): With voice chat, monsters hear you talk: a shout carries far, a whisper only close, walls muffle it. Loudness is measured against your own normal voice, so a quiet mic isn't free stealth. Ours: Simple Voice Chat is already in the pack (VoiceRange); guards at night hear trespassers too.
- [x] **ms-blood** Blood trails for every hunter (S): Below half health you leave a blood trail that spiders, wolves and werewolves follow too, not only zombies. Ours: Awareness blood spots and werewolf scent trails.
- [x] **ms-meter** Fuller stealth meter (S): The stealth gauge also shows how far your noise carries, how lit you are, whether you're bleeding, and when you're talking or whispering. Ours: Our existing gauge; bars and icons only.
- [ ] NOT PICKED **ms-ship** Ship Mob Senses as it is (S): Drop the 26.3 jar in unchanged, with its own HUD and settings screen. Ours: Not recommended: it doubles up with our senses and adds slash commands. Pick this only instead of the rows above.

- [x] **gb-faith** Pick your faith in the life story (S): A page of the eight patrons, each with its own icon, after heritage: who you grew up praying to. It sets your patron from the start. Ours: LifeStory has no faith page today; patrons are found later (PatronScreen).
- [x] **gb-realmcards** Realm page with its people (M): The realms page shows a person from each realm in that realm's dress; the one you pick walks over to a review card with your stats, realm and faith before you confirm. Ours: LifeStory's realms page and its read-back; dress from Outfits by climate.
- [x] **gb-customs** Realms with their own customs (M): Each land's realms keep their own ways: duels allowed in the north, a water toll in the sands, a bow on greeting in Blossom, market days on the plains, and what's a crime differs. - [x] **gb-troops** Troop ladders (L): Recruits you hire from a realm rise through a tree as they fight: militia, then soldier, then knight or archer, then veteran. Each realm has its own tree and kit. Ours: Drifters, Veterans and Learners become one ladder per realm.
- [x] **gb-formations** Formations and commands (M): Order your followers into a line, wedge, square or column, place them where you point, hold, charge, or set one onto a target. Ours: The Followers screen and quick-order keys gain formations.
- [x] **gb-castles** Castles change hands (M): Keeps and castles can be taken in war and held by the winner, flying their colours, with the map showing who holds what. Ours: Wars and vassal seats; Xaero's world map draws the realms' borders and holders (compat).
- [x] **gb-tent** Pitch a tent, claim a homestead (S): A tent pitched on a laid foundation claims a small patch as yours: villagers count it as your property and it's where your settlement starts. Ours: Own-blocks-no-crime and player crowns.
- [x] **gb-capes** Capes on armour (S): Lords' and knights' chest armour carries a cape in the realm's colours that sways as they move. Ours: Powers' ClothCape on Kingdoms armour sets.
- [x] **gb-sails** Sails that ripple (S): Sails billow with gusts, flap when you turn across the wind and hang slack in calm. Ours: Our bellied sails drawn as moving cloth.
- [x] **gb-haggle** Haggle a job's pay (S): Before taking work you can ask for more; the poster's temperament decides whether they agree, meet you halfway or take offence. Ours: Board work and tale offers.
