# Flintlock guns: credits

- `musket.bbmodel`: "Flintlock/Musket Rifle" by 9greenworks, Blockbench Workshop
  (https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/9greenworks/flintlock-musket-rifle-commission), CC BY 4.0.
  Changes: bones renamed, its cartridge and its own flash left out, a socket bayonet and a muzzle flash added, its reload
  kept (fitted to WeaponMod's reload time, muzzle turned up), the other animations ours.
- `flintlock.bbmodel`: "Dueling Pistol", Blockbench Workshop (maker's name not in the file), CC BY 4.0.
  Changes: cut into body, hammer and ramrod bones, a muzzle flash added, animations ours; the Void Flintlock's sheet is
  the same pistol recoloured.
- `blunderbuss.bbmodel`: the Flintlock (`pistol` model, `pistol_texture`: a long gun with a flared mouth) from Frontier
  Armaments by cleannrooster (https://github.com/cleannrooster/frontier_armaments), MIT licence (copy in
  `tools/bbmodels/frontier-armaments/LICENSE`).
  Changes: its display tilt dropped, laid along the long-gun hold, its cock cut into its own bone, the faces its maker
  left unpainted (Blockbench's template colours) painted like their neighbours, a ramrod (our own) and a muzzle flash
  added, animations ours.
- `musketoon.bbmodel` (Kingdoms' musketoon): the Firecannon (`rifle_dwarven` model, `gun_texture`) from the same mod, MIT.
  Changes: its display tilt dropped, scaled 1.6x and laid along the long-gun hold, its hammer cut into its own bone,
  its red breech gem painted steel, a ramrod (our own) and a muzzle flash added, animations ours.
- The muzzle flash picture is original, painted by the same script.

All of it is generated by `tools/gen_guns_geckolib.py`; change the numbers there and run it again.
`<gun>-animations.png` shows each gun's poses (idle, fire, reload, draw, spent) as `tools/bbmodel_preview.py` draws them;
`blunderbuss-preview.png` and `musketoon-preview.png` show those two from three sides (no pose sheets yet).
GeckoLib (MIT, Tslat and contributors) is a runtime library; none of its code or art is in these files.
