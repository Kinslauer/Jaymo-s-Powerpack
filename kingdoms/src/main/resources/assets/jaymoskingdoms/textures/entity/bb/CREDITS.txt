The makers claim them as their own work; we've changed them only as noted.

chieftain.png, chieftain_1.png (bbmodels/chieftain.json): the orc warchief's body
  "Chieftain" by nyxaris, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/nyxaris/chieftain
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; drawn at 0.69 size; its animations played by the warchief's pace, blows,
  slam and roar.

textures/entity/silver_skeleton.png (geckolib/models/entity/silver_skeleton.geo.json): the silver skeleton
  "Dead Traveler (Skeleton)" by polyjo, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/polyjo/dead-traveler-skeleton
  Changes: turned into GeckoLib's format (tools/bbmodel_geckolib.py), its three textures (body, sword, buckler) stacked
  on one sheet; a "rise" animation of our own added (kingdoms/art/silver_skeleton.bbmodel).

fire_dragon.png, geckolib/models/entity/fire_dragon.geo.json, geckolib/animations/entity/fire_dragon.animation.json,
and dragon/notus/ and dragon/boreas/ hide_*.png, marks_*.png: Notus and Boreas
  "Fire Dragon" by nyxaris, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/nyxaris/fire-dragon-minecraft
  Changes: turned into GeckoLib's format without the small floating piece over its head; animations of our own added
  to its wingbeat: standing, walking, sitting, crashed, landing, gliding, a bite and a tail sweep
  (kingdoms/art/fire_dragon.bbmodel, kingdoms/tools/gen_fire_dragon_geo.py); drawn at 0.53 (Notus) and 0.45
  (Boreas) size. Notus's hides are its texture and three re-shadings of it; Boreas's are it recoloured to ice; both
  species' glowing markings are its glow half (kingdoms/tools/gen_fire_dragons.py).

geckolib/models/entity/zephyrus.geo.json, geckolib/animations/entity/zephyrus.animation.json,
and dragon/zephyrus/hide_*.png, marks_*.png: Zephyrus
  Our own model and art (no external source). kingdoms/art/zephyrus.bbmodel and kingdoms/tools/gen_zephyrus_geo.py:
  the old ZephyrusDragonModel's body and poses as idle, walk, fly, hover, glide, landing, sitting, crashed and
  swimming, plus a bite, a tail sweep and a death; hides and markings from kingdoms/tools/gen_zephyrus.py.

geckolib/models/entity/aetherion.geo.json, geckolib/animations/entity/aetherion.animation.json,
and dragon/aetherion/hide_*.png, marks_*.png: Aetherion
  Body-plan reference: the public-domain/CC0 GeckoLib Aetherion supplied by Andy (its author and URL weren't recorded).
  The model, hides, markings and animations are rebuilt as our own art; no external model, texture or animation is used.
  kingdoms/art/aetherion.bbmodel and kingdoms/tools/gen_aetherion_geo.py: idle, walk, fly, glide, landing, sitting,
  crashed, bite, Cataclysm and tail sweep; drawn at 1.25 size, hatchlings at the usual 0.35 of that.

kintsugi.png (bbmodels/kintsugi.json), and dragon/eurus/, dragon/aeolus/ and dragon/ignivorus/ hide_*.png, marks_*.png:
  Eurus, Aeolus and Ignivorus
  "Kintsugi" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/kintsugi
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; walking, sitting and gliding posed by our own code over its own animations;
  drawn at 0.28 (Eurus) and 0.75 (Aeolus) size. Aeolus's hides are its texture lifted grey-white in four shades, his
  markings its gold seams glowing (gold, the four winds' colours, white-gold, silver); Eurus's hides are it turned
  storm-blue and violet, his markings its seams in electric cyan; Ignivorus (drawn at 0.55) has it in obsidian, basalt
  ash, ember and crimson, its seams glowing as lava cracks (kingdoms/tools/gen_kintsugi_dragons.py).
