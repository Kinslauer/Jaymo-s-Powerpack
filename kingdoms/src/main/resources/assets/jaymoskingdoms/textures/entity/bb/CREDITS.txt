Blockbench Workshop models drawn by Kingdoms (baked by tools/bbmodel_bake.py into assets/jaymoskingdoms/bbmodels/,
their textures here). All are licensed under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The makers claim them as their own work; we've changed them only as noted.

chieftain.png, chieftain_1.png (bbmodels/chieftain.json): the orc warchief's body
  "Chieftain" by nyxaris, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/nyxaris/chieftain
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; drawn at 0.69 size; its animations played by the warchief's pace, blows,
  slam and roar.

canon.png (bbmodels/canon.json): the field cannon
  "Canon" by iexogen, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/iexogen/canon
  Changes: baked to our loader's format, the barrel's pivot moved onto its trunnions so it can aim; drawn at 0.93 size.

dead_traveler.png (bbmodels/dead_traveler.json): the silver skeleton
  "Dead Traveler (Skeleton)" by polyjo, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/polyjo/dead-traveler-skeleton
  Changes: baked to our loader's format, its three textures (body, sword, buckler) stacked on one sheet.

fire_dragon.png (bbmodels/fire_dragon.json), and dragon/notus/ and dragon/boreas/ hide_*.png, marks_*.png: Notus and Boreas
  "Fire Dragon" by nyxaris, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/nyxaris/fire-dragon-minecraft
  Changes: baked to our loader's format without the small floating piece over its head; posed on the ground, walking,
  sitting and landing by our own code (it came with a flying pose and wingbeat only); drawn at 0.53 (Notus) and 0.45
  (Boreas) size. Notus's hides are its texture and three re-shadings of it; Boreas's are it recoloured to ice; both
  species' glowing markings are its glow half (kingdoms/tools/gen_fire_dragons.py).

clockwork_golem.png, clockwork_golem_1.png (bbmodels/clockwork_golem.json), and textures/item/clockwork_golem.png:
the clockwork golem
  "Mech Golem" by octopusstudio, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/octopusstudio/mech-golem
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; drawn at 0.94 size; its lit texture while it's awake, its unlit one folded;
  the item icon is the model drawn small (kingdoms/tools/gen_clockwork_golem.py).

reverent_warplate.png (bbmodels/reverent_warplate.json), and textures/item/reverent_*.png: the Reverent Warplate armour
  "Reverent Warplate" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/reverent-warplate
  Changes: baked to our loader's format and hung on the wearer's bones by our own armour layer (it was made for
  GeckoLib); the item icons are its pieces drawn small with an outline (kingdoms/tools/gen_reverent.py).

kintsugi.png (bbmodels/kintsugi.json), and dragon/eurus/ and dragon/aeolus/ hide_*.png, marks_*.png: Eurus and Aeolus
  "Kintsugi" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/kintsugi
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; walking, sitting and gliding posed by our own code over its own animations;
  drawn at 0.28 (Eurus) and 0.75 (Aeolus) size. Aeolus's hides are its texture lifted grey-white in four shades, his
  markings its gold seams glowing (gold, the four winds' colours, white-gold, silver); Eurus's hides are it turned
  storm-blue and violet, his markings its seams in electric cyan (kingdoms/tools/gen_kintsugi_dragons.py).

minotaur.png (bbmodels/minotaur.json): the minotaur
  "Minotaur" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/minotaur
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; drawn at 0.85 size; walks with its run (it came without a walk).

piglin_king.png (bbmodels/piglin_king.json): the Piglin King
  "Musclebound Piglin King" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/piglin-king
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; drawn at 0.96 size; its ice-themed moves play as fire attacks.

redstone_golem.png, redstone_golem_1.png (bbmodels/redstone_golem.json): the redstone golem
  "Redstone Golem (Minecraft Dungeons)" by polyjo, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/polyjo/redstone-golem-minecraft-dungeons
  Changes: baked to our loader's format; drawn at 0.94 size; its glow texture drawn bright in the dark; its pound and
  eruption posed by our own code (it came with an idle and a walk). The design is Mojang's Minecraft Dungeons mob.

scalebane_archer.png (bbmodels/scalebane_archer.json): the scalebane sellswords
  "Scalebane Archer" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/scalebane-archer
