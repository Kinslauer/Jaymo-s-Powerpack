The illager kinds' textures here, their spawn-egg icons (textures/item/*_spawn_egg.png for the provoker, invoker, basher,
archivist, illager inquisitor, marauder, alchemist, firecaller and surrendered) and their sounds (sounds/illagers/) are
from Illager Invasion by Dricky, Safro, Zeckemith and Fuzs (a port of Illager Expansion), under the MIT licence.
Their code was ported to 26.3 in com.jaymoskingdoms.illagers and com.jaymoskingdoms.client.illagers. The inquisitor's
texture is inquisitor.png; ours calls the mob the illager inquisitor.
