Used in Jaymo's Kingdoms:
- The musketoon's item model and texture (assets/jaymoskingdoms/models/item/musketoon.json, textures/item/musketoon.png):
  the mod's Firecannon (rifle_dwarven model, gun_texture), its display tilt dropped, scaled and laid along our long-gun
  hold, its red breech gem painted steel, a ramrod of our own added. Built by tools/gen_guns_geckolib.py.
- The ten armour sets of FrontierArmour (assets/jaymoskingdoms/bbmodels/frontier_*.json, textures/entity/bb/frontier_*.png,
  textures/item/<look>_<piece>.png): the mod's Templar, Battlemage (our Hedge garb), Primalist, Artillerist (our Ranger's
  harness), Peasant (our settler's gear), Juggernaut, Thief (our Scoundrel's wrappings), Sanguine, Despot and Foxshade
  models and textures, baked for our worn-model layer. Faces its maker left unpainted are painted like their
  neighbours; the Despot's helm and greaves and the blue and purple Sanguine hat and boots are our own additions.
  Built by kingdoms/tools/gen_frontier_armour.py from tools/bbmodels/frontier-armaments/armour/.
- The item-use speed mixin (client ItemUseSpeedMixin) follows the mod's own LocalPlayerMixin.
