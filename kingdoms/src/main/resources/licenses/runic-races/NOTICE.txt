The heritage kits of Jaymo's Kingdoms (each heritage and look has one ability on the heritage key, one gift and one flaw;
com.jaymoskingdoms.HeritageKits) take their ideas, their ability and race names (Shadowmeld, Frostbind, Tremorsense, Forge
Blessing, Web Snare, Wind Burst, Howl of the Pack, Pounce, Foxfire Illusion, Shed Skin, Undying Hunger, Conscript the Dead,
Spectral Phase, Soul Harvest, Mirror Shift, Phase Shift, Faerie Bargain, Arcane Reflex, Moonlit Veil, Blood Frenzy and the
rest) and their rough numbers from Runic Races (https://github.com/otectus/runic-races) by Otectus, licensed under the MIT
License (LICENSE, beside this file).

No code, texture or other asset of Runic Races is copied: the code is written new for Minecraft 26.3 and Fabric.
