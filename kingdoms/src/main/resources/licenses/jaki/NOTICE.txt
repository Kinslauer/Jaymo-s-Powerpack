The galleon, the cargo ship, the trader ship, the war trireme and the drakkar that Jaymo's Kingdoms' pirates, navies
and merchants sail are the ships of Jaki Versatile Structures: Sails & Sea
(https://github.com/jackieLama/jvssailandsea, https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jaki-versatile-structures-sails-sea),
by Jackiefr (structures) and TheGoldenWorld, licensed under the GNU General Public License v3 (LICENSE, beside this
file).

Taken from it, under that licence, unchanged: the structure files illager_galleon.nbt, cargo_ship.nbt,
villager_trader_ship.nbt, roman_war_trireme.nbt and drakkar.nbt (data/jaymoskingdoms/structure/jaki/).

When a ship is launched (com.jaymoskingdoms.sailing.JakiShips) the game reads them and changes its copy: their crews,
water and decorative block data are left out, a helm is added, the galleon's blackstone guns become ship's cannons,
the trireme and the drakkar are given guns along their rails, one colour of their sails takes the crew's colours, and
their barrels' loot comes from vanilla shipwreck and outpost tables.
