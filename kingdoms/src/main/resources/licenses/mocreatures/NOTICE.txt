The war elephant and the woolly mammoth in Jaymo's Kingdoms wear the elephant of DrZhark's Mo' Creatures, and our
horses, skeleton horses and zombie horses still move as its horse does
(https://github.com/DrZhark/mocreaturesdev, https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mo-creatures), by DrZhark
(code, animation) and BlockDaddy and DrZhark (models, textures), licensed under the GNU General Public License v3
(LICENSE, beside this file).

Taken from it, under that licence:
- the elephant model and its animation (MoCModelElephant, 1.10.2 branch), ported to 26.3 as WarElephantModel
- the elephant textures elephantindian, elephantafrican, elephantindianpretty and mammoth (the originals are in
  kingdoms/tools/mocreatures/), which became the hides in assets/jaymoskingdoms/textures/entity/war_elephant/:
  indian, african, royal and woolly as they are, russet and frost tinted from the mammoth; every hide also carries the
  mammoth's war platform and a banner of ours (kingdoms/tools/gen_elephant_hides.py)

- the horse's animation (MoCModelNewHorse, 1.10.2 branch: the jointed legs, the head, neck and mane moving together,
  the mouth, the tail, the saddle's stirrups and reins, and the pegasus wings, for the bat horse), which moved its
  model as MocHorseModel from 0.5.44 and moves our own horse model, BreedHorseModel, since 0.7.305, on bones of the
  same names and pivots. (From 0.5.44 to 0.7.304 we also drew its horse model and coats painted over its textures
  horsewhite, horseskeleton, horseundead, horsebat, horsenightmare and the armoured horses; since 0.7.305 the model's
  boxes and every coat and armour are our own, kingdoms/tools/gen_horses.py, and those textures are no longer used.)

- (0.5.56) the monsters of legend: the werewolf (MoCModelWere, and MoCEntityWerewolf's change by night, its hide that
  only gold or silver cuts, its four faces and four coats), the ogre (MoCModelOgre: one head or two and a club), the
  wraith (MoCModelWraith) and the silver skeleton (MoCModelSilverSkeleton), each with its animation, ported to 26.3 as
  WerewolfModel, OgreModel, WraithModel and SilverSkeletonModel; the textures wolfblack, wolfbrown, wolftimber,
  wolffire1-3, weredude, werehuman, wereoldie, werewoman, ogregreen, ogrered, wraith, flamewraith and silverskeleton
  (the originals are in kingdoms/tools/mocreatures/), as they are, under our names in
  assets/jaymoskingdoms/textures/entity/legend/, and ogre_cave there: ogregreen's skin turned slate grey
  (kingdoms/tools/gen_legends.py). (Since 0.7.321 the silver skeleton is drawn as polyjo's Dead Traveler, CC BY 4.0;
  SilverSkeletonModel and silverskeleton are no longer used.)

WarElephantModel and BreedHorseModel are likewise licensed under the GNU GPL v3. Their source is in
the Jaymo's Powerpack repository (kingdoms/src/client/java/com/jaymoskingdoms/client/equestrian/). So are the monsters
of legend (kingdoms/src/main/java/com/jaymoskingdoms/monsters/ and kingdoms/src/client/java/com/jaymoskingdoms/client/
monsters/).
