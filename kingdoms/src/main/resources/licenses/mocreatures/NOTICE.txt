The horses, skeleton horses and zombie horses, the war elephant and the woolly mammoth in Jaymo's Kingdoms wear the
horse and the elephant of DrZhark's Mo' Creatures
(https://github.com/DrZhark/mocreaturesdev, https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mo-creatures), by DrZhark
(code, animation) and BlockDaddy and DrZhark (models, textures), licensed under the GNU General Public License v3
(LICENSE, beside this file).

Taken from it, under that licence:
- the elephant model and its animation (MoCModelElephant, 1.10.2 branch), ported to 26.3 as WarElephantModel
- the elephant textures elephantindian, elephantafrican, elephantindianpretty and mammoth (the originals are in
  kingdoms/tools/mocreatures/), which became the hides in assets/jaymoskingdoms/textures/entity/war_elephant/:
  indian, african, royal and woolly as they are, russet and frost tinted from the mammoth; every hide also carries the
  mammoth's war platform and a banner of ours (kingdoms/tools/gen_elephant_hides.py)

- the horse model and its animation (MoCModelNewHorse, 1.10.2 branch), without its wings, horn, mule ears and bags,
  ported to 26.3 as MocHorseModel
- the horse textures horsewhite, horseskeleton and horseundead, and the armoured horsewhitemetal, horsewhitegold and
  horsewhitediamond (the originals are in kingdoms/tools/mocreatures/), which became the textures in
  assets/jaymoskingdoms/textures/entity/moc_horse/: our breed coats painted onto horsewhite and shaded with it; the
  skeleton and undead horse as they are (bone, rotten) and recoloured (sooty, mossy, pale, mottled); the armour lifted
  off the armoured horses as overlays (iron, gold, diamond) and recoloured from the metal one (copper, netherite,
  leather, chain and plate barding) (kingdoms/tools/gen_moc_horses.py)

WarElephantModel, MocHorseModel and MocHorseRenderer are likewise licensed under the GNU GPL v3. Their source is in
the Jaymo's Powerpack repository (kingdoms/src/client/java/com/jaymoskingdoms/client/equestrian/).
