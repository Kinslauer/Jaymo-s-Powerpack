Loot goblins in Jaymo's Kingdoms are a port to Minecraft 26.3 of Loot Goblins by SkylorBeck
(https://github.com/Skylorbeck/lootgoblins, https://modrinth.com/mod/loot-goblins), Copyright (c) 2022 SkylorBeck,
licensed under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3 (LICENSE, beside this file).

Taken from it, under that licence:
- the textures in assets/jaymoskingdoms/textures/entity/loot_goblin/ (the goblin's eight skins and the minor goblins' skins)
- the item textures gold_bone, iron_eye, stoneflesh, quartzchop, red_bomb, prismarine_pearl and emerald_crossbow_*
- the goblin's model and animations (carried over into LootGoblinModel), its loot tables and odds

The Java code in com.jaymoskingdoms.lootgoblins and com.jaymoskingdoms.client.lootgoblins is rewritten for 26.3 and is
likewise licensed under the LGPL v3. Its source is in the Jaymo's Powerpack repository (kingdoms/src).
