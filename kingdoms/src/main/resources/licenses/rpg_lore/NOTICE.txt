The war journals, the data-driven lore books (data/<namespace>/lore/*.json) and the Lore Codex in Jaymo's Kingdoms follow an
idea from RPG Lore (lore books dropped by mobs and kept in a collection, with books added as data and given drop filters),
a mod licensed under the MIT License.

Only the idea is used. No code, text, texture or data file of RPG Lore is copied: the book builder, the drop filters, the
codex, its screen, every page of every journal and all the art here are written and drawn for Jaymo's Kingdoms.
