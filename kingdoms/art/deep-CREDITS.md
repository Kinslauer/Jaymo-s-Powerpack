# Deep beasts: asset credit

The body shapes of the Deep Lancer, Deep Gloomwing and Deep Behemoth are the sea-beast rigs of the **Rim** mod
(ThePoupyBuTers, CC0-1.0; CurseForge project 1749934, file 9566319, `rim-1.4.1+26.3-fabric.jar`), kept as Andy asked
(2026-11-25: sea beasts "can keep the shapes", repainted and renamed).

- `tools/rim_port.py` copies each rig's parts and boxes to `assets/jaymoskingdoms/deep_models/<kind>.json`, repaints its
  sheet (sea-teal hide to abyssal violet; the glow sheet to amber) and draws the spawn egg. The rigs' names, textures and
  film names are not kept: nothing here carries a Pacific Rim name.
- The animation, behaviour and everything in `com.jaymoskingdoms.deep` are ours.
