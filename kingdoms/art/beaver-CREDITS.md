# Beaver

The beaver's model (`geckolib/models/entity/beaver.geo.json`), animations (`geckolib/animations/entity/beaver.animation.json`),
texture (`textures/entity/beaver.png`), call (`sounds/entity/beaver_ambient.ogg`) and pelt icon (`textures/item/beaver_pelt.png`)
are from **Eager Beavers** by sqwigly (https://github.com/bodhiahn/eager-beavers), released under CC0 1.0
(`beaver-LICENSE.txt`). Its ways (felling, carrying logs, damming, swimming and diving, taming with sticks) are the mod's,
rewritten for Minecraft 26.3 in `inhabitants/Beaver.java`. The mod ships no Blockbench project, so the GeckoLib files
were copied as they are.
