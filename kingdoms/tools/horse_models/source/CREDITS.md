# mystical_deer.bbmodel

"Mystical Deer" by jsellier23, from Blockbench Workshop (https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/jsellier23/mystical-deer),
licensed CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The author notes AI was used in making it.

The file here is unchanged. tools/horseify.py and tools/moonlit_horse.py make the moonlit horse from it: they take off
the antlers and wisteria, bulk up and lengthen the body, bulk the legs and head, shorten and lean the neck, shorten the
cannons, lengthen the snout, stand the ears up, add a silver mane, forelock and tail, scale it to the classic horse's
height, and bake its texture into our box-UV layout (textures/entity/breed_horse/moonlit/moonlit.png).
