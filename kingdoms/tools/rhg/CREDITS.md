# Credits for tools/rhg

Horse coat patterns (dapples, roan, tobiano pinto, leopard spots, face markings, socks and stockings, the dun stripe,
and the coat shading) are drawn from the masks of Realistic Horse Genetics by sekelsta
(https://github.com/sekelsta/horse-colors), with leopard textures by faainleog, licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: the masks were carried from that mod's horse model onto ours (each pixel matched to the same place on the
horse, scaled to our proportions), firmed up or flecked, and coloured and combined with our own coats by
kingdoms/tools/gen_horses.py. Nothing else of that mod is used.

The files here are unchanged copies of src/main/resources/assets/horse_colors/textures/entity/horse/ from that repository.
