Klei_Wright's Tundra art
========================

Every image in this folder (and pack.png) is by Klei_Wright, from "Tundra Game Drop Assets":
https://www.planetminecraft.com/texture-pack/tundra-game-drop-assets/
made for the video "How can we make Minecraft feel alive? (It isn't structures)":
https://youtu.be/cBmt2FNP1Ug

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
(Klei_Wright offers these assets "under the same license as Matcha", his Matcha Flavoured pack.)

This folder is its own resource pack under that licence, separate from the mod's code and art. The files are his,
unchanged; only a subset is here (the textures that have a block of ours to go on), with pack.mcmeta and this file
added. Switch it off under Options > Resource Packs to see the mod's own art for the same blocks.

Not included from his pack: the muskox model and coats (they fit his own model, not ours), his glow lichen models,
and the recoloured wool and packed ice.

Klei_Wright does not endorse this mod.
