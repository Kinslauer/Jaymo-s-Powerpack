Blockbench models used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They're baked from their .bbmodel files (tools/bbmodels/) to our own loader's format by tools/bbmodel_bake.py; the
loader itself (client/bb/BbModel.java, Molang.java) is our own code, shared with Jaymo's Kingdoms.

visage_of_war.png (bbmodels/visage_of_war.json), and textures/item/aether/visage_of_war_spawn_egg.png: the Visage of War,
the infected guardian tree's boss
  "Visage of War" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/visage-of-war
  Changes: baked to our loader's format (it was made for GeckoLib); drawn at 1.5 size; its animations played by our
  own boss's moves. The spawn egg is the Slider's egg recoloured gold, not part of the model.

Item and costume models (also CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

models/item/armory/iron_dagger.json and textures/item/armory/iron_dagger.png: the Armory's iron dagger
  "Traveler Dagger" by polyjo, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/polyjo/traveler-dagger-polyjo
  Changes: turned into an item model (tools/blockbench/traveler_dagger.py); held like our other 3D blades.

models/item/soul_saber.json and textures/item/dreaded_blade(_fx).png: the Dreaded Blade (the relic once called the Soul Saber)
  "Weapon - Dreaded Blade (Animated)" by capellastudio, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/capellastudio/weapon-dreaded-blade-animated
  Changes: turned into an item model (tools/blockbench/dreaded_blade.py), its runes drawn at full light; held like our
  other 3D blades. Its maker asks that it not be resold on its own.

textures/entity/costume/suit/thief*.png and mask/thief_hood*.png: the costume table's Thief's Leathers and Thief's Hood
  "Thief Armor" by eanncrod, https://blockbenchworkshop.com/model/eanncrod/thief-armor
  Changes: its cubes built into our costume models (client/costume/CostumeModels.thief); its texture split into the two
  dye masks and turned grey for tinting (tools/blockbench/thief_costume.py).
