The future's four cars (runabout, hauler, bruiser, dasher) are ported from the Cars mod by Pokato (MIT, LICENSE beside this file;
cars-fabric-26.3 1.16.2). tools/car_port.py converts four of its meshes and textures; the glass and the roof are left off, and
the doors and the wheels are placed by our own code. Names, behaviour and fuel are ours.
The Frostborn and Tinkerer rigs are our own android-mech model scaled up; they use nothing from Rim.
